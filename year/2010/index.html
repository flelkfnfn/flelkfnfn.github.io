Born in 2010 · Gen Z

Baby names of 2010

The Social Security name files, which cover every name given to at least 5 babies, list 3,696,271 babies born in 2010: 1,777,595 girls and 1,918,676 boys. Isabella and Jacob were the most popular names, and 9.0% of all babies got a top-10 name, down from 11.9% in 2000. The name most tied to 2010 is Addyson: among girls, it was 11× as common that year as in the decades around it.

How many babies got a top-10 name?

In 2010, 9.0% of babies were given one of that year's 10 most popular names. Across all years, that share fell from 29.0% in 1935 to 7.5% in 2025.

Born in 1998 · Gen Z

Baby names of 1998

The Social Security name files, which cover every name given to at least 5 babies, list 3,678,315 babies born in 1998: 1,766,949 girls and 1,911,366 boys. Emily and Michael were the most popular names, and 13.0% of all babies got a top-10 name, down from 19.4% in 1988. The name most tied to 1998 is Bailey: among boys, it was 18× as common that year as in the decades around it.

How many babies got a top-10 name?

Baby names of 1992

The Social Security name files, which cover every name given to at least 5 babies, list 3,841,340 babies born in 1992: 1,843,931 girls and 1,997,409 boys. Ashley and Michael were the most popular names, and 15.6% of all babies got a top-10 name, down from 20.7% in 1982. The name most tied to 1992 is Shanice: among girls, it was 32× as common that year as in the decades around it.

How many babies got a top-10 name?

In 1992, 15.6% of babies were given one of that year's 10 most popular names. Across all years, that share fell from 29.0% in 1935 to 7.5% in 2025.

Baby names of 1950

The Social Security name files, which cover every name given to at least 5 babies, list 3,503,316 babies born in 1950: 1,713,054 girls and 1,790,262 boys. Linda and James were the most popular names, and 29.1% of all babies got a top-10 name, up from 28.3% in 1940. The name most tied to 1950 is Linda: among girls, it was 5.0× as common that year as in the decades around it.

How many babies got a top-10 name?

In 1950, 29.1% of babies were given one of that year's 10 most popular names. Across all years, that share fell from 29.0% in 1935 to 7.5% in 2025.

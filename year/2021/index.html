Baby names of 2021

The Social Security name files, which cover every name given to at least 5 babies, list 3,390,343 babies born in 2021: 1,640,861 girls and 1,749,482 boys. Olivia and Liam were the most popular names, and 7.8% of all babies got a top-10 name, down from 9.0% in 2011. The name most tied to 2021 is Kehlani: among girls, it was 817× as common that year as in the decades before it.

How many babies got a top-10 name?

In 2021, 7.8% of babies were given one of that year's 10 most popular names. Across all years, that share fell from 29.0% in 1935 to 7.5% in 2025.

Patsy name popularity since 1925

120,031 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Patsy (96.5% girls, 3.5% boys). Half of them were born in 1943 or earlier, so the typical Patsy is about 83 years old in 2026. Patsy peaked in 1936 as the #52 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Patsy births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1941. Measured as a share of all babies, Patsy peaked in 1936.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1925 and 1930, Patsy became 3.9× more common.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1964 and 1969, its share of baby girls fell by 59%, from 1 in 2,320 to 1 in 5,720.

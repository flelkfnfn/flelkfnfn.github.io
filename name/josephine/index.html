Josephine name popularity since 1925

192,820 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Josephine. Half of them were born in 1952 or earlier, so the typical Josephine is about 74 years old in 2026. Josephine peaked in 1916 as the #21 name for girls, and it ranked #53 in 2025.

Josephine births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year on this chart was 1925. Measured as a share of all babies, Josephine peaked earlier, in 1916.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1941 and 1946, Josephine's share of baby girls fell by 37%, from 1 in 443 to 1 in 705.
  • Fastest rise: Between 2011 and 2016, it became 1.6× more common, from 1 in 1,020 baby girls to 1 in 634.

Sabrina name popularity since 1925

142,937 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Sabrina. Half of them were born in 1991 or earlier, so the typical Sabrina is about 35 years old in 2026. Sabrina peaked in 1997 as the #53 name for girls, and it ranked #321 in 2025.

Sabrina births per year, 1925–2025

1997 was the biggest year for Sabrina both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1954 and 1959, Sabrina became 6× more common, from 1 in 14,200 baby girls to 1 in 2,360.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1997 and 2002, its share of baby girls fell by 58%, from 1 in 299 to 1 in 709.

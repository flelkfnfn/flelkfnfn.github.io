Carl name popularity since 1925

400,937 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Carl (0.6% girls, 99.4% boys). Half of them were born in 1953 or earlier, so the typical Carl is about 73 years old in 2026. Carl peaked in 1915 as the #22 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Karl name popularity since 1925

92,480 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Karl (1.0% girls, 99.0% boys). Half of them were born in 1962 or earlier, so the typical Karl is about 64 years old in 2026. Karl peaked in 1963 as the #136 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

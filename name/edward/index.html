Edward name popularity since 1925

1,009,398 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Edward. Half of them were born in 1954 or earlier, so the typical Edward is about 72 years old in 2026. Edward peaked in 1882 as the #11 name for boys, and it ranked #224 in 2025.

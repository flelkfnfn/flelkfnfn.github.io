Claudette name popularity since 1925

18,695 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Claudette. Half of them were born in 1949 or earlier, so the typical Claudette is about 77 years old in 2026. Claudette peaked in 1936 as the #232 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Claudette births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1935. Measured as a share of all babies, Claudette peaked in 1936.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1931 and 1936, Claudette became 6.1× more common.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1938 and 1943, its share of baby girls fell by 45%, from 1 in 1,610 to 1 in 2,900.

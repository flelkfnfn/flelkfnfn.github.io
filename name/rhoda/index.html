Rhoda name popularity since 1925

17,161 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Rhoda. Half of them were born in 1947 or earlier, so the typical Rhoda is about 79 years old in 2026. Rhoda peaked in 1881 as the #161 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Alisha name popularity since 1925

57,127 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Alisha. Half of them were born in 1988 or earlier, so the typical Alisha is about 38 years old in 2026. Alisha peaked in 1987 as the #118 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Alisha births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1989. Measured as a share of all babies, Alisha peaked in 1987.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1966 and 1971, Alisha became 3.9× more common, from 1 in 12,400 baby girls to 1 in 3,150.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1993 and 1998, its share of baby girls fell by 49%, from 1 in 1,050 to 1 in 2,060.

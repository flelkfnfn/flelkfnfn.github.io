Jenifer name popularity since 1925

25,573 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Jenifer. Half of them were born in 1979 or earlier, so the typical Jenifer is about 47 years old in 2026. Jenifer peaked in 1974 as the #278 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Jenifer births per year, 1925–2025

1974 was the biggest year for Jenifer both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1967 and 1972, Jenifer became 3.2× more common, from 1 in 5,410 baby girls to 1 in 1,670.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2005 and 2010, its share of baby girls fell by 58%, from 1 in 6,200 to 1 in 14,700.

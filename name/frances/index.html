Frances name popularity since 1925

360,598 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Frances (99.1% girls, 0.9% boys). Half of them were born in 1942 or earlier, so the typical Frances is about 84 years old in 2026. Frances peaked in 1918 as the #8 name for girls, and it ranked #318 in 2025.

Edmond name popularity since 1925

18,753 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Edmond. Half of them were born in 1954 or earlier, so the typical Edmond is about 72 years old in 2026. Edmond peaked in 1908 as the #219 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

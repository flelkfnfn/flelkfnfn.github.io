Charlie name popularity since 1925

166,906 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Charlie (22.6% girls, 77.4% boys). Half of them were born in 1975 or earlier, so the typical Charlie is about 51 years old in 2026. Charlie peaked in 1900 as the #25 name for boys, and it ranked #145 in 2025.

George name popularity since 1925

1,046,889 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named George (0.7% girls, 99.3% boys). Half of them were born in 1949 or earlier, so the typical George is about 77 years old in 2026. George peaked in 1880 as the #5 name for boys, and it ranked #126 in 2025.

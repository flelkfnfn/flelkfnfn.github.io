Kareem name popularity since 1925

18,359 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Kareem (1.1% girls, 98.9% boys). Half of them were born in 1999 or earlier, so the typical Kareem is about 27 years old in 2026. Kareem peaked in 1977 as the #367 name for boys, and it ranked #702 in 2025.

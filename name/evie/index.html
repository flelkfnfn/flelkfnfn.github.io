Evie name popularity since 1925

18,739 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Evie. Half of them were born in 2017 or earlier, so the typical Evie is about 9 years old in 2026. Evie peaked in 2023 as the #266 name for girls, and it ranked #270 in 2025.

Toby name popularity since 1925

42,341 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Toby (18.5% girls, 81.5% boys). Half of them were born in 1977 or earlier, so the typical Toby is about 49 years old in 2026. Toby peaked in 1975 as the #190 name for boys, and it ranked #979 in 2025.

Toby births per year, 1925–2025

1975 was the biggest year for Toby both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1966 and 1971, Toby became 3× more common, from 1 in 5,080 baby boys to 1 in 1,720.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1976 and 1981, its share of baby boys fell by 63%, from 1 in 1,430 to 1 in 3,930.

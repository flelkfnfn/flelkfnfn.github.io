Sherman name popularity since 1925

31,238 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Sherman (0.6% girls, 99.4% boys). Half of them were born in 1954 or earlier, so the typical Sherman is about 72 years old in 2026. Sherman peaked in 1894 as the #154 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

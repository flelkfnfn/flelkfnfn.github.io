Colleen name popularity since 1925

161,388 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Colleen. Half of them were born in 1964 or earlier, so the typical Colleen is about 62 years old in 2026. Colleen peaked in 1964 as the #94 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Colleen births per year, 1925–2025

1964 was the biggest year for Colleen both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1947 and 1952, Colleen became 2.6× more common, from 1 in 1,400 baby girls to 1 in 534.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2001 and 2006, its share of baby girls fell by 56%, from 1 in 2,830 to 1 in 6,420.

Debra name popularity since 1925

551,770 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Debra. Half of them were born in 1957 or earlier, so the typical Debra is about 69 years old in 2026. Debra peaked in 1955 as the #4 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Debra births per year, 1925–2025

1955 was the biggest year for Debra both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1947 and 1952, Debra became 38× more common, from 1 in 2,610 baby girls to 1 in 69.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1971 and 1976, its share of baby girls fell by 60%, from 1 in 281 to 1 in 708.

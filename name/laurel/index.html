Laurel name popularity since 1925

47,147 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Laurel (97.4% girls, 2.6% boys). Half of them were born in 1969 or earlier, so the typical Laurel is about 57 years old in 2026. Laurel peaked in 1956 as the #241 name for girls, and it ranked #734 in 2025.

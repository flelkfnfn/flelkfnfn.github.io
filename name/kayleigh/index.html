Kayleigh name popularity since 1925

34,568 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Kayleigh. Half of them were born in 2007 or earlier, so the typical Kayleigh is about 19 years old in 2026. Kayleigh peaked in 2009 as the #227 name for girls, and it ranked #804 in 2025.

Kayleigh births per year, 1925–2025

2009 was the biggest year for Kayleigh both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1985 and 1990, Kayleigh became 2.7× more common, from 1 in 8,160 baby girls to 1 in 3,060.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2020 and 2025, its share of baby girls fell by 52%, from 1 in 2,290 to 1 in 4,730.

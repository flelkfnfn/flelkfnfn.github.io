Johanna name popularity since 1925

47,012 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Johanna. Half of them were born in 1986 or earlier, so the typical Johanna is about 40 years old in 2026. Johanna peaked in 1883 as the #180 name for girls, and it ranked #871 in 2025.

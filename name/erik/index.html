Erik name popularity since 1925

158,005 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Erik. Half of them were born in 1987 or earlier, so the typical Erik is about 39 years old in 2026. Erik peaked in 1980 as the #64 name for boys, and it ranked #546 in 2025.

Erik births per year, 1925–2025

1980 was the biggest year for Erik both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1962 and 1967, Erik became 5.2× more common, from 1 in 4,010 baby boys to 1 in 775.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2005 and 2010, its share of baby boys fell by 38%, from 1 in 896 to 1 in 1,440.

Ricardo name popularity since 1925

146,357 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Ricardo. Half of them were born in 1991 or earlier, so the typical Ricardo is about 35 years old in 2026. Ricardo peaked in 1998 as the #126 name for boys, and it ranked #380 in 2025.

Ricardo births per year, 1925–2025

1998 was the biggest year for Ricardo both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1947 and 1952, Ricardo became 2.1× more common, from 1 in 4,830 baby boys to 1 in 2,250.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2008 and 2013, its share of baby boys fell by 44%, from 1 in 740 to 1 in 1,320.

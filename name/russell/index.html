Russell name popularity since 1925

308,383 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Russell. Half of them were born in 1960 or earlier, so the typical Russell is about 66 years old in 2026. Russell peaked in 1914 as the #49 name for boys, and it ranked #345 in 2025.

Elsa name popularity since 1925

25,773 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Elsa. Half of them were born in 1988 or earlier, so the typical Elsa is about 38 years old in 2026. Elsa peaked in 1895 as the #215 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Elsa births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year on this chart was 2014. Measured as a share of all babies, Elsa peaked earlier, in 1895.

  • Fastest rise: Between 2009 and 2014, Elsa became 2.8× more common, from 1 in 4,310 baby girls to 1 in 1,570.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2014 and 2019, its share of baby girls fell by 67%, from 1 in 1,570 to 1 in 4,800.

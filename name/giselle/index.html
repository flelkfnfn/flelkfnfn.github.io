Giselle name popularity since 1925

50,230 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Giselle. Half of them were born in 2008 or earlier, so the typical Giselle is about 18 years old in 2026. Giselle peaked in 2007 as the #134 name for girls, and it ranked #382 in 2025.

Giselle births per year, 1925–2025

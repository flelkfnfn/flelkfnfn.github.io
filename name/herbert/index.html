Herbert name popularity since 1925

152,666 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Herbert. Half of them were born in 1942 or earlier, so the typical Herbert is about 84 years old in 2026. Herbert peaked in 1929 as the #25 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

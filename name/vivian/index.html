Vivian name popularity since 1925

179,027 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Vivian (99.2% girls, 0.8% boys). Half of them were born in 1961 or earlier, so the typical Vivian is about 65 years old in 2026. Vivian peaked in 1920 as the #64 name for girls, and it ranked #72 in 2025.

Gladys name popularity since 1925

114,925 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Gladys. Half of them were born in 1936 or earlier, so the typical Gladys is about 90 years old in 2026. Gladys peaked in 1901 as the #11 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Gladys births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year on this chart was 1925. Measured as a share of all babies, Gladys peaked earlier, in 1901.

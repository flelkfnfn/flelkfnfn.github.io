Christian name popularity since 1925

470,744 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Christian (4.2% girls, 95.8% boys). Half of them were born in 2001 or earlier, so the typical Christian is about 25 years old in 2026. Christian peaked in 1996 as the #24 name for boys, and it ranked #96 in 2025.

Christian births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 2000. Measured as a share of all babies, Christian peaked in 1996.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1965 and 1970, Christian became 3.6× more common, from 1 in 2,550 baby boys to 1 in 708.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2016 and 2021, its share of baby boys fell by 34%, from 1 in 247 to 1 in 377.

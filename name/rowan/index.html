Rowan name popularity since 1925

72,797 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Rowan (30.5% girls, 69.5% boys). Half of them were born in 2019 or earlier, so the typical Rowan is about 7 years old in 2026. Rowan peaked in 2025 as the #59 name for boys, and it ranked #59 in 2025.

Ophelia name popularity since 1925

18,939 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Ophelia. Half of them were born in 2016 or earlier, so the typical Ophelia is about 10 years old in 2026. Ophelia peaked in 2024 as the #259 name for girls, and it ranked #264 in 2025.

Ophelia births per year, 1925–2025

2024 was the biggest year for Ophelia both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1950 and 1955, Ophelia's share of baby girls fell by 43%, from 1 in 9,960 to 1 in 17,600.
  • Fastest rise: Between 2012 and 2017, it became 5.6× more common, from 1 in 12,700 baby girls to 1 in 2,260.

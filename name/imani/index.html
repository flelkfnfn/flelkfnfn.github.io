Imani name popularity since 1925

29,212 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Imani (95.2% girls, 4.8% boys). Half of them were born in 2005 or earlier, so the typical Imani is about 21 years old in 2026. Imani peaked in 1998 as the #253 name for girls, and it ranked #579 in 2025.

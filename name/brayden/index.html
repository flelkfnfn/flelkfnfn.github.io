Brayden name popularity since 1925

137,606 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Brayden (0.8% girls, 99.2% boys). Half of them were born in 2011 or earlier, so the typical Brayden is about 15 years old in 2026. Brayden peaked in 2010 as the #40 name for boys, and it ranked #229 in 2025.

Brayden births per year, 1925–2025

2010 was the biggest year for Brayden both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1994 and 1999, Brayden became 4.9× more common, from 1 in 6,230 baby boys to 1 in 1,260.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2020 and 2025, its share of baby boys fell by 53%, from 1 in 524 to 1 in 1,110.

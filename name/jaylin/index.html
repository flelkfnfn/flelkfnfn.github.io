Jaylin name popularity since 1925

19,608 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Jaylin (47.0% girls, 53.0% boys). Half of them were born in 2007 or earlier, so the typical Jaylin is about 19 years old in 2026. Jaylin peaked in 2002 as the #387 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Jaylin births per year, 1925–2025

2002 was the biggest year for Jaylin both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1994 and 1999, Jaylin became 2.9× more common, from 1 in 13,300 baby boys to 1 in 4,600.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2009 and 2014, its share of baby boys fell by 65%, from 1 in 3,410 to 1 in 9,700.

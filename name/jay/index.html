Jay name popularity since 1925

186,608 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Jay (1.2% girls, 98.8% boys). Half of them were born in 1964 or earlier, so the typical Jay is about 62 years old in 2026. Jay peaked in 1960 as the #78 name for boys, and it ranked #392 in 2025.

Jay births per year, 1925–2025

1960 was the biggest year for Jay both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1955 and 1960, Jay became 1.5× more common, from 1 in 607 baby boys to 1 in 396.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1978 and 1983, its share of baby boys fell by 34%, from 1 in 763 to 1 in 1,160.

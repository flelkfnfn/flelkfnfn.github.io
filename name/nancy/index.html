Nancy name popularity since 1925

956,635 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Nancy. Half of them were born in 1951 or earlier, so the typical Nancy is about 75 years old in 2026. Nancy peaked in 1947 as the #7 name for girls, and it ranked #967 in 2025.

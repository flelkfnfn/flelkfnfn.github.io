Leonard name popularity since 1925

225,578 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Leonard. Half of them were born in 1950 or earlier, so the typical Leonard is about 76 years old in 2026. Leonard peaked in 1920 as the #38 name for boys, and it ranked #637 in 2025.

Sonya name popularity since 1925

65,593 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Sonya. Half of them were born in 1971 or earlier, so the typical Sonya is about 55 years old in 2026. Sonya peaked in 1967 as the #114 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Sonya births per year, 1925–2025

1967 was the biggest year for Sonya both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1957 and 1962, Sonya became 4.6× more common, from 1 in 5,160 baby girls to 1 in 1,130.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1986 and 1991, its share of baby girls fell by 51%, from 1 in 2,330 to 1 in 4,760.

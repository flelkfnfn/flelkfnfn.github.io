Nicole name popularity since 1925

596,465 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Nicole. Half of them were born in 1986 or earlier, so the typical Nicole is about 40 years old in 2026. Nicole peaked in 1983 as the #7 name for girls, and it ranked #383 in 2025.

Nicole births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1985. Measured as a share of all babies, Nicole peaked in 1983.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1965 and 1970, Nicole became 12× more common, from 1 in 1,970 baby girls to 1 in 164.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2013 and 2018, its share of baby girls fell by 56%, from 1 in 523 to 1 in 1,190.

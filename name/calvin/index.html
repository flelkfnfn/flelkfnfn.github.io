Calvin name popularity since 1925

201,870 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Calvin (0.6% girls, 99.4% boys). Half of them were born in 1970 or earlier, so the typical Calvin is about 56 years old in 2026. Calvin peaked in 1924 as the #44 name for boys, and it ranked #132 in 2025.

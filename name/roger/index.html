Roger name popularity since 1925

424,160 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Roger. Half of them were born in 1953 or earlier, so the typical Roger is about 73 years old in 2026. Roger peaked in 1945 as the #22 name for boys, and it ranked #834 in 2025.

Roger births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1953. Measured as a share of all babies, Roger peaked in 1945.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1926 and 1931, Roger became 1.7× more common.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1953 and 1958, its share of baby boys fell by 41%, from 1 in 150 to 1 in 254.

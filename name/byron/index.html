Byron name popularity since 1925

74,490 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Byron. Half of them were born in 1970 or earlier, so the typical Byron is about 56 years old in 2026. Byron peaked in 1967 as the #196 name for boys, and it ranked #981 in 2025.

Byron births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1964. Measured as a share of all babies, Byron peaked in 1967.

  • Fastest rise: Between 2004 and 2009, Byron became 1.5× more common, from 1 in 4,140 baby boys to 1 in 2,690.

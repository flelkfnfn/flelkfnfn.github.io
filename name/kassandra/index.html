Kassandra name popularity since 1925

33,528 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Kassandra. Half of them were born in 1997 or earlier, so the typical Kassandra is about 29 years old in 2026. Kassandra peaked in 1993 as the #120 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Kassandra births per year, 1925–2025

1993 was the biggest year for Kassandra both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1988 and 1993, Kassandra became 4.4× more common, from 1 in 2,900 baby girls to 1 in 662.

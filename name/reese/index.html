Reese name popularity since 1925

63,445 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Reese (70.6% girls, 29.4% boys). Half of them were born in 2011 or earlier, so the typical Reese is about 15 years old in 2026. Reese peaked in 2012 as the #128 name for girls, and it ranked #192 in 2025.

Olivia name popularity since 1925

558,696 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Olivia. Half of them were born in 2010 or earlier, so the typical Olivia is about 16 years old in 2026. Olivia peaked in 2014 as the #2 name for girls, and it ranked #1 in 2025.

Olivia births per year, 1925–2025

2014 was the biggest year for Olivia both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1948 and 1953, Olivia's share of baby girls fell by 34%, from 1 in 2,000 to 1 in 3,030.
  • Fastest rise: Between 1971 and 1976, it became 4.2× more common, from 1 in 4,970 baby girls to 1 in 1,190.

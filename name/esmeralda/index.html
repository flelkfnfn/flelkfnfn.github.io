Esmeralda name popularity since 1925

51,696 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Esmeralda. Half of them were born in 2002 or earlier, so the typical Esmeralda is about 24 years old in 2026. Esmeralda peaked in 1998 as the #133 name for girls, and it ranked #373 in 2025.

Esmeralda births per year, 1925–2025

1998 was the biggest year for Esmeralda both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1993 and 1998, Esmeralda became 3.6× more common, from 1 in 2,590 baby girls to 1 in 713.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2007 and 2012, its share of baby girls fell by 45%, from 1 in 1,100 to 1 in 2,000.

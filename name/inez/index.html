Inez name popularity since 1925

31,448 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Inez (97.7% girls, 2.3% boys). Half of them were born in 1938 or earlier, so the typical Inez is about 88 years old in 2026. Inez peaked in 1909 as the #96 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

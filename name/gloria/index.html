Gloria name popularity since 1925

393,563 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Gloria. Half of them were born in 1948 or earlier, so the typical Gloria is about 78 years old in 2026. Gloria peaked in 1945 as the #23 name for girls, and it ranked #655 in 2025.

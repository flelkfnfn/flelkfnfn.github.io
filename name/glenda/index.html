Glenda name popularity since 1925

108,287 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Glenda. Half of them were born in 1952 or earlier, so the typical Glenda is about 74 years old in 2026. Glenda peaked in 1944 as the #79 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Glenda births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1950. Measured as a share of all babies, Glenda peaked in 1944.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1930 and 1935, Glenda became 6.8× more common.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1974 and 1979, its share of baby girls fell by 47%, from 1 in 2,880 to 1 in 5,400.

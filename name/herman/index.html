Herman name popularity since 1925

85,687 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Herman (0.6% girls, 99.4% boys). Half of them were born in 1944 or earlier, so the typical Herman is about 82 years old in 2026. Herman peaked in 1893 as the #44 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Faye name popularity since 1925

57,240 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Faye. Half of them were born in 1947 or earlier, so the typical Faye is about 79 years old in 2026. Faye peaked in 1934 as the #146 name for girls, and it ranked #515 in 2025.

Faye births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1943. Measured as a share of all babies, Faye peaked in 1934.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1961 and 1966, Faye's share of baby girls fell by 51%, from 1 in 3,070 to 1 in 6,270.
  • Fastest rise: Between 2011 and 2016, it became 2.3× more common, from 1 in 11,600 baby girls to 1 in 5,080.

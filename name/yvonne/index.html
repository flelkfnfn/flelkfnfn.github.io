Yvonne name popularity since 1925

148,173 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Yvonne. Half of them were born in 1956 or earlier, so the typical Yvonne is about 70 years old in 2026. Yvonne peaked in 1937 as the #77 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

The biggest year by count was 1953. Measured as a share of all babies, Yvonne peaked in 1937.

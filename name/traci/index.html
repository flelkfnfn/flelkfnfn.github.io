Traci name popularity since 1925

52,406 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Traci. Half of them were born in 1971 or earlier, so the typical Traci is about 55 years old in 2026. Traci peaked in 1970 as the #100 name for girls, and fewer than 5 girls got it in 2025.

Traci births per year, 1925–2025

1970 was the biggest year for Traci both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1956 and 1961, Traci became 8.6× more common, from 1 in 19,900 baby girls to 1 in 2,300.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1990 and 1995, its share of baby girls fell by 63%, from 1 in 3,670 to 1 in 9,880.

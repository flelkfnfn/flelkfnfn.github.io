Estella name popularity since 1925

26,287 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Estella. Half of them were born in 1957 or earlier, so the typical Estella is about 69 years old in 2026. Estella peaked in 1883 as the #106 name for girls, and it ranked #509 in 2025.

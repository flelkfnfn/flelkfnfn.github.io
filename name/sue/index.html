Sue name popularity since 1925

131,638 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Sue. Half of them were born in 1950 or earlier, so the typical Sue is about 76 years old in 2026. Sue peaked in 1946 as the #61 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Sue births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1947. Measured as a share of all babies, Sue peaked in 1946.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1927 and 1932, Sue became 2.5× more common.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1965 and 1970, its share of baby girls fell by 62%, from 1 in 994 to 1 in 2,620.

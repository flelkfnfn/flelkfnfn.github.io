Alfonso name popularity since 1925

37,829 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Alfonso. Half of them were born in 1978 or earlier, so the typical Alfonso is about 48 years old in 2026. Alfonso peaked in 1909 as the #302 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

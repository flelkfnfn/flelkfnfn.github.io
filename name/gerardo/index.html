Gerardo name popularity since 1925

55,027 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Gerardo. Half of them were born in 1997 or earlier, so the typical Gerardo is about 29 years old in 2026. Gerardo peaked in 2000 as the #211 name for boys, and it ranked #678 in 2025.

Denis name popularity since 1925

20,851 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Denis (4.2% girls, 95.8% boys). Half of them were born in 1959 or earlier, so the typical Denis is about 67 years old in 2026. Denis peaked in 1952 as the #277 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

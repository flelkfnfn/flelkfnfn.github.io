Vincent name popularity since 1925

329,316 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Vincent. Half of them were born in 1978 or earlier, so the typical Vincent is about 48 years old in 2026. Vincent peaked in 1963 as the #61 name for boys, and it ranked #107 in 2025.

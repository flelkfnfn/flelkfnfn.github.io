Paul name popularity since 1925

1,234,598 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Paul. Half of them were born in 1959 or earlier, so the typical Paul is about 67 years old in 2026. Paul peaked in 1964 as the #16 name for boys, and it ranked #262 in 2025.

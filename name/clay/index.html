Clay name popularity since 1925

37,800 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Clay. Half of them were born in 1983 or earlier, so the typical Clay is about 43 years old in 2026. Clay peaked in 1960 as the #265 name for boys, and it ranked #573 in 2025.

Clay births per year, 1925–2025

1960 was the biggest year for Clay both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1953 and 1958, Clay became 2.5× more common, from 1 in 7,300 baby boys to 1 in 2,940.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2002 and 2007, its share of baby boys fell by 56%, from 1 in 3,250 to 1 in 7,390.

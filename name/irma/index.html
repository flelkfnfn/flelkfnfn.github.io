Irma name popularity since 1925

50,115 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Irma. Half of them were born in 1953 or earlier, so the typical Irma is about 73 years old in 2026. Irma peaked in 1911 as the #137 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

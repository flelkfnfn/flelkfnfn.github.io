Leanna name popularity since 1925

25,043 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Leanna. Half of them were born in 1992 or earlier, so the typical Leanna is about 34 years old in 2026. Leanna peaked in 1989 as the #384 name for girls, and it ranked #890 in 2025.

Leanna births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1990. Measured as a share of all babies, Leanna peaked in 1989.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1950 and 1955, Leanna's share of baby girls fell by 44%, from 1 in 10,500 to 1 in 18,600.
  • Fastest rise: Between 1984 and 1989, it became 1.8× more common, from 1 in 5,100 baby girls to 1 in 2,800.

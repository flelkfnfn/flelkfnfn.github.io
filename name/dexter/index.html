Dexter name popularity since 1925

35,567 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Dexter. Half of them were born in 1987 or earlier, so the typical Dexter is about 39 years old in 2026. Dexter peaked in 2012 as the #361 name for boys, and it ranked #693 in 2025.

Dexter births per year, 1925–2025

2012 was the biggest year for Dexter both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1970 and 1975, Dexter's share of baby boys fell by 49%, from 1 in 2,660 to 1 in 5,240.
  • Fastest rise: Between 2006 and 2011, it became 3.9× more common, from 1 in 9,830 baby boys to 1 in 2,520.

Isabel name popularity since 1925

115,392 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Isabel (98.7% girls, 1.3% boys). Half of them were born in 2003 or earlier, so the typical Isabel is about 23 years old in 2026. Isabel peaked in 2003 as the #83 name for girls, and it ranked #177 in 2025.

Isabel births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 2006. Measured as a share of all babies, Isabel peaked in 2003.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1994 and 1999, Isabel became 3× more common, from 1 in 1,580 baby girls to 1 in 521.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2006 and 2011, its share of baby girls fell by 35%, from 1 in 474 to 1 in 729.

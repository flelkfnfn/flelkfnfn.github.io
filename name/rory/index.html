Rory name popularity since 1925

49,421 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Rory (27.5% girls, 72.5% boys). Half of them were born in 2010 or earlier, so the typical Rory is about 16 years old in 2026. Rory peaked in 2025 as the #199 name for boys, and it ranked #199 in 2025.

Rory births per year, 1925–2025

2025 was the biggest year for Rory both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1951 and 1956, Rory became 3.4× more common, from 1 in 14,100 baby boys to 1 in 4,140.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1962 and 1967, its share of baby boys fell by 53%, from 1 in 4,070 to 1 in 8,630.

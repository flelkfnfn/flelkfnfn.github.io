Alaina name popularity since 1925

50,584 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Alaina. Half of them were born in 2010 or earlier, so the typical Alaina is about 16 years old in 2026. Alaina peaked in 2011 as the #156 name for girls, and it ranked #224 in 2025.

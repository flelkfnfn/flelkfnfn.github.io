Annmarie name popularity since 1925

18,938 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Annmarie. Half of them were born in 1971 or earlier, so the typical Annmarie is about 55 years old in 2026. Annmarie peaked in 1969 as the #356 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Annmarie births per year, 1925–2025

1969 was the biggest year for Annmarie both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1956 and 1961, Annmarie became 2.4× more common, from 1 in 12,000 baby girls to 1 in 5,010.

Rae name popularity since 1925

19,861 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Rae (96.4% girls, 3.6% boys). Half of them were born in 1961 or earlier, so the typical Rae is about 65 years old in 2026. Rae peaked in 1892 as the #313 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

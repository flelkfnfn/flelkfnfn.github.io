Giovanni name popularity since 1925

90,075 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Giovanni (1.1% girls, 98.9% boys). Half of them were born in 2011 or earlier, so the typical Giovanni is about 15 years old in 2026. Giovanni peaked in 2010 as the #117 name for boys, and it ranked #123 in 2025.

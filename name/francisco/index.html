Francisco name popularity since 1925

138,777 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Francisco (0.7% girls, 99.3% boys). Half of them were born in 1992 or earlier, so the typical Francisco is about 34 years old in 2026. Francisco peaked in 1997 as the #133 name for boys, and it ranked #322 in 2025.

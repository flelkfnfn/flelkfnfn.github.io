Jesus name popularity since 1925

240,885 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Jesus (1.0% girls, 99.0% boys). Half of them were born in 1997 or earlier, so the typical Jesus is about 29 years old in 2026. Jesus peaked in 2001 as the #66 name for boys, and it ranked #173 in 2025.

Jesus births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 2004. Measured as a share of all babies, Jesus peaked in 2001.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1987 and 1992, Jesus became 1.6× more common, from 1 in 669 baby boys to 1 in 414.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2007 and 2012, its share of baby boys fell by 33%, from 1 in 334 to 1 in 501.

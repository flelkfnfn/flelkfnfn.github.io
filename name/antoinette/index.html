Antoinette name popularity since 1925

61,021 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Antoinette. Half of them were born in 1960 or earlier, so the typical Antoinette is about 66 years old in 2026. Antoinette peaked in 1914 as the #170 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

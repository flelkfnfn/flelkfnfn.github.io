Kenny name popularity since 1925

52,287 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Kenny (1.0% girls, 99.0% boys). Half of them were born in 1968 or earlier, so the typical Kenny is about 58 years old in 2026. Kenny peaked in 1960 as the #169 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Kenny births per year, 1925–2025

1960 was the biggest year for Kenny both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1954 and 1959, Kenny became 2.9× more common, from 1 in 3,570 baby boys to 1 in 1,210.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1964 and 1969, its share of baby boys fell by 46%, from 1 in 1,420 to 1 in 2,650.

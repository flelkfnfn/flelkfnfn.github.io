Erin name popularity since 1925

325,548 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Erin (97.1% girls, 2.9% boys). Half of them were born in 1984 or earlier, so the typical Erin is about 42 years old in 2026. Erin peaked in 1983 as the #18 name for girls, and it ranked #833 in 2025.

Erin births per year, 1925–2025

1983 was the biggest year for Erin both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1954 and 1959, Erin became 6.7× more common, from 1 in 11,100 baby girls to 1 in 1,650.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2004 and 2009, its share of baby girls fell by 50%, from 1 in 545 to 1 in 1,080.

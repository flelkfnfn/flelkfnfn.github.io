Myrna name popularity since 1925

29,710 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Myrna. Half of them were born in 1942 or earlier, so the typical Myrna is about 84 years old in 2026. Myrna peaked in 1938 as the #134 name for girls, and fewer than 5 girls got it in 2025.

Myrna births per year, 1925–2025

1938 was the biggest year for Myrna both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1932 and 1937, Myrna became 3.9× more common.

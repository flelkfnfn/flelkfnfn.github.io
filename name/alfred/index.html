Alfred name popularity since 1925

170,947 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Alfred (0.6% girls, 99.4% boys). Half of them were born in 1947 or earlier, so the typical Alfred is about 79 years old in 2026. Alfred peaked in 1928 as the #33 name for boys, and it ranked #840 in 2025.

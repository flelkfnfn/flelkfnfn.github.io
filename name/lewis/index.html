Lewis name popularity since 1925

113,292 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Lewis (0.6% girls, 99.4% boys). Half of them were born in 1951 or earlier, so the typical Lewis is about 75 years old in 2026. Lewis peaked in 1880 as the #30 name for boys, and it ranked #433 in 2025.

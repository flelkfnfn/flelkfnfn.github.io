Marty name popularity since 1925

35,983 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Marty (11.2% girls, 88.8% boys). Half of them were born in 1964 or earlier, so the typical Marty is about 62 years old in 2026. Marty peaked in 1963 as the #186 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Marty births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1962. Measured as a share of all babies, Marty peaked in 1963.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1952 and 1957, Marty became 4.3× more common, from 1 in 6,970 baby boys to 1 in 1,600.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1976 and 1981, its share of baby boys fell by 56%, from 1 in 3,270 to 1 in 7,460.

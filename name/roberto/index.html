Roberto name popularity since 1925

111,895 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Roberto (0.7% girls, 99.3% boys). Half of them were born in 1987 or earlier, so the typical Roberto is about 39 years old in 2026. Roberto peaked in 1981 as the #147 name for boys, and it ranked #570 in 2025.

The biggest year by count was 1991. Measured as a share of all babies, Roberto peaked in 1981.

Johnathan name popularity since 1925

98,506 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Johnathan. Half of them were born in 1995 or earlier, so the typical Johnathan is about 31 years old in 2026. Johnathan peaked in 1990 as the #108 name for boys, and it ranked #508 in 2025.

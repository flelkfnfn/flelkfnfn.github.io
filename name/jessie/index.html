Jessie name popularity since 1925

178,385 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Jessie (51.9% girls, 48.1% boys). Half of them were born in 1959 or earlier, so the typical Jessie is about 67 years old in 2026. Jessie peaked in 1882 as the #37 name for girls, and it ranked #824 in 2025.

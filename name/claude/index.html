Claude name popularity since 1925

59,237 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Claude (0.9% girls, 99.1% boys). Half of them were born in 1946 or earlier, so the typical Claude is about 80 years old in 2026. Claude peaked in 1887 as the #46 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

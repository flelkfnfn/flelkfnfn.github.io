Zachariah name popularity since 1925

31,190 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Zachariah. Half of them were born in 2001 or earlier, so the typical Zachariah is about 25 years old in 2026. Zachariah peaked in 1992 as the #305 name for boys, and it ranked #538 in 2025.

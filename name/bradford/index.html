Bradford name popularity since 1925

31,057 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Bradford. Half of them were born in 1968 or earlier, so the typical Bradford is about 58 years old in 2026. Bradford peaked in 1953 as the #240 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Bradford births per year, 1925–2025

1953 was the biggest year for Bradford both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1945 and 1950, Bradford became 2.4× more common, from 1 in 7,310 baby boys to 1 in 3,110.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1996 and 2001, its share of baby boys fell by 52%, from 1 in 8,980 to 1 in 18,900.

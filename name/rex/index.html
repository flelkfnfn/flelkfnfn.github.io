Rex name popularity since 1925

53,349 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Rex. Half of them were born in 1958 or earlier, so the typical Rex is about 68 years old in 2026. Rex peaked in 1951 as the #170 name for boys, and it ranked #859 in 2025.

Rex births per year, 1925–2025

1951 was the biggest year for Rex both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1964 and 1969, Rex's share of baby boys fell by 44%, from 1 in 2,080 to 1 in 3,740.
  • Fastest rise: Between 2000 and 2005, it became 2.1× more common, from 1 in 17,500 baby boys to 1 in 8,430.

Jacqueline name popularity since 1925

419,888 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Jacqueline. Half of them were born in 1966 or earlier, so the typical Jacqueline is about 60 years old in 2026. Jacqueline peaked in 1964 as the #37 name for girls, and it ranked #558 in 2025.

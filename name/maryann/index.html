Maryann name popularity since 1925

60,359 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Maryann. Half of them were born in 1952 or earlier, so the typical Maryann is about 74 years old in 2026. Maryann peaked in 1943 as the #135 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

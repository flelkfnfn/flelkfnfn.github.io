Adam name popularity since 1925

572,528 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Adam. Half of them were born in 1987 or earlier, so the typical Adam is about 39 years old in 2026. Adam peaked in 1984 as the #18 name for boys, and it ranked #101 in 2025.

Adam births per year, 1925–2025

1984 was the biggest year for Adam both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1925 and 1930, Adam's share of baby boys fell by 38%.
  • Fastest rise: Between 1958 and 1963, it became 5.2× more common, from 1 in 4,540 baby boys to 1 in 866.

Clifford name popularity since 1925

144,075 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Clifford (0.8% girls, 99.2% boys). Half of them were born in 1952 or earlier, so the typical Clifford is about 74 years old in 2026. Clifford peaked in 1908 as the #58 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

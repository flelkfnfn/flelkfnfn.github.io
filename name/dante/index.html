Dante name popularity since 1925

51,080 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Dante (0.9% girls, 99.1% boys). Half of them were born in 2003 or earlier, so the typical Dante is about 23 years old in 2026. Dante peaked in 1998 as the #201 name for boys, and it ranked #308 in 2025.

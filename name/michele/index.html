Michele name popularity since 1925

225,734 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Michele (98.9% girls, 1.1% boys). Half of them were born in 1967 or earlier, so the typical Michele is about 59 years old in 2026. Michele peaked in 1968 as the #29 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Michele births per year, 1925–2025

1968 was the biggest year for Michele both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1940 and 1945, Michele became 8.1× more common, from 1 in 11,200 baby girls to 1 in 1,380.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1992 and 1997, its share of baby girls fell by 58%, from 1 in 2,240 to 1 in 5,370.

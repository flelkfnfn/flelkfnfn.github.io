Ida name popularity since 1925

75,138 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Ida. Half of them were born in 1940 or earlier, so the typical Ida is about 86 years old in 2026. Ida peaked in 1880 as the #7 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Ida births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year on this chart was 1925. Measured as a share of all babies, Ida peaked earlier, in 1880.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1979 and 1984, Ida's share of baby girls fell by 38%, from 1 in 6,630 to 1 in 10,700.
  • Fastest rise: Between 2011 and 2016, it became 1.9× more common, from 1 in 17,600 baby girls to 1 in 9,040.

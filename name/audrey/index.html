Audrey name popularity since 1925

274,867 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Audrey (99.2% girls, 0.8% boys). Half of them were born in 1987 or earlier, so the typical Audrey is about 39 years old in 2026. Audrey peaked in 1928 as the #60 name for girls, and it ranked #86 in 2025.

Audrey births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 2015. Measured as a share of all babies, Audrey peaked in 1928.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1939 and 1944, Audrey's share of baby girls fell by 47%, from 1 in 427 to 1 in 807.
  • Fastest rise: Between 1998 and 2003, it became 1.8× more common, from 1 in 802 baby girls to 1 in 457.

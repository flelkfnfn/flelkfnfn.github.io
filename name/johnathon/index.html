Johnathon name popularity since 1925

25,647 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Johnathon. Half of them were born in 1993 or earlier, so the typical Johnathon is about 33 years old in 2026. Johnathon peaked in 1990 as the #250 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Johnathon births per year, 1925–2025

1990 was the biggest year for Johnathon both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1977 and 1982, Johnathon became 2.1× more common, from 1 in 7,340 baby boys to 1 in 3,450.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2012 and 2017, its share of baby boys fell by 49%, from 1 in 7,160 to 1 in 14,000.

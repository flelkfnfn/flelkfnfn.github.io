Monte name popularity since 1925

23,286 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Monte (3.1% girls, 96.9% boys). Half of them were born in 1962 or earlier, so the typical Monte is about 64 years old in 2026. Monte peaked in 1950 as the #296 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Monte births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1956. Measured as a share of all babies, Monte peaked in 1950.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1938 and 1943, Monte's share of baby boys fell by 50%, from 1 in 4,200 to 1 in 8,350.
  • Fastest rise: Between 1945 and 1950, it became 2.9× more common, from 1 in 9,680 baby boys to 1 in 3,380.

Abbie name popularity since 1925

17,809 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Abbie (98.8% girls, 1.2% boys). Half of them were born in 1996 or earlier, so the typical Abbie is about 30 years old in 2026. Abbie peaked in 1881 as the #166 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Abbie births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year on this chart was 2003. Measured as a share of all babies, Abbie peaked earlier, in 1881.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1992 and 1997, Abbie became 1.6× more common, from 1 in 7,090 baby girls to 1 in 4,510.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2012 and 2017, its share of baby girls fell by 63%, from 1 in 5,120 to 1 in 13,800.

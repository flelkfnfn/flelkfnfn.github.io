Gregory name popularity since 1925

712,363 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Gregory. Half of them were born in 1966 or earlier, so the typical Gregory is about 60 years old in 2026. Gregory peaked in 1962 as the #21 name for boys, and it ranked #593 in 2025.

Gregory births per year, 1925–2025

1962 was the biggest year for Gregory both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1943 and 1948, Gregory became 7× more common, from 1 in 1,260 baby boys to 1 in 180.

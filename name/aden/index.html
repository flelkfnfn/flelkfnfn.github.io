Aden name popularity since 1925

22,331 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Aden (3.0% girls, 97.0% boys). Half of them were born in 2009 or earlier, so the typical Aden is about 17 years old in 2026. Aden peaked in 2009 as the #253 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Aden births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 2006. Measured as a share of all babies, Aden peaked in 2009.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1999 and 2004, Aden became 8.3× more common, from 1 in 15,900 baby boys to 1 in 1,920.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2014 and 2019, its share of baby boys fell by 53%, from 1 in 1,920 to 1 in 4,050.

Brittany name popularity since 1925

361,751 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Brittany. Half of them were born in 1990 or earlier, so the typical Brittany is about 36 years old in 2026. Brittany peaked in 1989 as the #3 name for girls, and it ranked #928 in 2025.

Brittany births per year, 1925–2025

1989 was the biggest year for Brittany both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1981 and 1986, Brittany became 12× more common, from 1 in 973 baby girls to 1 in 84.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1998 and 2003, its share of baby girls fell by 84%, from 1 in 179 to 1 in 1,160.

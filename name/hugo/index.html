Hugo name popularity since 1925

34,456 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Hugo. Half of them were born in 2002 or earlier, so the typical Hugo is about 24 years old in 2026. Hugo peaked in 2025 as the #378 name for boys, and it ranked #378 in 2025.

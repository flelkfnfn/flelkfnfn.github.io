Alfredo name popularity since 1925

55,439 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Alfredo. Half of them were born in 1988 or earlier, so the typical Alfredo is about 38 years old in 2026. Alfredo peaked in 1993 as the #268 name for boys, and it ranked #820 in 2025.

Alison name popularity since 1925

117,188 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Alison. Half of them were born in 1985 or earlier, so the typical Alison is about 41 years old in 2026. Alison peaked in 1979 as the #102 name for girls, and it ranked #476 in 2025.

Alison births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1980. Measured as a share of all babies, Alison peaked in 1979.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1957 and 1962, Alison became 2.6× more common, from 1 in 3,610 baby girls to 1 in 1,380.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1986 and 1991, its share of baby girls fell by 34%, from 1 in 577 to 1 in 871.

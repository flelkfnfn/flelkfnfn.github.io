Derick name popularity since 1925

19,810 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Derick. Half of them were born in 1989 or earlier, so the typical Derick is about 37 years old in 2026. Derick peaked in 1980 as the #338 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Derick births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1989. Measured as a share of all babies, Derick peaked in 1980.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1962 and 1967, Derick became 2.2× more common, from 1 in 17,100 baby boys to 1 in 7,750.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2011 and 2016, its share of baby boys fell by 37%, from 1 in 7,110 to 1 in 11,300.

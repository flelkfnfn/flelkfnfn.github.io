Bill name popularity since 1925

143,001 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Bill. Half of them were born in 1946 or earlier, so the typical Bill is about 80 years old in 2026. Bill peaked in 1934 as the #59 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Bill births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1947. Measured as a share of all babies, Bill peaked in 1934.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1947 and 1952, Bill's share of baby boys fell by 68%, from 1 in 391 to 1 in 1,220.
  • Fastest rise: Between 1954 and 1959, it became 2.9× more common, from 1 in 1,340 baby boys to 1 in 464.

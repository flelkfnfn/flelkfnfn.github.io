Lynda name popularity since 1925

77,737 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Lynda. Half of them were born in 1952 or earlier, so the typical Lynda is about 74 years old in 2026. Lynda peaked in 1947 as the #77 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Lynda births per year, 1925–2025

1947 was the biggest year for Lynda both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1935 and 1940, Lynda became 11× more common, from 1 in 9,530 baby girls to 1 in 903.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1969 and 1974, its share of baby girls fell by 59%, from 1 in 1,660 to 1 in 4,080.

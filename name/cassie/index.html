Cassie name popularity since 1925

42,570 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Cassie (99.4% girls, 0.6% boys). Half of them were born in 1987 or earlier, so the typical Cassie is about 39 years old in 2026. Cassie peaked in 1982 as the #137 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Cassie births per year, 1925–2025

1982 was the biggest year for Cassie both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1977 and 1982, Cassie became 4× more common, from 1 in 3,100 baby girls to 1 in 772.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1999 and 2004, its share of baby girls fell by 59%, from 1 in 1,850 to 1 in 4,460.

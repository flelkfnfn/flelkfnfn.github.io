Conner name popularity since 1925

57,196 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Conner (1.5% girls, 98.5% boys). Half of them were born in 2005 or earlier, so the typical Conner is about 21 years old in 2026. Conner peaked in 2005 as the #127 name for boys, and it ranked #864 in 2025.

Conner births per year, 1925–2025

2005 was the biggest year for Conner both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1988 and 1993, Conner became 7.2× more common, from 1 in 10,900 baby boys to 1 in 1,520.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2019 and 2024, its share of baby boys fell by 60%, from 1 in 2,120 to 1 in 5,350.

Ryan name popularity since 1925

991,628 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Ryan (2.8% girls, 97.2% boys). Half of them were born in 1991 or earlier, so the typical Ryan is about 35 years old in 2026. Ryan peaked in 1985 as the #12 name for boys, and it ranked #99 in 2025.

Ryan births per year, 1925–2025

1985 was the biggest year for Ryan both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1970 and 1975, Ryan became 7.8× more common, from 1 in 800 baby boys to 1 in 102.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2017 and 2022, its share of baby boys fell by 39%, from 1 in 240 to 1 in 397.

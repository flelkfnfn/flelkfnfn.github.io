Mildred name popularity since 1925

191,151 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Mildred. Half of them were born in 1934 or earlier, so the typical Mildred is about 92 years old in 2026. Mildred peaked in 1913 as the #6 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

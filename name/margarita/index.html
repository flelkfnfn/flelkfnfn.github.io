Margarita name popularity since 1925

37,128 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Margarita. Half of them were born in 1970 or earlier, so the typical Margarita is about 56 years old in 2026. Margarita peaked in 1976 as the #402 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

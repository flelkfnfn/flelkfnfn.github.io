Avery name popularity since 1925

234,757 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Avery (72.3% girls, 27.7% boys). Half of them were born in 2013 or earlier, so the typical Avery is about 13 years old in 2026. Avery peaked in 2014 as the #14 name for girls, and it ranked #37 in 2025.

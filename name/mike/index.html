Mike name popularity since 1925

190,614 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Mike. Half of them were born in 1959 or earlier, so the typical Mike is about 67 years old in 2026. Mike peaked in 1960 as the #36 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Mike births per year, 1925–2025

1960 was the biggest year for Mike both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1954 and 1959, Mike became 4.6× more common, from 1 in 815 baby boys to 1 in 178.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1969 and 1974, its share of baby boys fell by 68%, from 1 in 623 to 1 in 1,930.

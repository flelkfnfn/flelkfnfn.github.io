Pedro name popularity since 1925

90,215 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Pedro (0.6% girls, 99.4% boys). Half of them were born in 1990 or earlier, so the typical Pedro is about 36 years old in 2026. Pedro peaked in 1999 as the #203 name for boys, and it ranked #455 in 2025.

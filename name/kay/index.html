Kay name popularity since 1925

97,574 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Kay (96.7% girls, 3.3% boys). Half of them were born in 1948 or earlier, so the typical Kay is about 78 years old in 2026. Kay peaked in 1939 as the #76 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Kay births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1942. Measured as a share of all babies, Kay peaked in 1939.

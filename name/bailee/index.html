Bailee name popularity since 1925

16,483 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Bailee (98.5% girls, 1.5% boys). Half of them were born in 2008 or earlier, so the typical Bailee is about 18 years old in 2026. Bailee peaked in 1997 as the #407 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Bailee births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 2000. Measured as a share of all babies, Bailee peaked in 1997.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1992 and 1997, Bailee became 4.5× more common, from 1 in 12,100 baby girls to 1 in 2,710.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2015 and 2020, its share of baby girls fell by 37%, from 1 in 3,180 to 1 in 5,050.

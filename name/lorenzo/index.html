Lorenzo name popularity since 1925

82,772 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Lorenzo. Half of them were born in 2002 or earlier, so the typical Lorenzo is about 24 years old in 2026. Lorenzo peaked in 2025 as the #117 name for boys, and it ranked #117 in 2025.

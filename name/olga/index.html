Olga name popularity since 1925

35,487 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Olga. Half of them were born in 1955 or earlier, so the typical Olga is about 71 years old in 2026. Olga peaked in 1916 as the #126 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

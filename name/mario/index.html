Mario name popularity since 1925

147,385 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Mario (0.7% girls, 99.3% boys). Half of them were born in 1987 or earlier, so the typical Mario is about 39 years old in 2026. Mario peaked in 1980 as the #102 name for boys, and it ranked #438 in 2025.

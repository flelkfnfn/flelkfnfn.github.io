Alejandra name popularity since 1925

54,376 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Alejandra (99.0% girls, 1.0% boys). Half of them were born in 1999 or earlier, so the typical Alejandra is about 27 years old in 2026. Alejandra peaked in 1994 as the #145 name for girls, and it ranked #604 in 2025.

Alejandra births per year, 1925–2025

1994 was the biggest year for Alejandra both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1987 and 1992, Alejandra became 3.7× more common, from 1 in 3,290 baby girls to 1 in 902.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2008 and 2013, its share of baby girls fell by 41%, from 1 in 1,420 to 1 in 2,420.

Burton name popularity since 1925

16,241 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Burton. Half of them were born in 1943 or earlier, so the typical Burton is about 83 years old in 2026. Burton peaked in 1925 as the #222 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

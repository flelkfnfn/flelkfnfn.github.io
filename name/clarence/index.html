Clarence name popularity since 1925

186,273 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Clarence (0.7% girls, 99.3% boys). Half of them were born in 1945 or earlier, so the typical Clarence is about 81 years old in 2026. Clarence peaked in 1895 as the #17 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Freddie name popularity since 1925

75,326 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Freddie (10.6% girls, 89.4% boys). Half of them were born in 1951 or earlier, so the typical Freddie is about 75 years old in 2026. Freddie peaked in 1938 as the #132 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

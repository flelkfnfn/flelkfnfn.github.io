Carmen name popularity since 1925

146,350 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Carmen (90.8% girls, 9.2% boys). Half of them were born in 1968 or earlier, so the typical Carmen is about 58 years old in 2026. Carmen peaked in 1968 as the #141 name for girls, and it ranked #389 in 2025.

Kaitlin name popularity since 1925

57,430 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Kaitlin. Half of them were born in 1994 or earlier, so the typical Kaitlin is about 32 years old in 2026. Kaitlin peaked in 1995 as the #94 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Kaitlin births per year, 1925–2025

1995 was the biggest year for Kaitlin both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1980 and 1985, Kaitlin became 8.8× more common, from 1 in 12,300 baby girls to 1 in 1,390.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2006 and 2011, its share of baby girls fell by 61%, from 1 in 1,990 to 1 in 5,140.

Todd name popularity since 1925

283,111 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Todd. Half of them were born in 1969 or earlier, so the typical Todd is about 57 years old in 2026. Todd peaked in 1968 as the #28 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Todd births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1964. Measured as a share of all babies, Todd peaked in 1968.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1957 and 1962, Todd became 5.5× more common, from 1 in 897 baby boys to 1 in 162.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1975 and 1980, its share of baby boys fell by 49%, from 1 in 218 to 1 in 432.

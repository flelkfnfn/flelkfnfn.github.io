Jared name popularity since 1925

206,958 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Jared. Half of them were born in 1992 or earlier, so the typical Jared is about 34 years old in 2026. Jared peaked in 1998 as the #51 name for boys, and it ranked #396 in 2025.

Jared births per year, 1925–2025

1998 was the biggest year for Jared both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1966 and 1971, Jared became 4.2× more common, from 1 in 6,890 baby boys to 1 in 1,650.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2000 and 2005, its share of baby boys fell by 61%, from 1 in 268 to 1 in 683.

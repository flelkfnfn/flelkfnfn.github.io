Sonia name popularity since 1925

65,884 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Sonia. Half of them were born in 1974 or earlier, so the typical Sonia is about 52 years old in 2026. Sonia peaked in 1973 as the #174 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Sonia births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1970. Measured as a share of all babies, Sonia peaked in 1973.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1938 and 1943, Sonia's share of baby girls fell by 52%, from 1 in 2,570 to 1 in 5,330.
  • Fastest rise: Between 1961 and 1966, it became 1.9× more common, from 1 in 2,050 baby girls to 1 in 1,110.

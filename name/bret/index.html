Bret name popularity since 1925

30,051 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Bret (0.8% girls, 99.2% boys). Half of them were born in 1973 or earlier, so the typical Bret is about 53 years old in 2026. Bret peaked in 1959 as the #170 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Bret births per year, 1925–2025

1959 was the biggest year for Bret both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1957 and 1962, Bret became 4.2× more common, from 1 in 11,100 baby boys to 1 in 2,630.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1959 and 1964, its share of baby boys fell by 54%, from 1 in 1,160 to 1 in 2,540.

Terri name popularity since 1925

147,017 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Terri (99.4% girls, 0.6% boys). Half of them were born in 1962 or earlier, so the typical Terri is about 64 years old in 2026. Terri peaked in 1959 as the #46 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Terri births per year, 1925–2025

1959 was the biggest year for Terri both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1942 and 1947, Terri became 4.2× more common, from 1 in 9,450 baby girls to 1 in 2,270.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1982 and 1987, its share of baby girls fell by 52%, from 1 in 1,550 to 1 in 3,210.

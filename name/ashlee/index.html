Ashlee name popularity since 1925

43,791 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Ashlee (99.4% girls, 0.6% boys). Half of them were born in 1992 or earlier, so the typical Ashlee is about 34 years old in 2026. Ashlee peaked in 1987 as the #112 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Ashlee births per year, 1925–2025

1987 was the biggest year for Ashlee both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1978 and 1983, Ashlee became 6.9× more common, from 1 in 7,740 baby girls to 1 in 1,120.

Stanley name popularity since 1925

233,407 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Stanley. Half of them were born in 1951 or earlier, so the typical Stanley is about 75 years old in 2026. Stanley peaked in 1916 as the #34 name for boys, and it ranked #863 in 2025.

Clinton name popularity since 1925

85,425 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Clinton. Half of them were born in 1972 or earlier, so the typical Clinton is about 54 years old in 2026. Clinton peaked in 1981 as the #124 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Clinton births per year, 1925–2025

1981 was the biggest year for Clinton both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1966 and 1971, Clinton became 1.8× more common, from 1 in 2,160 baby boys to 1 in 1,190.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1991 and 1996, its share of baby boys fell by 75%, from 1 in 1,210 to 1 in 4,810.

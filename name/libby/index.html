Libby name popularity since 1925

16,372 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Libby. Half of them were born in 1989 or earlier, so the typical Libby is about 37 years old in 2026. Libby peaked in 2010 as the #793 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Libby births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 2009. Measured as a share of all babies, Libby peaked in 2010.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1967 and 1972, Libby's share of baby girls fell by 34%, from 1 in 8,090 to 1 in 12,300.
  • Fastest rise: Between 1999 and 2004, it became 1.9× more common, from 1 in 11,800 baby girls to 1 in 6,100.

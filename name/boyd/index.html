Boyd name popularity since 1925

21,443 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Boyd. Half of them were born in 1952 or earlier, so the typical Boyd is about 74 years old in 2026. Boyd peaked in 1899 as the #228 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Shirley name popularity since 1925

643,668 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Shirley (99.0% girls, 1.0% boys). Half of them were born in 1941 or earlier, so the typical Shirley is about 85 years old in 2026. Shirley peaked in 1935 as the #2 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Shirley births per year, 1925–2025

1935 was the biggest year for Shirley both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1930 and 1935, Shirley became 3.1× more common.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1935 and 1940, its share of baby girls fell by 61%, from 1 in 25 to 1 in 64.

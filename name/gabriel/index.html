Gabriel name popularity since 1925

399,646 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Gabriel (1.7% girls, 98.3% boys). Half of them were born in 2005 or earlier, so the typical Gabriel is about 21 years old in 2026. Gabriel peaked in 2010 as the #21 name for boys, and it ranked #37 in 2025.

Gabriel births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 2008. Measured as a share of all babies, Gabriel peaked in 2010.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1941 and 1946, Gabriel's share of baby boys fell by 36%, from 1 in 5,960 to 1 in 9,390.
  • Fastest rise: Between 1967 and 1972, it became 3.2× more common, from 1 in 2,710 baby boys to 1 in 845.

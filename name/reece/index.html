Reece name popularity since 1925

26,245 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Reece (18.9% girls, 81.1% boys). Half of them were born in 2008 or earlier, so the typical Reece is about 18 years old in 2026. Reece peaked in 2001 as the #344 name for boys, and it ranked #460 in 2025.

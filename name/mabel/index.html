Mabel name popularity since 1925

39,928 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Mabel. Half of them were born in 1942 or earlier, so the typical Mabel is about 84 years old in 2026. Mabel peaked in 1891 as the #15 name for girls, and it ranked #201 in 2025.

Mabel births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year on this chart was 1925. Measured as a share of all babies, Mabel peaked earlier, in 1891.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1945 and 1950, Mabel's share of baby girls fell by 49%, from 1 in 3,800 to 1 in 7,510.
  • Fastest rise: Between 2011 and 2016, it became 3.1× more common, from 1 in 8,970 baby girls to 1 in 2,930.

Staci name popularity since 1925

25,539 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Staci. Half of them were born in 1977 or earlier, so the typical Staci is about 49 years old in 2026. Staci peaked in 1973 as the #225 name for girls, and fewer than 5 girls got it in 2025.

Staci births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1971. Measured as a share of all babies, Staci peaked in 1973.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1961 and 1966, Staci became 5.9× more common, from 1 in 17,100 baby girls to 1 in 2,910.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1992 and 1997, its share of baby girls fell by 72%, from 1 in 4,200 to 1 in 15,000.

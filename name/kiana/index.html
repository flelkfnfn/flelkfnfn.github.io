Kiana name popularity since 1925

26,713 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Kiana. Half of them were born in 2000 or earlier, so the typical Kiana is about 26 years old in 2026. Kiana peaked in 1996 as the #190 name for girls, and it ranked #743 in 2025.

Kiana births per year, 1925–2025

1996 was the biggest year for Kiana both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1990 and 1995, Kiana became 6.1× more common, from 1 in 6,980 baby girls to 1 in 1,150.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2008 and 2013, its share of baby girls fell by 45%, from 1 in 2,810 to 1 in 5,120.

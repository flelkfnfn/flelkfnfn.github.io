Eve name popularity since 1925

27,094 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Eve. Half of them were born in 2002 or earlier, so the typical Eve is about 24 years old in 2026. Eve peaked in 2018 as the #441 name for girls, and it ranked #519 in 2025.

Eve births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 2017. Measured as a share of all babies, Eve peaked in 2018.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1982 and 1987, Eve's share of baby girls fell by 42%, from 1 in 8,960 to 1 in 15,400.
  • Fastest rise: Between 1995 and 2000, it became 3.4× more common, from 1 in 12,800 baby girls to 1 in 3,770.

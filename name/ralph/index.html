Ralph name popularity since 1925

298,988 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Ralph. Half of them were born in 1946 or earlier, so the typical Ralph is about 80 years old in 2026. Ralph peaked in 1918 as the #22 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

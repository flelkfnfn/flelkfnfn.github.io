Jon name popularity since 1925

168,934 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Jon (0.7% girls, 99.3% boys). Half of them were born in 1966 or earlier, so the typical Jon is about 60 years old in 2026. Jon peaked in 1965 as the #78 name for boys, and it ranked #951 in 2025.

Jon births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1964. Measured as a share of all babies, Jon peaked in 1965.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1932 and 1937, Jon became 6.1× more common.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2006 and 2011, its share of baby boys fell by 37%, from 1 in 3,450 to 1 in 5,470.

Dan name popularity since 1925

94,314 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Dan. Half of them were born in 1956 or earlier, so the typical Dan is about 70 years old in 2026. Dan peaked in 1958 as the #109 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Dan births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1960. Measured as a share of all babies, Dan peaked in 1958.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1954 and 1959, Dan became 1.6× more common, from 1 in 946 baby boys to 1 in 581.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1969 and 1974, its share of baby boys fell by 53%, from 1 in 1,540 to 1 in 3,280.

Presley name popularity since 1925

36,396 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Presley (87.3% girls, 12.7% boys). Half of them were born in 2014 or earlier, so the typical Presley is about 12 years old in 2026. Presley peaked in 2014 as the #190 name for girls, and it ranked #225 in 2025.

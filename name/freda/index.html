Freda name popularity since 1925

24,595 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Freda. Half of them were born in 1944 or earlier, so the typical Freda is about 82 years old in 2026. Freda peaked in 1897 as the #161 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Autumn name popularity since 1925

146,233 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Autumn. Half of them were born in 2005 or earlier, so the typical Autumn is about 21 years old in 2026. Autumn peaked in 1998 as the #77 name for girls, and it ranked #88 in 2025.

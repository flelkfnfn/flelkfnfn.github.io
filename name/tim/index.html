Tim name popularity since 1925

82,337 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Tim. Half of them were born in 1961 or earlier, so the typical Tim is about 65 years old in 2026. Tim peaked in 1960 as the #60 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Tim births per year, 1925–2025

1960 was the biggest year for Tim both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1954 and 1959, Tim became 8× more common, from 1 in 2,500 baby boys to 1 in 313.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1968 and 1973, its share of baby boys fell by 72%, from 1 in 838 to 1 in 2,980.

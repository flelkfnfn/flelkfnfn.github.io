Diana name popularity since 1925

366,436 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Diana. Half of them were born in 1964 or earlier, so the typical Diana is about 62 years old in 2026. Diana peaked in 1946 as the #42 name for girls, and it ranked #244 in 2025.

Diana births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1957. Measured as a share of all babies, Diana peaked in 1946.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1936 and 1941, Diana became 2.6× more common, from 1 in 1,250 baby girls to 1 in 475.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2007 and 2012, its share of baby girls fell by 56%, from 1 in 604 to 1 in 1,370.

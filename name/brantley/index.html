Brantley name popularity since 1925

32,313 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Brantley (2.5% girls, 97.5% boys). Half of them were born in 2015 or earlier, so the typical Brantley is about 11 years old in 2026. Brantley peaked in 2014 as the #121 name for boys, and it ranked #499 in 2025.

Brantley births per year, 1925–2025

2014 was the biggest year for Brantley both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 2009 and 2014, Brantley became 25× more common, from 1 in 14,500 baby boys to 1 in 573.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2020 and 2025, its share of baby boys fell by 61%, from 1 in 1,090 to 1 in 2,820.

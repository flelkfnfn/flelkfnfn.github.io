Darius name popularity since 1925

54,028 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Darius. Half of them were born in 1997 or earlier, so the typical Darius is about 29 years old in 2026. Darius peaked in 1994 as the #155 name for boys, and it ranked #788 in 2025.

Darius births per year, 1925–2025

1994 was the biggest year for Darius both by count and as a share of all babies.

Constance name popularity since 1925

123,300 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Constance. Half of them were born in 1950 or earlier, so the typical Constance is about 76 years old in 2026. Constance peaked in 1950 as the #85 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Nayeli name popularity since 1925

24,665 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Nayeli. Half of them were born in 2010 or earlier, so the typical Nayeli is about 16 years old in 2026. Nayeli peaked in 2001 as the #175 name for girls, and it ranked #271 in 2025.

Nayeli births per year, 1925–2025

Buddy name popularity since 1925

21,179 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Buddy. Half of them were born in 1950 or earlier, so the typical Buddy is about 76 years old in 2026. Buddy peaked in 1935 as the #202 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Buddy births per year, 1925–2025

1935 was the biggest year for Buddy both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1925 and 1930, Buddy became 2.6× more common.

Penny name popularity since 1925

100,536 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Penny. Half of them were born in 1962 or earlier, so the typical Penny is about 64 years old in 2026. Penny peaked in 1963 as the #86 name for girls, and it ranked #740 in 2025.

Penny births per year, 1925–2025

1963 was the biggest year for Penny both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1936 and 1941, Penny became 5.5× more common, from 1 in 10,400 baby girls to 1 in 1,900.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1978 and 1983, its share of baby girls fell by 64%, from 1 in 2,040 to 1 in 5,660.

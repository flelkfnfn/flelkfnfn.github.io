Alexis name popularity since 1925

412,407 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Alexis (83.5% girls, 16.5% boys). Half of them were born in 2000 or earlier, so the typical Alexis is about 26 years old in 2026. Alexis peaked in 1998 as the #6 name for girls, and it ranked #554 in 2025.

Alexis births per year, 1925–2025

1998 was the biggest year for Alexis both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1978 and 1983, Alexis became 4.1× more common, from 1 in 1,990 baby girls to 1 in 483.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2015 and 2020, its share of baby girls fell by 67%, from 1 in 520 to 1 in 1,560.

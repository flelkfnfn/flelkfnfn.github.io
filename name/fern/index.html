Fern name popularity since 1925

17,425 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Fern (98.7% girls, 1.3% boys). Half of them were born in 1939 or earlier, so the typical Fern is about 87 years old in 2026. Fern peaked in 1916 as the #152 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Fern births per year, 1925–2025

Lawrence name popularity since 1925

383,073 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Lawrence. Half of them were born in 1952 or earlier, so the typical Lawrence is about 74 years old in 2026. Lawrence peaked in 1949 as the #32 name for boys, and it ranked #520 in 2025.

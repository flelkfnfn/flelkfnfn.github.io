Tia name popularity since 1925

33,896 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Tia. Half of them were born in 1990 or earlier, so the typical Tia is about 36 years old in 2026. Tia peaked in 1982 as the #221 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Tia births per year, 1925–2025

1982 was the biggest year for Tia both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1963 and 1968, Tia became 4× more common, from 1 in 14,700 baby girls to 1 in 3,650.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2004 and 2009, its share of baby girls fell by 51%, from 1 in 3,190 to 1 in 6,540.

Angeline name popularity since 1925

22,773 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Angeline. Half of them were born in 1969 or earlier, so the typical Angeline is about 57 years old in 2026. Angeline peaked in 1915 as the #183 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

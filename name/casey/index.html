Casey name popularity since 1925

193,988 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Casey (40.3% girls, 59.7% boys). Half of them were born in 1989 or earlier, so the typical Casey is about 37 years old in 2026. Casey peaked in 1988 as the #83 name for boys, and it ranked #320 in 2025.

Casey births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1990. Measured as a share of all babies, Casey peaked in 1988.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1969 and 1974, Casey became 2.9× more common, from 1 in 2,640 baby boys to 1 in 910.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2000 and 2005, its share of baby boys fell by 41%, from 1 in 1,080 to 1 in 1,810.

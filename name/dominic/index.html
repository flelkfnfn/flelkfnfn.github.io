Dominic name popularity since 1925

187,976 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Dominic (0.7% girls, 99.3% boys). Half of them were born in 2006 or earlier, so the typical Dominic is about 20 years old in 2026. Dominic peaked in 2013 as the #68 name for boys, and it ranked #106 in 2025.

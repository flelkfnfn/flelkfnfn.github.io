Claire name popularity since 1925

196,615 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Claire (99.4% girls, 0.6% boys). Half of them were born in 2002 or earlier, so the typical Claire is about 24 years old in 2026. Claire peaked in 2016 as the #40 name for girls, and it ranked #68 in 2025.

Claire births per year, 1925–2025

2016 was the biggest year for Claire both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1962 and 1967, Claire's share of baby girls fell by 43%, from 1 in 2,440 to 1 in 4,240.
  • Fastest rise: Between 1980 and 1985, it became 2× more common, from 1 in 2,930 baby girls to 1 in 1,450.

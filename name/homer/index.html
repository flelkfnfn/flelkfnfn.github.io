Homer name popularity since 1925

33,669 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Homer (0.6% girls, 99.4% boys). Half of them were born in 1940 or earlier, so the typical Homer is about 86 years old in 2026. Homer peaked in 1893 as the #64 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

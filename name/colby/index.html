Colby name popularity since 1925

58,068 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Colby (7.2% girls, 92.8% boys). Half of them were born in 2001 or earlier, so the typical Colby is about 25 years old in 2026. Colby peaked in 2001 as the #99 name for boys, and it ranked #615 in 2025.

Colby births per year, 1925–2025

2001 was the biggest year for Colby both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1981 and 1986, Colby became 3.6× more common, from 1 in 7,400 baby boys to 1 in 2,070.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2001 and 2006, its share of baby boys fell by 69%, from 1 in 503 to 1 in 1,620.

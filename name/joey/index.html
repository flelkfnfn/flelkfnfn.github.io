Joey name popularity since 1925

61,287 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Joey (12.4% girls, 87.6% boys). Half of them were born in 1977 or earlier, so the typical Joey is about 49 years old in 2026. Joey peaked in 1976 as the #194 name for boys, and it ranked #877 in 2025.

Joey births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1962. Measured as a share of all babies, Joey peaked in 1976.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1951 and 1956, Joey became 4.3× more common, from 1 in 9,610 baby boys to 1 in 2,240.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1981 and 1986, its share of baby boys fell by 41%, from 1 in 1,670 to 1 in 2,810.

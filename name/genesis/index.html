Genesis name popularity since 1925

97,244 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Genesis (96.2% girls, 3.8% boys). Half of them were born in 2014 or earlier, so the typical Genesis is about 12 years old in 2026. Genesis peaked in 2017 as the #56 name for girls, and it ranked #66 in 2025.

Linda name popularity since 1925

1,452,186 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Linda. Half of them were born in 1952 or earlier, so the typical Linda is about 74 years old in 2026. Linda peaked in 1948 as the #1 name for girls, and it ranked #902 in 2025.

Linda births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1947. Measured as a share of all babies, Linda peaked in 1948.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1935 and 1940, Linda became 14× more common, from 1 in 876 baby girls to 1 in 62.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1965 and 1970, its share of baby girls fell by 54%, from 1 in 91 to 1 in 200.

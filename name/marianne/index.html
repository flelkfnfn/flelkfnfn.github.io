Marianne name popularity since 1925

63,022 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Marianne. Half of them were born in 1956 or earlier, so the typical Marianne is about 70 years old in 2026. Marianne peaked in 1957 as the #139 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Rodolfo name popularity since 1925

32,956 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Rodolfo. Half of them were born in 1983 or earlier, so the typical Rodolfo is about 43 years old in 2026. Rodolfo peaked in 1981 as the #311 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

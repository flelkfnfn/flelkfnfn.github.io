Shelley name popularity since 1925

73,896 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Shelley (98.1% girls, 1.9% boys). Half of them were born in 1966 or earlier, so the typical Shelley is about 60 years old in 2026. Shelley peaked in 1972 as the #126 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Shelley births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1964. Measured as a share of all babies, Shelley peaked in 1972.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1947 and 1952, Shelley became 8.2× more common, from 1 in 8,090 baby girls to 1 in 983.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1986 and 1991, its share of baby girls fell by 58%, from 1 in 3,050 to 1 in 7,300.

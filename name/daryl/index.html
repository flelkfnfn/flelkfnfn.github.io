Daryl name popularity since 1925

66,720 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Daryl (6.5% girls, 93.5% boys). Half of them were born in 1965 or earlier, so the typical Daryl is about 61 years old in 2026. Daryl peaked in 1970 as the #161 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Daryl births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1956. Measured as a share of all babies, Daryl peaked in 1970.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1935 and 1940, Daryl became 2.2× more common, from 1 in 7,770 baby boys to 1 in 3,480.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1970 and 1975, its share of baby boys fell by 44%, from 1 in 1,040 to 1 in 1,860.

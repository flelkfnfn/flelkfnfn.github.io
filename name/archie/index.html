Archie name popularity since 1925

39,169 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Archie (2.1% girls, 97.9% boys). Half of them were born in 1952 or earlier, so the typical Archie is about 74 years old in 2026. Archie peaked in 1890 as the #79 name for boys, and it ranked #301 in 2025.

Archie births per year, 1925–2025

Ricky name popularity since 1925

225,089 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Ricky (0.9% girls, 99.1% boys). Half of them were born in 1962 or earlier, so the typical Ricky is about 64 years old in 2026. Ricky peaked in 1958 as the #34 name for boys, and it ranked #949 in 2025.

Ricky births per year, 1925–2025

1958 was the biggest year for Ricky both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1942 and 1947, Ricky became 8.9× more common, from 1 in 12,600 baby boys to 1 in 1,410.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1968 and 1973, its share of baby boys fell by 46%, from 1 in 431 to 1 in 804.

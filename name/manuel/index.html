Manuel name popularity since 1925

173,101 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Manuel (0.6% girls, 99.4% boys). Half of them were born in 1980 or earlier, so the typical Manuel is about 46 years old in 2026. Manuel peaked in 1906 as the #104 name for boys, and it ranked #371 in 2025.

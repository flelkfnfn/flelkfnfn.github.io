Penelope name popularity since 1925

108,903 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Penelope. Half of them were born in 2017 or earlier, so the typical Penelope is about 9 years old in 2026. Penelope peaked in 2019 as the #22 name for girls, and it ranked #22 in 2025.

Penelope births per year, 1925–2025

2019 was the biggest year for Penelope both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1973 and 1978, Penelope's share of baby girls fell by 53%, from 1 in 6,600 to 1 in 14,200.
  • Fastest rise: Between 2008 and 2013, it became 5× more common, from 1 in 2,060 baby girls to 1 in 409.

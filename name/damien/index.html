Damien name popularity since 1925

65,018 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Damien. Half of them were born in 2004 or earlier, so the typical Damien is about 22 years old in 2026. Damien peaked in 2007 as the #182 name for boys, and it ranked #352 in 2025.

Damien births per year, 1925–2025

2007 was the biggest year for Damien both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1970 and 1975, Damien became 5.2× more common, from 1 in 15,000 baby boys to 1 in 2,880.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1978 and 1983, its share of baby boys fell by 52%, from 1 in 1,090 to 1 in 2,270.

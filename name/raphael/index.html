Raphael name popularity since 1925

22,917 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Raphael (1.0% girls, 99.0% boys). Half of them were born in 2001 or earlier, so the typical Raphael is about 25 years old in 2026. Raphael peaked in 2025 as the #394 name for boys, and it ranked #394 in 2025.

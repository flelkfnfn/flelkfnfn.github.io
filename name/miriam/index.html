Miriam name popularity since 1925

93,996 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Miriam. Half of them were born in 1980 or earlier, so the typical Miriam is about 46 years old in 2026. Miriam peaked in 1917 as the #134 name for girls, and it ranked #246 in 2025.

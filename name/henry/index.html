Henry name popularity since 1925

568,582 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Henry. Half of them were born in 1966 or earlier, so the typical Henry is about 60 years old in 2026. Henry peaked in 1881 as the #8 name for boys, and it ranked #5 in 2025.

Thomas name popularity since 1925

2,129,694 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Thomas. Half of them were born in 1959 or earlier, so the typical Thomas is about 67 years old in 2026. Thomas peaked in 1951 as the #8 name for boys, and it ranked #34 in 2025.

Camden name popularity since 1925

63,069 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Camden (6.2% girls, 93.8% boys). Half of them were born in 2014 or earlier, so the typical Camden is about 12 years old in 2026. Camden peaked in 2013 as the #99 name for boys, and it ranked #205 in 2025.

Esteban name popularity since 1925

35,307 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Esteban. Half of them were born in 2003 or earlier, so the typical Esteban is about 23 years old in 2026. Esteban peaked in 2005 as the #320 name for boys, and it ranked #461 in 2025.

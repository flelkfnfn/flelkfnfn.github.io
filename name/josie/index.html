Josie name popularity since 1925

64,013 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Josie (99.2% girls, 0.8% boys). Half of them were born in 2007 or earlier, so the typical Josie is about 19 years old in 2026. Josie peaked in 2025 as the #82 name for girls, and it ranked #82 in 2025.

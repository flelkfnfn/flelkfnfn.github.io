Marvin name popularity since 1925

215,433 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Marvin (0.6% girls, 99.4% boys). Half of them were born in 1952 or earlier, so the typical Marvin is about 74 years old in 2026. Marvin peaked in 1931 as the #45 name for boys, and it ranked #699 in 2025.

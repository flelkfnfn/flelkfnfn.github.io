Kaylin name popularity since 1925

19,934 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Kaylin (96.9% girls, 3.1% boys). Half of them were born in 2005 or earlier, so the typical Kaylin is about 21 years old in 2026. Kaylin peaked in 2009 as the #371 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Kaylin births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 2008. Measured as a share of all babies, Kaylin peaked in 2009.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1986 and 1991, Kaylin became 2.7× more common, from 1 in 16,800 baby girls to 1 in 6,230.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2012 and 2017, its share of baby girls fell by 57%, from 1 in 2,640 to 1 in 6,100.

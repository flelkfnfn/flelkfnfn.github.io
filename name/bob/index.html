Bob name popularity since 1925

84,404 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Bob. Half of them were born in 1944 or earlier, so the typical Bob is about 82 years old in 2026. Bob peaked in 1934 as the #75 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Bob births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1959. Measured as a share of all babies, Bob peaked in 1934.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1946 and 1951, Bob's share of baby boys fell by 72%, from 1 in 651 to 1 in 2,340.
  • Fastest rise: Between 1954 and 1959, it became 3.9× more common, from 1 in 2,930 baby boys to 1 in 755.

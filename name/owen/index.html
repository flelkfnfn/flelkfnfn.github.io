Owen name popularity since 1925

236,967 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Owen. Half of them were born in 2012 or earlier, so the typical Owen is about 14 years old in 2026. Owen peaked in 2016 as the #24 name for boys, and it ranked #31 in 2025.

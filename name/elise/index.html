Elise name popularity since 1925

71,244 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Elise. Half of them were born in 2003 or earlier, so the typical Elise is about 23 years old in 2026. Elise peaked in 2012 as the #152 name for girls, and it ranked #223 in 2025.

Marjorie name popularity since 1925

168,578 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Marjorie. Half of them were born in 1938 or earlier, so the typical Marjorie is about 88 years old in 2026. Marjorie peaked in 1921 as the #16 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

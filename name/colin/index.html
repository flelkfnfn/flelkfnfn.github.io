Colin name popularity since 1925

133,579 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Colin. Half of them were born in 2001 or earlier, so the typical Colin is about 25 years old in 2026. Colin peaked in 2004 as the #85 name for boys, and it ranked #343 in 2025.

Colin births per year, 1925–2025

2004 was the biggest year for Colin both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1942 and 1947, Colin's share of baby boys fell by 62%, from 1 in 3,960 to 1 in 10,400.
  • Fastest rise: Between 1960 and 1965, it became 1.8× more common, from 1 in 7,230 baby boys to 1 in 4,120.

Sammy name popularity since 1925

43,762 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Sammy (3.7% girls, 96.3% boys). Half of them were born in 1958 or earlier, so the typical Sammy is about 68 years old in 2026. Sammy peaked in 1946 as the #170 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Sammy births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1947. Measured as a share of all babies, Sammy peaked in 1946.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1925 and 1930, Sammy became 2.2× more common.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1947 and 1952, its share of baby boys fell by 39%, from 1 in 1,510 to 1 in 2,490.

Marissa name popularity since 1925

121,112 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Marissa. Half of them were born in 1996 or earlier, so the typical Marissa is about 30 years old in 2026. Marissa peaked in 1994 as the #53 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Marissa births per year, 1925–2025

1994 was the biggest year for Marissa both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1972 and 1977, Marissa became 3.9× more common, from 1 in 5,570 baby girls to 1 in 1,430.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2010 and 2015, its share of baby girls fell by 62%, from 1 in 1,160 to 1 in 3,060.

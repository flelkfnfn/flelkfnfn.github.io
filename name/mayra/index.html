Mayra name popularity since 1925

33,561 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Mayra (99.0% girls, 1.0% boys). Half of them were born in 1991 or earlier, so the typical Mayra is about 35 years old in 2026. Mayra peaked in 1986 as the #179 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Mayra births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1987. Measured as a share of all babies, Mayra peaked in 1986.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1975 and 1980, Mayra became 3× more common, from 1 in 5,610 baby girls to 1 in 1,900.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2009 and 2014, its share of baby girls fell by 49%, from 1 in 5,620 to 1 in 11,000.

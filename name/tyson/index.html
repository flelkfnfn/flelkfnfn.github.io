Tyson name popularity since 1925

48,599 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Tyson (0.7% girls, 99.3% boys). Half of them were born in 2004 or earlier, so the typical Tyson is about 22 years old in 2026. Tyson peaked in 2010 as the #238 name for boys, and it ranked #531 in 2025.

Tyson births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 2009. Measured as a share of all babies, Tyson peaked in 2010.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1969 and 1974, Tyson became 6.2× more common, from 1 in 16,700 baby boys to 1 in 2,680.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1987 and 1992, its share of baby boys fell by 63%, from 1 in 2,000 to 1 in 5,460.

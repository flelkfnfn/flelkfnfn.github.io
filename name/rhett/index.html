Rhett name popularity since 1925

36,905 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Rhett (0.7% girls, 99.3% boys). Half of them were born in 2018 or earlier, so the typical Rhett is about 8 years old in 2026. Rhett peaked in 2021 as the #149 name for boys, and it ranked #188 in 2025.

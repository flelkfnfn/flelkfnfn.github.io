Miguel name popularity since 1925

189,601 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Miguel. Half of them were born in 1997 or earlier, so the typical Miguel is about 29 years old in 2026. Miguel peaked in 2002 as the #89 name for boys, and it ranked #212 in 2025.

Miguel births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 2007. Measured as a share of all babies, Miguel peaked in 2002.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1929 and 1934, Miguel's share of baby boys fell by 33%.
  • Fastest rise: Between 1947 and 1952, it became 1.6× more common, from 1 in 6,270 baby boys to 1 in 3,800.

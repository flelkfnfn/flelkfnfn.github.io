Sam name popularity since 1925

82,334 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Sam (1.4% girls, 98.6% boys). Half of them were born in 1955 or earlier, so the typical Sam is about 71 years old in 2026. Sam peaked in 1900 as the #34 name for boys, and it ranked #647 in 2025.

Sam births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year on this chart was 1925. Measured as a share of all babies, Sam peaked earlier, in 1900.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1947 and 1952, Sam's share of baby boys fell by 38%, from 1 in 1,290 to 1 in 2,070.
  • Fastest rise: Between 1985 and 1990, it became 1.5× more common, from 1 in 5,310 baby boys to 1 in 3,510.

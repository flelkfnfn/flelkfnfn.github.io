Dwayne name popularity since 1925

76,724 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Dwayne (0.6% girls, 99.4% boys). Half of them were born in 1969 or earlier, so the typical Dwayne is about 57 years old in 2026. Dwayne peaked in 1961 as the #111 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Dwayne births per year, 1925–2025

1961 was the biggest year for Dwayne both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1955 and 1960, Dwayne became 5× more common, from 1 in 3,500 baby boys to 1 in 702.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1977 and 1982, its share of baby boys fell by 40%, from 1 in 1,130 to 1 in 1,880.

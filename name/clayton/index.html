Clayton name popularity since 1925

126,859 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Clayton. Half of them were born in 1989 or earlier, so the typical Clayton is about 37 years old in 2026. Clayton peaked in 2000 as the #151 name for boys, and it ranked #300 in 2025.

Clayton births per year, 1925–2025

2000 was the biggest year for Clayton both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1970 and 1975, Clayton became 1.7× more common, from 1 in 2,230 baby boys to 1 in 1,330.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2002 and 2007, its share of baby boys fell by 37%, from 1 in 899 to 1 in 1,440.

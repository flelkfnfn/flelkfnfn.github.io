Axel name popularity since 1925

71,826 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Axel. Half of them were born in 2017 or earlier, so the typical Axel is about 9 years old in 2026. Axel peaked in 2020 as the #72 name for boys, and it ranked #79 in 2025.

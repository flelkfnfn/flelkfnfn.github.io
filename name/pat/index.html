Pat name popularity since 1925

62,499 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Pat (62.5% girls, 37.5% boys). Half of them were born in 1944 or earlier, so the typical Pat is about 82 years old in 2026. Pat peaked in 1940 as the #134 name for girls, and fewer than 5 girls got it in 2025.

Pat births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1941. Measured as a share of all babies, Pat peaked in 1940.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1925 and 1930, Pat became 4× more common.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1946 and 1951, its share of baby girls fell by 85%, from 1 in 840 to 1 in 5,540.

Ray name popularity since 1925

163,810 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Ray (1.9% girls, 98.1% boys). Half of them were born in 1951 or earlier, so the typical Ray is about 75 years old in 2026. Ray peaked in 1896 as the #50 name for boys, and it ranked #792 in 2025.

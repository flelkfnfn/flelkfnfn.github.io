Mohammad births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 2022. Measured as a share of all babies, Mohammad peaked in 2025.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1986 and 1991, Mohammad became 1.7× more common, from 1 in 10,500 baby boys to 1 in 6,200.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2000 and 2005, its share of baby boys fell by 33%, from 1 in 3,850 to 1 in 5,790.

Dion name popularity since 1925

20,164 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Dion (6.2% girls, 93.8% boys). Half of them were born in 1983 or earlier, so the typical Dion is about 43 years old in 2026. Dion peaked in 1970 as the #344 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Dion births per year, 1925–2025

1970 was the biggest year for Dion both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1959 and 1964, Dion became 2.3× more common, from 1 in 16,800 baby boys to 1 in 7,300.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1975 and 1980, its share of baby boys fell by 48%, from 1 in 3,430 to 1 in 6,560.

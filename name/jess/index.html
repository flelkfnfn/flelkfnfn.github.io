Jess name popularity since 1925

17,925 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Jess (2.1% girls, 97.9% boys). Half of them were born in 1966 or earlier, so the typical Jess is about 60 years old in 2026. Jess peaked in 1886 as the #111 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

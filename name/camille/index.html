Camille name popularity since 1925

73,643 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Camille (98.7% girls, 1.3% boys). Half of them were born in 1995 or earlier, so the typical Camille is about 31 years old in 2026. Camille peaked in 2025 as the #240 name for girls, and it ranked #240 in 2025.

Lottie name popularity since 1925

17,271 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Lottie. Half of them were born in 1941 or earlier, so the typical Lottie is about 85 years old in 2026. Lottie peaked in 1883 as the #76 name for girls, and it ranked #528 in 2025.

Lottie births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year on this chart was 1925. Measured as a share of all babies, Lottie peaked earlier, in 1883.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1941 and 1946, Lottie's share of baby girls fell by 50%, from 1 in 3,640 to 1 in 7,210.

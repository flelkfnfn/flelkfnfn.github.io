Crystal name popularity since 1925

331,013 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Crystal. Half of them were born in 1983 or earlier, so the typical Crystal is about 43 years old in 2026. Crystal peaked in 1982 as the #9 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Crystal births per year, 1925–2025

1982 was the biggest year for Crystal both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1949 and 1954, Crystal became 2.5× more common, from 1 in 6,340 baby girls to 1 in 2,560.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1984 and 1989, its share of baby girls fell by 50%, from 1 in 102 to 1 in 203.

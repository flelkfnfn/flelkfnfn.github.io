Nick name popularity since 1925

40,845 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Nick. Half of them were born in 1961 or earlier, so the typical Nick is about 65 years old in 2026. Nick peaked in 1917 as the #177 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Nick births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year on this chart was 1960. Measured as a share of all babies, Nick peaked earlier, in 1917.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1954 and 1959, Nick became 2.3× more common, from 1 in 4,740 baby boys to 1 in 2,030.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1977 and 1982, its share of baby boys fell by 43%, from 1 in 3,870 to 1 in 6,800.

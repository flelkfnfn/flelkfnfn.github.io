Mickey name popularity since 1925

32,849 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Mickey (17.8% girls, 82.2% boys). Half of them were born in 1958 or earlier, so the typical Mickey is about 68 years old in 2026. Mickey peaked in 1957 as the #248 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Mickey births per year, 1925–2025

1957 was the biggest year for Mickey both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1937 and 1942, Mickey became 1.9× more common, from 1 in 6,200 baby boys to 1 in 3,180.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1977 and 1982, its share of baby boys fell by 50%, from 1 in 5,090 to 1 in 10,200.

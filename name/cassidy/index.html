Cassidy name popularity since 1925

64,583 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Cassidy (94.2% girls, 5.8% boys). Half of them were born in 2001 or earlier, so the typical Cassidy is about 25 years old in 2026. Cassidy peaked in 1999 as the #99 name for girls, and it ranked #541 in 2025.

Cassidy births per year, 1925–2025

1999 was the biggest year for Cassidy both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1990 and 1995, Cassidy became 6.3× more common, from 1 in 3,640 baby girls to 1 in 578.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2001 and 2006, its share of baby girls fell by 45%, from 1 in 594 to 1 in 1,070.

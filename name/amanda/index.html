Amanda name popularity since 1925

777,236 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Amanda. Half of them were born in 1986 or earlier, so the typical Amanda is about 40 years old in 2026. Amanda peaked in 1987 as the #3 name for girls, and it ranked #493 in 2025.

Amanda births per year, 1925–2025

1987 was the biggest year for Amanda both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1972 and 1977, Amanda became 4.3× more common, from 1 in 364 baby girls to 1 in 84.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2004 and 2009, its share of baby girls fell by 58%, from 1 in 391 to 1 in 936.

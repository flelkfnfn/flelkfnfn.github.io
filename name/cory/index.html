Cory name popularity since 1925

133,771 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Cory (4.9% girls, 95.1% boys). Half of them were born in 1987 or earlier, so the typical Cory is about 39 years old in 2026. Cory peaked in 1988 as the #59 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Cory births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1989. Measured as a share of all babies, Cory peaked in 1988.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1967 and 1972, Cory became 4.8× more common, from 1 in 3,980 baby boys to 1 in 829.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1998 and 2003, its share of baby boys fell by 57%, from 1 in 921 to 1 in 2,140.

Shea name popularity since 1925

21,102 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Shea (59.5% girls, 40.5% boys). Half of them were born in 1998 or earlier, so the typical Shea is about 28 years old in 2026. Shea peaked in 2005 as the #764 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Shea births per year, 1925–2025

2005 was the biggest year for Shea both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1983 and 1988, Shea became 2× more common, from 1 in 14,000 baby girls to 1 in 7,040.

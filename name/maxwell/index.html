Maxwell name popularity since 1925

107,204 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Maxwell (0.7% girls, 99.3% boys). Half of them were born in 2007 or earlier, so the typical Maxwell is about 19 years old in 2026. Maxwell peaked in 2014 as the #107 name for boys, and it ranked #189 in 2025.

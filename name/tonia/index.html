Tonia name popularity since 1925

22,210 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Tonia. Half of them were born in 1971 or earlier, so the typical Tonia is about 55 years old in 2026. Tonia peaked in 1971 as the #212 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Tonia births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1970. Measured as a share of all babies, Tonia peaked in 1971.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1963 and 1968, Tonia became 3.4× more common, from 1 in 4,840 baby girls to 1 in 1,430.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1977 and 1982, its share of baby girls fell by 66%, from 1 in 2,120 to 1 in 6,320.

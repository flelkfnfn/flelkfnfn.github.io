Clarissa name popularity since 1925

38,942 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Clarissa. Half of them were born in 1994 or earlier, so the typical Clarissa is about 32 years old in 2026. Clarissa peaked in 1995 as the #239 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Clarissa births per year, 1925–2025

1995 was the biggest year for Clarissa both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1965 and 1970, Clarissa became 1.6× more common, from 1 in 8,830 baby girls to 1 in 5,450.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2019 and 2024, its share of baby girls fell by 43%, from 1 in 4,440 to 1 in 7,830.

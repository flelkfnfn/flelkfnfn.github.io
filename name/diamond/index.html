Diamond name popularity since 1925

36,077 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Diamond (93.2% girls, 6.8% boys). Half of them were born in 1999 or earlier, so the typical Diamond is about 27 years old in 2026. Diamond peaked in 1999 as the #150 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Diamond births per year, 1925–2025

1999 was the biggest year for Diamond both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1987 and 1992, Diamond became 4.7× more common, from 1 in 6,690 baby girls to 1 in 1,420.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2006 and 2011, its share of baby girls fell by 63%, from 1 in 1,810 to 1 in 4,850.

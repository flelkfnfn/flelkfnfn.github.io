Myrtle name popularity since 1925

39,124 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Myrtle. Half of them were born in 1934 or earlier, so the typical Myrtle is about 92 years old in 2026. Myrtle peaked in 1895 as the #28 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

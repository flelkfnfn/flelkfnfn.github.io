Jeri name popularity since 1925

22,974 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Jeri (98.4% girls, 1.6% boys). Half of them were born in 1961 or earlier, so the typical Jeri is about 65 years old in 2026. Jeri peaked in 1957 as the #300 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Jeri births per year, 1925–2025

1957 was the biggest year for Jeri both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1941 and 1946, Jeri became 2.1× more common, from 1 in 9,900 baby girls to 1 in 4,790.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1984 and 1989, its share of baby girls fell by 49%, from 1 in 7,910 to 1 in 15,500.

Easton name popularity since 1925

80,358 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Easton (1.2% girls, 98.8% boys). Half of them were born in 2016 or earlier, so the typical Easton is about 10 years old in 2026. Easton peaked in 2018 as the #66 name for boys, and it ranked #110 in 2025.

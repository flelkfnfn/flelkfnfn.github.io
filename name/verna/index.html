Verna name popularity since 1925

38,650 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Verna (99.2% girls, 0.8% boys). Half of them were born in 1941 or earlier, so the typical Verna is about 85 years old in 2026. Verna peaked in 1906 as the #117 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Cora name popularity since 1925

96,944 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Cora. Half of them were born in 2006 or earlier, so the typical Cora is about 20 years old in 2026. Cora peaked in 1880 as the #15 name for girls, and it ranked #113 in 2025.

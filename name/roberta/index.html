Roberta name popularity since 1925

149,389 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Roberta. Half of them were born in 1949 or earlier, so the typical Roberta is about 77 years old in 2026. Roberta peaked in 1937 as the #66 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Roberta births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1953. Measured as a share of all babies, Roberta peaked in 1937.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1931 and 1936, Roberta became 1.8× more common.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1989 and 1994, its share of baby girls fell by 47%, from 1 in 7,950 to 1 in 15,000.

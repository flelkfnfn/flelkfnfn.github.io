Marlene name popularity since 1925

130,306 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Marlene. Half of them were born in 1949 or earlier, so the typical Marlene is about 77 years old in 2026. Marlene peaked in 1936 as the #39 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

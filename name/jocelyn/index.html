Jocelyn name popularity since 1925

113,092 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Jocelyn. Half of them were born in 2004 or earlier, so the typical Jocelyn is about 22 years old in 2026. Jocelyn peaked in 2007 as the #50 name for girls, and it ranked #393 in 2025.

Jocelyn births per year, 1925–2025

2007 was the biggest year for Jocelyn both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1972 and 1977, Jocelyn became 2.7× more common, from 1 in 4,330 baby girls to 1 in 1,630.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2013 and 2018, its share of baby girls fell by 49%, from 1 in 557 to 1 in 1,090.

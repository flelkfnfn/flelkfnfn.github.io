Nolan name popularity since 1925

133,876 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Nolan. Half of them were born in 2013 or earlier, so the typical Nolan is about 13 years old in 2026. Nolan peaked in 2018 as the #65 name for boys, and it ranked #65 in 2025.

Marshall name popularity since 1925

85,844 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Marshall (1.3% girls, 98.7% boys). Half of them were born in 1973 or earlier, so the typical Marshall is about 53 years old in 2026. Marshall peaked in 1908 as the #148 name for boys, and it ranked #340 in 2025.

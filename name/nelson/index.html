Nelson name popularity since 1925

68,966 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Nelson (0.6% girls, 99.4% boys). Half of them were born in 1968 or earlier, so the typical Nelson is about 58 years old in 2026. Nelson peaked in 1899 as the #147 name for boys, and it ranked #825 in 2025.

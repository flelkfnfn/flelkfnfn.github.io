Asia name popularity since 1925

32,687 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Asia (99.4% girls, 0.6% boys). Half of them were born in 1999 or earlier, so the typical Asia is about 27 years old in 2026. Asia peaked in 1997 as the #195 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Asia births per year, 1925–2025

1997 was the biggest year for Asia both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1987 and 1992, Asia became 2.7× more common, from 1 in 4,460 baby girls to 1 in 1,660.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2007 and 2012, its share of baby girls fell by 64%, from 1 in 1,870 to 1 in 5,160.

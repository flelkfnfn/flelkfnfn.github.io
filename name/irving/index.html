Irving name popularity since 1925

23,673 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Irving. Half of them were born in 1948 or earlier, so the typical Irving is about 78 years old in 2026. Irving peaked in 1911 as the #93 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

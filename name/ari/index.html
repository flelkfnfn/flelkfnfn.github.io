Ari name popularity since 1925

28,254 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Ari (27.1% girls, 72.9% boys). Half of them were born in 2015 or earlier, so the typical Ari is about 11 years old in 2026. Ari peaked in 2022 as the #328 name for boys, and it ranked #393 in 2025.

Ari births per year, 1925–2025

2022 was the biggest year for Ari both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1979 and 1984, Ari's share of baby boys fell by 36%, from 1 in 11,000 to 1 in 17,200.
  • Fastest rise: Between 2005 and 2010, it became 1.9× more common, from 1 in 6,940 baby boys to 1 in 3,710.

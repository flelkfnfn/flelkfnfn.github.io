Shelia name popularity since 1925

47,813 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Shelia. Half of them were born in 1961 or earlier, so the typical Shelia is about 65 years old in 2026. Shelia peaked in 1962 as the #173 name for girls, and fewer than 5 girls got it in 2025.

Shelia births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1959. Measured as a share of all babies, Shelia peaked in 1962.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1942 and 1947, Shelia became 3.7× more common, from 1 in 8,890 baby girls to 1 in 2,380.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1973 and 1978, its share of baby girls fell by 65%, from 1 in 2,110 to 1 in 6,100.

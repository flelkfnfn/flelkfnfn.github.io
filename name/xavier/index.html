Xavier name popularity since 1925

160,991 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Xavier. Half of them were born in 2008 or earlier, so the typical Xavier is about 18 years old in 2026. Xavier peaked in 2007 as the #68 name for boys, and it ranked #108 in 2025.

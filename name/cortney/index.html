Cortney name popularity since 1925

20,096 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Cortney (84.2% girls, 15.8% boys). Half of them were born in 1989 or earlier, so the typical Cortney is about 37 years old in 2026. Cortney peaked in 1990 as the #226 name for girls, and fewer than 5 girls got it in 2025.

Cortney births per year, 1925–2025

1990 was the biggest year for Cortney both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1975 and 1980, Cortney became 2.1× more common, from 1 in 7,630 baby girls to 1 in 3,590.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1997 and 2002, its share of baby girls fell by 66%, from 1 in 4,350 to 1 in 12,700.

Aaliyah name popularity since 1925

110,077 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Aaliyah. Half of them were born in 2012 or earlier, so the typical Aaliyah is about 14 years old in 2026. Aaliyah peaked in 2012 as the #36 name for girls, and it ranked #101 in 2025.

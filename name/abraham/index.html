Abraham name popularity since 1925

92,317 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Abraham. Half of them were born in 2003 or earlier, so the typical Abraham is about 23 years old in 2026. Abraham peaked in 2016 as the #157 name for boys, and it ranked #209 in 2025.

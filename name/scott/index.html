Scott name popularity since 1925

774,088 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Scott. Half of them were born in 1969 or earlier, so the typical Scott is about 57 years old in 2026. Scott peaked in 1971 as the #10 name for boys, and it ranked #534 in 2025.

Scott births per year, 1925–2025

1971 was the biggest year for Scott both by count and as a share of all babies.

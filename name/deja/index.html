Deja name popularity since 1925

16,360 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Deja. Half of them were born in 1998 or earlier, so the typical Deja is about 28 years old in 2026. Deja peaked in 1996 as the #182 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Deja births per year, 1925–2025

1996 was the biggest year for Deja both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1991 and 1996, Deja became 5.6× more common, from 1 in 5,950 baby girls to 1 in 1,070.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2007 and 2012, its share of baby girls fell by 66%, from 1 in 4,440 to 1 in 12,900.

Troy name popularity since 1925

211,963 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Troy (1.2% girls, 98.8% boys). Half of them were born in 1972 or earlier, so the typical Troy is about 54 years old in 2026. Troy peaked in 1968 as the #40 name for boys, and it ranked #533 in 2025.

Troy births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1970. Measured as a share of all babies, Troy peaked in 1968.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1958 and 1963, Troy became 8.2× more common, from 1 in 2,770 baby boys to 1 in 340.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1972 and 1977, its share of baby boys fell by 49%, from 1 in 255 to 1 in 497.

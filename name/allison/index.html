Allison name popularity since 1925

321,717 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Allison (99.1% girls, 0.9% boys). Half of them were born in 1995 or earlier, so the typical Allison is about 31 years old in 2026. Allison peaked in 1995 as the #34 name for girls, and it ranked #103 in 2025.

Allison births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1994. Measured as a share of all babies, Allison peaked in 1995.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1954 and 1959, Allison became 3.7× more common, from 1 in 6,700 baby girls to 1 in 1,800.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2015 and 2020, its share of baby girls fell by 35%, from 1 in 333 to 1 in 511.

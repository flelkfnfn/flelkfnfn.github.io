Jazmin name popularity since 1925

44,648 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Jazmin. Half of them were born in 2003 or earlier, so the typical Jazmin is about 23 years old in 2026. Jazmin peaked in 2006 as the #155 name for girls, and it ranked #710 in 2025.

Rickie name popularity since 1925

18,230 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Rickie (10.1% girls, 89.9% boys). Half of them were born in 1959 or earlier, so the typical Rickie is about 67 years old in 2026. Rickie peaked in 1954 as the #218 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Rickie births per year, 1925–2025

1954 was the biggest year for Rickie both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1946 and 1951, Rickie became 2.8× more common, from 1 in 9,840 baby boys to 1 in 3,550.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1959 and 1964, its share of baby boys fell by 52%, from 1 in 2,880 to 1 in 5,980.

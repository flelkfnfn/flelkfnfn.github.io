Jamie name popularity since 1925

358,078 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Jamie (75.4% girls, 24.6% boys). Half of them were born in 1981 or earlier, so the typical Jamie is about 45 years old in 2026. Jamie peaked in 1976 as the #18 name for girls, and it ranked #858 in 2025.

Jamie births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1977. Measured as a share of all babies, Jamie peaked in 1976.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1971 and 1976, Jamie became 4.7× more common, from 1 in 545 baby girls to 1 in 117.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2008 and 2013, its share of baby girls fell by 50%, from 1 in 1,450 to 1 in 2,880.

Elliot name popularity since 1925

64,564 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Elliot (10.9% girls, 89.1% boys). Half of them were born in 2013 or earlier, so the typical Elliot is about 13 years old in 2026. Elliot peaked in 2022 as the #150 name for boys, and it ranked #151 in 2025.

Elliot births per year, 1925–2025

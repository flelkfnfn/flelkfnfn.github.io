Erick name popularity since 1925

79,543 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Erick. Half of them were born in 2001 or earlier, so the typical Erick is about 25 years old in 2026. Erick peaked in 2007 as the #166 name for boys, and it ranked #366 in 2025.

Erick births per year, 1925–2025

2007 was the biggest year for Erick both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1961 and 1966, Erick became 3.4× more common, from 1 in 15,300 baby boys to 1 in 4,500.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2008 and 2013, its share of baby boys fell by 34%, from 1 in 919 to 1 in 1,390.

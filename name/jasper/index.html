Jasper name popularity since 1925

56,605 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Jasper (2.2% girls, 97.8% boys). Half of them were born in 2015 or earlier, so the typical Jasper is about 11 years old in 2026. Jasper peaked in 2023 as the #122 name for boys, and it ranked #129 in 2025.

Jasper births per year, 1925–2025

2023 was the biggest year for Jasper both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1942 and 1947, Jasper's share of baby boys fell by 36%, from 1 in 5,730 to 1 in 9,010.
  • Fastest rise: Between 2006 and 2011, it became 3.1× more common, from 1 in 4,650 baby boys to 1 in 1,520.

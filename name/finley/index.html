Finley name popularity since 1925

41,567 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Finley (55.1% girls, 44.9% boys). Half of them were born in 2017 or earlier, so the typical Finley is about 9 years old in 2026. Finley peaked in 2017 as the #159 name for girls, and it ranked #415 in 2025.

Finley births per year, 1925–2025

2017 was the biggest year for Finley both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 2004 and 2009, Finley became 3.4× more common, from 1 in 10,900 baby girls to 1 in 3,190.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2020 and 2025, its share of baby girls fell by 47%, from 1 in 1,120 to 1 in 2,130.

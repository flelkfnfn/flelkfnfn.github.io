Rosemary name popularity since 1925

144,449 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Rosemary. Half of them were born in 1949 or earlier, so the typical Rosemary is about 77 years old in 2026. Rosemary peaked in 1946 as the #74 name for girls, and it ranked #251 in 2025.

Rosemary births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1947. Measured as a share of all babies, Rosemary peaked in 1946.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1950 and 1955, Rosemary's share of baby girls fell by 49%, from 1 in 496 to 1 in 970.
  • Fastest rise: Between 2020 and 2025, it became 1.8× more common, from 1 in 2,320 baby girls to 1 in 1,320.

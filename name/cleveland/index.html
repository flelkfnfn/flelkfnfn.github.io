Cleveland name popularity since 1925

16,308 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Cleveland. Half of them were born in 1953 or earlier, so the typical Cleveland is about 73 years old in 2026. Cleveland peaked in 1884 as the #92 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Peter name popularity since 1925

535,804 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Peter. Half of them were born in 1964 or earlier, so the typical Peter is about 62 years old in 2026. Peter peaked in 1957 as the #37 name for boys, and it ranked #187 in 2025.

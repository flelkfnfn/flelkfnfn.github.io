Ora name popularity since 1925

19,764 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Ora (87.5% girls, 12.5% boys). Half of them were born in 1939 or earlier, so the typical Ora is about 87 years old in 2026. Ora peaked in 1881 as the #104 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

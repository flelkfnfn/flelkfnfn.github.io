Marilyn name popularity since 1925

365,342 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Marilyn. Half of them were born in 1946 or earlier, so the typical Marilyn is about 80 years old in 2026. Marilyn peaked in 1936 as the #13 name for girls, and it ranked #761 in 2025.

Marilyn births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1947. Measured as a share of all babies, Marilyn peaked in 1936.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1925 and 1930, Marilyn became 2.9× more common.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1954 and 1959, its share of baby girls fell by 61%, from 1 in 180 to 1 in 466.

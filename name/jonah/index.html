Jonah name popularity since 1925

85,449 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Jonah (0.9% girls, 99.1% boys). Half of them were born in 2010 or earlier, so the typical Jonah is about 16 years old in 2026. Jonah peaked in 2023 as the #124 name for boys, and it ranked #128 in 2025.

Jonah births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 2008. Measured as a share of all babies, Jonah peaked in 2023.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1981 and 1986, Jonah's share of baby boys fell by 40%, from 1 in 4,660 to 1 in 7,840.
  • Fastest rise: Between 1992 and 1997, it became 7.7× more common, from 1 in 8,920 baby boys to 1 in 1,160.

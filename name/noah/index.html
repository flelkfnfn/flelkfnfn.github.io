Noah name popularity since 1925

529,462 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Noah (0.9% girls, 99.1% boys). Half of them were born in 2012 or earlier, so the typical Noah is about 14 years old in 2026. Noah peaked in 2025 as the #2 name for boys, and it ranked #2 in 2025.

Noah births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 2024. Measured as a share of all babies, Noah peaked in 2025.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1982 and 1987, Noah's share of baby boys fell by 47%, from 1 in 999 to 1 in 1,870.
  • Fastest rise: Between 1993 and 1998, it became 9.2× more common, from 1 in 1,300 baby boys to 1 in 142.

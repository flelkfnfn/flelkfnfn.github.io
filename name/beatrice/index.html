Beatrice name popularity since 1925

97,123 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Beatrice. Half of them were born in 1941 or earlier, so the typical Beatrice is about 85 years old in 2026. Beatrice peaked in 1910 as the #36 name for girls, and it ranked #508 in 2025.

Beatrice births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year on this chart was 1925. Measured as a share of all babies, Beatrice peaked earlier, in 1910.

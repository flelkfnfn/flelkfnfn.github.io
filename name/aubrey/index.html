Aubrey name popularity since 1925

157,595 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Aubrey (84.5% girls, 15.5% boys). Half of them were born in 2011 or earlier, so the typical Aubrey is about 15 years old in 2026. Aubrey peaked in 2012 as the #15 name for girls, and it ranked #146 in 2025.

Aubrey births per year, 1925–2025

2012 was the biggest year for Aubrey both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 2003 and 2008, Aubrey became 3.1× more common, from 1 in 1,040 baby girls to 1 in 339.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2019 and 2024, its share of baby girls fell by 50%, from 1 in 365 to 1 in 731.

Tamara name popularity since 1925

131,697 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Tamara. Half of them were born in 1972 or earlier, so the typical Tamara is about 54 years old in 2026. Tamara peaked in 1974 as the #64 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Tamara births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1970. Measured as a share of all babies, Tamara peaked in 1974.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1954 and 1959, Tamara became 5.5× more common, from 1 in 2,920 baby girls to 1 in 533.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2002 and 2007, its share of baby girls fell by 51%, from 1 in 2,250 to 1 in 4,620.

Martina name popularity since 1925

16,971 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Martina. Half of them were born in 1982 or earlier, so the typical Martina is about 44 years old in 2026. Martina peaked in 1984 as the #577 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

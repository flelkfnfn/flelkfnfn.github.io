Samuel name popularity since 1925

740,376 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Samuel. Half of them were born in 1994 or earlier, so the typical Samuel is about 32 years old in 2026. Samuel peaked in 1880 as the #17 name for boys, and it ranked #18 in 2025.

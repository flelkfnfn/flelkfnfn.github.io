Walker name popularity since 1925

46,693 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Walker (2.2% girls, 97.8% boys). Half of them were born in 2019 or earlier, so the typical Walker is about 7 years old in 2026. Walker peaked in 2025 as the #78 name for boys, and it ranked #78 in 2025.

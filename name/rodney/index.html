Rodney name popularity since 1925

244,171 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Rodney. Half of them were born in 1965 or earlier, so the typical Rodney is about 61 years old in 2026. Rodney peaked in 1965 as the #33 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Rodney births per year, 1925–2025

1965 was the biggest year for Rodney both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1960 and 1965, Rodney became 2.2× more common, from 1 in 364 baby boys to 1 in 168.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1966 and 1971, its share of baby boys fell by 49%, from 1 in 174 to 1 in 340.

Patti name popularity since 1925

44,476 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Patti. Half of them were born in 1958 or earlier, so the typical Patti is about 68 years old in 2026. Patti peaked in 1958 as the #137 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Patti births per year, 1925–2025

1958 was the biggest year for Patti both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1948 and 1953, Patti became 3.2× more common, from 1 in 2,930 baby girls to 1 in 925.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1972 and 1977, its share of baby girls fell by 65%, from 1 in 4,610 to 1 in 13,300.

Kendra name popularity since 1925

98,788 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Kendra. Half of them were born in 1990 or earlier, so the typical Kendra is about 36 years old in 2026. Kendra peaked in 1987 as the #77 name for girls, and it ranked #849 in 2025.

Kendra births per year, 1925–2025

1987 was the biggest year for Kendra both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1962 and 1967, Kendra became 2.6× more common, from 1 in 5,390 baby girls to 1 in 2,070.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2013 and 2018, its share of baby girls fell by 63%, from 1 in 1,020 to 1 in 2,720.

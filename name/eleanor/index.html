Eleanor name popularity since 1925

225,677 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Eleanor. Half of them were born in 1956 or earlier, so the typical Eleanor is about 70 years old in 2026. Eleanor peaked in 1915 as the #28 name for girls, and it ranked #12 in 2025.

Eleanor births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year on this chart was 1925. Measured as a share of all babies, Eleanor peaked earlier, in 1915.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1942 and 1947, Eleanor's share of baby girls fell by 55%, from 1 in 427 to 1 in 957.
  • Fastest rise: Between 2009 and 2014, it became 2.6× more common, from 1 in 1,240 baby girls to 1 in 478.

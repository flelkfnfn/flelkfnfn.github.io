Madeline name popularity since 1925

178,959 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Madeline. Half of them were born in 2001 or earlier, so the typical Madeline is about 25 years old in 2026. Madeline peaked in 1998 as the #50 name for girls, and it ranked #81 in 2025.

Madeline births per year, 1925–2025

1998 was the biggest year for Madeline both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1949 and 1954, Madeline's share of baby girls fell by 43%, from 1 in 1,900 to 1 in 3,300.

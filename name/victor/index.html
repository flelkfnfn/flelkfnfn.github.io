Victor name popularity since 1925

297,875 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Victor. Half of them were born in 1980 or earlier, so the typical Victor is about 46 years old in 2026. Victor peaked in 1918 as the #63 name for boys, and it ranked #211 in 2025.

Randolph name popularity since 1925

45,398 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Randolph. Half of them were born in 1956 or earlier, so the typical Randolph is about 70 years old in 2026. Randolph peaked in 1952 as the #154 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Randolph births per year, 1925–2025

1952 was the biggest year for Randolph both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1945 and 1950, Randolph became 1.8× more common, from 1 in 2,090 baby boys to 1 in 1,160.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1954 and 1959, its share of baby boys fell by 42%, from 1 in 1,160 to 1 in 1,990.

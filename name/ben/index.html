Ben name popularity since 1925

60,859 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Ben. Half of them were born in 1960 or earlier, so the typical Ben is about 66 years old in 2026. Ben peaked in 1889 as the #43 name for boys, and it ranked #843 in 2025.

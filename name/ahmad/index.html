Ahmad name popularity since 1925

20,366 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Ahmad. Half of them were born in 2004 or earlier, so the typical Ahmad is about 22 years old in 2026. Ahmad peaked in 2001 as the #386 name for boys, and it ranked #669 in 2025.

Ahmad births per year, 1925–2025

2001 was the biggest year for Ahmad both by count and as a share of all babies.

Teddy name popularity since 1925

32,588 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Teddy (5.0% girls, 95.0% boys). Half of them were born in 1957 or earlier, so the typical Teddy is about 69 years old in 2026. Teddy peaked in 1933 as the #239 name for boys, and it ranked #823 in 2025.

Teddy births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1949. Measured as a share of all babies, Teddy peaked in 1933.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1979 and 1984, Teddy's share of baby boys fell by 48%, from 1 in 5,920 to 1 in 11,300.
  • Fastest rise: Between 2019 and 2024, it became 2.3× more common, from 1 in 14,800 baby boys to 1 in 6,500.

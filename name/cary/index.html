Cary name popularity since 1925

28,465 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Cary (17.7% girls, 82.3% boys). Half of them were born in 1964 or earlier, so the typical Cary is about 62 years old in 2026. Cary peaked in 1966 as the #264 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Cary births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1957. Measured as a share of all babies, Cary peaked in 1966.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1952 and 1957, Cary became 1.5× more common, from 1 in 4,210 baby boys to 1 in 2,740.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1975 and 1980, its share of baby boys fell by 53%, from 1 in 4,340 to 1 in 9,150.

Griffin name popularity since 1925

51,388 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Griffin (1.5% girls, 98.5% boys). Half of them were born in 2010 or earlier, so the typical Griffin is about 16 years old in 2026. Griffin peaked in 2023 as the #214 name for boys, and it ranked #222 in 2025.

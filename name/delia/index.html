Delia name popularity since 1925

28,592 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Delia. Half of them were born in 1964 or earlier, so the typical Delia is about 62 years old in 2026. Delia peaked in 1883 as the #128 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Kasey name popularity since 1925

45,949 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Kasey (72.5% girls, 27.5% boys). Half of them were born in 1992 or earlier, so the typical Kasey is about 34 years old in 2026. Kasey peaked in 1987 as the #203 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

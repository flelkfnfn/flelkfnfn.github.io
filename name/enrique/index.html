Enrique name popularity since 1925

60,249 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Enrique. Half of them were born in 1994 or earlier, so the typical Enrique is about 32 years old in 2026. Enrique peaked in 2000 as the #221 name for boys, and it ranked #571 in 2025.

Sonja name popularity since 1925

39,365 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Sonja. Half of them were born in 1966 or earlier, so the typical Sonja is about 60 years old in 2026. Sonja peaked in 1938 as the #180 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Sonja births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1968. Measured as a share of all babies, Sonja peaked in 1938.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1936 and 1941, Sonja became 2.7× more common, from 1 in 5,780 baby girls to 1 in 2,130.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1938 and 1943, its share of baby girls fell by 63%, from 1 in 988 to 1 in 2,660.

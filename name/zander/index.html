Zander name popularity since 1925

28,777 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Zander. Half of them were born in 2014 or earlier, so the typical Zander is about 12 years old in 2026. Zander peaked in 2013 as the #230 name for boys, and it ranked #397 in 2025.

Kate name popularity since 1925

73,748 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Kate. Half of them were born in 2001 or earlier, so the typical Kate is about 25 years old in 2026. Kate peaked in 1880 as the #79 name for girls, and it ranked #522 in 2025.

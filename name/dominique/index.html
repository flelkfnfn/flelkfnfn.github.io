Dominique name popularity since 1925

74,332 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Dominique (68.3% girls, 31.7% boys). Half of them were born in 1993 or earlier, so the typical Dominique is about 33 years old in 2026. Dominique peaked in 1985 as the #83 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Dominique births per year, 1925–2025

1985 was the biggest year for Dominique both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1980 and 1985, Dominique became 10× more common, from 1 in 5,270 baby girls to 1 in 522.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1997 and 2002, its share of baby girls fell by 70%, from 1 in 652 to 1 in 2,170.

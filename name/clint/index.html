Clint name popularity since 1925

31,576 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Clint. Half of them were born in 1978 or earlier, so the typical Clint is about 48 years old in 2026. Clint peaked in 1980 as the #195 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Clint births per year, 1925–2025

1980 was the biggest year for Clint both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1953 and 1958, Clint became 4.1× more common, from 1 in 18,300 baby boys to 1 in 4,440.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1994 and 1999, its share of baby boys fell by 59%, from 1 in 4,540 to 1 in 11,000.

Kimberly name popularity since 1925

850,666 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Kimberly. Half of them were born in 1973 or earlier, so the typical Kimberly is about 53 years old in 2026. Kimberly peaked in 1967 as the #2 name for girls, and it ranked #303 in 2025.

Kimberly births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1970. Measured as a share of all babies, Kimberly peaked in 1967.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1951 and 1956, Kimberly became 12× more common, from 1 in 3,710 baby girls to 1 in 298.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2014 and 2019, its share of baby girls fell by 44%, from 1 in 616 to 1 in 1,090.

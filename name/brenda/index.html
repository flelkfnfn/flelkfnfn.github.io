Brenda name popularity since 1925

610,183 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Brenda. Half of them were born in 1958 or earlier, so the typical Brenda is about 68 years old in 2026. Brenda peaked in 1950 as the #14 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Brenda births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1957. Measured as a share of all babies, Brenda peaked in 1950.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1935 and 1940, Brenda became 38× more common, from 1 in 7,940 baby girls to 1 in 210.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2006 and 2011, its share of baby girls fell by 58%, from 1 in 1,340 to 1 in 3,220.

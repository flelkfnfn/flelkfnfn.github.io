Isaac name popularity since 1925

318,173 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Isaac. Half of them were born in 2007 or earlier, so the typical Isaac is about 19 years old in 2026. Isaac peaked in 2013 as the #28 name for boys, and it ranked #47 in 2025.

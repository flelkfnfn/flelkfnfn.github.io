Arya name popularity since 1925

31,871 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Arya (92.7% girls, 7.3% boys). Half of them were born in 2019 or earlier, so the typical Arya is about 7 years old in 2026. Arya peaked in 2019 as the #92 name for girls, and it ranked #158 in 2025.

Arya births per year, 1925–2025

2019 was the biggest year for Arya both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 2009 and 2014, Arya became 6.8× more common, from 1 in 7,750 baby girls to 1 in 1,150.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2019 and 2024, its share of baby girls fell by 37%, from 1 in 550 to 1 in 868.

Stefanie name popularity since 1925

37,043 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Stefanie. Half of them were born in 1983 or earlier, so the typical Stefanie is about 43 years old in 2026. Stefanie peaked in 1983 as the #105 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Stefanie births per year, 1925–2025

1983 was the biggest year for Stefanie both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1961 and 1966, Stefanie became 5.9× more common, from 1 in 18,300 baby girls to 1 in 3,090.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1983 and 1988, its share of baby girls fell by 59%, from 1 in 626 to 1 in 1,540.

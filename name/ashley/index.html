Ashley name popularity since 1925

874,837 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Ashley (98.3% girls, 1.7% boys). Half of them were born in 1991 or earlier, so the typical Ashley is about 35 years old in 2026. Ashley peaked in 1987 as the #2 name for girls, and it ranked #164 in 2025.

Ashley births per year, 1925–2025

1987 was the biggest year for Ashley both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1978 and 1983, Ashley became 8.8× more common, from 1 in 440 baby girls to 1 in 50.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2007 and 2012, its share of baby girls fell by 55%, from 1 in 168 to 1 in 375.

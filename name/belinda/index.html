Belinda name popularity since 1925

72,915 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Belinda. Half of them were born in 1964 or earlier, so the typical Belinda is about 62 years old in 2026. Belinda peaked in 1961 as the #141 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Belinda births per year, 1925–2025

1961 was the biggest year for Belinda both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1945 and 1950, Belinda became 12× more common, from 1 in 12,100 baby girls to 1 in 1,050.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1994 and 1999, its share of baby girls fell by 51%, from 1 in 7,230 to 1 in 14,800.

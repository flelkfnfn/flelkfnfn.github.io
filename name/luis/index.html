Luis name popularity since 1925

291,610 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Luis. Half of them were born in 1997 or earlier, so the typical Luis is about 29 years old in 2026. Luis peaked in 2000 as the #49 name for boys, and it ranked #144 in 2025.

Luis births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 2007. Measured as a share of all babies, Luis peaked in 2000.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1946 and 1951, Luis became 1.6× more common, from 1 in 4,190 baby boys to 1 in 2,580.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2007 and 2012, its share of baby boys fell by 41%, from 1 in 258 to 1 in 434.

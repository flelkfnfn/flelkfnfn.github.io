Willis name popularity since 1925

35,217 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Willis (1.4% girls, 98.6% boys). Half of them were born in 1945 or earlier, so the typical Willis is about 81 years old in 2026. Willis peaked in 1880 as the #86 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Dina name popularity since 1925

31,377 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Dina. Half of them were born in 1971 or earlier, so the typical Dina is about 55 years old in 2026. Dina peaked in 1969 as the #199 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Dina births per year, 1925–2025

1969 was the biggest year for Dina both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1957 and 1962, Dina became 4.6× more common, from 1 in 8,700 baby girls to 1 in 1,910.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1972 and 1977, its share of baby girls fell by 57%, from 1 in 1,390 to 1 in 3,260.

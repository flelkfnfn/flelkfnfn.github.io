Julianne name popularity since 1925

30,361 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Julianne. Half of them were born in 1986 or earlier, so the typical Julianne is about 40 years old in 2026. Julianne peaked in 1985 as the #364 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Julianne births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1989. Measured as a share of all babies, Julianne peaked in 1985.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1983 and 1988, Julianne became 2.4× more common, from 1 in 6,820 baby girls to 1 in 2,870.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2016 and 2021, its share of baby girls fell by 51%, from 1 in 5,930 to 1 in 12,100.

Maya name popularity since 1925

123,750 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Maya. Half of them were born in 2010 or earlier, so the typical Maya is about 16 years old in 2026. Maya peaked in 2006 as the #57 name for girls, and it ranked #62 in 2025.

Maya births per year, 1925–2025

2006 was the biggest year for Maya both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1979 and 1984, Maya's share of baby girls fell by 41%, from 1 in 4,170 to 1 in 7,020.
  • Fastest rise: Between 1988 and 1993, it became 3.5× more common, from 1 in 6,180 baby girls to 1 in 1,780.

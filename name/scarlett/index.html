Scarlett name popularity since 1925

111,739 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Scarlett. Half of them were born in 2017 or earlier, so the typical Scarlett is about 9 years old in 2026. Scarlett peaked in 2017 as the #18 name for girls, and it ranked #32 in 2025.

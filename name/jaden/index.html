Jaden name popularity since 1925

95,059 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Jaden (14.3% girls, 85.7% boys). Half of them were born in 2007 or earlier, so the typical Jaden is about 19 years old in 2026. Jaden peaked in 2007 as the #74 name for boys, and it ranked #421 in 2025.

Jaden births per year, 1925–2025

2007 was the biggest year for Jaden both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1997 and 2002, Jaden became 9.3× more common, from 1 in 3,890 baby boys to 1 in 417.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2010 and 2015, its share of baby boys fell by 55%, from 1 in 424 to 1 in 950.

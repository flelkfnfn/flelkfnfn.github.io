Sheldon name popularity since 1925

34,722 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Sheldon (1.4% girls, 98.6% boys). Half of them were born in 1967 or earlier, so the typical Sheldon is about 59 years old in 2026. Sheldon peaked in 1934 as the #250 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Sheldon births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1992. Measured as a share of all babies, Sheldon peaked in 1934.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1987 and 1992, Sheldon became 1.5× more common, from 1 in 5,090 baby boys to 1 in 3,290.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1998 and 2003, its share of baby boys fell by 45%, from 1 in 4,770 to 1 in 8,590.

Anastasia name popularity since 1925

54,698 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Anastasia. Half of them were born in 2007 or earlier, so the typical Anastasia is about 19 years old in 2026. Anastasia peaked in 2025 as the #163 name for girls, and it ranked #163 in 2025.

Julia name popularity since 1925

376,169 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Julia. Half of them were born in 1988 or earlier, so the typical Julia is about 38 years old in 2026. Julia peaked in 1880 as the #26 name for girls, and it ranked #131 in 2025.

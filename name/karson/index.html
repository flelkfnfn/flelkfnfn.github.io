Karson name popularity since 1925

23,500 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Karson (9.0% girls, 91.0% boys). Half of them were born in 2015 or earlier, so the typical Karson is about 11 years old in 2026. Karson peaked in 2020 as the #266 name for boys, and it ranked #453 in 2025.

Karson births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 2018. Measured as a share of all babies, Karson peaked in 2020.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1998 and 2003, Karson became 3.2× more common, from 1 in 18,900 baby boys to 1 in 5,880.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2020 and 2025, its share of baby boys fell by 46%, from 1 in 1,340 to 1 in 2,480.

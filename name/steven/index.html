Steven name popularity since 1925

1,292,447 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Steven. Half of them were born in 1967 or earlier, so the typical Steven is about 59 years old in 2026. Steven peaked in 1955 as the #10 name for boys, and it ranked #271 in 2025.

Steven births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1956. Measured as a share of all babies, Steven peaked in 1955.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1937 and 1942, Steven became 5.1× more common, from 1 in 2,000 baby boys to 1 in 394.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1991 and 1996, its share of baby boys fell by 39%, from 1 in 120 to 1 in 196.

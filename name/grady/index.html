Grady name popularity since 1925

44,727 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Grady (1.1% girls, 98.9% boys). Half of them were born in 1987 or earlier, so the typical Grady is about 39 years old in 2026. Grady peaked in 1911 as the #180 name for boys, and it ranked #373 in 2025.

Barrett name popularity since 1925

34,243 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Barrett (2.9% girls, 97.1% boys). Half of them were born in 2017 or earlier, so the typical Barrett is about 9 years old in 2026. Barrett peaked in 2025 as the #171 name for boys, and it ranked #171 in 2025.

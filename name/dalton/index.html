Dalton name popularity since 1925

84,430 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Dalton. Half of them were born in 1999 or earlier, so the typical Dalton is about 27 years old in 2026. Dalton peaked in 1998 as the #88 name for boys, and it ranked #448 in 2025.

Dalton births per year, 1925–2025

1998 was the biggest year for Dalton both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1988 and 1993, Dalton became 15× more common, from 1 in 8,900 baby boys to 1 in 578.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2001 and 2006, its share of baby boys fell by 53%, from 1 in 499 to 1 in 1,060.

Payton name popularity since 1925

84,553 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Payton (71.0% girls, 29.0% boys). Half of them were born in 2008 or earlier, so the typical Payton is about 18 years old in 2026. Payton peaked in 2010 as the #79 name for girls, and it ranked #440 in 2025.

Payton births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 2009. Measured as a share of all babies, Payton peaked in 2010.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1992 and 1997, Payton became 4.6× more common, from 1 in 6,010 baby girls to 1 in 1,300.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2020 and 2025, its share of baby girls fell by 47%, from 1 in 1,210 to 1 in 2,290.

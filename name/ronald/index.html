Ronald name popularity since 1925

1,074,507 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Ronald. Half of them were born in 1953 or earlier, so the typical Ronald is about 73 years old in 2026. Ronald peaked in 1943 as the #9 name for boys, and it ranked #638 in 2025.

Ronald births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1947. Measured as a share of all babies, Ronald peaked in 1943.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1927 and 1932, Ronald became 3.9× more common.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1969 and 1974, its share of baby boys fell by 40%, from 1 in 122 to 1 in 205.

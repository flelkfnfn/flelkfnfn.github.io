Orion name popularity since 1925

23,273 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Orion (2.7% girls, 97.3% boys). Half of them were born in 2014 or earlier, so the typical Orion is about 12 years old in 2026. Orion peaked in 2018 as the #300 name for boys, and it ranked #334 in 2025.

Jacklyn name popularity since 1925

16,920 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Jacklyn. Half of them were born in 1987 or earlier, so the typical Jacklyn is about 39 years old in 2026. Jacklyn peaked in 1987 as the #454 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Jacklyn births per year, 1925–2025

1987 was the biggest year for Jacklyn both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1954 and 1959, Jacklyn's share of baby girls fell by 42%, from 1 in 11,200 to 1 in 19,300.

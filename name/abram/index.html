Abram name popularity since 1925

18,928 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Abram. Half of them were born in 2010 or earlier, so the typical Abram is about 16 years old in 2026. Abram peaked in 2016 as the #379 name for boys, and it ranked #742 in 2025.

Abram births per year, 1925–2025

2016 was the biggest year for Abram both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 2002 and 2007, Abram became 2.2× more common, from 1 in 9,860 baby boys to 1 in 4,480.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2018 and 2023, its share of baby boys fell by 43%, from 1 in 2,600 to 1 in 4,570.

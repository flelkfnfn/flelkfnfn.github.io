Terrence name popularity since 1925

72,350 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Terrence (0.7% girls, 99.3% boys). Half of them were born in 1971 or earlier, so the typical Terrence is about 55 years old in 2026. Terrence peaked in 1970 as the #187 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Kaylee name popularity since 1925

140,601 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Kaylee. Half of them were born in 2007 or earlier, so the typical Kaylee is about 19 years old in 2026. Kaylee peaked in 2009 as the #26 name for girls, and it ranked #255 in 2025.

Kaylee births per year, 1925–2025

2009 was the biggest year for Kaylee both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1984 and 1989, Kaylee became 4.7× more common, from 1 in 8,820 baby girls to 1 in 1,870.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2020 and 2025, its share of baby girls fell by 48%, from 1 in 692 to 1 in 1,330.

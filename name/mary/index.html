Mary name popularity since 1925

2,927,489 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Mary. Half of them were born in 1948 or earlier, so the typical Mary is about 78 years old in 2026. Mary peaked in 1880 as the #1 name for girls, and it ranked #125 in 2025.

Thaddeus name popularity since 1925

22,523 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Thaddeus. Half of them were born in 1981 or earlier, so the typical Thaddeus is about 45 years old in 2026. Thaddeus peaked in 1918 as the #307 name for boys, and it ranked #798 in 2025.

Thaddeus births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year on this chart was 2016. Measured as a share of all babies, Thaddeus peaked earlier, in 1918.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1979 and 1984, Thaddeus's share of baby boys fell by 46%, from 1 in 5,610 to 1 in 10,300.

Harmony name popularity since 1925

36,253 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Harmony. Half of them were born in 2014 or earlier, so the typical Harmony is about 12 years old in 2026. Harmony peaked in 2019 as the #169 name for girls, and it ranked #276 in 2025.

Harmony births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 2015. Measured as a share of all babies, Harmony peaked in 2019.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1981 and 1986, Harmony's share of baby girls fell by 46%, from 1 in 8,600 to 1 in 16,000.
  • Fastest rise: Between 2002 and 2007, it became 2.6× more common, from 1 in 4,720 baby girls to 1 in 1,780.

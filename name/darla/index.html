Darla name popularity since 1925

43,687 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Darla. Half of them were born in 1963 or earlier, so the typical Darla is about 63 years old in 2026. Darla peaked in 1963 as the #165 name for girls, and it ranked #941 in 2025.

Darla births per year, 1925–2025

1963 was the biggest year for Darla both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1954 and 1959, Darla became 2.3× more common, from 1 in 2,770 baby girls to 1 in 1,210.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1981 and 1986, its share of baby girls fell by 54%, from 1 in 5,810 to 1 in 12,600.

Nadia name popularity since 1925

46,458 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Nadia. Half of them were born in 2004 or earlier, so the typical Nadia is about 22 years old in 2026. Nadia peaked in 2005 as the #178 name for girls, and it ranked #565 in 2025.

Nadia births per year, 1925–2025

2005 was the biggest year for Nadia both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1977 and 1982, Nadia's share of baby girls fell by 49%, from 1 in 1,940 to 1 in 3,810.

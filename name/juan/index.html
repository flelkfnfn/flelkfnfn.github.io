Juan name popularity since 1925

360,024 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Juan (0.7% girls, 99.3% boys). Half of them were born in 1992 or earlier, so the typical Juan is about 34 years old in 2026. Juan peaked in 2005 as the #55 name for boys, and it ranked #159 in 2025.

Juan births per year, 1925–2025

2005 was the biggest year for Juan both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1968 and 1973, Juan became 1.7× more common, from 1 in 663 baby boys to 1 in 381.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2008 and 2013, its share of baby boys fell by 40%, from 1 in 290 to 1 in 486.

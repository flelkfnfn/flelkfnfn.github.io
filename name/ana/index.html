Ana name popularity since 1925

110,923 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Ana. Half of them were born in 1995 or earlier, so the typical Ana is about 31 years old in 2026. Ana peaked in 2003 as the #129 name for girls, and it ranked #245 in 2025.

Ana births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1991. Measured as a share of all babies, Ana peaked in 2003.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1943 and 1948, Ana became 1.7× more common, from 1 in 12,900 baby girls to 1 in 7,510.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2006 and 2011, its share of baby girls fell by 40%, from 1 in 788 to 1 in 1,320.

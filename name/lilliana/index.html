Lilliana name popularity since 1925

22,826 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Lilliana. Half of them were born in 2014 or earlier, so the typical Lilliana is about 12 years old in 2026. Lilliana peaked in 2013 as the #262 name for girls, and it ranked #446 in 2025.

Lori name popularity since 1925

342,758 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Lori. Half of them were born in 1965 or earlier, so the typical Lori is about 61 years old in 2026. Lori peaked in 1963 as the #8 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Lori births per year, 1925–2025

1963 was the biggest year for Lori both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1950 and 1955, Lori became 23× more common, from 1 in 9,210 baby girls to 1 in 393.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1982 and 1987, its share of baby girls fell by 62%, from 1 in 569 to 1 in 1,480.

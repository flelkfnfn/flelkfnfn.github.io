Marco name popularity since 1925

76,402 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Marco. Half of them were born in 1998 or earlier, so the typical Marco is about 28 years old in 2026. Marco peaked in 2001 as the #179 name for boys, and it ranked #370 in 2025.

Marco births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 2005. Measured as a share of all babies, Marco peaked in 2001.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1957 and 1962, Marco became 2.3× more common, from 1 in 15,100 baby boys to 1 in 6,610.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2005 and 2010, its share of baby boys fell by 35%, from 1 in 943 to 1 in 1,460.

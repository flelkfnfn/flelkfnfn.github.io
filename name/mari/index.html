Mari name popularity since 1925

16,425 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Mari. Half of them were born in 1972 or earlier, so the typical Mari is about 54 years old in 2026. Mari peaked in 1957 as the #467 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

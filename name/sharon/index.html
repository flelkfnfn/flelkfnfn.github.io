Sharon name popularity since 1925

725,652 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Sharon. Half of them were born in 1954 or earlier, so the typical Sharon is about 72 years old in 2026. Sharon peaked in 1943 as the #8 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Sharon births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1947. Measured as a share of all babies, Sharon peaked in 1943.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1933 and 1938, Sharon became 6.9× more common.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1970 and 1975, its share of baby girls fell by 51%, from 1 in 190 to 1 in 391.

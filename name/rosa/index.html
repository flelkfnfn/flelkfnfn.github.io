Rosa name popularity since 1925

128,586 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Rosa (99.3% girls, 0.7% boys). Half of them were born in 1960 or earlier, so the typical Rosa is about 66 years old in 2026. Rosa peaked in 1880 as the #52 name for girls, and it ranked #653 in 2025.

Caden name popularity since 1925

72,993 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Caden (1.3% girls, 98.7% boys). Half of them were born in 2009 or earlier, so the typical Caden is about 17 years old in 2026. Caden peaked in 2007 as the #92 name for boys, and it ranked #201 in 2025.

Caden births per year, 1925–2025

2007 was the biggest year for Caden both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1997 and 2002, Caden became 7.7× more common, from 1 in 5,780 baby boys to 1 in 746.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2008 and 2013, its share of baby boys fell by 50%, from 1 in 427 to 1 in 854.

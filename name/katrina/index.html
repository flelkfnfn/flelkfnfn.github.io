Katrina name popularity since 1925

101,429 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Katrina. Half of them were born in 1982 or earlier, so the typical Katrina is about 44 years old in 2026. Katrina peaked in 1980 as the #90 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Katrina births per year, 1925–2025

1980 was the biggest year for Katrina both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1953 and 1958, Katrina became 2.3× more common, from 1 in 6,920 baby girls to 1 in 2,960.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2005 and 2010, its share of baby girls fell by 75%, from 1 in 1,390 to 1 in 5,660.

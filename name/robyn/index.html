Robyn name popularity since 1925

64,586 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Robyn (98.1% girls, 1.9% boys). Half of them were born in 1974 or earlier, so the typical Robyn is about 52 years old in 2026. Robyn peaked in 1974 as the #169 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Robyn births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1964. Measured as a share of all babies, Robyn peaked in 1974.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1951 and 1956, Robyn became 2.7× more common, from 1 in 4,410 baby girls to 1 in 1,640.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1998 and 2003, its share of baby girls fell by 52%, from 1 in 3,360 to 1 in 6,950.

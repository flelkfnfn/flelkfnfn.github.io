Debbie name popularity since 1925

187,976 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Debbie. Half of them were born in 1960 or earlier, so the typical Debbie is about 66 years old in 2026. Debbie peaked in 1959 as the #20 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Debbie births per year, 1925–2025

1959 was the biggest year for Debbie both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1945 and 1950, Debbie became 6.3× more common, from 1 in 9,270 baby girls to 1 in 1,470.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1963 and 1968, its share of baby girls fell by 66%, from 1 in 185 to 1 in 543.

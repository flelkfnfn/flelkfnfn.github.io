Elisabeth name popularity since 1925

43,258 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Elisabeth. Half of them were born in 1988 or earlier, so the typical Elisabeth is about 38 years old in 2026. Elisabeth peaked in 2001 as the #286 name for girls, and it ranked #925 in 2025.

Elisabeth births per year, 1925–2025

2001 was the biggest year for Elisabeth both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1972 and 1977, Elisabeth became 1.6× more common, from 1 in 4,310 baby girls to 1 in 2,660.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2001 and 2006, its share of baby girls fell by 47%, from 1 in 1,650 to 1 in 3,100.

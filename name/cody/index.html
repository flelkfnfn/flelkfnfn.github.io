Cody name popularity since 1925

296,861 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Cody (1.7% girls, 98.3% boys). Half of them were born in 1993 or earlier, so the typical Cody is about 33 years old in 2026. Cody peaked in 1992 as the #24 name for boys, and it ranked #318 in 2025.

Cody births per year, 1925–2025

1992 was the biggest year for Cody both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1969 and 1974, Cody became 3.6× more common, from 1 in 7,010 baby boys to 1 in 1,970.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1995 and 2000, its share of baby boys fell by 56%, from 1 in 122 to 1 in 275.

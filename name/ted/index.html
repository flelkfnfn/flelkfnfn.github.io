Ted name popularity since 1925

67,529 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Ted. Half of them were born in 1954 or earlier, so the typical Ted is about 72 years old in 2026. Ted peaked in 1933 as the #145 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Ted births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1958. Measured as a share of all babies, Ted peaked in 1933.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1927 and 1932, Ted became 1.5× more common.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1969 and 1974, its share of baby boys fell by 49%, from 1 in 1,690 to 1 in 3,290.

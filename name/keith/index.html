Keith name popularity since 1925

431,272 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Keith. Half of them were born in 1966 or earlier, so the typical Keith is about 60 years old in 2026. Keith peaked in 1966 as the #32 name for boys, and it ranked #801 in 2025.

Keith births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1957. Measured as a share of all babies, Keith peaked in 1966.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1950 and 1955, Keith became 2× more common, from 1 in 392 baby boys to 1 in 198.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1993 and 1998, its share of baby boys fell by 44%, from 1 in 601 to 1 in 1,070.

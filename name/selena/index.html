Selena name popularity since 1925

58,988 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Selena. Half of them were born in 2001 or earlier, so the typical Selena is about 25 years old in 2026. Selena peaked in 1995 as the #91 name for girls, and it ranked #268 in 2025.

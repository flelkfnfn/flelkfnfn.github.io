Sasha name popularity since 1925

40,238 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Sasha (95.7% girls, 4.3% boys). Half of them were born in 1998 or earlier, so the typical Sasha is about 28 years old in 2026. Sasha peaked in 1988 as the #147 name for girls, and it ranked #684 in 2025.

Sasha births per year, 1925–2025

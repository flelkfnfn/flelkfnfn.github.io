Juliet name popularity since 1925

36,382 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Juliet. Half of them were born in 2011 or earlier, so the typical Juliet is about 15 years old in 2026. Juliet peaked in 2017 as the #228 name for girls, and it ranked #274 in 2025.

Juliet births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 2016. Measured as a share of all babies, Juliet peaked in 2017.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1971 and 1976, Juliet's share of baby girls fell by 51%, from 1 in 4,500 to 1 in 9,210.
  • Fastest rise: Between 2006 and 2011, it became 2.7× more common, from 1 in 3,780 baby girls to 1 in 1,380.

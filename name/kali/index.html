Kali name popularity since 1925

33,438 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Kali (97.3% girls, 2.7% boys). Half of them were born in 2010 or earlier, so the typical Kali is about 16 years old in 2026. Kali peaked in 2019 as the #242 name for girls, and it ranked #323 in 2025.

Kali births per year, 1925–2025

2019 was the biggest year for Kali both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1981 and 1986, Kali became 2.7× more common, from 1 in 12,900 baby girls to 1 in 4,780.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1997 and 2002, its share of baby girls fell by 40%, from 1 in 2,180 to 1 in 3,630.

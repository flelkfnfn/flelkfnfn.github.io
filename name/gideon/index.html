Gideon name popularity since 1925

21,848 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Gideon. Half of them were born in 2015 or earlier, so the typical Gideon is about 11 years old in 2026. Gideon peaked in 2017 as the #304 name for boys, and it ranked #337 in 2025.

Deshawn name popularity since 1925

17,257 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Deshawn (6.9% girls, 93.1% boys). Half of them were born in 1998 or earlier, so the typical Deshawn is about 28 years old in 2026. Deshawn peaked in 1997 as the #411 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Deshawn births per year, 1925–2025

1997 was the biggest year for Deshawn both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1987 and 1992, Deshawn became 1.9× more common, from 1 in 7,810 baby boys to 1 in 4,040.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2010 and 2015, its share of baby boys fell by 45%, from 1 in 5,190 to 1 in 9,400.

Hudson name popularity since 1925

115,451 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Hudson (1.6% girls, 98.4% boys). Half of them were born in 2018 or earlier, so the typical Hudson is about 8 years old in 2026. Hudson peaked in 2025 as the #17 name for boys, and it ranked #17 in 2025.

David name popularity since 1925

3,581,660 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named David. Half of them were born in 1964 or earlier, so the typical David is about 62 years old in 2026. David peaked in 1955 as the #2 name for boys, and it ranked #35 in 2025.

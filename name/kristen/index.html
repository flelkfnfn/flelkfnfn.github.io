Kristen name popularity since 1925

220,952 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Kristen (99.0% girls, 1.0% boys). Half of them were born in 1985 or earlier, so the typical Kristen is about 41 years old in 2026. Kristen peaked in 1982 as the #33 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Kristen births per year, 1925–2025

1982 was the biggest year for Kristen both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1961 and 1966, Kristen became 5.1× more common, from 1 in 3,740 baby girls to 1 in 728.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1996 and 2001, its share of baby girls fell by 60%, from 1 in 361 to 1 in 904.

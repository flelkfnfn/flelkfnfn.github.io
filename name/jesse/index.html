Jesse name popularity since 1925

399,810 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Jesse (2.9% girls, 97.1% boys). Half of them were born in 1985 or earlier, so the typical Jesse is about 41 years old in 2026. Jesse peaked in 1887 as the #26 name for boys, and it ranked #184 in 2025.

Willie name popularity since 1925

411,101 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Willie (18.4% girls, 81.6% boys). Half of them were born in 1947 or earlier, so the typical Willie is about 79 years old in 2026. Willie peaked in 1910 as the #11 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Dorothea name popularity since 1925

18,753 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Dorothea. Half of them were born in 1945 or earlier, so the typical Dorothea is about 81 years old in 2026. Dorothea peaked in 1913 as the #176 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

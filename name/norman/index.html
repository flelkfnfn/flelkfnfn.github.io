Norman name popularity since 1925

200,217 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Norman (0.6% girls, 99.4% boys). Half of them were born in 1945 or earlier, so the typical Norman is about 81 years old in 2026. Norman peaked in 1931 as the #36 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Dennis name popularity since 1925

612,827 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Dennis. Half of them were born in 1954 or earlier, so the typical Dennis is about 72 years old in 2026. Dennis peaked in 1946 as the #17 name for boys, and it ranked #707 in 2025.

Dennis births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1952. Measured as a share of all babies, Dennis peaked in 1946.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1937 and 1942, Dennis became 3.2× more common, from 1 in 338 baby boys to 1 in 104.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1952 and 1957, its share of baby boys fell by 38%, from 1 in 79 to 1 in 128.

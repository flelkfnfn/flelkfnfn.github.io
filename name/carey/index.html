Carey name popularity since 1925

28,096 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Carey (44.6% girls, 55.4% boys). Half of them were born in 1970 or earlier, so the typical Carey is about 56 years old in 2026. Carey peaked in 1957 as the #353 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Carey births per year, 1925–2025

1957 was the biggest year for Carey both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1949 and 1954, Carey became 1.8× more common, from 1 in 9,050 baby boys to 1 in 5,060.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1975 and 1980, its share of baby boys fell by 51%, from 1 in 5,170 to 1 in 10,600.

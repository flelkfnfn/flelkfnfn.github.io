Zachary name popularity since 1925

553,087 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Zachary. Half of them were born in 1996 or earlier, so the typical Zachary is about 30 years old in 2026. Zachary peaked in 1993 as the #15 name for boys, and it ranked #216 in 2025.

Zachary births per year, 1925–2025

1993 was the biggest year for Zachary both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1971 and 1976, Zachary became 4.3× more common, from 1 in 2,520 baby boys to 1 in 593.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2001 and 2006, its share of baby boys fell by 43%, from 1 in 107 to 1 in 187.

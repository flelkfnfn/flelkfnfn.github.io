Tammy name popularity since 1925

333,407 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Tammy. Half of them were born in 1967 or earlier, so the typical Tammy is about 59 years old in 2026. Tammy peaked in 1968 as the #8 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Tammy births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1963. Measured as a share of all babies, Tammy peaked in 1968.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1955 and 1960, Tammy became 73× more common, from 1 in 10,300 baby girls to 1 in 141.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1973 and 1978, its share of baby girls fell by 66%, from 1 in 130 to 1 in 376.

Ann name popularity since 1925

389,970 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Ann. Half of them were born in 1953 or earlier, so the typical Ann is about 73 years old in 2026. Ann peaked in 1936 as the #28 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

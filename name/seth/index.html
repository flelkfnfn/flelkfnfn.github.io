Seth name popularity since 1925

164,537 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Seth. Half of them were born in 1996 or earlier, so the typical Seth is about 30 years old in 2026. Seth peaked in 2000 as the #63 name for boys, and it ranked #581 in 2025.

Seth births per year, 1925–2025

2000 was the biggest year for Seth both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1971 and 1976, Seth became 3× more common, from 1 in 2,260 baby boys to 1 in 748.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2006 and 2011, its share of baby boys fell by 48%, from 1 in 475 to 1 in 907.

Donald name popularity since 1925

1,280,929 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Donald. Half of them were born in 1949 or earlier, so the typical Donald is about 77 years old in 2026. Donald peaked in 1934 as the #6 name for boys, and it ranked #690 in 2025.

Donald births per year, 1925–2025

1934 was the biggest year for Donald both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1925 and 1930, Donald became 1.5× more common.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1990 and 1995, its share of baby boys fell by 42%, from 1 in 492 to 1 in 845.

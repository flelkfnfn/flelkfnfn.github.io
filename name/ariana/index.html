Ariana name popularity since 1925

120,580 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Ariana. Half of them were born in 2009 or earlier, so the typical Ariana is about 17 years old in 2026. Ariana peaked in 2014 as the #37 name for girls, and it ranked #128 in 2025.

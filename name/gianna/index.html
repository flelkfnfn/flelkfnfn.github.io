Gianna name popularity since 1925

110,388 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Gianna. Half of them were born in 2015 or earlier, so the typical Gianna is about 11 years old in 2026. Gianna peaked in 2020 as the #13 name for girls, and it ranked #33 in 2025.

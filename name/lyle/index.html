Lyle name popularity since 1925

47,927 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Lyle (1.3% girls, 98.7% boys). Half of them were born in 1950 or earlier, so the typical Lyle is about 76 years old in 2026. Lyle peaked in 1918 as the #145 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

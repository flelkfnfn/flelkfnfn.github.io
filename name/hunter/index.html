Hunter name popularity since 1925

275,573 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Hunter (5.2% girls, 94.8% boys). Half of them were born in 2005 or earlier, so the typical Hunter is about 21 years old in 2026. Hunter peaked in 2000 as the #35 name for boys, and it ranked #130 in 2025.

Hunter births per year, 1925–2025

2000 was the biggest year for Hunter both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1990 and 1995, Hunter became 5.2× more common, from 1 in 1,480 baby boys to 1 in 283.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2018 and 2023, its share of baby boys fell by 46%, from 1 in 297 to 1 in 546.

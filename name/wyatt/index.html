Wyatt name popularity since 1925

208,742 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Wyatt (0.7% girls, 99.3% boys). Half of them were born in 2013 or earlier, so the typical Wyatt is about 13 years old in 2026. Wyatt peaked in 2017 as the #25 name for boys, and it ranked #38 in 2025.

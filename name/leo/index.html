Leo name popularity since 1925

202,061 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Leo (0.6% girls, 99.4% boys). Half of them were born in 2004 or earlier, so the typical Leo is about 22 years old in 2026. Leo peaked in 1903 as the #38 name for boys, and it ranked #19 in 2025.

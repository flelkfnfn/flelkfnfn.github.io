Charles name popularity since 1925

2,005,626 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Charles. Half of them were born in 1954 or earlier, so the typical Charles is about 72 years old in 2026. Charles peaked in 1880 as the #4 name for boys, and it ranked #48 in 2025.

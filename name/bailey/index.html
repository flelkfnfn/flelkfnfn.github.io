Bailey name popularity since 1925

131,726 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Bailey (84.1% girls, 15.9% boys). Half of them were born in 2003 or earlier, so the typical Bailey is about 23 years old in 2026. Bailey peaked in 1998 as the #60 name for girls, and it ranked #209 in 2025.

Bailey births per year, 1925–2025

1998 was the biggest year for Bailey both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1983 and 1988, Bailey became 5.8× more common, from 1 in 11,000 baby girls to 1 in 1,890.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2000 and 2005, its share of baby girls fell by 37%, from 1 in 391 to 1 in 618.

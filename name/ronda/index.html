Ronda name popularity since 1925

34,616 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Ronda (99.1% girls, 0.9% boys). Half of them were born in 1965 or earlier, so the typical Ronda is about 61 years old in 2026. Ronda peaked in 1967 as the #179 name for girls, and fewer than 5 girls got it in 2025.

Ronda births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1965. Measured as a share of all babies, Ronda peaked in 1967.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1952 and 1957, Ronda became 2.1× more common, from 1 in 4,290 baby girls to 1 in 2,030.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1976 and 1981, its share of baby girls fell by 60%, from 1 in 2,950 to 1 in 7,350.

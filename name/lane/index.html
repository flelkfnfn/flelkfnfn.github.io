Lane name popularity since 1925

51,737 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Lane (8.9% girls, 91.1% boys). Half of them were born in 2006 or earlier, so the typical Lane is about 20 years old in 2026. Lane peaked in 2025 as the #250 name for boys, and it ranked #250 in 2025.

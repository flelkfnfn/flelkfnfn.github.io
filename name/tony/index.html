Tony name popularity since 1925

239,324 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Tony (1.1% girls, 98.9% boys). Half of them were born in 1966 or earlier, so the typical Tony is about 60 years old in 2026. Tony peaked in 1962 as the #52 name for boys, and it ranked #784 in 2025.

Tony births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1961. Measured as a share of all babies, Tony peaked in 1962.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1952 and 1957, Tony became 2.4× more common, from 1 in 890 baby boys to 1 in 368.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1968 and 1973, its share of baby boys fell by 36%, from 1 in 296 to 1 in 466.

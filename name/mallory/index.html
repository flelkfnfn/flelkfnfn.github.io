Mallory name popularity since 1925

60,083 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Mallory (98.3% girls, 1.7% boys). Half of them were born in 1997 or earlier, so the typical Mallory is about 29 years old in 2026. Mallory peaked in 1986 as the #83 name for girls, and it ranked #372 in 2025.

Mallory births per year, 1925–2025

1986 was the biggest year for Mallory both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1983 and 1988, Mallory became 3.2× more common, from 1 in 2,420 baby girls to 1 in 753.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1986 and 1991, its share of baby girls fell by 50%, from 1 in 516 to 1 in 1,040.

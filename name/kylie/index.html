Kylie name popularity since 1925

122,340 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Kylie. Half of them were born in 2006 or earlier, so the typical Kylie is about 20 years old in 2026. Kylie peaked in 2003 as the #52 name for girls, and it ranked #204 in 2025.

Kylie births per year, 1925–2025

2003 was the biggest year for Kylie both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1978 and 1983, Kylie's share of baby girls fell by 53%, from 1 in 3,010 to 1 in 6,430.
  • Fastest rise: Between 1984 and 1989, it became 5.6× more common, from 1 in 5,380 baby girls to 1 in 957.

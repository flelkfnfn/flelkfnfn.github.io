Alvin name popularity since 1925

128,153 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Alvin (0.7% girls, 99.3% boys). Half of them were born in 1953 or earlier, so the typical Alvin is about 73 years old in 2026. Alvin peaked in 1927 as the #66 name for boys, and it ranked #838 in 2025.

Joel name popularity since 1925

276,829 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Joel (0.9% girls, 99.1% boys). Half of them were born in 1982 or earlier, so the typical Joel is about 44 years old in 2026. Joel peaked in 1980 as the #67 name for boys, and it ranked #219 in 2025.

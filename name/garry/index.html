Garry name popularity since 1925

47,512 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Garry. Half of them were born in 1955 or earlier, so the typical Garry is about 71 years old in 2026. Garry peaked in 1954 as the #138 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

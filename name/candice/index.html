Candice name popularity since 1925

68,139 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Candice. Half of them were born in 1982 or earlier, so the typical Candice is about 44 years old in 2026. Candice peaked in 1982 as the #78 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Candice births per year, 1925–2025

1982 was the biggest year for Candice both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1945 and 1950, Candice became 5.8× more common, from 1 in 12,900 baby girls to 1 in 2,240.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1992 and 1997, its share of baby girls fell by 69%, from 1 in 1,240 to 1 in 4,040.

Jayme name popularity since 1925

17,968 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Jayme (78.3% girls, 21.7% boys). Half of them were born in 1985 or earlier, so the typical Jayme is about 41 years old in 2026. Jayme peaked in 1985 as the #383 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Jayme births per year, 1925–2025

1985 was the biggest year for Jayme both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1971 and 1976, Jayme became 3.8× more common, from 1 in 11,700 baby girls to 1 in 3,050.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1985 and 1990, its share of baby girls fell by 42%, from 1 in 2,880 to 1 in 4,980.

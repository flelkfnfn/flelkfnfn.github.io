Stephan name popularity since 1925

27,470 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Stephan (0.8% girls, 99.2% boys). Half of them were born in 1977 or earlier, so the typical Stephan is about 49 years old in 2026. Stephan peaked in 1987 as the #347 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

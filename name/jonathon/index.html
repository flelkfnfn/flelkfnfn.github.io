Jonathon name popularity since 1925

65,781 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Jonathon. Half of them were born in 1990 or earlier, so the typical Jonathon is about 36 years old in 2026. Jonathon peaked in 1990 as the #124 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Jonathon births per year, 1925–2025

1990 was the biggest year for Jonathon both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1966 and 1971, Jonathon became 2.4× more common, from 1 in 7,280 baby boys to 1 in 3,080.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2008 and 2013, its share of baby boys fell by 50%, from 1 in 3,330 to 1 in 6,610.

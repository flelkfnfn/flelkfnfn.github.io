Amara name popularity since 1925

29,279 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Amara (99.0% girls, 1.0% boys). Half of them were born in 2019 or earlier, so the typical Amara is about 7 years old in 2026. Amara peaked in 2025 as the #98 name for girls, and it ranked #98 in 2025.

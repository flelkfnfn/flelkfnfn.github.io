Destinee name popularity since 1925

16,800 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Destinee. Half of them were born in 2000 or earlier, so the typical Destinee is about 26 years old in 2026. Destinee peaked in 1999 as the #308 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Destinee births per year, 1925–2025

1999 was the biggest year for Destinee both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1990 and 1995, Destinee became 4× more common, from 1 in 8,180 baby girls to 1 in 2,070.

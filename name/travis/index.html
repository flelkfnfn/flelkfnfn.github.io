Travis name popularity since 1925

307,520 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Travis (0.6% girls, 99.4% boys). Half of them were born in 1986 or earlier, so the typical Travis is about 40 years old in 2026. Travis peaked in 1979 as the #36 name for boys, and it ranked #434 in 2025.

Travis births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1989. Measured as a share of all babies, Travis peaked in 1979.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1965 and 1970, Travis became 4× more common, from 1 in 1,730 baby boys to 1 in 430.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1993 and 1998, its share of baby boys fell by 49%, from 1 in 247 to 1 in 487.

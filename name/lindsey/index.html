Lindsey name popularity since 1925

164,117 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Lindsey (96.0% girls, 4.0% boys). Half of them were born in 1989 or earlier, so the typical Lindsey is about 37 years old in 2026. Lindsey peaked in 1984 as the #35 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Lindsey births per year, 1925–2025

1984 was the biggest year for Lindsey both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1973 and 1978, Lindsey became 17× more common, from 1 in 14,400 baby girls to 1 in 845.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2006 and 2011, its share of baby girls fell by 63%, from 1 in 1,010 to 1 in 2,720.

Aidan name popularity since 1925

121,158 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Aidan (2.3% girls, 97.7% boys). Half of them were born in 2006 or earlier, so the typical Aidan is about 20 years old in 2026. Aidan peaked in 2003 as the #39 name for boys, and it ranked #326 in 2025.

Aidan births per year, 1925–2025

2003 was the biggest year for Aidan both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1990 and 1995, Aidan became 7.8× more common, from 1 in 15,300 baby boys to 1 in 1,960.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2008 and 2013, its share of baby boys fell by 63%, from 1 in 265 to 1 in 707.

Desiree name popularity since 1925

83,719 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Desiree. Half of them were born in 1989 or earlier, so the typical Desiree is about 37 years old in 2026. Desiree peaked in 1983 as the #94 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Desiree births per year, 1925–2025

1983 was the biggest year for Desiree both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1973 and 1978, Desiree became 2.3× more common, from 1 in 1,940 baby girls to 1 in 847.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2006 and 2011, its share of baby girls fell by 52%, from 1 in 1,720 to 1 in 3,630.

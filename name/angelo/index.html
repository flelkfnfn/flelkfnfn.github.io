Angelo name popularity since 1925

75,201 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Angelo (1.0% girls, 99.0% boys). Half of them were born in 1989 or earlier, so the typical Angelo is about 37 years old in 2026. Angelo peaked in 1914 as the #146 name for boys, and it ranked #304 in 2025.

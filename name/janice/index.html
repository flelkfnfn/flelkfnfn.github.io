Janice name popularity since 1925

400,082 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Janice. Half of them were born in 1951 or earlier, so the typical Janice is about 75 years old in 2026. Janice peaked in 1946 as the #22 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Janice births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1951. Measured as a share of all babies, Janice peaked in 1946.

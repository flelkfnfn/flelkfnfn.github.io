Caitlyn name popularity since 1925

52,637 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Caitlyn. Half of them were born in 1999 or earlier, so the typical Caitlyn is about 27 years old in 2026. Caitlyn peaked in 1998 as the #115 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Caitlyn births per year, 1925–2025

1998 was the biggest year for Caitlyn both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1983 and 1988, Caitlyn became 7× more common, from 1 in 10,600 baby girls to 1 in 1,520.

Jean name popularity since 1925

385,763 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Jean (94.4% girls, 5.6% boys). Half of them were born in 1944 or earlier, so the typical Jean is about 82 years old in 2026. Jean peaked in 1930 as the #14 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

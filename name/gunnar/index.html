Gunnar name popularity since 1925

17,946 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Gunnar. Half of them were born in 2011 or earlier, so the typical Gunnar is about 15 years old in 2026. Gunnar peaked in 2015 as the #388 name for boys, and it ranked #612 in 2025.

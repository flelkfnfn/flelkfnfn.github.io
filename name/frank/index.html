Frank name popularity since 1925

633,192 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Frank. Half of them were born in 1951 or earlier, so the typical Frank is about 75 years old in 2026. Frank peaked in 1880 as the #6 name for boys, and it ranked #514 in 2025.

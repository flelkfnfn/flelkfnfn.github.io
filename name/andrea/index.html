Andrea name popularity since 1925

449,826 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Andrea (98.7% girls, 1.3% boys). Half of them were born in 1982 or earlier, so the typical Andrea is about 44 years old in 2026. Andrea peaked in 1981 as the #24 name for girls, and it ranked #235 in 2025.

Andrea births per year, 1925–2025

1981 was the biggest year for Andrea both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1935 and 1940, Andrea became 4.9× more common, from 1 in 7,880 baby girls to 1 in 1,620.

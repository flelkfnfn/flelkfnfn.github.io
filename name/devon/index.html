Devon name popularity since 1925

92,780 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Devon (21.9% girls, 78.1% boys). Half of them were born in 1996 or earlier, so the typical Devon is about 30 years old in 2026. Devon peaked in 1997 as the #119 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Devon births per year, 1925–2025

1997 was the biggest year for Devon both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1981 and 1986, Devon became 2.7× more common, from 1 in 4,660 baby boys to 1 in 1,750.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2007 and 2012, its share of baby boys fell by 56%, from 1 in 1,110 to 1 in 2,560.

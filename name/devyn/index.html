Devyn name popularity since 1925

16,215 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Devyn (58.1% girls, 41.9% boys). Half of them were born in 2004 or earlier, so the typical Devyn is about 22 years old in 2026. Devyn peaked in 2000 as the #699 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

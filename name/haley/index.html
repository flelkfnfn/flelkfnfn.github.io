Haley name popularity since 1925

160,100 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Haley. Half of them were born in 1999 or earlier, so the typical Haley is about 27 years old in 2026. Haley peaked in 1996 as the #30 name for girls, and it ranked #861 in 2025.

Haley births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 2000. Measured as a share of all babies, Haley peaked in 1996.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1976 and 1981, Haley became 5.8× more common, from 1 in 12,500 baby girls to 1 in 2,160.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2005 and 2010, its share of baby girls fell by 60%, from 1 in 360 to 1 in 894.

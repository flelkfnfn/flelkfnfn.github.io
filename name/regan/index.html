Regan name popularity since 1925

20,048 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Regan (81.3% girls, 18.7% boys). Half of them were born in 2000 or earlier, so the typical Regan is about 26 years old in 2026. Regan peaked in 2001 as the #391 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Regan births per year, 1925–2025

2001 was the biggest year for Regan both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1980 and 1985, Regan's share of baby girls fell by 64%, from 1 in 5,910 to 1 in 16,500.
  • Fastest rise: Between 1989 and 1994, it became 2.8× more common, from 1 in 13,500 baby girls to 1 in 4,890.

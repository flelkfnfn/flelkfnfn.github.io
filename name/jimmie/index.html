Jimmie name popularity since 1925

115,450 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Jimmie (14.5% girls, 85.5% boys). Half of them were born in 1945 or earlier, so the typical Jimmie is about 81 years old in 2026. Jimmie peaked in 1934 as the #68 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Jimmie births per year, 1925–2025

1934 was the biggest year for Jimmie both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1927 and 1932, Jimmie became 1.6× more common.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1997 and 2002, its share of baby boys fell by 44%, from 1 in 9,770 to 1 in 17,500.

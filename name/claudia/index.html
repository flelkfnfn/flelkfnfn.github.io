Claudia name popularity since 1925

121,346 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Claudia. Half of them were born in 1970 or earlier, so the typical Claudia is about 56 years old in 2026. Claudia peaked in 1952 as the #111 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Claudia births per year, 1925–2025

1952 was the biggest year for Claudia both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1932 and 1937, Claudia became 2.4× more common.

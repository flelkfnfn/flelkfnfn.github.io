Reed name popularity since 1925

35,166 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Reed (2.6% girls, 97.4% boys). Half of them were born in 2004 or earlier, so the typical Reed is about 22 years old in 2026. Reed peaked in 2014 as the #318 name for boys, and it ranked #419 in 2025.

Reed births per year, 1925–2025

2014 was the biggest year for Reed both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1956 and 1961, Reed's share of baby boys fell by 37%, from 1 in 7,500 to 1 in 11,900.
  • Fastest rise: Between 1990 and 1995, it became 2.5× more common, from 1 in 8,630 baby boys to 1 in 3,460.

Malinda name popularity since 1925

18,449 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Malinda. Half of them were born in 1971 or earlier, so the typical Malinda is about 55 years old in 2026. Malinda peaked in 1881 as the #254 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Maximiliano name popularity since 1925

23,945 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Maximiliano. Half of them were born in 2016 or earlier, so the typical Maximiliano is about 10 years old in 2026. Maximiliano peaked in 2019 as the #251 name for boys, and it ranked #280 in 2025.

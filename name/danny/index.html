Danny name popularity since 1925

287,331 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Danny (0.6% girls, 99.4% boys). Half of them were born in 1960 or earlier, so the typical Danny is about 66 years old in 2026. Danny peaked in 1948 as the #39 name for boys, and it ranked #628 in 2025.

Danny births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1954. Measured as a share of all babies, Danny peaked in 1948.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1941 and 1946, Danny became 3.1× more common, from 1 in 747 baby boys to 1 in 244.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1963 and 1968, its share of baby boys fell by 34%, from 1 in 286 to 1 in 436.

Hazel name popularity since 1925

165,547 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Hazel (99.0% girls, 1.0% boys). Half of them were born in 1964 or earlier, so the typical Hazel is about 62 years old in 2026. Hazel peaked in 1897 as the #18 name for girls, and it ranked #21 in 2025.

Chandler name popularity since 1925

46,529 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Chandler (22.3% girls, 77.7% boys). Half of them were born in 2000 or earlier, so the typical Chandler is about 26 years old in 2026. Chandler peaked in 1999 as the #151 name for boys, and it ranked #753 in 2025.

Chandler births per year, 1925–2025

1999 was the biggest year for Chandler both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1990 and 1995, Chandler became 5× more common, from 1 in 5,170 baby boys to 1 in 1,030.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2001 and 2006, its share of baby boys fell by 60%, from 1 in 1,080 to 1 in 2,730.

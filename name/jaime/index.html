Jaime name popularity since 1925

121,466 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Jaime (41.2% girls, 58.8% boys). Half of them were born in 1983 or earlier, so the typical Jaime is about 43 years old in 2026. Jaime peaked in 1976 as the #168 name for boys, and it ranked #657 in 2025.

Jaime births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1980. Measured as a share of all babies, Jaime peaked in 1976.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1950 and 1955, Jaime became 2.1× more common, from 1 in 11,600 baby boys to 1 in 5,480.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2007 and 2012, its share of baby boys fell by 35%, from 1 in 1,690 to 1 in 2,590.

Marisa name popularity since 1925

48,773 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Marisa. Half of them were born in 1990 or earlier, so the typical Marisa is about 36 years old in 2026. Marisa peaked in 1995 as the #147 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Marisa births per year, 1925–2025

1995 was the biggest year for Marisa both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1972 and 1977, Marisa became 3.3× more common, from 1 in 3,140 baby girls to 1 in 953.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2010 and 2015, its share of baby girls fell by 67%, from 1 in 5,390 to 1 in 16,500.

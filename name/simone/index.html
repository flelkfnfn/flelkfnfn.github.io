Simone name popularity since 1925

27,553 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Simone (98.2% girls, 1.8% boys). Half of them were born in 1995 or earlier, so the typical Simone is about 31 years old in 2026. Simone peaked in 1988 as the #311 name for girls, and it ranked #998 in 2025.

Simone births per year, 1925–2025

1988 was the biggest year for Simone both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1981 and 1986, Simone's share of baby girls fell by 39%, from 1 in 4,060 to 1 in 6,620.
  • Fastest rise: Between 1985 and 1990, it became 3.1× more common, from 1 in 7,650 baby girls to 1 in 2,480.

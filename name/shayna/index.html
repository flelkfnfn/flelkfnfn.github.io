Shayna name popularity since 1925

16,272 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Shayna. Half of them were born in 1994 or earlier, so the typical Shayna is about 32 years old in 2026. Shayna peaked in 1991 as the #381 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Shayna births per year, 1925–2025

Peyton name popularity since 1925

140,250 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Peyton (62.9% girls, 37.1% boys). Half of them were born in 2010 or earlier, so the typical Peyton is about 16 years old in 2026. Peyton peaked in 2009 as the #42 name for girls, and it ranked #199 in 2025.

Peyton births per year, 1925–2025

2009 was the biggest year for Peyton both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1992 and 1997, Peyton became 3.5× more common, from 1 in 4,630 baby girls to 1 in 1,340.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2020 and 2025, its share of baby girls fell by 43%, from 1 in 592 to 1 in 1,030.

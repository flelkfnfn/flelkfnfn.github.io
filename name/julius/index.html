Julius name popularity since 1925

63,154 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Julius. Half of them were born in 1983 or earlier, so the typical Julius is about 43 years old in 2026. Julius peaked in 1910 as the #95 name for boys, and it ranked #424 in 2025.

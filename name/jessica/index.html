Jessica name popularity since 1925

1,053,284 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Jessica. Half of them were born in 1987 or earlier, so the typical Jessica is about 39 years old in 2026. Jessica peaked in 1987 as the #1 name for girls, and it ranked #665 in 2025.

Jessica births per year, 1925–2025

1987 was the biggest year for Jessica both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1969 and 1974, Jessica became 4.9× more common, from 1 in 677 baby girls to 1 in 138.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2004 and 2009, its share of baby girls fell by 60%, from 1 in 194 to 1 in 483.

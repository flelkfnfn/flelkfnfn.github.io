Gerry name popularity since 1925

20,460 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Gerry (30.1% girls, 69.9% boys). Half of them were born in 1954 or earlier, so the typical Gerry is about 72 years old in 2026. Gerry peaked in 1958 as the #336 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Gerry births per year, 1925–2025

1958 was the biggest year for Gerry both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1935 and 1940, Gerry became 1.7× more common, from 1 in 8,980 baby boys to 1 in 5,370.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1958 and 1963, its share of baby boys fell by 42%, from 1 in 4,060 to 1 in 7,000.

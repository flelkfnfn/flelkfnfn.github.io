Porter name popularity since 1925

19,546 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Porter (5.0% girls, 95.0% boys). Half of them were born in 2012 or earlier, so the typical Porter is about 14 years old in 2026. Porter peaked in 2015 as the #377 name for boys, and it ranked #560 in 2025.

Oliver name popularity since 1925

249,508 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Oliver. Half of them were born in 2017 or earlier, so the typical Oliver is about 9 years old in 2026. Oliver peaked in 2024 as the #3 name for boys, and it ranked #3 in 2025.

Oliver births per year, 1925–2025

2024 was the biggest year for Oliver both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1954 and 1959, Oliver's share of baby boys fell by 34%, from 1 in 3,800 to 1 in 5,770.
  • Fastest rise: Between 2004 and 2009, it became 2.9× more common, from 1 in 1,330 baby boys to 1 in 463.

Zion name popularity since 1925

53,891 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Zion (13.0% girls, 87.0% boys). Half of them were born in 2015 or earlier, so the typical Zion is about 11 years old in 2026. Zion peaked in 2023 as the #125 name for boys, and it ranked #142 in 2025.

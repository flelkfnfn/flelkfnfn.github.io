Tracy name popularity since 1925

311,296 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Tracy (80.6% girls, 19.4% boys). Half of them were born in 1970 or earlier, so the typical Tracy is about 56 years old in 2026. Tracy peaked in 1970 as the #10 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Tracy births per year, 1925–2025

1970 was the biggest year for Tracy both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1951 and 1956, Tracy became 6.4× more common, from 1 in 9,330 baby girls to 1 in 1,450.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1986 and 1991, its share of baby girls fell by 62%, from 1 in 708 to 1 in 1,880.

Emilie name popularity since 1925

22,781 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Emilie. Half of them were born in 1997 or earlier, so the typical Emilie is about 29 years old in 2026. Emilie peaked in 1880 as the #228 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Samara name popularity since 1925

22,769 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Samara. Half of them were born in 2012 or earlier, so the typical Samara is about 14 years old in 2026. Samara peaked in 2018 as the #256 name for girls, and it ranked #320 in 2025.

Samara births per year, 1925–2025

2018 was the biggest year for Samara both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1999 and 2004, Samara became 3.7× more common, from 1 in 7,950 baby girls to 1 in 2,140.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2006 and 2011, its share of baby girls fell by 40%, from 1 in 2,010 to 1 in 3,350.

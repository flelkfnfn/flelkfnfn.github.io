Jane name popularity since 1925

316,699 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Jane. Half of them were born in 1951 or earlier, so the typical Jane is about 75 years old in 2026. Jane peaked in 1945 as the #36 name for girls, and it ranked #221 in 2025.

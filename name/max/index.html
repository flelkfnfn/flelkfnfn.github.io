Max name popularity since 1925

146,707 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Max (0.7% girls, 99.3% boys). Half of them were born in 2001 or earlier, so the typical Max is about 25 years old in 2026. Max peaked in 2011 as the #96 name for boys, and it ranked #180 in 2025.

Max births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 2009. Measured as a share of all babies, Max peaked in 2011.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1938 and 1943, Max's share of baby boys fell by 42%, from 1 in 953 to 1 in 1,630.
  • Fastest rise: Between 1982 and 1987, it became 3.1× more common, from 1 in 3,930 baby boys to 1 in 1,290.

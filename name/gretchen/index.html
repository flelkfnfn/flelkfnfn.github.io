Gretchen name popularity since 1925

50,450 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Gretchen. Half of them were born in 1970 or earlier, so the typical Gretchen is about 56 years old in 2026. Gretchen peaked in 1973 as the #191 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Gretchen births per year, 1925–2025

1973 was the biggest year for Gretchen both by count and as a share of all babies.

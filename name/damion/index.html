Damion name popularity since 1925

16,927 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Damion. Half of them were born in 1998 or earlier, so the typical Damion is about 28 years old in 2026. Damion peaked in 1977 as the #352 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Damion births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 2002. Measured as a share of all babies, Damion peaked in 1977.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1971 and 1976, Damion became 3.8× more common, from 1 in 13,000 baby boys to 1 in 3,440.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1977 and 1982, its share of baby boys fell by 63%, from 1 in 3,310 to 1 in 8,850.

Glenn name popularity since 1925

219,275 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Glenn (0.7% girls, 99.3% boys). Half of them were born in 1956 or earlier, so the typical Glenn is about 70 years old in 2026. Glenn peaked in 1962 as the #55 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Glenn births per year, 1925–2025

1962 was the biggest year for Glenn both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1945 and 1950, Glenn became 1.5× more common, from 1 in 492 baby boys to 1 in 328.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1969 and 1974, its share of baby boys fell by 49%, from 1 in 516 to 1 in 1,020.

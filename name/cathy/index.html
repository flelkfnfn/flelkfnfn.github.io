Cathy name popularity since 1925

170,595 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Cathy. Half of them were born in 1958 or earlier, so the typical Cathy is about 68 years old in 2026. Cathy peaked in 1958 as the #34 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Cathy births per year, 1925–2025

1958 was the biggest year for Cathy both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1944 and 1949, Cathy became 5× more common, from 1 in 1,950 baby girls to 1 in 388.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1971 and 1976, its share of baby girls fell by 60%, from 1 in 733 to 1 in 1,810.

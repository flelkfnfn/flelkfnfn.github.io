Ingrid name popularity since 1925

27,188 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Ingrid. Half of them were born in 1979 or earlier, so the typical Ingrid is about 47 years old in 2026. Ingrid peaked in 1967 as the #380 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Ingrid births per year, 1925–2025

1967 was the biggest year for Ingrid both by count and as a share of all babies.

Allan name popularity since 1925

89,410 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Allan. Half of them were born in 1955 or earlier, so the typical Allan is about 71 years old in 2026. Allan peaked in 1938 as the #119 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

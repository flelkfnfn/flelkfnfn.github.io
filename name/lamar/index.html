Lamar name popularity since 1925

32,830 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Lamar (3.7% girls, 96.3% boys). Half of them were born in 1981 or earlier, so the typical Lamar is about 45 years old in 2026. Lamar peaked in 1989 as the #325 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Lamar births per year, 1925–2025

1989 was the biggest year for Lamar both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1966 and 1971, Lamar became 1.8× more common, from 1 in 8,110 baby boys to 1 in 4,560.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1990 and 1995, its share of baby boys fell by 52%, from 1 in 2,980 to 1 in 6,220.

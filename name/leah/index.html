Leah name popularity since 1925

245,960 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Leah. Half of them were born in 2001 or earlier, so the typical Leah is about 25 years old in 2026. Leah peaked in 2009 as the #29 name for girls, and it ranked #58 in 2025.

Leah births per year, 1925–2025

2009 was the biggest year for Leah both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1937 and 1942, Leah's share of baby girls fell by 36%, from 1 in 2,610 to 1 in 4,090.
  • Fastest rise: Between 1959 and 1964, it became 2.1× more common, from 1 in 2,740 baby girls to 1 in 1,330.

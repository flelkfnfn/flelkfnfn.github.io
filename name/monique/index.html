Monique name popularity since 1925

79,134 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Monique. Half of them were born in 1982 or earlier, so the typical Monique is about 44 years old in 2026. Monique peaked in 1980 as the #93 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Monique births per year, 1925–2025

1980 was the biggest year for Monique both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1955 and 1960, Monique became 5.4× more common, from 1 in 17,500 baby girls to 1 in 3,220.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2006 and 2011, its share of baby girls fell by 68%, from 1 in 4,290 to 1 in 13,300.

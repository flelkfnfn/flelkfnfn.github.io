Hallie name popularity since 1925

34,201 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Hallie (98.1% girls, 1.9% boys). Half of them were born in 2006 or earlier, so the typical Hallie is about 20 years old in 2026. Hallie peaked in 2025 as the #120 name for girls, and it ranked #120 in 2025.

Hallie births per year, 1925–2025

2025 was the biggest year for Hallie both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Steepest fall: Between 2000 and 2005, Hallie's share of baby girls fell by 41%, from 1 in 1,680 to 1 in 2,820.
  • Fastest rise: Between 2020 and 2025, it became 2.7× more common, from 1 in 1,950 baby girls to 1 in 711.

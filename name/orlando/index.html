Orlando name popularity since 1925

47,277 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Orlando. Half of them were born in 1986 or earlier, so the typical Orlando is about 40 years old in 2026. Orlando peaked in 1975 as the #246 name for boys, and it ranked #873 in 2025.

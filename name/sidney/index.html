Sidney name popularity since 1925

82,030 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Sidney (28.7% girls, 71.3% boys). Half of them were born in 1963 or earlier, so the typical Sidney is about 63 years old in 2026. Sidney peaked in 1912 as the #80 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

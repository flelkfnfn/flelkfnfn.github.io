Lester name popularity since 1925

86,824 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Lester (0.9% girls, 99.1% boys). Half of them were born in 1948 or earlier, so the typical Lester is about 78 years old in 2026. Lester peaked in 1906 as the #52 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

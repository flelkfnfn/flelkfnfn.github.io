Javier name popularity since 1925

108,401 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Javier. Half of them were born in 1998 or earlier, so the typical Javier is about 28 years old in 2026. Javier peaked in 2001 as the #149 name for boys, and it ranked #274 in 2025.

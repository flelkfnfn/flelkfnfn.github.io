Lila name popularity since 1925

61,496 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Lila. Half of them were born in 2006 or earlier, so the typical Lila is about 20 years old in 2026. Lila peaked in 2010 as the #155 name for girls, and it ranked #187 in 2025.

Lila births per year, 1925–2025

2010 was the biggest year for Lila both by count and as a share of all babies.

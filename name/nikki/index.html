Nikki name popularity since 1925

41,005 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Nikki (97.8% girls, 2.2% boys). Half of them were born in 1982 or earlier, so the typical Nikki is about 44 years old in 2026. Nikki peaked in 1972 as the #173 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Nikki births per year, 1925–2025

1972 was the biggest year for Nikki both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1967 and 1972, Nikki became 4.4× more common, from 1 in 3,950 baby girls to 1 in 893.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1996 and 2001, its share of baby girls fell by 53%, from 1 in 2,540 to 1 in 5,340.

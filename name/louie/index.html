Louie name popularity since 1925

25,515 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Louie (3.9% girls, 96.1% boys). Half of them were born in 1958 or earlier, so the typical Louie is about 68 years old in 2026. Louie peaked in 1901 as the #230 name for boys, and it ranked #596 in 2025.

Jenna name popularity since 1925

148,780 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Jenna. Half of them were born in 1997 or earlier, so the typical Jenna is about 29 years old in 2026. Jenna peaked in 1985 as the #50 name for girls, and it ranked #679 in 2025.

Jenna births per year, 1925–2025

1985 was the biggest year for Jenna both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1979 and 1984, Jenna became 9× more common, from 1 in 2,580 baby girls to 1 in 286.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2008 and 2013, its share of baby girls fell by 53%, from 1 in 583 to 1 in 1,240.

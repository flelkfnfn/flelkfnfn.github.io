Santos name popularity since 1925

19,675 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Santos (9.7% girls, 90.3% boys). Half of them were born in 1984 or earlier, so the typical Santos is about 42 years old in 2026. Santos peaked in 2025 as the #766 name for boys, and it ranked #766 in 2025.

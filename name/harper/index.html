Harper name popularity since 1925

145,400 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Harper (95.6% girls, 4.4% boys). Half of them were born in 2017 or earlier, so the typical Harper is about 9 years old in 2026. Harper peaked in 2018 as the #9 name for girls, and it ranked #16 in 2025.

Kristy name popularity since 1925

70,703 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Kristy. Half of them were born in 1979 or earlier, so the typical Kristy is about 47 years old in 2026. Kristy peaked in 1978 as the #51 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Kristy births per year, 1925–2025

1978 was the biggest year for Kristy both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1973 and 1978, Kristy became 2.7× more common, from 1 in 771 baby girls to 1 in 287.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1978 and 1983, its share of baby girls fell by 61%, from 1 in 287 to 1 in 745.

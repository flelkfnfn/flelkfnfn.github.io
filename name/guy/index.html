Guy name popularity since 1925

72,160 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Guy. Half of them were born in 1957 or earlier, so the typical Guy is about 69 years old in 2026. Guy peaked in 1891 as the #67 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Guy births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year on this chart was 1957. Measured as a share of all babies, Guy peaked earlier, in 1891.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1948 and 1953, Guy became 1.6× more common, from 1 in 1,720 baby boys to 1 in 1,050.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1969 and 1974, its share of baby boys fell by 42%, from 1 in 1,530 to 1 in 2,650.

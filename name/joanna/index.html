Joanna name popularity since 1925

108,892 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Joanna. Half of them were born in 1986 or earlier, so the typical Joanna is about 40 years old in 2026. Joanna peaked in 1984 as the #88 name for girls, and it ranked #306 in 2025.

Joanna births per year, 1925–2025

1984 was the biggest year for Joanna both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1979 and 1984, Joanna became 2.3× more common, from 1 in 1,160 baby girls to 1 in 505.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1984 and 1989, its share of baby girls fell by 42%, from 1 in 505 to 1 in 871.

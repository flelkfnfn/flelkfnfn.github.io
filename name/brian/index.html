Brian name popularity since 1925

1,178,008 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Brian. Half of them were born in 1975 or earlier, so the typical Brian is about 51 years old in 2026. Brian peaked in 1973 as the #8 name for boys, and it ranked #339 in 2025.

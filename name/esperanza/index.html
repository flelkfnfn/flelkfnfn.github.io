Esperanza name popularity since 1925

19,708 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Esperanza. Half of them were born in 1993 or earlier, so the typical Esperanza is about 33 years old in 2026. Esperanza peaked in 2000 as the #528 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Esperanza births per year, 1925–2025

2000 was the biggest year for Esperanza both by count and as a share of all babies.

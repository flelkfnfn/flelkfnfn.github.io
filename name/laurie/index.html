Laurie name popularity since 1925

167,273 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Laurie (99.1% girls, 0.9% boys). Half of them were born in 1962 or earlier, so the typical Laurie is about 64 years old in 2026. Laurie peaked in 1962 as the #42 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Laurie births per year, 1925–2025

1962 was the biggest year for Laurie both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1948 and 1953, Laurie became 4.1× more common, from 1 in 2,110 baby girls to 1 in 510.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1985 and 1990, its share of baby girls fell by 60%, from 1 in 2,210 to 1 in 5,500.

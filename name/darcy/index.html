Darcy name popularity since 1925

26,110 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Darcy (89.7% girls, 10.3% boys). Half of them were born in 1974 or earlier, so the typical Darcy is about 52 years old in 2026. Darcy peaked in 1968 as the #349 name for girls, and it ranked #857 in 2025.

Darcy births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1970. Measured as a share of all babies, Darcy peaked in 1968.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1950 and 1955, Darcy became 2× more common, from 1 in 13,400 baby girls to 1 in 6,580.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1982 and 1987, its share of baby girls fell by 50%, from 1 in 4,230 to 1 in 8,520.

Rosetta name popularity since 1925

20,782 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Rosetta. Half of them were born in 1948 or earlier, so the typical Rosetta is about 78 years old in 2026. Rosetta peaked in 1881 as the #220 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

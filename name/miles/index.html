Miles name popularity since 1925

119,137 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Miles. Half of them were born in 2014 or earlier, so the typical Miles is about 12 years old in 2026. Miles peaked in 2023 as the #43 name for boys, and it ranked #44 in 2025.

Miles births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 2024. Measured as a share of all babies, Miles peaked in 2023.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1962 and 1967, Miles's share of baby boys fell by 38%, from 1 in 5,360 to 1 in 8,590.

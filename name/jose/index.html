Jose name popularity since 1925

580,726 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Jose (0.7% girls, 99.3% boys). Half of them were born in 1992 or earlier, so the typical Jose is about 34 years old in 2026. Jose peaked in 2002 as the #30 name for boys, and it ranked #95 in 2025.

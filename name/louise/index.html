Louise name popularity since 1925

177,270 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Louise (99.2% girls, 0.8% boys). Half of them were born in 1940 or earlier, so the typical Louise is about 86 years old in 2026. Louise peaked in 1912 as the #17 name for girls, and it ranked #516 in 2025.

Louise births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year on this chart was 1925. Measured as a share of all babies, Louise peaked earlier, in 1912.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1969 and 1974, Louise's share of baby girls fell by 56%, from 1 in 2,800 to 1 in 6,410.
  • Fastest rise: Between 2011 and 2016, it became 2.5× more common, from 1 in 14,100 baby girls to 1 in 5,660.

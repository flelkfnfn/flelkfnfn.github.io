Callie name popularity since 1925

60,012 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Callie (99.4% girls, 0.6% boys). Half of them were born in 2006 or earlier, so the typical Callie is about 20 years old in 2026. Callie peaked in 1880 as the #124 name for girls, and it ranked #175 in 2025.

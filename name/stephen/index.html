Stephen name popularity since 1925

839,820 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Stephen. Half of them were born in 1965 or earlier, so the typical Stephen is about 61 years old in 2026. Stephen peaked in 1951 as the #19 name for boys, and it ranked #383 in 2025.

Stephen births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1952. Measured as a share of all babies, Stephen peaked in 1951.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1937 and 1942, Stephen became 2.7× more common, from 1 in 500 baby boys to 1 in 184.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1990 and 1995, its share of baby boys fell by 41%, from 1 in 172 to 1 in 290.

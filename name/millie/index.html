Millie name popularity since 1925

34,186 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Millie. Half of them were born in 2017 or earlier, so the typical Millie is about 9 years old in 2026. Millie peaked in 2025 as the #73 name for girls, and it ranked #73 in 2025.

Millie births per year, 1925–2025

2025 was the biggest year for Millie both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1945 and 1950, Millie's share of baby girls fell by 36%, from 1 in 5,910 to 1 in 9,310.
  • Fastest rise: Between 2007 and 2012, it became 2.8× more common, from 1 in 12,700 baby girls to 1 in 4,570.

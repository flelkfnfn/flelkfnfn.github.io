Brennan name popularity since 1925

35,482 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Brennan (6.3% girls, 93.7% boys). Half of them were born in 2003 or earlier, so the typical Brennan is about 23 years old in 2026. Brennan peaked in 2009 as the #242 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Brennan births per year, 1925–2025

2009 was the biggest year for Brennan both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1982 and 1987, Brennan became 2.7× more common, from 1 in 11,900 baby boys to 1 in 4,420.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2013 and 2018, its share of baby boys fell by 57%, from 1 in 2,040 to 1 in 4,750.

Rebecca name popularity since 1925

731,545 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Rebecca. Half of them were born in 1976 or earlier, so the typical Rebecca is about 50 years old in 2026. Rebecca peaked in 1974 as the #10 name for girls, and it ranked #350 in 2025.

Rebecca births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1981. Measured as a share of all babies, Rebecca peaked in 1974.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1945 and 1950, Rebecca became 2.2× more common, from 1 in 392 baby girls to 1 in 182.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1997 and 2002, its share of baby girls fell by 47%, from 1 in 181 to 1 in 344.

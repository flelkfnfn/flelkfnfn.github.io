Duncan name popularity since 1925

19,924 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Duncan. Half of them were born in 1996 or earlier, so the typical Duncan is about 30 years old in 2026. Duncan peaked in 1997 as the #378 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Duncan births per year, 1925–2025

1997 was the biggest year for Duncan both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1992 and 1997, Duncan became 2.6× more common, from 1 in 7,680 baby boys to 1 in 2,930.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2002 and 2007, its share of baby boys fell by 43%, from 1 in 4,110 to 1 in 7,210.

Oscar name popularity since 1925

173,325 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Oscar. Half of them were born in 1993 or earlier, so the typical Oscar is about 33 years old in 2026. Oscar peaked in 1886 as the #26 name for boys, and it ranked #223 in 2025.

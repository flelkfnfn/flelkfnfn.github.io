Jarrod name popularity since 1925

25,351 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Jarrod. Half of them were born in 1984 or earlier, so the typical Jarrod is about 42 years old in 2026. Jarrod peaked in 1980 as the #213 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Jarrod births per year, 1925–2025

1980 was the biggest year for Jarrod both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1966 and 1971, Jarrod became 2.3× more common, from 1 in 8,140 baby boys to 1 in 3,480.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1999 and 2004, its share of baby boys fell by 71%, from 1 in 3,640 to 1 in 12,600.

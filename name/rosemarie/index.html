Rosemarie name popularity since 1925

49,459 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Rosemarie. Half of them were born in 1949 or earlier, so the typical Rosemarie is about 77 years old in 2026. Rosemarie peaked in 1936 as the #158 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

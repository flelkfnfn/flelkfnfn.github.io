Tanner name popularity since 1925

100,128 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Tanner (2.5% girls, 97.5% boys). Half of them were born in 2001 or earlier, so the typical Tanner is about 25 years old in 2026. Tanner peaked in 1997 as the #82 name for boys, and it ranked #475 in 2025.

Tanner births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1998. Measured as a share of all babies, Tanner peaked in 1997.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1987 and 1992, Tanner became 4.7× more common, from 1 in 2,600 baby boys to 1 in 549.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2013 and 2018, its share of baby boys fell by 42%, from 1 in 923 to 1 in 1,590.

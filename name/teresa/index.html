Teresa name popularity since 1925

404,085 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Teresa. Half of them were born in 1962 or earlier, so the typical Teresa is about 64 years old in 2026. Teresa peaked in 1962 as the #18 name for girls, and it ranked #911 in 2025.

Teresa births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1961. Measured as a share of all babies, Teresa peaked in 1962.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1946 and 1951, Teresa became 3× more common, from 1 in 857 baby girls to 1 in 290.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1972 and 1977, its share of baby girls fell by 49%, from 1 in 212 to 1 in 420.

Neil name popularity since 1925

99,339 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Neil. Half of them were born in 1964 or earlier, so the typical Neil is about 62 years old in 2026. Neil peaked in 1933 as the #149 name for boys, and it ranked #851 in 2025.

Neil births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1954. Measured as a share of all babies, Neil peaked in 1933.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1927 and 1932, Neil became 1.7× more common.

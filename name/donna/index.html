Donna name popularity since 1925

820,954 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Donna. Half of them were born in 1955 or earlier, so the typical Donna is about 71 years old in 2026. Donna peaked in 1959 as the #5 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Donna births per year, 1925–2025

1959 was the biggest year for Donna both by count and as a share of all babies.

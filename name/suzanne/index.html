Suzanne name popularity since 1925

216,202 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Suzanne. Half of them were born in 1960 or earlier, so the typical Suzanne is about 66 years old in 2026. Suzanne peaked in 1946 as the #52 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Suzanne births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1962. Measured as a share of all babies, Suzanne peaked in 1946.

Jonathan name popularity since 1925

872,776 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Jonathan. Half of them were born in 1990 or earlier, so the typical Jonathan is about 36 years old in 2026. Jonathan peaked in 1985 as the #17 name for boys, and it ranked #89 in 2025.

Chloe name popularity since 1925

252,020 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Chloe. Half of them were born in 2009 or earlier, so the typical Chloe is about 17 years old in 2026. Chloe peaked in 2010 as the #9 name for girls, and it ranked #23 in 2025.

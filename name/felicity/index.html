Felicity name popularity since 1925

18,565 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Felicity. Half of them were born in 2014 or earlier, so the typical Felicity is about 12 years old in 2026. Felicity peaked in 2017 as the #345 name for girls, and it ranked #473 in 2025.

Felicity births per year, 1925–2025

2017 was the biggest year for Felicity both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1994 and 1999, Felicity became 7× more common, from 1 in 17,300 baby girls to 1 in 2,470.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1999 and 2004, its share of baby girls fell by 49%, from 1 in 2,470 to 1 in 4,840.

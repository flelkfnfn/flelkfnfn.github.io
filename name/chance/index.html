Chance name popularity since 1925

55,988 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Chance (2.5% girls, 97.5% boys). Half of them were born in 2004 or earlier, so the typical Chance is about 22 years old in 2026. Chance peaked in 1996 as the #154 name for boys, and it ranked #442 in 2025.

Chance births per year, 1925–2025

1996 was the biggest year for Chance both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1979 and 1984, Chance became 3.1× more common, from 1 in 6,420 baby boys to 1 in 2,050.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2020 and 2025, its share of baby boys fell by 40%, from 1 in 1,440 to 1 in 2,400.

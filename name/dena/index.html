Dena name popularity since 1925

30,239 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Dena. Half of them were born in 1969 or earlier, so the typical Dena is about 57 years old in 2026. Dena peaked in 1970 as the #208 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Dena births per year, 1925–2025

1970 was the biggest year for Dena both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1949 and 1954, Dena became 2.1× more common, from 1 in 9,610 baby girls to 1 in 4,550.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1984 and 1989, its share of baby girls fell by 51%, from 1 in 4,150 to 1 in 8,460.

Lonnie name popularity since 1925

88,163 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Lonnie (6.1% girls, 93.9% boys). Half of them were born in 1956 or earlier, so the typical Lonnie is about 70 years old in 2026. Lonnie peaked in 1901 as the #114 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

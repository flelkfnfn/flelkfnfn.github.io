Deandre name popularity since 1925

29,947 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Deandre (1.5% girls, 98.5% boys). Half of them were born in 1997 or earlier, so the typical Deandre is about 29 years old in 2026. Deandre peaked in 1995 as the #250 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Deandre births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1994. Measured as a share of all babies, Deandre peaked in 1995.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1981 and 1986, Deandre became 2.1× more common, from 1 in 7,280 baby boys to 1 in 3,430.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2020 and 2025, its share of baby boys fell by 44%, from 1 in 5,460 to 1 in 9,760.

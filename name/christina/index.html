Christina name popularity since 1925

473,718 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Christina. Half of them were born in 1981 or earlier, so the typical Christina is about 45 years old in 2026. Christina peaked in 1978 as the #15 name for girls, and it ranked #770 in 2025.

Christina births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1985. Measured as a share of all babies, Christina peaked in 1978.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1939 and 1944, Christina became 2.4× more common, from 1 in 4,190 baby girls to 1 in 1,750.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2003 and 2008, its share of baby girls fell by 48%, from 1 in 586 to 1 in 1,130.

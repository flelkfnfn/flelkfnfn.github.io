Delores name popularity since 1925

110,571 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Delores. Half of them were born in 1941 or earlier, so the typical Delores is about 85 years old in 2026. Delores peaked in 1932 as the #56 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

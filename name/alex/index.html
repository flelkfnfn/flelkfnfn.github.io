Alex name popularity since 1925

278,387 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Alex (3.5% girls, 96.5% boys). Half of them were born in 1996 or earlier, so the typical Alex is about 30 years old in 2026. Alex peaked in 1995 as the #47 name for boys, and it ranked #232 in 2025.

Alex births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1993. Measured as a share of all babies, Alex peaked in 1995.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1982 and 1987, Alex became 3× more common, from 1 in 924 baby boys to 1 in 309.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2009 and 2014, its share of baby boys fell by 36%, from 1 in 383 to 1 in 602.

Helena name popularity since 1925

32,064 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Helena. Half of them were born in 1997 or earlier, so the typical Helena is about 29 years old in 2026. Helena peaked in 1884 as the #201 name for girls, and it ranked #363 in 2025.

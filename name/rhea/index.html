Rhea name popularity since 1925

16,700 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Rhea (98.3% girls, 1.7% boys). Half of them were born in 1994 or earlier, so the typical Rhea is about 32 years old in 2026. Rhea peaked in 2023 as the #527 name for girls, and it ranked #639 in 2025.

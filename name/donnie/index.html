Donnie name popularity since 1925

65,293 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Donnie (7.7% girls, 92.3% boys). Half of them were born in 1957 or earlier, so the typical Donnie is about 69 years old in 2026. Donnie peaked in 1950 as the #150 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Donnie births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1952. Measured as a share of all babies, Donnie peaked in 1950.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1929 and 1934, Donnie became 3× more common.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1990 and 1995, its share of baby boys fell by 43%, from 1 in 6,060 to 1 in 10,600.

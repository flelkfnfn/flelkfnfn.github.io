Marcia name popularity since 1925

128,940 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Marcia. Half of them were born in 1952 or earlier, so the typical Marcia is about 74 years old in 2026. Marcia peaked in 1951 as the #74 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

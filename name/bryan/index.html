Bryan name popularity since 1925

389,712 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Bryan. Half of them were born in 1985 or earlier, so the typical Bryan is about 41 years old in 2026. Bryan peaked in 1977 as the #42 name for boys, and it ranked #336 in 2025.

Bryan births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1985. Measured as a share of all babies, Bryan peaked in 1977.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1953 and 1958, Bryan became 3.6× more common, from 1 in 1,880 baby boys to 1 in 519.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2007 and 2012, its share of baby boys fell by 45%, from 1 in 336 to 1 in 615.

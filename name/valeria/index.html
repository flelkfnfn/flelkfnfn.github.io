Valeria name popularity since 1925

76,813 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Valeria. Half of them were born in 2008 or earlier, so the typical Valeria is about 18 years old in 2026. Valeria peaked in 2009 as the #72 name for girls, and it ranked #162 in 2025.

Valeria births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 2008. Measured as a share of all babies, Valeria peaked in 2009.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1988 and 1993, Valeria became 3.9× more common, from 1 in 7,950 baby girls to 1 in 2,040.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2008 and 2013, its share of baby girls fell by 53%, from 1 in 450 to 1 in 966.

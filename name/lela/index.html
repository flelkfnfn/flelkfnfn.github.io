Lela name popularity since 1925

21,427 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Lela. Half of them were born in 1947 or earlier, so the typical Lela is about 79 years old in 2026. Lela peaked in 1897 as the #116 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

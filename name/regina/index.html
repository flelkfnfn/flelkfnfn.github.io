Regina name popularity since 1925

167,409 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Regina. Half of them were born in 1965 or earlier, so the typical Regina is about 61 years old in 2026. Regina peaked in 1967 as the #80 name for girls, and it ranked #395 in 2025.

Regina births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1962. Measured as a share of all babies, Regina peaked in 1967.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1944 and 1949, Regina became 1.9× more common, from 1 in 1,410 baby girls to 1 in 755.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1980 and 1985, its share of baby girls fell by 40%, from 1 in 812 to 1 in 1,350.

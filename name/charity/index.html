Charity name popularity since 1925

33,460 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Charity. Half of them were born in 1984 or earlier, so the typical Charity is about 42 years old in 2026. Charity peaked in 1975 as the #183 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Charity births per year, 1925–2025

1975 was the biggest year for Charity both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1968 and 1973, Charity became 9.4× more common, from 1 in 14,300 baby girls to 1 in 1,520.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1985 and 1990, its share of baby girls fell by 47%, from 1 in 1,890 to 1 in 3,540.

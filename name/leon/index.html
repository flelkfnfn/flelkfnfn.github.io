Leon name popularity since 1925

148,932 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Leon (0.6% girls, 99.4% boys). Half of them were born in 1956 or earlier, so the typical Leon is about 70 years old in 2026. Leon peaked in 1918 as the #77 name for boys, and it ranked #138 in 2025.

Jameson name popularity since 1925

63,085 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Jameson (1.9% girls, 98.1% boys). Half of them were born in 2017 or earlier, so the typical Jameson is about 9 years old in 2026. Jameson peaked in 2019 as the #77 name for boys, and it ranked #137 in 2025.

Jameson births per year, 1925–2025

2019 was the biggest year for Jameson both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1985 and 1990, Jameson's share of baby boys fell by 50%, from 1 in 4,380 to 1 in 8,820.
  • Fastest rise: Between 2008 and 2013, it became 3× more common, from 1 in 2,520 baby boys to 1 in 846.

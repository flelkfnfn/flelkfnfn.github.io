Loretta name popularity since 1925

143,116 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Loretta. Half of them were born in 1949 or earlier, so the typical Loretta is about 77 years old in 2026. Loretta peaked in 1938 as the #62 name for girls, and it ranked #606 in 2025.

Loretta births per year, 1925–2025

1938 was the biggest year for Loretta both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1973 and 1978, Loretta's share of baby girls fell by 53%, from 1 in 1,500 to 1 in 3,180.
  • Fastest rise: Between 2011 and 2016, it became 2.1× more common, from 1 in 15,800 baby girls to 1 in 7,380.

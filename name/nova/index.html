Nova name popularity since 1925

57,317 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Nova (94.5% girls, 5.5% boys). Half of them were born in 2020 or earlier, so the typical Nova is about 6 years old in 2026. Nova peaked in 2022 as the #32 name for girls, and it ranked #46 in 2025.

Kaleigh name popularity since 1925

16,250 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Kaleigh. Half of them were born in 2002 or earlier, so the typical Kaleigh is about 24 years old in 2026. Kaleigh peaked in 2001 as the #399 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Kaleigh births per year, 1925–2025

2001 was the biggest year for Kaleigh both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1987 and 1992, Kaleigh became 1.9× more common, from 1 in 6,000 baby girls to 1 in 3,200.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2011 and 2016, its share of baby girls fell by 55%, from 1 in 4,310 to 1 in 9,680.

Darwin name popularity since 1925

24,354 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Darwin. Half of them were born in 1963 or earlier, so the typical Darwin is about 63 years old in 2026. Darwin peaked in 1935 as the #299 name for boys, and it ranked #961 in 2025.

Darwin births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1958. Measured as a share of all babies, Darwin peaked in 1935.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1967 and 1972, Darwin's share of baby boys fell by 49%, from 1 in 5,830 to 1 in 11,400.
  • Fastest rise: Between 1996 and 2001, it became 1.7× more common, from 1 in 18,000 baby boys to 1 in 10,800.

Kathy name popularity since 1925

334,746 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Kathy. Half of them were born in 1958 or earlier, so the typical Kathy is about 68 years old in 2026. Kathy peaked in 1958 as the #14 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Kathy births per year, 1925–2025

1958 was the biggest year for Kathy both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1937 and 1942, Kathy became 7.7× more common, from 1 in 6,780 baby girls to 1 in 879.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1971 and 1976, its share of baby girls fell by 59%, from 1 in 431 to 1 in 1,060.

Kristie name popularity since 1925

38,405 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Kristie. Half of them were born in 1975 or earlier, so the typical Kristie is about 51 years old in 2026. Kristie peaked in 1974 as the #138 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Kristie births per year, 1925–2025

1974 was the biggest year for Kristie both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1965 and 1970, Kristie became 4.2× more common, from 1 in 4,080 baby girls to 1 in 976.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1991 and 1996, its share of baby girls fell by 72%, from 1 in 2,950 to 1 in 10,400.

Jamal name popularity since 1925

33,501 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Jamal (0.8% girls, 99.2% boys). Half of them were born in 1993 or earlier, so the typical Jamal is about 33 years old in 2026. Jamal peaked in 1994 as the #240 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Jamal births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1993. Measured as a share of all babies, Jamal peaked in 1994.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1983 and 1988, Jamal became 2× more common, from 1 in 3,850 baby boys to 1 in 1,880.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1993 and 1998, its share of baby boys fell by 43%, from 1 in 1,580 to 1 in 2,770.

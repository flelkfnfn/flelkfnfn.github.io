Moses name popularity since 1925

36,407 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Moses. Half of them were born in 1990 or earlier, so the typical Moses is about 36 years old in 2026. Moses peaked in 1880 as the #119 name for boys, and it ranked #515 in 2025.

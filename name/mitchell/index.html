Mitchell name popularity since 1925

159,468 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Mitchell (0.7% girls, 99.3% boys). Half of them were born in 1985 or earlier, so the typical Mitchell is about 41 years old in 2026. Mitchell peaked in 1994 as the #71 name for boys, and it ranked #954 in 2025.

Mitchell births per year, 1925–2025

1994 was the biggest year for Mitchell both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1947 and 1952, Mitchell became 2.1× more common, from 1 in 2,310 baby boys to 1 in 1,110.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2001 and 2006, its share of baby boys fell by 54%, from 1 in 757 to 1 in 1,640.

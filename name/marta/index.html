Marta name popularity since 1925

17,140 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Marta. Half of them were born in 1966 or earlier, so the typical Marta is about 60 years old in 2026. Marta peaked in 1950 as the #384 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Marta births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1957. Measured as a share of all babies, Marta peaked in 1950.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1945 and 1950, Marta became 3× more common, from 1 in 11,600 baby girls to 1 in 3,870.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1982 and 1987, its share of baby girls fell by 35%, from 1 in 8,510 to 1 in 13,100.

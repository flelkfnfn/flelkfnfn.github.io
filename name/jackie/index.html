Jackie name popularity since 1925

167,808 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Jackie (53.4% girls, 46.6% boys). Half of them were born in 1956 or earlier, so the typical Jackie is about 70 years old in 2026. Jackie peaked in 1961 as the #112 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

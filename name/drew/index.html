Drew name popularity since 1925

87,593 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Drew (10.8% girls, 89.2% boys). Half of them were born in 1996 or earlier, so the typical Drew is about 30 years old in 2026. Drew peaked in 1985 as the #126 name for boys, and it ranked #553 in 2025.

Drew births per year, 1925–2025

1985 was the biggest year for Drew both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1980 and 1985, Drew became 3.1× more common, from 1 in 2,480 baby boys to 1 in 812.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2014 and 2019, its share of baby boys fell by 44%, from 1 in 1,810 to 1 in 3,250.

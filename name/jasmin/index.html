Jasmin name popularity since 1925

45,649 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Jasmin (98.7% girls, 1.3% boys). Half of them were born in 1999 or earlier, so the typical Jasmin is about 27 years old in 2026. Jasmin peaked in 2005 as the #181 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Jasmin births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 2006. Measured as a share of all babies, Jasmin peaked in 2005.

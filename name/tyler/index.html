Tyler name popularity since 1925

621,064 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Tyler (2.8% girls, 97.2% boys). Half of them were born in 1997 or earlier, so the typical Tyler is about 29 years old in 2026. Tyler peaked in 1994 as the #5 name for boys, and it ranked #220 in 2025.

Tyler births per year, 1925–2025

1994 was the biggest year for Tyler both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1979 and 1984, Tyler became 4.1× more common, from 1 in 842 baby boys to 1 in 207.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2010 and 2015, its share of baby boys fell by 50%, from 1 in 183 to 1 in 370.

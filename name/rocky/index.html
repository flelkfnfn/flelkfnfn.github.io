Rocky name popularity since 1925

29,003 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Rocky (1.5% girls, 98.5% boys). Half of them were born in 1975 or earlier, so the typical Rocky is about 51 years old in 2026. Rocky peaked in 1957 as the #252 name for boys, and it ranked #568 in 2025.

Rocky births per year, 1925–2025

1957 was the biggest year for Rocky both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1948 and 1953, Rocky became 2.9× more common, from 1 in 8,440 baby boys to 1 in 2,880.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1986 and 1991, its share of baby boys fell by 54%, from 1 in 4,490 to 1 in 9,670.

Quinton name popularity since 1925

30,739 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Quinton. Half of them were born in 1996 or earlier, so the typical Quinton is about 30 years old in 2026. Quinton peaked in 1996 as the #304 name for boys, and it ranked #976 in 2025.

Quinton births per year, 1925–2025

1996 was the biggest year for Quinton both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1978 and 1983, Quinton became 2.7× more common, from 1 in 7,430 baby boys to 1 in 2,780.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2020 and 2025, its share of baby boys fell by 37%, from 1 in 4,590 to 1 in 7,270.

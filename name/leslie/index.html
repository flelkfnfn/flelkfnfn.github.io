Leslie name popularity since 1925

356,778 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Leslie (75.2% girls, 24.8% boys). Half of them were born in 1968 or earlier, so the typical Leslie is about 58 years old in 2026. Leslie peaked in 1969 as the #64 name for girls, and it ranked #712 in 2025.

Leslie births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1957. Measured as a share of all babies, Leslie peaked in 1969.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1940 and 1945, Leslie became 5.9× more common, from 1 in 4,500 baby girls to 1 in 767.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2006 and 2011, its share of baby girls fell by 54%, from 1 in 626 to 1 in 1,370.

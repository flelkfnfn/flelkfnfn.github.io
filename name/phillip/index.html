Phillip name popularity since 1925

300,361 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Phillip. Half of them were born in 1967 or earlier, so the typical Phillip is about 59 years old in 2026. Phillip peaked in 1949 as the #66 name for boys, and it ranked #651 in 2025.

Phillip births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1960. Measured as a share of all babies, Phillip peaked in 1949.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1929 and 1934, Phillip became 1.6× more common.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1989 and 1994, its share of baby boys fell by 41%, from 1 in 424 to 1 in 722.

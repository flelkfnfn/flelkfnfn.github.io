Timmy name popularity since 1925

25,923 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Timmy (0.6% girls, 99.4% boys). Half of them were born in 1964 or earlier, so the typical Timmy is about 62 years old in 2026. Timmy peaked in 1960 as the #192 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Timmy births per year, 1925–2025

1960 was the biggest year for Timmy both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1953 and 1958, Timmy became 6.1× more common, from 1 in 11,100 baby boys to 1 in 1,800.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1968 and 1973, its share of baby boys fell by 56%, from 1 in 1,800 to 1 in 4,080.

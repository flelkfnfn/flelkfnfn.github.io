Matthew name popularity since 1925

1,645,381 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Matthew. Half of them were born in 1988 or earlier, so the typical Matthew is about 38 years old in 2026. Matthew peaked in 1983 as the #3 name for boys, and it ranked #32 in 2025.

Matthew births per year, 1925–2025

1983 was the biggest year for Matthew both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1953 and 1958, Matthew became 2.5× more common, from 1 in 831 baby boys to 1 in 333.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2017 and 2022, its share of baby boys fell by 36%, from 1 in 158 to 1 in 247.

Katelyn name popularity since 1925

134,206 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Katelyn. Half of them were born in 1999 or earlier, so the typical Katelyn is about 27 years old in 2026. Katelyn peaked in 1993 as the #54 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Katelyn births per year, 1925–2025

1993 was the biggest year for Katelyn both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1981 and 1986, Katelyn became 12× more common, from 1 in 11,000 baby girls to 1 in 885.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2013 and 2018, its share of baby girls fell by 70%, from 1 in 817 to 1 in 2,750.

Jewel name popularity since 1925

32,593 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Jewel (89.4% girls, 10.6% boys). Half of them were born in 1955 or earlier, so the typical Jewel is about 71 years old in 2026. Jewel peaked in 1904 as the #198 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

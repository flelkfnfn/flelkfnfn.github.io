Stacey name popularity since 1925

161,588 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Stacey (91.4% girls, 8.6% boys). Half of them were born in 1974 or earlier, so the typical Stacey is about 52 years old in 2026. Stacey peaked in 1971 as the #41 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

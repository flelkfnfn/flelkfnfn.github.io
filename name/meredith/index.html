Meredith name popularity since 1925

79,921 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Meredith (96.1% girls, 3.9% boys). Half of them were born in 1984 or earlier, so the typical Meredith is about 42 years old in 2026. Meredith peaked in 1980 as the #140 name for girls, and it ranked #469 in 2025.

Meredith births per year, 1925–2025

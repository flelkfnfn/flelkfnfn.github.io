Gerald name popularity since 1925

413,703 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Gerald. Half of them were born in 1949 or earlier, so the typical Gerald is about 77 years old in 2026. Gerald peaked in 1939 as the #19 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Gerald births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1942. Measured as a share of all babies, Gerald peaked in 1939.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1928 and 1933, Gerald became 1.5× more common.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1993 and 1998, its share of baby boys fell by 38%, from 1 in 1,760 to 1 in 2,850.

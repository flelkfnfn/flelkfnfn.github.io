Maura name popularity since 1925

19,856 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Maura. Half of them were born in 1985 or earlier, so the typical Maura is about 41 years old in 2026. Maura peaked in 1964 as the #468 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Maura births per year, 1925–2025

1964 was the biggest year for Maura both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1953 and 1958, Maura became 2× more common, from 1 in 13,400 baby girls to 1 in 6,870.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2004 and 2009, its share of baby girls fell by 46%, from 1 in 5,130 to 1 in 9,420.

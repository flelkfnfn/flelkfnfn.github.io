Sallie name popularity since 1925

23,002 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Sallie. Half of them were born in 1945 or earlier, so the typical Sallie is about 81 years old in 2026. Sallie peaked in 1881 as the #63 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

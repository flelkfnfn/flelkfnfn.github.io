Wade name popularity since 1925

70,876 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Wade. Half of them were born in 1974 or earlier, so the typical Wade is about 52 years old in 2026. Wade peaked in 1970 as the #189 name for boys, and it ranked #344 in 2025.

Wade births per year, 1925–2025

1970 was the biggest year for Wade both by count and as a share of all babies.

Savannah name popularity since 1925

189,606 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Savannah. Half of them were born in 2006 or earlier, so the typical Savannah is about 20 years old in 2026. Savannah peaked in 1996 as the #43 name for girls, and it ranked #135 in 2025.

Brandi name popularity since 1925

102,295 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Brandi. Half of them were born in 1983 or earlier, so the typical Brandi is about 43 years old in 2026. Brandi peaked in 1981 as the #50 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Brandi births per year, 1925–2025

1981 was the biggest year for Brandi both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1967 and 1972, Brandi became 13× more common, from 1 in 9,430 baby girls to 1 in 740.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1995 and 2000, its share of baby girls fell by 72%, from 1 in 684 to 1 in 2,440.

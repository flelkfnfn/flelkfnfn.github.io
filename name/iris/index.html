Iris name popularity since 1925

92,642 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Iris (99.3% girls, 0.7% boys). Half of them were born in 1991 or earlier, so the typical Iris is about 35 years old in 2026. Iris peaked in 2025 as the #61 name for girls, and it ranked #61 in 2025.

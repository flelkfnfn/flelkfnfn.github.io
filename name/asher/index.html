Asher name popularity since 1925

112,453 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Asher (1.3% girls, 98.7% boys). Half of them were born in 2018 or earlier, so the typical Asher is about 8 years old in 2026. Asher peaked in 2022 as the #20 name for boys, and it ranked #28 in 2025.

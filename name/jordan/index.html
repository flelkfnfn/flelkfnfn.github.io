Jordan name popularity since 1925

535,611 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Jordan (25.3% girls, 74.7% boys). Half of them were born in 1998 or earlier, so the typical Jordan is about 28 years old in 2026. Jordan peaked in 1991 as the #29 name for boys, and it ranked #131 in 2025.

Jordan births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1990. Measured as a share of all babies, Jordan peaked in 1991.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1976 and 1981, Jordan became 5× more common, from 1 in 3,050 baby boys to 1 in 613.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2020 and 2025, its share of baby boys fell by 36%, from 1 in 403 to 1 in 625.

Vernon name popularity since 1925

111,487 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Vernon (1.0% girls, 99.0% boys). Half of them were born in 1949 or earlier, so the typical Vernon is about 77 years old in 2026. Vernon peaked in 1919 as the #65 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Reginald name popularity since 1925

108,060 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Reginald (0.7% girls, 99.3% boys). Half of them were born in 1968 or earlier, so the typical Reginald is about 58 years old in 2026. Reginald peaked in 1968 as the #117 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Reginald births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1962. Measured as a share of all babies, Reginald peaked in 1968.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1946 and 1951, Reginald became 1.8× more common, from 1 in 2,260 baby boys to 1 in 1,280.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2010 and 2015, its share of baby boys fell by 37%, from 1 in 4,760 to 1 in 7,580.

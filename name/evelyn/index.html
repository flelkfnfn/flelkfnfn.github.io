Evelyn name popularity since 1925

455,674 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Evelyn. Half of them were born in 1965 or earlier, so the typical Evelyn is about 61 years old in 2026. Evelyn peaked in 1919 as the #11 name for girls, and it ranked #8 in 2025.

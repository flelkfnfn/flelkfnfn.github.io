William name popularity since 1925

3,424,247 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named William. Half of them were born in 1957 or earlier, so the typical William is about 69 years old in 2026. William peaked in 1880 as the #2 name for boys, and it ranked #9 in 2025.

Larry name popularity since 1925

804,903 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Larry. Half of them were born in 1952 or earlier, so the typical Larry is about 74 years old in 2026. Larry peaked in 1947 as the #10 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

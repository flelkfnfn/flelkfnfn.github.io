Sylvia name popularity since 1925

195,324 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Sylvia. Half of them were born in 1954 or earlier, so the typical Sylvia is about 72 years old in 2026. Sylvia peaked in 1937 as the #50 name for girls, and it ranked #300 in 2025.

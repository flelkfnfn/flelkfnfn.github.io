Gregg name popularity since 1925

36,467 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Gregg. Half of them were born in 1962 or earlier, so the typical Gregg is about 64 years old in 2026. Gregg peaked in 1962 as the #167 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Gregg births per year, 1925–2025

1962 was the biggest year for Gregg both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1943 and 1948, Gregg became 3.7× more common, from 1 in 12,300 baby boys to 1 in 3,350.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1977 and 1982, its share of baby boys fell by 59%, from 1 in 3,500 to 1 in 8,600.

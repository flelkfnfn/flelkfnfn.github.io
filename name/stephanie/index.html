Stephanie name popularity since 1925

743,341 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Stephanie. Half of them were born in 1983 or earlier, so the typical Stephanie is about 43 years old in 2026. Stephanie peaked in 1984 as the #6 name for girls, and it ranked #557 in 2025.

Stephanie births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1990. Measured as a share of all babies, Stephanie peaked in 1984.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1938 and 1943, Stephanie became 2.7× more common, from 1 in 4,760 baby girls to 1 in 1,740.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2015 and 2020, its share of baby girls fell by 50%, from 1 in 1,150 to 1 in 2,300.

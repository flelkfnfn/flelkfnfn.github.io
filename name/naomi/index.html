Naomi name popularity since 1925

163,777 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Naomi. Half of them were born in 2002 or earlier, so the typical Naomi is about 24 years old in 2026. Naomi peaked in 2025 as the #47 name for girls, and it ranked #47 in 2025.

Naomi births per year, 1925–2025

2025 was the biggest year for Naomi both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1938 and 1943, Naomi's share of baby girls fell by 42%, from 1 in 891 to 1 in 1,540.

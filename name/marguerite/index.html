Marguerite name popularity since 1925

42,669 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Marguerite. Half of them were born in 1944 or earlier, so the typical Marguerite is about 82 years old in 2026. Marguerite peaked in 1897 as the #78 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

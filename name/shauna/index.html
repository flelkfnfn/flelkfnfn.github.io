Shauna name popularity since 1925

28,121 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Shauna. Half of them were born in 1980 or earlier, so the typical Shauna is about 46 years old in 2026. Shauna peaked in 1979 as the #189 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

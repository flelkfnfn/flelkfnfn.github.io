Will name popularity since 1925

26,580 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Will (1.0% girls, 99.0% boys). Half of them were born in 1982 or earlier, so the typical Will is about 44 years old in 2026. Will peaked in 1882 as the #25 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

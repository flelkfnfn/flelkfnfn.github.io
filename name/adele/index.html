Adele name popularity since 1925

27,020 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Adele. Half of them were born in 1955 or earlier, so the typical Adele is about 71 years old in 2026. Adele peaked in 1914 as the #187 name for girls, and it ranked #751 in 2025.

Adele births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year on this chart was 1925. Measured as a share of all babies, Adele peaked earlier, in 1914.

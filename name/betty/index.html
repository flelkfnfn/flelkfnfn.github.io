Betty name popularity since 1925

837,849 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Betty. Half of them were born in 1937 or earlier, so the typical Betty is about 89 years old in 2026. Betty peaked in 1930 as the #2 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Betty births per year, 1925–2025

1930 was the biggest year for Betty both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1973 and 1978, Betty's share of baby girls fell by 49%, from 1 in 1,100 to 1 in 2,150.
  • Fastest rise: Between 2019 and 2024, it became 1.6× more common, from 1 in 10,300 baby girls to 1 in 6,400.

Judah name popularity since 1925

36,364 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Judah (2.3% girls, 97.7% boys). Half of them were born in 2017 or earlier, so the typical Judah is about 9 years old in 2026. Judah peaked in 2023 as the #176 name for boys, and it ranked #178 in 2025.

Adan name popularity since 1925

31,388 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Adan. Half of them were born in 2006 or earlier, so the typical Adan is about 20 years old in 2026. Adan peaked in 2005 as the #287 name for boys, and it ranked #482 in 2025.

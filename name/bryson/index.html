Bryson name popularity since 1925

83,440 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Bryson. Half of them were born in 2013 or earlier, so the typical Bryson is about 13 years old in 2026. Bryson peaked in 2017 as the #86 name for boys, and it ranked #152 in 2025.

Bryson births per year, 1925–2025

2017 was the biggest year for Bryson both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1994 and 1999, Bryson became 2.3× more common, from 1 in 4,140 baby boys to 1 in 1,800.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2017 and 2022, its share of baby boys fell by 39%, from 1 in 396 to 1 in 644.

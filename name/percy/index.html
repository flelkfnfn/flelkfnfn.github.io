Percy name popularity since 1925

21,249 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Percy (1.9% girls, 98.1% boys). Half of them were born in 1950 or earlier, so the typical Percy is about 76 years old in 2026. Percy peaked in 1897 as the #98 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

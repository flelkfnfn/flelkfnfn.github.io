Holly name popularity since 1925

206,221 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Holly (99.4% girls, 0.6% boys). Half of them were born in 1979 or earlier, so the typical Holly is about 47 years old in 2026. Holly peaked in 1983 as the #48 name for girls, and it ranked #416 in 2025.

Holly births per year, 1925–2025

1983 was the biggest year for Holly both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1949 and 1954, Holly became 2.9× more common, from 1 in 2,710 baby girls to 1 in 928.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1996 and 2001, its share of baby girls fell by 42%, from 1 in 719 to 1 in 1,230.

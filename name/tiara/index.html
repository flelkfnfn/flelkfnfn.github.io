Tiara name popularity since 1925

23,929 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Tiara. Half of them were born in 1994 or earlier, so the typical Tiara is about 32 years old in 2026. Tiara peaked in 1995 as the #222 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Tiara births per year, 1925–2025

1995 was the biggest year for Tiara both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1980 and 1985, Tiara became 4.7× more common, from 1 in 13,700 baby girls to 1 in 2,930.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2009 and 2014, its share of baby girls fell by 62%, from 1 in 6,850 to 1 in 17,900.

Leroy name popularity since 1925

153,676 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Leroy (0.6% girls, 99.4% boys). Half of them were born in 1945 or earlier, so the typical Leroy is about 81 years old in 2026. Leroy peaked in 1910 as the #50 name for boys, and it ranked #888 in 2025.

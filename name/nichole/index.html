Nichole name popularity since 1925

63,954 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Nichole. Half of them were born in 1984 or earlier, so the typical Nichole is about 42 years old in 2026. Nichole peaked in 1979 as the #103 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Nichole births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1985. Measured as a share of all babies, Nichole peaked in 1979.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1967 and 1972, Nichole became 14× more common, from 1 in 11,200 baby girls to 1 in 783.

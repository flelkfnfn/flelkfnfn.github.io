Cole name popularity since 1925

154,345 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Cole. Half of them were born in 2005 or earlier, so the typical Cole is about 21 years old in 2026. Cole peaked in 2002 as the #69 name for boys, and it ranked #182 in 2025.

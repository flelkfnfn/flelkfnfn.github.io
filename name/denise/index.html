Denise name popularity since 1925

374,260 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Denise. Half of them were born in 1963 or earlier, so the typical Denise is about 63 years old in 2026. Denise peaked in 1964 as the #25 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Denise births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1961. Measured as a share of all babies, Denise peaked in 1964.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1947 and 1952, Denise became 6.6× more common, from 1 in 1,040 baby girls to 1 in 159.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2020 and 2025, its share of baby girls fell by 60%, from 1 in 5,270 to 1 in 13,100.

Alexander name popularity since 1925

715,937 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Alexander (0.6% girls, 99.4% boys). Half of them were born in 2000 or earlier, so the typical Alexander is about 26 years old in 2026. Alexander peaked in 1993 as the #21 name for boys, and it ranked #30 in 2025.

Joseph name popularity since 1925

2,280,322 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Joseph. Half of them were born in 1970 or earlier, so the typical Joseph is about 56 years old in 2026. Joseph peaked in 1914 as the #5 name for boys, and it ranked #29 in 2025.

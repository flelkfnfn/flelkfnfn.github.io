Marlon name popularity since 1925

31,157 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Marlon (1.0% girls, 99.0% boys). Half of them were born in 1985 or earlier, so the typical Marlon is about 41 years old in 2026. Marlon peaked in 1972 as the #217 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

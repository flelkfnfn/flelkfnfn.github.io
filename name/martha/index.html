Martha name popularity since 1925

393,416 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Martha. Half of them were born in 1948 or earlier, so the typical Martha is about 78 years old in 2026. Martha peaked in 1880 as the #16 name for girls, and it ranked #713 in 2025.

Grayson name popularity since 1925

132,844 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Grayson (3.2% girls, 96.8% boys). Half of them were born in 2017 or earlier, so the typical Grayson is about 9 years old in 2026. Grayson peaked in 2018 as the #32 name for boys, and it ranked #51 in 2025.

Jacob name popularity since 1925

968,394 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Jacob. Half of them were born in 1999 or earlier, so the typical Jacob is about 27 years old in 2026. Jacob peaked in 1998 as the #2 name for boys, and it ranked #43 in 2025.

Jacob births per year, 1925–2025

1998 was the biggest year for Jacob both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1969 and 1974, Jacob became 5.1× more common, from 1 in 2,730 baby boys to 1 in 532.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2017 and 2022, its share of baby boys fell by 39%, from 1 in 139 to 1 in 228.

Andre name popularity since 1925

132,340 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Andre (1.7% girls, 98.3% boys). Half of them were born in 1986 or earlier, so the typical Andre is about 40 years old in 2026. Andre peaked in 1971 as the #129 name for boys, and it ranked #368 in 2025.

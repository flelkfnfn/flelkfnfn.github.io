Kristina name popularity since 1925

137,103 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Kristina. Half of them were born in 1984 or earlier, so the typical Kristina is about 42 years old in 2026. Kristina peaked in 1985 as the #57 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Kristina births per year, 1925–2025

1985 was the biggest year for Kristina both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1960 and 1965, Kristina became 3.7× more common, from 1 in 4,780 baby girls to 1 in 1,290.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2015 and 2020, its share of baby girls fell by 51%, from 1 in 4,700 to 1 in 9,510.

Corrine name popularity since 1925

17,384 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Corrine. Half of them were born in 1961 or earlier, so the typical Corrine is about 65 years old in 2026. Corrine peaked in 1926 as the #352 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Corrine births per year, 1925–2025

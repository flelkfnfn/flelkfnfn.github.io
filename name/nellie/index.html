Nellie name popularity since 1925

57,857 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Nellie. Half of them were born in 1938 or earlier, so the typical Nellie is about 88 years old in 2026. Nellie peaked in 1881 as the #14 name for girls, and it ranked #533 in 2025.

Nellie births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year on this chart was 1925. Measured as a share of all babies, Nellie peaked earlier, in 1881.

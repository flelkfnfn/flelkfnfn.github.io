Harley name popularity since 1925

64,083 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Harley (48.6% girls, 51.4% boys). Half of them were born in 2002 or earlier, so the typical Harley is about 24 years old in 2026. Harley peaked in 1887 as the #164 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

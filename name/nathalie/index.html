Nathalie name popularity since 1925

19,363 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Nathalie. Half of them were born in 2002 or earlier, so the typical Nathalie is about 24 years old in 2026. Nathalie peaked in 2009 as the #489 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

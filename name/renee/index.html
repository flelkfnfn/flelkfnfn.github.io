Renee name popularity since 1925

186,000 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Renee (99.1% girls, 0.9% boys). Half of them were born in 1968 or earlier, so the typical Renee is about 58 years old in 2026. Renee peaked in 1967 as the #62 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Renee births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1964. Measured as a share of all babies, Renee peaked in 1967.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1944 and 1949, Renee became 2.3× more common, from 1 in 2,230 baby girls to 1 in 987.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2002 and 2007, its share of baby girls fell by 45%, from 1 in 2,590 to 1 in 4,680.

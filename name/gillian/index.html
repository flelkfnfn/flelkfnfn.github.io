Gillian name popularity since 1925

16,122 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Gillian. Half of them were born in 1999 or earlier, so the typical Gillian is about 27 years old in 2026. Gillian peaked in 1999 as the #303 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Gillian births per year, 1925–2025

1999 was the biggest year for Gillian both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1994 and 1999, Gillian became 6.4× more common, from 1 in 11,000 baby girls to 1 in 1,740.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2008 and 2013, its share of baby girls fell by 62%, from 1 in 5,580 to 1 in 14,600.

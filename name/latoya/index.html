Latoya name popularity since 1925

45,748 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Latoya. Half of them were born in 1983 or earlier, so the typical Latoya is about 43 years old in 2026. Latoya peaked in 1984 as the #60 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Latoya births per year, 1925–2025

1984 was the biggest year for Latoya both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1971 and 1976, Latoya became 16× more common, from 1 in 11,500 baby girls to 1 in 714.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1989 and 1994, its share of baby girls fell by 84%, from 1 in 1,260 to 1 in 8,120.

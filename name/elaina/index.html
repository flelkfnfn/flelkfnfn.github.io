Elaina name popularity since 1925

32,220 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Elaina. Half of them were born in 2012 or earlier, so the typical Elaina is about 14 years old in 2026. Elaina peaked in 2024 as the #254 name for girls, and it ranked #267 in 2025.

Juanita name popularity since 1925

157,853 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Juanita. Half of them were born in 1946 or earlier, so the typical Juanita is about 80 years old in 2026. Juanita peaked in 1924 as the #48 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

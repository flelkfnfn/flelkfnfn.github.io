Blair name popularity since 1925

37,195 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Blair (62.5% girls, 37.5% boys). Half of them were born in 1993 or earlier, so the typical Blair is about 33 years old in 2026. Blair peaked in 2025 as the #169 name for girls, and it ranked #169 in 2025.

Blair births per year, 1925–2025

2025 was the biggest year for Blair both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1980 and 1985, Blair became 2.9× more common, from 1 in 9,770 baby girls to 1 in 3,350.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1996 and 2001, its share of baby girls fell by 55%, from 1 in 5,250 to 1 in 11,700.

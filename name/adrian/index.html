Adrian name popularity since 1925

283,741 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Adrian (5.5% girls, 94.5% boys). Half of them were born in 2003 or earlier, so the typical Adrian is about 23 years old in 2026. Adrian peaked in 2008 as the #56 name for boys, and it ranked #74 in 2025.

Florence name popularity since 1925

122,253 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Florence. Half of them were born in 1934 or earlier, so the typical Florence is about 92 years old in 2026. Florence peaked in 1893 as the #7 name for girls, and it ranked #391 in 2025.

Florence births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year on this chart was 1925. Measured as a share of all babies, Florence peaked earlier, in 1893.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1973 and 1978, Florence's share of baby girls fell by 48%, from 1 in 5,500 to 1 in 10,500.
  • Fastest rise: Between 2019 and 2024, it became 2.6× more common, from 1 in 5,950 baby girls to 1 in 2,260.

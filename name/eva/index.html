Eva name popularity since 1925

184,806 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Eva. Half of them were born in 1974 or earlier, so the typical Eva is about 52 years old in 2026. Eva peaked in 1886 as the #32 name for girls, and it ranked #134 in 2025.

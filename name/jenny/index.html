Jenny name popularity since 1925

90,076 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Jenny (99.3% girls, 0.7% boys). Half of them were born in 1978 or earlier, so the typical Jenny is about 48 years old in 2026. Jenny peaked in 1977 as the #108 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Jenny births per year, 1925–2025

1977 was the biggest year for Jenny both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1967 and 1972, Jenny became 3.7× more common, from 1 in 2,150 baby girls to 1 in 577.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2007 and 2012, its share of baby girls fell by 45%, from 1 in 3,120 to 1 in 5,720.

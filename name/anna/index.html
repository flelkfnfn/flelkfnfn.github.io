Anna name popularity since 1925

580,846 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Anna. Half of them were born in 1981 or earlier, so the typical Anna is about 45 years old in 2026. Anna peaked in 1885 as the #2 name for girls, and it ranked #107 in 2025.

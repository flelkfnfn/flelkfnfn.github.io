Genevieve name popularity since 1925

78,117 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Genevieve. Half of them were born in 1990 or earlier, so the typical Genevieve is about 36 years old in 2026. Genevieve peaked in 1915 as the #76 name for girls, and it ranked #148 in 2025.

Genevieve births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year on this chart was 1925. Measured as a share of all babies, Genevieve peaked earlier, in 1915.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1942 and 1947, Genevieve's share of baby girls fell by 49%, from 1 in 2,220 to 1 in 4,310.
  • Fastest rise: Between 1967 and 1972, it became 1.8× more common, from 1 in 8,260 baby girls to 1 in 4,670.

Johnnie name popularity since 1925

114,874 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Johnnie (31.4% girls, 68.6% boys). Half of them were born in 1947 or earlier, so the typical Johnnie is about 79 years old in 2026. Johnnie peaked in 1910 as the #74 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

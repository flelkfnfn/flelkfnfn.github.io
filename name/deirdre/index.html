Deirdre name popularity since 1925

16,831 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Deirdre. Half of them were born in 1967 or earlier, so the typical Deirdre is about 59 years old in 2026. Deirdre peaked in 1961 as the #333 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Deirdre births per year, 1925–2025

1961 was the biggest year for Deirdre both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1956 and 1961, Deirdre became 2.9× more common, from 1 in 7,300 baby girls to 1 in 2,530.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1969 and 1974, its share of baby girls fell by 45%, from 1 in 2,670 to 1 in 4,840.

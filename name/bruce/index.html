Bruce name popularity since 1925

374,884 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Bruce. Half of them were born in 1955 or earlier, so the typical Bruce is about 71 years old in 2026. Bruce peaked in 1951 as the #26 name for boys, and it ranked #536 in 2025.

Bruce births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1956. Measured as a share of all babies, Bruce peaked in 1951.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1944 and 1949, Bruce became 1.8× more common, from 1 in 259 baby boys to 1 in 145.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1968 and 1973, its share of baby boys fell by 52%, from 1 in 387 to 1 in 814.

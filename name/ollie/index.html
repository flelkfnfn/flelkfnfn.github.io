Ollie name popularity since 1925

28,785 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Ollie (61.1% girls, 38.9% boys). Half of them were born in 1942 or earlier, so the typical Ollie is about 84 years old in 2026. Ollie peaked in 1887 as the #97 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

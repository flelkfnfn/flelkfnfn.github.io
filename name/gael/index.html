Gael name popularity since 1925

49,742 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Gael (1.4% girls, 98.6% boys). Half of them were born in 2018 or earlier, so the typical Gael is about 8 years old in 2026. Gael peaked in 2025 as the #86 name for boys, and it ranked #86 in 2025.

Gael births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 2024. Measured as a share of all babies, Gael peaked in 2025.

  • Fastest rise: Between 2002 and 2007, Gael became 4.4× more common, from 1 in 7,890 baby boys to 1 in 1,780.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2005 and 2010, its share of baby boys fell by 35%, from 1 in 1,810 to 1 in 2,770.

Justice name popularity since 1925

38,520 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Justice (48.7% girls, 51.3% boys). Half of them were born in 2006 or earlier, so the typical Justice is about 20 years old in 2026. Justice peaked in 1996 as the #320 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Justice births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 2002. Measured as a share of all babies, Justice peaked in 1996.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1991 and 1996, Justice became 8.6× more common, from 1 in 19,600 baby boys to 1 in 2,290.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2019 and 2024, its share of baby boys fell by 54%, from 1 in 3,900 to 1 in 8,490.

Wilson name popularity since 1925

37,297 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Wilson. Half of them were born in 1980 or earlier, so the typical Wilson is about 46 years old in 2026. Wilson peaked in 1918 as the #122 name for boys, and it ranked #673 in 2025.

Margaret name popularity since 1925

824,439 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Margaret. Half of them were born in 1948 or earlier, so the typical Margaret is about 78 years old in 2026. Margaret peaked in 1916 as the #4 name for girls, and it ranked #112 in 2025.

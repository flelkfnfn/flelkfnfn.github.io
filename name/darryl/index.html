Darryl name popularity since 1925

93,339 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Darryl (1.2% girls, 98.8% boys). Half of them were born in 1964 or earlier, so the typical Darryl is about 62 years old in 2026. Darryl peaked in 1965 as the #68 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Darryl births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1961. Measured as a share of all babies, Darryl peaked in 1965.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1956 and 1961, Darryl became 3.4× more common, from 1 in 1,430 baby boys to 1 in 414.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1965 and 1970, its share of baby boys fell by 72%, from 1 in 378 to 1 in 1,360.

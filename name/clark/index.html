Clark name popularity since 1925

45,683 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Clark (0.7% girls, 99.3% boys). Half of them were born in 1969 or earlier, so the typical Clark is about 57 years old in 2026. Clark peaked in 1881 as the #176 name for boys, and it ranked #377 in 2025.

Clark births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year on this chart was 1961. Measured as a share of all babies, Clark peaked earlier, in 1881.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1969 and 1974, Clark's share of baby boys fell by 45%, from 1 in 4,350 to 1 in 7,980.
  • Fastest rise: Between 2010 and 2015, it became 2.6× more common, from 1 in 5,730 baby boys to 1 in 2,210.

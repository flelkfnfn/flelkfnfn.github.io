Ladonna name popularity since 1925

20,860 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Ladonna. Half of them were born in 1964 or earlier, so the typical Ladonna is about 62 years old in 2026. Ladonna peaked in 1968 as the #378 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Ladonna births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1964. Measured as a share of all babies, Ladonna peaked in 1968.

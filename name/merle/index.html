Merle name popularity since 1925

28,667 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Merle (27.6% girls, 72.4% boys). Half of them were born in 1940 or earlier, so the typical Merle is about 86 years old in 2026. Merle peaked in 1918 as the #170 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

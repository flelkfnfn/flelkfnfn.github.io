Hattie name popularity since 1925

45,294 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Hattie. Half of them were born in 1945 or earlier, so the typical Hattie is about 81 years old in 2026. Hattie peaked in 1880 as the #27 name for girls, and it ranked #334 in 2025.

Hattie births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year on this chart was 1925. Measured as a share of all babies, Hattie peaked earlier, in 1880.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1949 and 1954, Hattie's share of baby girls fell by 47%, from 1 in 2,370 to 1 in 4,450.
  • Fastest rise: Between 2009 and 2014, it became 4.2× more common, from 1 in 14,900 baby girls to 1 in 3,560.

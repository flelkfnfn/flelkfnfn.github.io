Kirby name popularity since 1925

17,529 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Kirby (22.5% girls, 77.5% boys). Half of them were born in 1972 or earlier, so the typical Kirby is about 54 years old in 2026. Kirby peaked in 1957 as the #411 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Kirby births per year, 1925–2025

1957 was the biggest year for Kirby both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1945 and 1950, Kirby became 1.6× more common, from 1 in 12,700 baby boys to 1 in 7,920.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1971 and 1976, its share of baby boys fell by 54%, from 1 in 6,100 to 1 in 13,300.

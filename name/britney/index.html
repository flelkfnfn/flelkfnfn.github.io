Britney name popularity since 1925

35,668 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Britney. Half of them were born in 1994 or earlier, so the typical Britney is about 32 years old in 2026. Britney peaked in 1989 as the #118 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Britney births per year, 1925–2025

1989 was the biggest year for Britney both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1981 and 1986, Britney became 6.3× more common, from 1 in 7,690 baby girls to 1 in 1,220.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2000 and 2005, its share of baby girls fell by 71%, from 1 in 755 to 1 in 2,580.

Myron name popularity since 1925

38,042 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Myron (1.1% girls, 98.9% boys). Half of them were born in 1956 or earlier, so the typical Myron is about 70 years old in 2026. Myron peaked in 1931 as the #192 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

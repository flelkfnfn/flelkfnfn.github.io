Chris name popularity since 1925

167,169 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Chris (13.7% girls, 86.3% boys). Half of them were born in 1964 or earlier, so the typical Chris is about 62 years old in 2026. Chris peaked in 1961 as the #57 name for boys, and it ranked #646 in 2025.

Chris births per year, 1925–2025

1961 was the biggest year for Chris both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1954 and 1959, Chris became 3× more common, from 1 in 1,130 baby boys to 1 in 376.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1971 and 1976, its share of baby boys fell by 58%, from 1 in 412 to 1 in 982.

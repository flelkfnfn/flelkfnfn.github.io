Collin name popularity since 1925

81,727 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Collin (0.6% girls, 99.4% boys). Half of them were born in 2002 or earlier, so the typical Collin is about 24 years old in 2026. Collin peaked in 1996 as the #115 name for boys, and it ranked #493 in 2025.

Collin births per year, 1925–2025

1996 was the biggest year for Collin both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1988 and 1993, Collin became 2.6× more common, from 1 in 2,250 baby boys to 1 in 871.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2015 and 2020, its share of baby boys fell by 49%, from 1 in 1,390 to 1 in 2,710.

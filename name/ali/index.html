Ali name popularity since 1925

44,878 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Ali (23.4% girls, 76.6% boys). Half of them were born in 2006 or earlier, so the typical Ali is about 20 years old in 2026. Ali peaked in 2025 as the #306 name for boys, and it ranked #306 in 2025.

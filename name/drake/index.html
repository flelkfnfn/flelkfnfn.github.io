Drake name popularity since 1925

36,864 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Drake. Half of them were born in 2006 or earlier, so the typical Drake is about 20 years old in 2026. Drake peaked in 2010 as the #196 name for boys, and it ranked #827 in 2025.

Drake births per year, 1925–2025

2010 was the biggest year for Drake both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1987 and 1992, Drake became 3.6× more common, from 1 in 13,500 baby boys to 1 in 3,800.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2010 and 2015, its share of baby boys fell by 58%, from 1 in 1,020 to 1 in 2,450.

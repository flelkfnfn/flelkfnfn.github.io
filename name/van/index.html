Van name popularity since 1925

27,593 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Van (5.9% girls, 94.1% boys). Half of them were born in 1962 or earlier, so the typical Van is about 64 years old in 2026. Van peaked in 1891 as the #234 name for boys, and it ranked #972 in 2025.

Van births per year, 1925–2025

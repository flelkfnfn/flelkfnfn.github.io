Rosalyn name popularity since 1925

21,676 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Rosalyn. Half of them were born in 1965 or earlier, so the typical Rosalyn is about 61 years old in 2026. Rosalyn peaked in 1952 as the #413 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Rosalyn births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1955. Measured as a share of all babies, Rosalyn peaked in 1952.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1985 and 1990, Rosalyn's share of baby girls fell by 42%, from 1 in 10,300 to 1 in 17,700.
  • Fastest rise: Between 2012 and 2017, it became 1.8× more common, from 1 in 10,700 baby girls to 1 in 5,790.

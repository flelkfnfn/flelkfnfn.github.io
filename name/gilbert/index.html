Gilbert name popularity since 1925

107,188 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Gilbert. Half of them were born in 1954 or earlier, so the typical Gilbert is about 72 years old in 2026. Gilbert peaked in 1930 as the #90 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

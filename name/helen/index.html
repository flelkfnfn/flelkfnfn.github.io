Helen name popularity since 1925

494,025 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Helen. Half of them were born in 1938 or earlier, so the typical Helen is about 88 years old in 2026. Helen peaked in 1917 as the #2 name for girls, and it ranked #462 in 2025.

Marie name popularity since 1925

317,810 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Marie. Half of them were born in 1947 or earlier, so the typical Marie is about 79 years old in 2026. Marie peaked in 1899 as the #8 name for girls, and it ranked #618 in 2025.

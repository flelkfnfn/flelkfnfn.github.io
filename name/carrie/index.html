Carrie name popularity since 1925

198,894 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Carrie. Half of them were born in 1973 or earlier, so the typical Carrie is about 53 years old in 2026. Carrie peaked in 1880 as the #20 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Carrie births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year on this chart was 1977. Measured as a share of all babies, Carrie peaked earlier, in 1880.

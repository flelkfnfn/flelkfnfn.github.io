Floyd name popularity since 1925

106,254 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Floyd (0.8% girls, 99.2% boys). Half of them were born in 1945 or earlier, so the typical Floyd is about 81 years old in 2026. Floyd peaked in 1897 as the #48 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Karin name popularity since 1925

28,605 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Karin (99.3% girls, 0.7% boys). Half of them were born in 1966 or earlier, so the typical Karin is about 60 years old in 2026. Karin peaked in 1970 as the #278 name for girls, and fewer than 5 girls got it in 2025.

Karin births per year, 1925–2025

1970 was the biggest year for Karin both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1938 and 1943, Karin became 1.8× more common, from 1 in 10,900 baby girls to 1 in 5,910.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1978 and 1983, its share of baby girls fell by 48%, from 1 in 3,650 to 1 in 6,960.

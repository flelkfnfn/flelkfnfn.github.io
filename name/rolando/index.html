Rolando name popularity since 1925

24,460 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Rolando. Half of them were born in 1988 or earlier, so the typical Rolando is about 38 years old in 2026. Rolando peaked in 1975 as the #375 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Rolando births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1994. Measured as a share of all babies, Rolando peaked in 1975.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1969 and 1974, Rolando became 1.6× more common, from 1 in 7,040 baby boys to 1 in 4,370.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2007 and 2012, its share of baby boys fell by 43%, from 1 in 4,590 to 1 in 8,040.

Dana name popularity since 1925

244,291 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Dana (78.8% girls, 21.2% boys). Half of them were born in 1971 or earlier, so the typical Dana is about 55 years old in 2026. Dana peaked in 1971 as the #44 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Dana births per year, 1925–2025

1971 was the biggest year for Dana both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1939 and 1944, Dana became 2.5× more common, from 1 in 6,490 baby girls to 1 in 2,640.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2010 and 2015, its share of baby girls fell by 50%, from 1 in 2,720 to 1 in 5,480.

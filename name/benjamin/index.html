Benjamin name popularity since 1925

786,425 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Benjamin. Half of them were born in 1995 or earlier, so the typical Benjamin is about 31 years old in 2026. Benjamin peaked in 1981 as the #32 name for boys, and it ranked #11 in 2025.

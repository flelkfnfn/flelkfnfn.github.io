Mattie name popularity since 1925

61,537 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Mattie (99.4% girls, 0.6% boys). Half of them were born in 1941 or earlier, so the typical Mattie is about 85 years old in 2026. Mattie peaked in 1882 as the #25 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Angel name popularity since 1925

365,101 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Angel (27.4% girls, 72.6% boys). Half of them were born in 2004 or earlier, so the typical Angel is about 22 years old in 2026. Angel peaked in 2006 as the #31 name for boys, and it ranked #67 in 2025.

Angel births per year, 1925–2025

2006 was the biggest year for Angel both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1949 and 1954, Angel became 2.1× more common, from 1 in 9,590 baby boys to 1 in 4,620.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2009 and 2014, its share of baby boys fell by 34%, from 1 in 201 to 1 in 304.

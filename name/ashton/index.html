Ashton name popularity since 1925

107,520 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Ashton (18.3% girls, 81.7% boys). Half of them were born in 2008 or earlier, so the typical Ashton is about 18 years old in 2026. Ashton peaked in 2004 as the #76 name for boys, and it ranked #197 in 2025.

Ashton births per year, 1925–2025

2004 was the biggest year for Ashton both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1999 and 2004, Ashton became 4.9× more common, from 1 in 1,800 baby boys to 1 in 365.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2004 and 2009, its share of baby boys fell by 34%, from 1 in 365 to 1 in 556.

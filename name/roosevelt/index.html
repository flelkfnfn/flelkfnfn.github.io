Roosevelt name popularity since 1925

31,456 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Roosevelt (1.0% girls, 99.0% boys). Half of them were born in 1949 or earlier, so the typical Roosevelt is about 77 years old in 2026. Roosevelt peaked in 1905 as the #91 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

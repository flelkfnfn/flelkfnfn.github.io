Angelina name popularity since 1925

111,926 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Angelina. Half of them were born in 2003 or earlier, so the typical Angelina is about 23 years old in 2026. Angelina peaked in 2005 as the #43 name for girls, and it ranked #347 in 2025.

Angelina births per year, 1925–2025

2005 was the biggest year for Angelina both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1930 and 1935, Angelina's share of baby girls fell by 52%.

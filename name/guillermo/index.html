Guillermo name popularity since 1925

31,488 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Guillermo. Half of them were born in 1993 or earlier, so the typical Guillermo is about 33 years old in 2026. Guillermo peaked in 1996 as the #352 name for boys, and it ranked #902 in 2025.

Guillermo births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1998. Measured as a share of all babies, Guillermo peaked in 1996.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1963 and 1968, Guillermo became 1.8× more common, from 1 in 10,500 baby boys to 1 in 5,950.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2008 and 2013, its share of baby boys fell by 36%, from 1 in 3,500 to 1 in 5,440.

Julian name popularity since 1925

257,952 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Julian (1.2% girls, 98.8% boys). Half of them were born in 2009 or earlier, so the typical Julian is about 17 years old in 2026. Julian peaked in 2019 as the #31 name for boys, and it ranked #25 in 2025.

Macy name popularity since 1925

32,599 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Macy (98.6% girls, 1.4% boys). Half of them were born in 2005 or earlier, so the typical Macy is about 21 years old in 2026. Macy peaked in 2003 as the #219 name for girls, and it ranked #720 in 2025.

Macy births per year, 1925–2025

2003 was the biggest year for Macy both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1989 and 1994, Macy became 4.5× more common, from 1 in 12,000 baby girls to 1 in 2,680.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2011 and 2016, its share of baby girls fell by 41%, from 1 in 1,650 to 1 in 2,800.

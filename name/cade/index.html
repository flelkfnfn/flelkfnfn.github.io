Cade name popularity since 1925

34,918 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Cade. Half of them were born in 2007 or earlier, so the typical Cade is about 19 years old in 2026. Cade peaked in 2001 as the #201 name for boys, and it ranked #248 in 2025.

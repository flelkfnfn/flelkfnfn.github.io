Deloris name popularity since 1925

31,692 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Deloris. Half of them were born in 1944 or earlier, so the typical Deloris is about 82 years old in 2026. Deloris peaked in 1933 as the #187 name for girls, and fewer than 5 girls got it in 2025.

The biggest year by count was 1947. Measured as a share of all babies, Deloris peaked in 1933.

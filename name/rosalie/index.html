Rosalie name popularity since 1925

69,466 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Rosalie. Half of them were born in 1953 or earlier, so the typical Rosalie is about 73 years old in 2026. Rosalie peaked in 1938 as the #66 name for girls, and it ranked #171 in 2025.

Rosalie births per year, 1925–2025

1938 was the biggest year for Rosalie both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1938 and 1943, Rosalie's share of baby girls fell by 70%, from 1 in 294 to 1 in 979.
  • Fastest rise: Between 2007 and 2012, it became 6.4× more common, from 1 in 14,600 baby girls to 1 in 2,280.

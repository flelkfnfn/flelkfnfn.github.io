Michaela name popularity since 1925

53,692 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Michaela. Half of them were born in 1998 or earlier, so the typical Michaela is about 28 years old in 2026. Michaela peaked in 1997 as the #100 name for girls, and it ranked #843 in 2025.

Michaela births per year, 1925–2025

1997 was the biggest year for Michaela both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1991 and 1996, Michaela became 4.3× more common, from 1 in 2,390 baby girls to 1 in 552.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1998 and 2003, its share of baby girls fell by 60%, from 1 in 529 to 1 in 1,320.

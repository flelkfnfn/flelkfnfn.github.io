Geraldine name popularity since 1925

166,499 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Geraldine. Half of them were born in 1941 or earlier, so the typical Geraldine is about 85 years old in 2026. Geraldine peaked in 1931 as the #38 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Geraldine births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1930. Measured as a share of all babies, Geraldine peaked in 1931.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1967 and 1972, Geraldine's share of baby girls fell by 68%, from 1 in 2,230 to 1 in 6,980.
  • Fastest rise: Between 1983 and 1988, it became 3.2× more common, from 1 in 16,200 baby girls to 1 in 5,020.

Abigail name popularity since 1925

413,119 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Abigail. Half of them were born in 2006 or earlier, so the typical Abigail is about 20 years old in 2026. Abigail peaked in 2003 as the #6 name for girls, and it ranked #41 in 2025.

Abigail births per year, 1925–2025

2003 was the biggest year for Abigail both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1992 and 1997, Abigail became 2.6× more common, from 1 in 462 baby girls to 1 in 181.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2018 and 2023, its share of baby girls fell by 39%, from 1 in 172 to 1 in 282.

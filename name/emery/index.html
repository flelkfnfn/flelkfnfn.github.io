Emery name popularity since 1925

59,535 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Emery (73.6% girls, 26.4% boys). Half of them were born in 2017 or earlier, so the typical Emery is about 9 years old in 2026. Emery peaked in 2025 as the #74 name for girls, and it ranked #74 in 2025.

Janelle name popularity since 1925

56,777 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Janelle. Half of them were born in 1986 or earlier, so the typical Janelle is about 40 years old in 2026. Janelle peaked in 1978 as the #186 name for girls, and it ranked #801 in 2025.

Janelle births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1979. Measured as a share of all babies, Janelle peaked in 1978.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1973 and 1978, Janelle became 2.2× more common, from 1 in 2,390 baby girls to 1 in 1,110.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1992 and 1997, its share of baby girls fell by 35%, from 1 in 1,720 to 1 in 2,660.

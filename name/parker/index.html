Parker name popularity since 1925

162,545 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Parker (20.2% girls, 79.8% boys). Half of them were born in 2013 or earlier, so the typical Parker is about 13 years old in 2026. Parker peaked in 2013 as the #74 name for boys, and it ranked #102 in 2025.

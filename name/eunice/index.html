Eunice name popularity since 1925

44,776 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Eunice (98.5% girls, 1.5% boys). Half of them were born in 1941 or earlier, so the typical Eunice is about 85 years old in 2026. Eunice peaked in 1908 as the #106 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Wallace name popularity since 1925

59,870 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Wallace (0.7% girls, 99.3% boys). Half of them were born in 1945 or earlier, so the typical Wallace is about 81 years old in 2026. Wallace peaked in 1923 as the #69 name for boys, and it ranked #964 in 2025.

Mandy name popularity since 1925

39,847 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Mandy. Half of them were born in 1979 or earlier, so the typical Mandy is about 47 years old in 2026. Mandy peaked in 1978 as the #96 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Mandy births per year, 1925–2025

1978 was the biggest year for Mandy both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1970 and 1975, Mandy became 14× more common, from 1 in 7,500 baby girls to 1 in 538.

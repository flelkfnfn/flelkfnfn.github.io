Allie name popularity since 1925

42,650 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Allie (96.3% girls, 3.7% boys). Half of them were born in 2006 or earlier, so the typical Allie is about 20 years old in 2026. Allie peaked in 1889 as the #145 name for girls, and it ranked #569 in 2025.

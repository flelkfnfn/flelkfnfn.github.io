Lucy name popularity since 1925

170,243 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Lucy. Half of them were born in 2004 or earlier, so the typical Lucy is about 22 years old in 2026. Lucy peaked in 1881 as the #44 name for girls, and it ranked #25 in 2025.

Lucy births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year on this chart was 2025. Measured as a share of all babies, Lucy peaked earlier, in 1881.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1966 and 1971, Lucy's share of baby girls fell by 43%, from 1 in 2,240 to 1 in 3,910.
  • Fastest rise: Between 2001 and 2006, it became 2.1× more common, from 1 in 1,730 baby girls to 1 in 842.

Carlton name popularity since 1925

43,164 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Carlton (0.6% girls, 99.4% boys). Half of them were born in 1960 or earlier, so the typical Carlton is about 66 years old in 2026. Carlton peaked in 1937 as the #217 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

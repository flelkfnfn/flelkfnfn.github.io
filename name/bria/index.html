Bria name popularity since 1925

16,870 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Bria. Half of them were born in 2002 or earlier, so the typical Bria is about 24 years old in 2026. Bria peaked in 1993 as the #175 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Bria births per year, 1925–2025

1993 was the biggest year for Bria both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1990 and 1995, Bria became 7.4× more common, from 1 in 16,800 baby girls to 1 in 2,270.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1993 and 1998, its share of baby girls fell by 71%, from 1 in 1,070 to 1 in 3,720.

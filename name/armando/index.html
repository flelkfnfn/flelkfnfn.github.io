Armando name popularity since 1925

74,978 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Armando. Half of them were born in 1989 or earlier, so the typical Armando is about 37 years old in 2026. Armando peaked in 1997 as the #205 name for boys, and it ranked #556 in 2025.

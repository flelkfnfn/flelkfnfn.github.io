Glen name popularity since 1925

108,122 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Glen (0.9% girls, 99.1% boys). Half of them were born in 1956 or earlier, so the typical Glen is about 70 years old in 2026. Glen peaked in 1931 as the #112 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

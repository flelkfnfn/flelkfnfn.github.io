Logan name popularity since 1925

418,339 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Logan (7.6% girls, 92.4% boys). Half of them were born in 2008 or earlier, so the typical Logan is about 18 years old in 2026. Logan peaked in 2017 as the #5 name for boys, and it ranked #53 in 2025.

Logan births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 2007. Measured as a share of all babies, Logan peaked in 2017.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1976 and 1981, Logan became 5.4× more common, from 1 in 13,900 baby boys to 1 in 2,580.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2018 and 2023, its share of baby boys fell by 43%, from 1 in 146 to 1 in 255.

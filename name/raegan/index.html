Raegan name popularity since 1925

25,492 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Raegan (98.9% girls, 1.1% boys). Half of them were born in 2013 or earlier, so the typical Raegan is about 13 years old in 2026. Raegan peaked in 2019 as the #219 name for girls, and it ranked #506 in 2025.

Raegan births per year, 1925–2025

2019 was the biggest year for Raegan both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1994 and 1999, Raegan became 2.6× more common, from 1 in 14,500 baby girls to 1 in 5,600.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2019 and 2024, its share of baby girls fell by 50%, from 1 in 1,190 to 1 in 2,370.

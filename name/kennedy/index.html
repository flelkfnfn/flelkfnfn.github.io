Kennedy name popularity since 1925

101,019 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Kennedy (92.7% girls, 7.3% boys). Half of them were born in 2012 or earlier, so the typical Kennedy is about 14 years old in 2026. Kennedy peaked in 2015 as the #58 name for girls, and it ranked #85 in 2025.

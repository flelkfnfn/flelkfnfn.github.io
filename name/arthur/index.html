Arthur name popularity since 1925

384,679 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Arthur (0.6% girls, 99.4% boys). Half of them were born in 1950 or earlier, so the typical Arthur is about 76 years old in 2026. Arthur peaked in 1881 as the #14 name for boys, and it ranked #87 in 2025.

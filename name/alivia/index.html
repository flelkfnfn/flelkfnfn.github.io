Alivia name popularity since 1925

34,801 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Alivia. Half of them were born in 2012 or earlier, so the typical Alivia is about 14 years old in 2026. Alivia peaked in 2009 as the #178 name for girls, and it ranked #484 in 2025.

Alivia births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 2008. Measured as a share of all babies, Alivia peaked in 2009.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1996 and 2001, Alivia became 3× more common, from 1 in 9,280 baby girls to 1 in 3,080.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2020 and 2025, its share of baby girls fell by 47%, from 1 in 1,370 to 1 in 2,600.

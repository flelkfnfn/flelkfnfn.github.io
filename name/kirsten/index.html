Kirsten name popularity since 1925

53,393 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Kirsten. Half of them were born in 1990 or earlier, so the typical Kirsten is about 36 years old in 2026. Kirsten peaked in 1991 as the #154 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Kirsten births per year, 1925–2025

1991 was the biggest year for Kirsten both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1958 and 1963, Kirsten became 4.5× more common, from 1 in 14,500 baby girls to 1 in 3,220.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2005 and 2010, its share of baby girls fell by 67%, from 1 in 1,840 to 1 in 5,570.

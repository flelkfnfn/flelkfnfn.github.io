Clara name popularity since 1925

163,268 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Clara. Half of them were born in 1955 or earlier, so the typical Clara is about 71 years old in 2026. Clara peaked in 1885 as the #7 name for girls, and it ranked #63 in 2025.

Clara births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year on this chart was 1925. Measured as a share of all babies, Clara peaked earlier, in 1885.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1949 and 1954, Clara's share of baby girls fell by 39%, from 1 in 1,000 to 1 in 1,640.
  • Fastest rise: Between 2009 and 2014, it became 1.8× more common, from 1 in 1,110 baby girls to 1 in 625.

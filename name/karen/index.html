Karen name popularity since 1925

990,004 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Karen. Half of them were born in 1959 or earlier, so the typical Karen is about 67 years old in 2026. Karen peaked in 1956 as the #7 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

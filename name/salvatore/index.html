Salvatore name popularity since 1925

45,917 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Salvatore. Half of them were born in 1959 or earlier, so the typical Salvatore is about 67 years old in 2026. Salvatore peaked in 1914 as the #148 name for boys, and it ranked #945 in 2025.

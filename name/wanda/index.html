Wanda name popularity since 1925

254,049 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Wanda. Half of them were born in 1951 or earlier, so the typical Wanda is about 75 years old in 2026. Wanda peaked in 1933 as the #48 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Julie name popularity since 1925

509,212 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Julie. Half of them were born in 1968 or earlier, so the typical Julie is about 58 years old in 2026. Julie peaked in 1969 as the #12 name for girls, and it ranked #873 in 2025.

Julie births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1958. Measured as a share of all babies, Julie peaked in 1969.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1953 and 1958, Julie became 3× more common, from 1 in 332 baby girls to 1 in 110.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1981 and 1986, its share of baby girls fell by 43%, from 1 in 195 to 1 in 342.

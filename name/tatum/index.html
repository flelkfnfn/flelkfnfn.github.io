Tatum name popularity since 1925

41,874 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Tatum (68.4% girls, 31.6% boys). Half of them were born in 2015 or earlier, so the typical Tatum is about 11 years old in 2026. Tatum peaked in 2025 as the #189 name for girls, and it ranked #189 in 2025.

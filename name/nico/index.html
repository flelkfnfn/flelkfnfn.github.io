Nico name popularity since 1925

22,322 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Nico (4.6% girls, 95.4% boys). Half of them were born in 2017 or earlier, so the typical Nico is about 9 years old in 2026. Nico peaked in 2025 as the #174 name for boys, and it ranked #174 in 2025.

Nico births per year, 1925–2025

2025 was the biggest year for Nico both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1992 and 1997, Nico's share of baby boys fell by 41%, from 1 in 7,060 to 1 in 11,900.

Pamela name popularity since 1925

596,091 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Pamela. Half of them were born in 1959 or earlier, so the typical Pamela is about 67 years old in 2026. Pamela peaked in 1954 as the #12 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Pamela births per year, 1925–2025

1954 was the biggest year for Pamela both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1939 and 1944, Pamela became 15× more common, from 1 in 3,250 baby girls to 1 in 214.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1972 and 1977, its share of baby girls fell by 62%, from 1 in 204 to 1 in 542.

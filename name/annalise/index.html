Annalise name popularity since 1925

17,165 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Annalise. Half of them were born in 2014 or earlier, so the typical Annalise is about 12 years old in 2026. Annalise peaked in 2021 as the #385 name for girls, and it ranked #409 in 2025.

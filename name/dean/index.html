Dean name popularity since 1925

185,770 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Dean (1.2% girls, 98.8% boys). Half of them were born in 1964 or earlier, so the typical Dean is about 62 years old in 2026. Dean peaked in 1961 as the #84 name for boys, and it ranked #125 in 2025.

Dean births per year, 1925–2025

1961 was the biggest year for Dean both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1969 and 1974, Dean's share of baby boys fell by 60%, from 1 in 519 to 1 in 1,290.
  • Fastest rise: Between 2006 and 2011, it became 1.7× more common, from 1 in 2,640 baby boys to 1 in 1,540.

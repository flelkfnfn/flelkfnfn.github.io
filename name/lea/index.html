Lea name popularity since 1925

32,836 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Lea (98.7% girls, 1.3% boys). Half of them were born in 1984 or earlier, so the typical Lea is about 42 years old in 2026. Lea peaked in 1970 as the #397 name for girls, and it ranked #848 in 2025.

Mable name popularity since 1925

24,032 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Mable. Half of them were born in 1936 or earlier, so the typical Mable is about 90 years old in 2026. Mable peaked in 1905 as the #112 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

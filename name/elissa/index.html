Elissa name popularity since 1925

17,955 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Elissa. Half of them were born in 1988 or earlier, so the typical Elissa is about 38 years old in 2026. Elissa peaked in 1979 as the #496 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Elissa births per year, 1925–2025

1979 was the biggest year for Elissa both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1965 and 1970, Elissa became 2.5× more common, from 1 in 17,100 baby girls to 1 in 6,780.

Emerson name popularity since 1925

63,900 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Emerson (53.3% girls, 46.7% boys). Half of them were born in 2015 or earlier, so the typical Emerson is about 11 years old in 2026. Emerson peaked in 2025 as the #122 name for girls, and it ranked #122 in 2025.

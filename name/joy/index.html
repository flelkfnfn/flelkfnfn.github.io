Joy name popularity since 1925

138,019 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Joy (98.5% girls, 1.5% boys). Half of them were born in 1963 or earlier, so the typical Joy is about 63 years old in 2026. Joy peaked in 1974 as the #109 name for girls, and it ranked #419 in 2025.

Joy births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1957. Measured as a share of all babies, Joy peaked in 1974.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1969 and 1974, Joy became 1.9× more common, from 1 in 998 baby girls to 1 in 532.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1978 and 1983, its share of baby girls fell by 44%, from 1 in 787 to 1 in 1,400.

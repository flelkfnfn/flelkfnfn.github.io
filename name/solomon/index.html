Solomon name popularity since 1925

32,299 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Solomon. Half of them were born in 2002 or earlier, so the typical Solomon is about 24 years old in 2026. Solomon peaked in 1911 as the #221 name for boys, and it ranked #422 in 2025.

Danielle name popularity since 1925

373,698 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Danielle. Half of them were born in 1988 or earlier, so the typical Danielle is about 38 years old in 2026. Danielle peaked in 1987 as the #14 name for girls, and it ranked #549 in 2025.

Danielle births per year, 1925–2025

1987 was the biggest year for Danielle both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1959 and 1964, Danielle became 5.4× more common, from 1 in 9,680 baby girls to 1 in 1,800.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1996 and 2001, its share of baby girls fell by 50%, from 1 in 189 to 1 in 376.

Chuck name popularity since 1925

17,785 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Chuck. Half of them were born in 1961 or earlier, so the typical Chuck is about 65 years old in 2026. Chuck peaked in 1961 as the #217 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Chuck births per year, 1925–2025

1961 was the biggest year for Chuck both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1946 and 1951, Chuck's share of baby boys fell by 62%, from 1 in 4,860 to 1 in 12,900.

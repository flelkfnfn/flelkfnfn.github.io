Halle name popularity since 1925

16,657 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Halle. Half of them were born in 2006 or earlier, so the typical Halle is about 20 years old in 2026. Halle peaked in 2002 as the #316 name for girls, and it ranked #596 in 2025.

Halle births per year, 1925–2025

2002 was the biggest year for Halle both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1997 and 2002, Halle became 2.5× more common, from 1 in 4,680 baby girls to 1 in 1,850.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2010 and 2015, its share of baby girls fell by 43%, from 1 in 3,360 to 1 in 5,910.

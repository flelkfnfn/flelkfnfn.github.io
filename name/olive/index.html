Olive name popularity since 1925

32,833 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Olive. Half of them were born in 2014 or earlier, so the typical Olive is about 12 years old in 2026. Olive peaked in 1886 as the #84 name for girls, and it ranked #197 in 2025.

Olive births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year on this chart was 2022. Measured as a share of all babies, Olive peaked earlier, in 1886.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1939 and 1944, Olive's share of baby girls fell by 61%, from 1 in 4,670 to 1 in 12,100.
  • Fastest rise: Between 2006 and 2011, it became 7.1× more common, from 1 in 16,400 baby girls to 1 in 2,310.

Kelsey name popularity since 1925

155,574 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Kelsey (96.7% girls, 3.3% boys). Half of them were born in 1994 or earlier, so the typical Kelsey is about 32 years old in 2026. Kelsey peaked in 1992 as the #23 name for girls, and it ranked #814 in 2025.

Kelsey births per year, 1925–2025

1992 was the biggest year for Kelsey both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1979 and 1984, Kelsey became 6.7× more common, from 1 in 7,230 baby girls to 1 in 1,080.

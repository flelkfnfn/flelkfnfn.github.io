Kash name popularity since 1925

17,632 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Kash (1.6% girls, 98.4% boys). Half of them were born in 2019 or earlier, so the typical Kash is about 7 years old in 2026. Kash peaked in 2021 as the #240 name for boys, and it ranked #286 in 2025.

Bonnie name popularity since 1925

309,678 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Bonnie (99.1% girls, 0.9% boys). Half of them were born in 1951 or earlier, so the typical Bonnie is about 75 years old in 2026. Bonnie peaked in 1942 as the #34 name for girls, and it ranked #396 in 2025.

Bonnie births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1947. Measured as a share of all babies, Bonnie peaked in 1942.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1986 and 1991, Bonnie's share of baby girls fell by 52%, from 1 in 1,570 to 1 in 3,270.
  • Fastest rise: Between 2016 and 2021, it became 2.1× more common, from 1 in 5,680 baby girls to 1 in 2,720.

Bernadette name popularity since 1925

50,185 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Bernadette. Half of them were born in 1960 or earlier, so the typical Bernadette is about 66 years old in 2026. Bernadette peaked in 1946 as the #188 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Bernadette births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1958. Measured as a share of all babies, Bernadette peaked in 1946.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1934 and 1939, Bernadette's share of baby girls fell by 44%.
  • Fastest rise: Between 1941 and 1946, it became 3.1× more common, from 1 in 3,670 baby girls to 1 in 1,190.

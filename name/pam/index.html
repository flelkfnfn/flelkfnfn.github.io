Pam name popularity since 1925

33,143 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Pam. Half of them were born in 1960 or earlier, so the typical Pam is about 66 years old in 2026. Pam peaked in 1959 as the #125 name for girls, and fewer than 5 girls got it in 2025.

Pam births per year, 1925–2025

1959 was the biggest year for Pam both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1953 and 1958, Pam became 12× more common, from 1 in 7,680 baby girls to 1 in 656.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1964 and 1969, its share of baby girls fell by 77%, from 1 in 1,010 to 1 in 4,420.

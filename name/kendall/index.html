Kendall name popularity since 1925

101,208 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Kendall (65.7% girls, 34.3% boys). Half of them were born in 2002 or earlier, so the typical Kendall is about 24 years old in 2026. Kendall peaked in 2012 as the #116 name for girls, and it ranked #328 in 2025.

Kendall births per year, 1925–2025

2012 was the biggest year for Kendall both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1989 and 1994, Kendall became 2.7× more common, from 1 in 2,950 baby girls to 1 in 1,080.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2013 and 2018, its share of baby girls fell by 50%, from 1 in 697 to 1 in 1,400.

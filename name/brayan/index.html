Brayan name popularity since 1925

16,558 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Brayan. Half of them were born in 2008 or earlier, so the typical Brayan is about 18 years old in 2026. Brayan peaked in 2006 as the #294 name for boys, and it ranked #768 in 2025.

Brayan births per year, 1925–2025

2006 was the biggest year for Brayan both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1995 and 2000, Brayan became 4.2× more common, from 1 in 17,000 baby boys to 1 in 4,090.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2013 and 2018, its share of baby boys fell by 46%, from 1 in 3,600 to 1 in 6,700.

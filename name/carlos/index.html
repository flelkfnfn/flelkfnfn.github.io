Carlos name popularity since 1925

303,848 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Carlos (0.7% girls, 99.3% boys). Half of them were born in 1993 or earlier, so the typical Carlos is about 33 years old in 2026. Carlos peaked in 2001 as the #59 name for boys, and it ranked #150 in 2025.

Carlos births per year, 1925–2025

2001 was the biggest year for Carlos both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1967 and 1972, Carlos became 1.6× more common, from 1 in 759 baby boys to 1 in 472.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2008 and 2013, its share of baby boys fell by 34%, from 1 in 337 to 1 in 513.

Jeffery name popularity since 1925

231,582 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Jeffery. Half of them were born in 1967 or earlier, so the typical Jeffery is about 59 years old in 2026. Jeffery peaked in 1962 as the #45 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Jeffery births per year, 1925–2025

1962 was the biggest year for Jeffery both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1950 and 1955, Jeffery became 3.9× more common, from 1 in 1,470 baby boys to 1 in 377.

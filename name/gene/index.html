Gene name popularity since 1925

121,309 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Gene (3.6% girls, 96.4% boys). Half of them were born in 1945 or earlier, so the typical Gene is about 81 years old in 2026. Gene peaked in 1937 as the #61 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

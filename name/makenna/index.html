Makenna name popularity since 1925

33,749 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Makenna. Half of them were born in 2009 or earlier, so the typical Makenna is about 17 years old in 2026. Makenna peaked in 2005 as the #213 name for girls, and it ranked #593 in 2025.

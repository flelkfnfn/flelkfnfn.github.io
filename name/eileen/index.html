Eileen name popularity since 1925

160,856 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Eileen. Half of them were born in 1952 or earlier, so the typical Eileen is about 74 years old in 2026. Eileen peaked in 1943 as the #68 name for girls, and it ranked #654 in 2025.

Eileen births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1947. Measured as a share of all babies, Eileen peaked in 1943.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1940 and 1945, Eileen became 1.6× more common, from 1 in 562 baby girls to 1 in 359.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1966 and 1971, its share of baby girls fell by 48%, from 1 in 830 to 1 in 1,610.

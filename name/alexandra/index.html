Alexandra name popularity since 1925

245,719 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Alexandra. Half of them were born in 1997 or earlier, so the typical Alexandra is about 29 years old in 2026. Alexandra peaked in 1993 as the #28 name for girls, and it ranked #237 in 2025.

Alexandra births per year, 1925–2025

1993 was the biggest year for Alexandra both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1983 and 1988, Alexandra became 3.8× more common, from 1 in 983 baby girls to 1 in 255.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2017 and 2022, its share of baby girls fell by 37%, from 1 in 662 to 1 in 1,060.

Milton name popularity since 1925

82,276 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Milton. Half of them were born in 1949 or earlier, so the typical Milton is about 77 years old in 2026. Milton peaked in 1912 as the #64 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

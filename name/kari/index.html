Kari name popularity since 1925

60,627 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Kari (98.5% girls, 1.5% boys). Half of them were born in 1978 or earlier, so the typical Kari is about 48 years old in 2026. Kari peaked in 1975 as the #129 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

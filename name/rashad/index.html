Rashad name popularity since 1925

16,332 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Rashad. Half of them were born in 1995 or earlier, so the typical Rashad is about 31 years old in 2026. Rashad peaked in 1989 as the #348 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Rashad births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1990. Measured as a share of all babies, Rashad peaked in 1989.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1985 and 1990, Rashad became 3.6× more common, from 1 in 10,900 baby boys to 1 in 3,030.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1994 and 1999, its share of baby boys fell by 50%, from 1 in 3,640 to 1 in 7,250.

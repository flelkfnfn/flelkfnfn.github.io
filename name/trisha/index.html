Trisha name popularity since 1925

40,355 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Trisha. Half of them were born in 1980 or earlier, so the typical Trisha is about 46 years old in 2026. Trisha peaked in 1979 as the #159 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Trisha births per year, 1925–2025

1979 was the biggest year for Trisha both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1960 and 1965, Trisha became 8.4× more common, from 1 in 19,600 baby girls to 1 in 2,330.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1993 and 1998, its share of baby girls fell by 59%, from 1 in 2,630 to 1 in 6,470.

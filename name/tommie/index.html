Tommie name popularity since 1925

41,595 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Tommie (32.6% girls, 67.4% boys). Half of them were born in 1949 or earlier, so the typical Tommie is about 77 years old in 2026. Tommie peaked in 1909 as the #195 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

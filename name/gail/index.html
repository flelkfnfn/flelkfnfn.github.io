Gail name popularity since 1925

205,782 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Gail (96.7% girls, 3.3% boys). Half of them were born in 1952 or earlier, so the typical Gail is about 74 years old in 2026. Gail peaked in 1951 as the #36 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

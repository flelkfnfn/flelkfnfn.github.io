Gale name popularity since 1925

29,879 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Gale (65.4% girls, 34.6% boys). Half of them were born in 1952 or earlier, so the typical Gale is about 74 years old in 2026. Gale peaked in 1957 as the #244 name for girls, and fewer than 5 girls got it in 2025.

Gale births per year, 1925–2025

1957 was the biggest year for Gale both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1935 and 1940, Gale became 2.9× more common, from 1 in 8,590 baby girls to 1 in 2,940.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1957 and 1962, its share of baby girls fell by 72%, from 1 in 1,690 to 1 in 5,980.

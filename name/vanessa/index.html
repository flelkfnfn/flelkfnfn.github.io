Vanessa name popularity since 1925

262,539 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Vanessa. Half of them were born in 1989 or earlier, so the typical Vanessa is about 37 years old in 2026. Vanessa peaked in 1985 as the #42 name for girls, and it ranked #377 in 2025.

Hayley name popularity since 1925

51,449 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Hayley. Half of them were born in 1998 or earlier, so the typical Hayley is about 28 years old in 2026. Hayley peaked in 1996 as the #120 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Hayley births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1993. Measured as a share of all babies, Hayley peaked in 1996.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1988 and 1993, Hayley became 3.2× more common, from 1 in 2,220 baby girls to 1 in 691.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2020 and 2025, its share of baby girls fell by 57%, from 1 in 3,820 to 1 in 8,980.

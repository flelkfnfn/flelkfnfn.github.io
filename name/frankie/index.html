Frankie name popularity since 1925

70,784 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Frankie (41.8% girls, 58.2% boys). Half of them were born in 1961 or earlier, so the typical Frankie is about 65 years old in 2026. Frankie peaked in 1945 as the #232 name for boys, and it ranked #922 in 2025.

Rose name popularity since 1925

322,914 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Rose. Half of them were born in 1951 or earlier, so the typical Rose is about 75 years old in 2026. Rose peaked in 1908 as the #16 name for girls, and it ranked #114 in 2025.

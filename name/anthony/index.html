Anthony name popularity since 1925

1,424,556 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Anthony. Half of them were born in 1983 or earlier, so the typical Anthony is about 43 years old in 2026. Anthony peaked in 1987 as the #17 name for boys, and it ranked #46 in 2025.

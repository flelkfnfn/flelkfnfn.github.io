Dwight name popularity since 1925

73,047 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Dwight. Half of them were born in 1957 or earlier, so the typical Dwight is about 69 years old in 2026. Dwight peaked in 1953 as the #122 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Dwight births per year, 1925–2025

1953 was the biggest year for Dwight both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1940 and 1945, Dwight became 3.3× more common, from 1 in 2,540 baby boys to 1 in 768.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1953 and 1958, its share of baby boys fell by 42%, from 1 in 731 to 1 in 1,250.

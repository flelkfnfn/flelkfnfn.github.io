Gustavo name popularity since 1925

40,185 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Gustavo. Half of them were born in 1999 or earlier, so the typical Gustavo is about 27 years old in 2026. Gustavo peaked in 2000 as the #260 name for boys, and it ranked #625 in 2025.

Gustavo births per year, 1925–2025

2000 was the biggest year for Gustavo both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1988 and 1993, Gustavo became 1.9× more common, from 1 in 3,640 baby boys to 1 in 1,940.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2006 and 2011, its share of baby boys fell by 37%, from 1 in 1,900 to 1 in 3,040.

Everett name popularity since 1925

109,379 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Everett (1.0% girls, 99.0% boys). Half of them were born in 2007 or earlier, so the typical Everett is about 19 years old in 2026. Everett peaked in 2025 as the #77 name for boys, and it ranked #77 in 2025.

Everett births per year, 1925–2025

2025 was the biggest year for Everett both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1942 and 1947, Everett's share of baby boys fell by 36%, from 1 in 1,310 to 1 in 2,040.
  • Fastest rise: Between 2008 and 2013, it became 2.9× more common, from 1 in 2,540 baby boys to 1 in 881.

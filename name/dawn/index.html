Dawn name popularity since 1925

289,701 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Dawn. Half of them were born in 1968 or earlier, so the typical Dawn is about 58 years old in 2026. Dawn peaked in 1970 as the #16 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Dawn births per year, 1925–2025

1970 was the biggest year for Dawn both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1951 and 1956, Dawn became 3× more common, from 1 in 1,170 baby girls to 1 in 392.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1982 and 1987, its share of baby girls fell by 64%, from 1 in 548 to 1 in 1,510.

Lorena name popularity since 1925

36,915 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Lorena (99.4% girls, 0.6% boys). Half of them were born in 1983 or earlier, so the typical Lorena is about 43 years old in 2026. Lorena peaked in 1899 as the #233 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Arianna name popularity since 1925

104,225 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Arianna. Half of them were born in 2010 or earlier, so the typical Arianna is about 16 years old in 2026. Arianna peaked in 2014 as the #40 name for girls, and it ranked #234 in 2025.

Arianna births per year, 1925–2025

2014 was the biggest year for Arianna both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1986 and 1991, Arianna became 4.8× more common, from 1 in 10,800 baby girls to 1 in 2,270.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2014 and 2019, its share of baby girls fell by 49%, from 1 in 339 to 1 in 667.

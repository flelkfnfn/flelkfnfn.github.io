Ruth name popularity since 1925

452,246 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Ruth. Half of them were born in 1942 or earlier, so the typical Ruth is about 84 years old in 2026. Ruth peaked in 1915 as the #5 name for girls, and it ranked #173 in 2025.

Ruth births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year on this chart was 1925. Measured as a share of all babies, Ruth peaked earlier, in 1915.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1965 and 1970, Ruth's share of baby girls fell by 43%, from 1 in 546 to 1 in 962.
  • Fastest rise: Between 2016 and 2021, it became 1.5× more common, from 1 in 1,610 baby girls to 1 in 1,040.

Taryn name popularity since 1925

26,439 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Taryn (97.2% girls, 2.8% boys). Half of them were born in 1995 or earlier, so the typical Taryn is about 31 years old in 2026. Taryn peaked in 1985 as the #227 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

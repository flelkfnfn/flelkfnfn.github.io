Lesley name popularity since 1925

38,382 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Lesley (89.1% girls, 10.9% boys). Half of them were born in 1977 or earlier, so the typical Lesley is about 49 years old in 2026. Lesley peaked in 1979 as the #268 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Lesley births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1980. Measured as a share of all babies, Lesley peaked in 1979.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1960 and 1965, Lesley became 3.5× more common, from 1 in 8,290 baby girls to 1 in 2,370.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2007 and 2012, its share of baby girls fell by 59%, from 1 in 5,450 to 1 in 13,400.

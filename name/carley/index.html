Carley name popularity since 1925

17,174 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Carley (98.2% girls, 1.8% boys). Half of them were born in 1999 or earlier, so the typical Carley is about 27 years old in 2026. Carley peaked in 1998 as the #389 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Carley births per year, 1925–2025

1998 was the biggest year for Carley both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1989 and 1994, Carley became 2.7× more common, from 1 in 8,050 baby girls to 1 in 2,930.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2015 and 2020, its share of baby girls fell by 49%, from 1 in 7,630 to 1 in 15,000.

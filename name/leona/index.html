Leona name popularity since 1925

57,826 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Leona. Half of them were born in 1943 or earlier, so the typical Leona is about 83 years old in 2026. Leona peaked in 1905 as the #69 name for girls, and it ranked #429 in 2025.

Leona births per year, 1925–2025

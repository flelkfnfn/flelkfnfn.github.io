Alma name popularity since 1925

91,415 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Alma (98.5% girls, 1.5% boys). Half of them were born in 1950 or earlier, so the typical Alma is about 76 years old in 2026. Alma peaked in 1894 as the #52 name for girls, and it ranked #449 in 2025.

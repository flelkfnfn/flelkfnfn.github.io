Katherine name popularity since 1925

562,689 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Katherine. Half of them were born in 1982 or earlier, so the typical Katherine is about 44 years old in 2026. Katherine peaked in 1988 as the #27 name for girls, and it ranked #186 in 2025.

Katherine births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1990. Measured as a share of all babies, Katherine peaked in 1988.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1974 and 1979, Katherine became 1.5× more common, from 1 in 324 baby girls to 1 in 215.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2016 and 2021, its share of baby girls fell by 40%, from 1 in 541 to 1 in 901.

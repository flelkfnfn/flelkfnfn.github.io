Cristina name popularity since 1925

52,411 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Cristina. Half of them were born in 1988 or earlier, so the typical Cristina is about 38 years old in 2026. Cristina peaked in 1986 as the #163 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Cristina births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1990. Measured as a share of all babies, Cristina peaked in 1986.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1959 and 1964, Cristina became 2.7× more common, from 1 in 16,300 baby girls to 1 in 6,130.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2007 and 2012, its share of baby girls fell by 50%, from 1 in 3,300 to 1 in 6,570.

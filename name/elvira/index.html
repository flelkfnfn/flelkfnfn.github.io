Elvira name popularity since 1925

17,448 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Elvira. Half of them were born in 1952 or earlier, so the typical Elvira is about 74 years old in 2026. Elvira peaked in 1909 as the #257 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

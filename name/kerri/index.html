Kerri name popularity since 1925

37,369 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Kerri (99.1% girls, 0.9% boys). Half of them were born in 1976 or earlier, so the typical Kerri is about 50 years old in 2026. Kerri peaked in 1975 as the #167 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Kerri births per year, 1925–2025

1975 was the biggest year for Kerri both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1955 and 1960, Kerri became 4.2× more common, from 1 in 17,900 baby girls to 1 in 4,230.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1996 and 2001, its share of baby girls fell by 60%, from 1 in 5,570 to 1 in 14,000.

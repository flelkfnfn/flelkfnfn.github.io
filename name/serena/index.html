Serena name popularity since 1925

46,354 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Serena. Half of them were born in 1999 or earlier, so the typical Serena is about 27 years old in 2026. Serena peaked in 2000 as the #209 name for girls, and it ranked #325 in 2025.

Serena births per year, 1925–2025

2000 was the biggest year for Serena both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1964 and 1969, Serena became 3.7× more common, from 1 in 9,330 baby girls to 1 in 2,500.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2000 and 2005, its share of baby girls fell by 43%, from 1 in 1,210 to 1 in 2,130.

Jerry name popularity since 1925

614,130 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Jerry (2.5% girls, 97.5% boys). Half of them were born in 1952 or earlier, so the typical Jerry is about 74 years old in 2026. Jerry peaked in 1941 as the #14 name for boys, and it ranked #907 in 2025.

Jerry births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1947. Measured as a share of all babies, Jerry peaked in 1941.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1928 and 1933, Jerry became 2.6× more common.

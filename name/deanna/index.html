Deanna name popularity since 1925

134,642 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Deanna. Half of them were born in 1970 or earlier, so the typical Deanna is about 56 years old in 2026. Deanna peaked in 1970 as the #90 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Deanna births per year, 1925–2025

1970 was the biggest year for Deanna both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1957 and 1962, Deanna became 2.5× more common, from 1 in 1,590 baby girls to 1 in 643.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2009 and 2014, its share of baby girls fell by 52%, from 1 in 3,680 to 1 in 7,670.

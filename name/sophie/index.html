Sophie name popularity since 1925

106,655 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Sophie. Half of them were born in 2010 or earlier, so the typical Sophie is about 16 years old in 2026. Sophie peaked in 2011 as the #51 name for girls, and it ranked #55 in 2025.

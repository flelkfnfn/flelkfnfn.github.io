Barbara name popularity since 1925

1,375,536 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Barbara. Half of them were born in 1947 or earlier, so the typical Barbara is about 79 years old in 2026. Barbara peaked in 1938 as the #2 name for girls, and it ranked #968 in 2025.

The biggest year by count was 1947. Measured as a share of all babies, Barbara peaked in 1938.

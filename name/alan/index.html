Alan name popularity since 1925

359,376 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Alan. Half of them were born in 1962 or earlier, so the typical Alan is about 64 years old in 2026. Alan peaked in 1951 as the #40 name for boys, and it ranked #191 in 2025.

Alan births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1955. Measured as a share of all babies, Alan peaked in 1951.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1942 and 1947, Alan became 1.9× more common, from 1 in 441 baby boys to 1 in 234.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1967 and 1972, its share of baby boys fell by 39%, from 1 in 381 to 1 in 629.

Sonny name popularity since 1925

22,906 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Sonny (4.5% girls, 95.5% boys). Half of them were born in 1995 or earlier, so the typical Sonny is about 31 years old in 2026. Sonny peaked in 2025 as the #298 name for boys, and it ranked #298 in 2025.

Sonny births per year, 1925–2025

2025 was the biggest year for Sonny both by count and as a share of all babies.

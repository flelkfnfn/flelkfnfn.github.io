Ira name popularity since 1925

44,353 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Ira (11.8% girls, 88.2% boys). Half of them were born in 1954 or earlier, so the typical Ira is about 72 years old in 2026. Ira peaked in 1881 as the #57 name for boys, and it ranked #822 in 2025.

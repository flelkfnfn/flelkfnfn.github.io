Lynette name popularity since 1925

42,983 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Lynette. Half of them were born in 1965 or earlier, so the typical Lynette is about 61 years old in 2026. Lynette peaked in 1965 as the #225 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Lynette births per year, 1925–2025

1965 was the biggest year for Lynette both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1940 and 1945, Lynette became 2.5× more common, from 1 in 7,890 baby girls to 1 in 3,210.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1972 and 1977, its share of baby girls fell by 46%, from 1 in 1,550 to 1 in 2,850.

Albert name popularity since 1925

332,275 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Albert. Half of them were born in 1949 or earlier, so the typical Albert is about 77 years old in 2026. Albert peaked in 1883 as the #16 name for boys, and it ranked #614 in 2025.

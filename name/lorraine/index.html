Lorraine name popularity since 1925

169,732 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Lorraine. Half of them were born in 1946 or earlier, so the typical Lorraine is about 80 years old in 2026. Lorraine peaked in 1928 as the #39 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

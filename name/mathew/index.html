Mathew name popularity since 1925

73,915 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Mathew. Half of them were born in 1989 or earlier, so the typical Mathew is about 37 years old in 2026. Mathew peaked in 1987 as the #140 name for boys, and it ranked #975 in 2025.

Mathew births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1990. Measured as a share of all babies, Mathew peaked in 1987.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1962 and 1967, Mathew became 2.2× more common, from 1 in 6,370 baby boys to 1 in 2,940.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2017 and 2022, its share of baby boys fell by 43%, from 1 in 3,680 to 1 in 6,440.

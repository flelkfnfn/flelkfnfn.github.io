Rudy name popularity since 1925

49,665 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Rudy (2.2% girls, 97.8% boys). Half of them were born in 1969 or earlier, so the typical Rudy is about 57 years old in 2026. Rudy peaked in 1934 as the #246 name for boys, and it ranked #739 in 2025.

Ariel name popularity since 1925

100,268 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Ariel (78.2% girls, 21.8% boys). Half of them were born in 2002 or earlier, so the typical Ariel is about 24 years old in 2026. Ariel peaked in 1991 as the #66 name for girls, and it ranked #356 in 2025.

Ariel births per year, 1925–2025

1991 was the biggest year for Ariel both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1986 and 1991, Ariel became 7.6× more common, from 1 in 2,630 baby girls to 1 in 347.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1991 and 1996, its share of baby girls fell by 62%, from 1 in 347 to 1 in 911.

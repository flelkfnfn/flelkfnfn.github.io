River name popularity since 1925

59,351 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named River (33.2% girls, 66.8% boys). Half of them were born in 2019 or earlier, so the typical River is about 7 years old in 2026. River peaked in 2022 as the #105 name for boys, and it ranked #111 in 2025.

Levi name popularity since 1925

211,139 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Levi (0.7% girls, 99.3% boys). Half of them were born in 2014 or earlier, so the typical Levi is about 12 years old in 2026. Levi peaked in 2022 as the #12 name for boys, and it ranked #12 in 2025.

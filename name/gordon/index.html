Gordon name popularity since 1925

126,200 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Gordon. Half of them were born in 1949 or earlier, so the typical Gordon is about 77 years old in 2026. Gordon peaked in 1934 as the #71 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Gordon births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1952. Measured as a share of all babies, Gordon peaked in 1934.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1963 and 1968, Gordon's share of baby boys fell by 39%, from 1 in 976 to 1 in 1,590.
  • Fastest rise: Between 2010 and 2015, it became 1.6× more common, from 1 in 10,700 baby boys to 1 in 6,870.

Dewey name popularity since 1925

24,747 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Dewey (0.9% girls, 99.1% boys). Half of them were born in 1946 or earlier, so the typical Dewey is about 80 years old in 2026. Dewey peaked in 1898 as the #19 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

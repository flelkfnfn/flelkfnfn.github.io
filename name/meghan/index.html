Meghan name popularity since 1925

96,727 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Meghan. Half of them were born in 1990 or earlier, so the typical Meghan is about 36 years old in 2026. Meghan peaked in 1985 as the #63 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Meghan births per year, 1925–2025

1985 was the biggest year for Meghan both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1967 and 1972, Meghan became 4× more common, from 1 in 13,900 baby girls to 1 in 3,450.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2020 and 2025, its share of baby girls fell by 66%, from 1 in 5,320 to 1 in 15,800.

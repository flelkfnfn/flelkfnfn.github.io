Felicia name popularity since 1925

89,400 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Felicia. Half of them were born in 1978 or earlier, so the typical Felicia is about 48 years old in 2026. Felicia peaked in 1986 as the #89 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Felicia births per year, 1925–2025

1986 was the biggest year for Felicia both by count and as a share of all babies.

Malik name popularity since 1925

49,866 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Malik. Half of them were born in 2001 or earlier, so the typical Malik is about 25 years old in 2026. Malik peaked in 1996 as the #97 name for boys, and it ranked #437 in 2025.

Malik births per year, 1925–2025

1996 was the biggest year for Malik both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1991 and 1996, Malik became 12× more common, from 1 in 5,490 baby boys to 1 in 471.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1996 and 2001, its share of baby boys fell by 55%, from 1 in 471 to 1 in 1,040.

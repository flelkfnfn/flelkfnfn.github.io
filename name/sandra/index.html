Sandra name popularity since 1925

877,158 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Sandra. Half of them were born in 1954 or earlier, so the typical Sandra is about 72 years old in 2026. Sandra peaked in 1946 as the #6 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

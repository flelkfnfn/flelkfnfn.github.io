Cassandra name popularity since 1925

171,278 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Cassandra. Half of them were born in 1989 or earlier, so the typical Cassandra is about 37 years old in 2026. Cassandra peaked in 1990 as the #49 name for girls, and it ranked #631 in 2025.

Cassandra births per year, 1925–2025

1990 was the biggest year for Cassandra both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1977 and 1982, Cassandra became 2.5× more common, from 1 in 917 baby girls to 1 in 363.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2002 and 2007, its share of baby girls fell by 55%, from 1 in 658 to 1 in 1,450.

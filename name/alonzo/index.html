Alonzo name popularity since 1925

39,514 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Alonzo. Half of them were born in 1983 or earlier, so the typical Alonzo is about 43 years old in 2026. Alonzo peaked in 1882 as the #111 name for boys, and it ranked #511 in 2025.

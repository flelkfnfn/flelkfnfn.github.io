Ethan name popularity since 1925

487,763 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Ethan. Half of them were born in 2007 or earlier, so the typical Ethan is about 19 years old in 2026. Ethan peaked in 2002 as the #5 name for boys, and it ranked #24 in 2025.

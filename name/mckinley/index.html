Mckinley name popularity since 1925

20,566 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Mckinley (63.7% girls, 36.3% boys). Half of them were born in 2011 or earlier, so the typical Mckinley is about 15 years old in 2026. Mckinley peaked in 2014 as the #379 name for girls, and it ranked #714 in 2025.

Mckinley births per year, 1925–2025

2014 was the biggest year for Mckinley both by count and as a share of all babies.

Addie name popularity since 1925

21,522 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Addie (98.7% girls, 1.3% boys). Half of them were born in 1950 or earlier, so the typical Addie is about 76 years old in 2026. Addie peaked in 1883 as the #77 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

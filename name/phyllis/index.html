Phyllis name popularity since 1925

277,472 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Phyllis. Half of them were born in 1943 or earlier, so the typical Phyllis is about 83 years old in 2026. Phyllis peaked in 1929 as the #24 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Roderick name popularity since 1925

45,939 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Roderick (0.6% girls, 99.4% boys). Half of them were born in 1971 or earlier, so the typical Roderick is about 55 years old in 2026. Roderick peaked in 1971 as the #224 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Roderick births per year, 1925–2025

1971 was the biggest year for Roderick both by count and as a share of all babies.

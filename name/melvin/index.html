Melvin name popularity since 1925

200,056 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Melvin (0.8% girls, 99.2% boys). Half of them were born in 1950 or earlier, so the typical Melvin is about 76 years old in 2026. Melvin peaked in 1928 as the #48 name for boys, and it ranked #959 in 2025.

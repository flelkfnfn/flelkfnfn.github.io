Garland name popularity since 1925

18,803 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Garland (3.2% girls, 96.8% boys). Half of them were born in 1949 or earlier, so the typical Garland is about 77 years old in 2026. Garland peaked in 1905 as the #251 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

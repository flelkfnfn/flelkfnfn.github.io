Richard name popularity since 1925

2,397,316 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Richard. Half of them were born in 1954 or earlier, so the typical Richard is about 72 years old in 2026. Richard peaked in 1946 as the #5 name for boys, and it ranked #234 in 2025.

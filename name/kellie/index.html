Kellie name popularity since 1925

51,034 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Kellie (99.4% girls, 0.6% boys). Half of them were born in 1977 or earlier, so the typical Kellie is about 49 years old in 2026. Kellie peaked in 1969 as the #130 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Kellie births per year, 1925–2025

1969 was the biggest year for Kellie both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1956 and 1961, Kellie became 7.1× more common, from 1 in 16,300 baby girls to 1 in 2,290.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2000 and 2005, its share of baby girls fell by 59%, from 1 in 4,700 to 1 in 11,500.

Shane name popularity since 1925

196,166 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Shane (1.8% girls, 98.2% boys). Half of them were born in 1987 or earlier, so the typical Shane is about 39 years old in 2026. Shane peaked in 1973 as the #54 name for boys, and it ranked #636 in 2025.

Shane births per year, 1925–2025

1973 was the biggest year for Shane both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1964 and 1969, Shane became 4.4× more common, from 1 in 2,210 baby boys to 1 in 506.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2015 and 2020, its share of baby boys fell by 48%, from 1 in 1,570 to 1 in 3,030.

Connie name popularity since 1925

265,090 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Connie (97.9% girls, 2.1% boys). Half of them were born in 1955 or earlier, so the typical Connie is about 71 years old in 2026. Connie peaked in 1955 as the #35 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

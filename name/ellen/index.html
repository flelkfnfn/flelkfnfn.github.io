Ellen name popularity since 1925

218,509 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Ellen. Half of them were born in 1954 or earlier, so the typical Ellen is about 72 years old in 2026. Ellen peaked in 1884 as the #59 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

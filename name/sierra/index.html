Sierra name popularity since 1925

100,866 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Sierra. Half of them were born in 1999 or earlier, so the typical Sierra is about 27 years old in 2026. Sierra peaked in 1998 as the #51 name for girls, and it ranked #616 in 2025.

Sierra births per year, 1925–2025

1998 was the biggest year for Sierra both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1981 and 1986, Sierra became 8.4× more common, from 1 in 8,920 baby girls to 1 in 1,070.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2002 and 2007, its share of baby girls fell by 59%, from 1 in 374 to 1 in 909.

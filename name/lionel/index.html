Lionel name popularity since 1925

30,030 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Lionel. Half of them were born in 1971 or earlier, so the typical Lionel is about 55 years old in 2026. Lionel peaked in 2023 as the #495 name for boys, and it ranked #659 in 2025.

Lionel births per year, 1925–2025

2023 was the biggest year for Lionel both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1985 and 1990, Lionel's share of baby boys fell by 48%, from 1 in 4,610 to 1 in 8,890.
  • Fastest rise: Between 2009 and 2014, it became 2.3× more common, from 1 in 11,500 baby boys to 1 in 4,950.

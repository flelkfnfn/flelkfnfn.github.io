Tami name popularity since 1925

38,017 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Tami. Half of them were born in 1966 or earlier, so the typical Tami is about 60 years old in 2026. Tami peaked in 1959 as the #154 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Tami births per year, 1925–2025

1959 was the biggest year for Tami both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1955 and 1960, Tami became 21× more common, from 1 in 18,600 baby girls to 1 in 895.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1983 and 1988, its share of baby girls fell by 66%, from 1 in 4,670 to 1 in 13,900.

Tiana name popularity since 1925

29,862 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Tiana. Half of them were born in 2000 or earlier, so the typical Tiana is about 26 years old in 2026. Tiana peaked in 1995 as the #268 name for girls, and it ranked #598 in 2025.

Tiana births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1998. Measured as a share of all babies, Tiana peaked in 1995.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1989 and 1994, Tiana became 2× more common, from 1 in 3,700 baby girls to 1 in 1,880.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2010 and 2015, its share of baby girls fell by 50%, from 1 in 1,830 to 1 in 3,660.

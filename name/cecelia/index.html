Cecelia name popularity since 1925

44,193 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Cecelia. Half of them were born in 1960 or earlier, so the typical Cecelia is about 66 years old in 2026. Cecelia peaked in 1901 as the #176 name for girls, and it ranked #529 in 2025.

Kelsie name popularity since 1925

19,797 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Kelsie (98.7% girls, 1.3% boys). Half of them were born in 1996 or earlier, so the typical Kelsie is about 30 years old in 2026. Kelsie peaked in 1993 as the #230 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Kelsie births per year, 1925–2025

1993 was the biggest year for Kelsie both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1984 and 1989, Kelsie became 5.2× more common, from 1 in 12,600 baby girls to 1 in 2,430.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2016 and 2021, its share of baby girls fell by 51%, from 1 in 7,740 to 1 in 15,900.

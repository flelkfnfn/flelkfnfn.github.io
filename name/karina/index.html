Karina name popularity since 1925

61,717 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Karina. Half of them were born in 1998 or earlier, so the typical Karina is about 28 years old in 2026. Karina peaked in 1995 as the #109 name for girls, and it ranked #750 in 2025.

Karina births per year, 1925–2025

1995 was the biggest year for Karina both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1973 and 1978, Karina became 2.5× more common, from 1 in 8,100 baby girls to 1 in 3,200.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2007 and 2012, its share of baby girls fell by 41%, from 1 in 1,210 to 1 in 2,050.

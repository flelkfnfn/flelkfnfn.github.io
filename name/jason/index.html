Jason name popularity since 1925

1,055,498 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Jason. Half of them were born in 1980 or earlier, so the typical Jason is about 46 years old in 2026. Jason peaked in 1974 as the #2 name for boys, and it ranked #165 in 2025.

Jason births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1977. Measured as a share of all babies, Jason peaked in 1974.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1965 and 1970, Jason became 9.5× more common, from 1 in 644 baby boys to 1 in 68.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1985 and 1990, its share of baby boys fell by 63%, from 1 in 71 to 1 in 192.

Jimmy name popularity since 1925

287,020 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Jimmy (0.7% girls, 99.3% boys). Half of them were born in 1957 or earlier, so the typical Jimmy is about 69 years old in 2026. Jimmy peaked in 1944 as the #41 name for boys, and it ranked #847 in 2025.

Jimmy births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1947. Measured as a share of all babies, Jimmy peaked in 1944.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1927 and 1932, Jimmy became 2.7× more common.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2010 and 2015, its share of baby boys fell by 37%, from 1 in 2,490 to 1 in 3,930.

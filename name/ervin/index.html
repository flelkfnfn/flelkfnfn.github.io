Ervin name popularity since 1925

28,253 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Ervin. Half of them were born in 1951 or earlier, so the typical Ervin is about 75 years old in 2026. Ervin peaked in 1905 as the #172 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Georgia name popularity since 1925

122,153 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Georgia. Half of them were born in 1954 or earlier, so the typical Georgia is about 72 years old in 2026. Georgia peaked in 1882 as the #81 name for girls, and it ranked #99 in 2025.

Georgia births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year on this chart was 2025. Measured as a share of all babies, Georgia peaked earlier, in 1882.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1953 and 1958, Georgia's share of baby girls fell by 40%, from 1 in 1,080 to 1 in 1,810.
  • Fastest rise: Between 1994 and 1999, it became 2.5× more common, from 1 in 5,220 baby girls to 1 in 2,070.

Bobbie name popularity since 1925

101,398 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Bobbie (83.2% girls, 16.8% boys). Half of them were born in 1948 or earlier, so the typical Bobbie is about 78 years old in 2026. Bobbie peaked in 1934 as the #110 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Bobbie births per year, 1925–2025

1934 was the biggest year for Bobbie both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1925 and 1930, Bobbie became 2.8× more common.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1997 and 2002, its share of baby girls fell by 59%, from 1 in 6,590 to 1 in 15,900.

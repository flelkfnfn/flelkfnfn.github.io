Marianna name popularity since 1925

16,880 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Marianna. Half of them were born in 2000 or earlier, so the typical Marianna is about 26 years old in 2026. Marianna peaked in 2006 as the #679 name for girls, and it ranked #874 in 2025.

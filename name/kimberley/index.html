Kimberley name popularity since 1925

38,278 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Kimberley (99.4% girls, 0.6% boys). Half of them were born in 1969 or earlier, so the typical Kimberley is about 57 years old in 2026. Kimberley peaked in 1967 as the #166 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Kimberley births per year, 1925–2025

1967 was the biggest year for Kimberley both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1952 and 1957, Kimberley became 5.4× more common, from 1 in 15,500 baby girls to 1 in 2,840.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1972 and 1977, its share of baby girls fell by 51%, from 1 in 1,200 to 1 in 2,440.

Kurt name popularity since 1925

85,563 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Kurt. Half of them were born in 1966 or earlier, so the typical Kurt is about 60 years old in 2026. Kurt peaked in 1966 as the #110 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Kurt births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1964. Measured as a share of all babies, Kurt peaked in 1966.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1948 and 1953, Kurt became 2.6× more common, from 1 in 3,380 baby boys to 1 in 1,300.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2002 and 2007, its share of baby boys fell by 47%, from 1 in 6,980 to 1 in 13,200.

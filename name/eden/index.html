Eden name popularity since 1925

63,373 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Eden (82.7% girls, 17.3% boys). Half of them were born in 2015 or earlier, so the typical Eden is about 11 years old in 2026. Eden peaked in 2025 as the #70 name for girls, and it ranked #70 in 2025.

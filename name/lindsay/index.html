Lindsay name popularity since 1925

134,718 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Lindsay (96.8% girls, 3.2% boys). Half of them were born in 1986 or earlier, so the typical Lindsay is about 40 years old in 2026. Lindsay peaked in 1983 as the #36 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Lindsay births per year, 1925–2025

1983 was the biggest year for Lindsay both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1973 and 1978, Lindsay became 26× more common, from 1 in 12,700 baby girls to 1 in 491.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2014 and 2019, its share of baby girls fell by 65%, from 1 in 4,130 to 1 in 11,700.

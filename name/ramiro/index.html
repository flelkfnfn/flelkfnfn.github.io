Ramiro name popularity since 1925

25,623 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Ramiro. Half of them were born in 1989 or earlier, so the typical Ramiro is about 37 years old in 2026. Ramiro peaked in 2001 as the #459 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Ramiro births per year, 1925–2025

2001 was the biggest year for Ramiro both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1970 and 1975, Ramiro became 1.6× more common, from 1 in 8,260 baby boys to 1 in 5,100.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2006 and 2011, its share of baby boys fell by 36%, from 1 in 4,640 to 1 in 7,190.

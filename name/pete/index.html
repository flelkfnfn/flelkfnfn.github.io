Pete name popularity since 1925

33,921 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Pete. Half of them were born in 1954 or earlier, so the typical Pete is about 72 years old in 2026. Pete peaked in 1908 as the #173 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Maritza name popularity since 1925

23,232 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Maritza. Half of them were born in 1993 or earlier, so the typical Maritza is about 33 years old in 2026. Maritza peaked in 2005 as the #403 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Maritza births per year, 1925–2025

2005 was the biggest year for Maritza both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1956 and 1961, Maritza became 2.3× more common, from 1 in 18,800 baby girls to 1 in 8,140.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2008 and 2013, its share of baby girls fell by 58%, from 1 in 3,470 to 1 in 8,320.

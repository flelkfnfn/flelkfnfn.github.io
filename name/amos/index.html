Amos name popularity since 1925

27,061 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Amos. Half of them were born in 1971 or earlier, so the typical Amos is about 55 years old in 2026. Amos peaked in 1884 as the #110 name for boys, and it ranked #730 in 2025.

Amos births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year on this chart was 1927. Measured as a share of all babies, Amos peaked earlier, in 1884.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1984 and 1989, Amos's share of baby boys fell by 38%, from 1 in 7,000 to 1 in 11,200.
  • Fastest rise: Between 2009 and 2014, it became 1.9× more common, from 1 in 9,970 baby boys to 1 in 5,230.

Billie name popularity since 1925

118,294 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Billie (77.9% girls, 22.1% boys). Half of them were born in 1943 or earlier, so the typical Billie is about 83 years old in 2026. Billie peaked in 1930 as the #79 name for girls, and it ranked #691 in 2025.

Billie births per year, 1925–2025

1930 was the biggest year for Billie both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1980 and 1985, Billie's share of baby girls fell by 60%, from 1 in 2,070 to 1 in 5,200.
  • Fastest rise: Between 2017 and 2022, it became 2.6× more common, from 1 in 14,800 baby girls to 1 in 5,750.

Marcus name popularity since 1925

237,989 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Marcus (0.6% girls, 99.4% boys). Half of them were born in 1989 or earlier, so the typical Marcus is about 37 years old in 2026. Marcus peaked in 1984 as the #57 name for boys, and it ranked #258 in 2025.

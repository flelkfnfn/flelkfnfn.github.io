Francis name popularity since 1925

222,798 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Francis (8.6% girls, 91.4% boys). Half of them were born in 1948 or earlier, so the typical Francis is about 78 years old in 2026. Francis peaked in 1915 as the #29 name for boys, and it ranked #420 in 2025.

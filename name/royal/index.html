Royal name popularity since 1925

21,112 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Royal (20.5% girls, 79.5% boys). Half of them were born in 2015 or earlier, so the typical Royal is about 11 years old in 2026. Royal peaked in 2021 as the #369 name for boys, and it ranked #476 in 2025.

Royal births per year, 1925–2025

2021 was the biggest year for Royal both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1945 and 1950, Royal's share of baby boys fell by 42%, from 1 in 9,350 to 1 in 16,000.
  • Fastest rise: Between 2011 and 2016, it became 4.5× more common, from 1 in 12,800 baby boys to 1 in 2,870.

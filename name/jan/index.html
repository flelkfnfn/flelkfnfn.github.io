Jan name popularity since 1925

76,421 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Jan (71.1% girls, 28.9% boys). Half of them were born in 1956 or earlier, so the typical Jan is about 70 years old in 2026. Jan peaked in 1956 as the #136 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Jan births per year, 1925–2025

1956 was the biggest year for Jan both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1940 and 1945, Jan became 2.3× more common, from 1 in 3,450 baby girls to 1 in 1,510.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1960 and 1965, its share of baby girls fell by 56%, from 1 in 826 to 1 in 1,880.

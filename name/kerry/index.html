Kerry name popularity since 1925

98,608 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Kerry (49.4% girls, 50.6% boys). Half of them were born in 1968 or earlier, so the typical Kerry is about 58 years old in 2026. Kerry peaked in 1960 as the #158 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Kerry births per year, 1925–2025

1960 was the biggest year for Kerry both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1942 and 1947, Kerry became 3.9× more common, from 1 in 10,200 baby boys to 1 in 2,600.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1991 and 1996, its share of baby boys fell by 53%, from 1 in 4,880 to 1 in 10,500.

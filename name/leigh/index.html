Leigh name popularity since 1925

44,749 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Leigh (89.7% girls, 10.3% boys). Half of them were born in 1972 or earlier, so the typical Leigh is about 54 years old in 2026. Leigh peaked in 1969 as the #201 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Leigh births per year, 1925–2025

1969 was the biggest year for Leigh both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1951 and 1956, Leigh became 3× more common, from 1 in 9,790 baby girls to 1 in 3,290.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1993 and 1998, its share of baby girls fell by 60%, from 1 in 4,860 to 1 in 12,000.

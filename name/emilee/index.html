Emilee name popularity since 1925

23,943 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Emilee. Half of them were born in 2002 or earlier, so the typical Emilee is about 24 years old in 2026. Emilee peaked in 2003 as the #304 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Emilee births per year, 1925–2025

2003 was the biggest year for Emilee both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1986 and 1991, Emilee became 2.1× more common, from 1 in 9,130 baby girls to 1 in 4,310.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2018 and 2023, its share of baby girls fell by 59%, from 1 in 4,790 to 1 in 11,800.

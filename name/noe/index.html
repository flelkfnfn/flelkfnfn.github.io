Noe name popularity since 1925

23,788 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Noe (1.8% girls, 98.2% boys). Half of them were born in 2000 or earlier, so the typical Noe is about 26 years old in 2026. Noe peaked in 2005 as the #431 name for boys, and it ranked #755 in 2025.

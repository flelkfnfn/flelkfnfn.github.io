Francine name popularity since 1925

35,669 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Francine. Half of them were born in 1957 or earlier, so the typical Francine is about 69 years old in 2026. Francine peaked in 1946 as the #216 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Francine births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1951. Measured as a share of all babies, Francine peaked in 1946.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1940 and 1945, Francine became 2.2× more common, from 1 in 3,820 baby girls to 1 in 1,750.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1972 and 1977, its share of baby girls fell by 49%, from 1 in 3,600 to 1 in 7,070.

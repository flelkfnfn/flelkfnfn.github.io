Junior name popularity since 1925

35,825 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Junior. Half of them were born in 1945 or earlier, so the typical Junior is about 81 years old in 2026. Junior peaked in 1926 as the #117 name for boys, and it ranked #752 in 2025.

Junior births per year, 1925–2025

1926 was the biggest year for Junior both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1932 and 1937, Junior's share of baby boys fell by 50%.
  • Fastest rise: Between 1990 and 1995, it became 1.6× more common, from 1 in 13,500 baby boys to 1 in 8,500.

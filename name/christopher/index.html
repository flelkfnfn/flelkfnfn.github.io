Christopher name popularity since 1925

2,073,757 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Christopher. Half of them were born in 1984 or earlier, so the typical Christopher is about 42 years old in 2026. Christopher peaked in 1984 as the #2 name for boys, and it ranked #68 in 2025.

Christopher births per year, 1925–2025

1984 was the biggest year for Christopher both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1945 and 1950, Christopher became 3.2× more common, from 1 in 1,180 baby boys to 1 in 370.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2007 and 2012, its share of baby boys fell by 35%, from 1 in 104 to 1 in 159.

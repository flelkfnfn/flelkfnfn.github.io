June name popularity since 1925

158,718 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named June (98.4% girls, 1.6% boys). Half of them were born in 1943 or earlier, so the typical June is about 83 years old in 2026. June peaked in 1925 as the #39 name for girls, and it ranked #150 in 2025.

June births per year, 1925–2025

1925 was the biggest year for June both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1964 and 1969, June's share of baby girls fell by 51%, from 1 in 1,380 to 1 in 2,810.
  • Fastest rise: Between 2005 and 2010, it became 4.5× more common, from 1 in 16,100 baby girls to 1 in 3,570.

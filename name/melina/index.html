Melina name popularity since 1925

19,722 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Melina. Half of them were born in 2006 or earlier, so the typical Melina is about 20 years old in 2026. Melina peaked in 2009 as the #456 name for girls, and it ranked #660 in 2025.

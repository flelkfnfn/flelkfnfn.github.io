Hope name popularity since 1925

92,998 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Hope. Half of them were born in 1996 or earlier, so the typical Hope is about 30 years old in 2026. Hope peaked in 2000 as the #146 name for girls, and it ranked #352 in 2025.

Hope births per year, 1925–2025

2000 was the biggest year for Hope both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1961 and 1966, Hope became 3.2× more common, from 1 in 3,940 baby girls to 1 in 1,250.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1977 and 1982, its share of baby girls fell by 51%, from 1 in 1,500 to 1 in 3,040.

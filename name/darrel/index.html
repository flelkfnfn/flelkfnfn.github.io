Darrel name popularity since 1925

28,428 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Darrel. Half of them were born in 1956 or earlier, so the typical Darrel is about 70 years old in 2026. Darrel peaked in 1939 as the #243 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Darrel births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1960. Measured as a share of all babies, Darrel peaked in 1939.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1927 and 1932, Darrel became 1.7× more common.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1987 and 1992, its share of baby boys fell by 43%, from 1 in 8,770 to 1 in 15,400.

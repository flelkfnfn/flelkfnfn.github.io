Rosie name popularity since 1925

60,141 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Rosie (99.4% girls, 0.6% boys). Half of them were born in 1949 or earlier, so the typical Rosie is about 77 years old in 2026. Rosie peaked in 1900 as the #116 name for girls, and it ranked #283 in 2025.

Rosie births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year on this chart was 1927. Measured as a share of all babies, Rosie peaked earlier, in 1900.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1965 and 1970, Rosie's share of baby girls fell by 45%, from 1 in 3,760 to 1 in 6,860.

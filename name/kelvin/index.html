Kelvin name popularity since 1925

51,829 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Kelvin (0.6% girls, 99.4% boys). Half of them were born in 1980 or earlier, so the typical Kelvin is about 46 years old in 2026. Kelvin peaked in 1963 as the #210 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Kelvin births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1961. Measured as a share of all babies, Kelvin peaked in 1963.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1951 and 1956, Kelvin became 3.7× more common, from 1 in 10,400 baby boys to 1 in 2,790.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2019 and 2024, its share of baby boys fell by 34%, from 1 in 5,660 to 1 in 8,570.

Skyler name popularity since 1925

69,539 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Skyler (39.8% girls, 60.2% boys). Half of them were born in 2004 or earlier, so the typical Skyler is about 22 years old in 2026. Skyler peaked in 2002 as the #222 name for boys, and it ranked #783 in 2025.

Skyler births per year, 1925–2025

2002 was the biggest year for Skyler both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1981 and 1986, Skyler became 2.4× more common, from 1 in 10,200 baby boys to 1 in 4,350.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2016 and 2021, its share of baby boys fell by 46%, from 1 in 2,460 to 1 in 4,520.

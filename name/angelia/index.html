Angelia name popularity since 1925

22,421 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Angelia. Half of them were born in 1970 or earlier, so the typical Angelia is about 56 years old in 2026. Angelia peaked in 1971 as the #294 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Angelia births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1966. Measured as a share of all babies, Angelia peaked in 1971.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1985 and 1990, Angelia became 3.1× more common, from 1 in 12,100 baby girls to 1 in 3,920.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1990 and 1995, its share of baby girls fell by 73%, from 1 in 3,920 to 1 in 14,800.

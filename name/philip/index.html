Philip name popularity since 1925

306,288 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Philip. Half of them were born in 1960 or earlier, so the typical Philip is about 66 years old in 2026. Philip peaked in 1948 as the #54 name for boys, and it ranked #528 in 2025.

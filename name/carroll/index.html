Carroll name popularity since 1925

32,205 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Carroll (16.3% girls, 83.7% boys). Half of them were born in 1943 or earlier, so the typical Carroll is about 83 years old in 2026. Carroll peaked in 1934 as the #164 name for boys, and fewer than 5 boys got it in 2025.

Brianne name popularity since 1925

20,504 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Brianne. Half of them were born in 1990 or earlier, so the typical Brianne is about 36 years old in 2026. Brianne peaked in 1979 as the #168 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Brianne births per year, 1925–2025

1979 was the biggest year for Brianne both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1978 and 1983, Brianne became 3.3× more common, from 1 in 7,580 baby girls to 1 in 2,300.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1979 and 1984, its share of baby girls fell by 53%, from 1 in 965 to 1 in 2,070.

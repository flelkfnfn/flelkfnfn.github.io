Celeste name popularity since 1925

62,953 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Celeste. Half of them were born in 1994 or earlier, so the typical Celeste is about 32 years old in 2026. Celeste peaked in 2025 as the #188 name for girls, and it ranked #188 in 2025.

Celeste births per year, 1925–2025

2025 was the biggest year for Celeste both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1946 and 1951, Celeste became 2.4× more common, from 1 in 7,210 baby girls to 1 in 3,020.

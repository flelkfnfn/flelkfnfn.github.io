Vance name popularity since 1925

24,452 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Vance. Half of them were born in 1970 or earlier, so the typical Vance is about 56 years old in 2026. Vance peaked in 1969 as the #328 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Vance births per year, 1925–2025

1969 was the biggest year for Vance both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1969 and 1974, Vance's share of baby boys fell by 64%, from 1 in 3,220 to 1 in 8,930.
  • Fastest rise: Between 2000 and 2005, it became 1.8× more common, from 1 in 13,900 baby boys to 1 in 7,650.

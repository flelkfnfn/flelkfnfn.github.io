Joyce name popularity since 1925

492,927 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Joyce. Half of them were born in 1946 or earlier, so the typical Joyce is about 80 years old in 2026. Joyce peaked in 1941 as the #13 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Joyce births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1942. Measured as a share of all babies, Joyce peaked in 1941.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1925 and 1930, Joyce became 2.4× more common.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1965 and 1970, its share of baby girls fell by 50%, from 1 in 428 to 1 in 861.

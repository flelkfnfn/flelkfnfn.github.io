Cristian name popularity since 1925

71,051 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Cristian (1.6% girls, 98.4% boys). Half of them were born in 2004 or earlier, so the typical Cristian is about 22 years old in 2026. Cristian peaked in 1996 as the #130 name for boys, and it ranked #362 in 2025.

Cristian births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 2006. Measured as a share of all babies, Cristian peaked in 1996.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1988 and 1993, Cristian became 7.1× more common, from 1 in 7,810 baby boys to 1 in 1,100.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2009 and 2014, its share of baby boys fell by 42%, from 1 in 827 to 1 in 1,430.

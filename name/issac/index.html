Issac name popularity since 1925

23,566 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Issac. Half of them were born in 2004 or earlier, so the typical Issac is about 22 years old in 2026. Issac peaked in 2008 as the #376 name for boys, and it ranked #915 in 2025.

Tommy name popularity since 1925

176,551 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Tommy (1.5% girls, 98.5% boys). Half of them were born in 1957 or earlier, so the typical Tommy is about 69 years old in 2026. Tommy peaked in 1944 as the #70 name for boys, and it ranked #658 in 2025.

Tommy births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1947. Measured as a share of all babies, Tommy peaked in 1944.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1926 and 1931, Tommy became 2.2× more common.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2002 and 2007, its share of baby boys fell by 37%, from 1 in 2,420 to 1 in 3,820.

Chad name popularity since 1925

241,660 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Chad. Half of them were born in 1978 or earlier, so the typical Chad is about 48 years old in 2026. Chad peaked in 1973 as the #25 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Chad births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1972. Measured as a share of all babies, Chad peaked in 1973.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1962 and 1967, Chad became 7.5× more common, from 1 in 4,460 baby boys to 1 in 592.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1998 and 2003, its share of baby boys fell by 54%, from 1 in 1,060 to 1 in 2,290.

Sadie name popularity since 1925

111,578 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Sadie. Half of them were born in 2010 or earlier, so the typical Sadie is about 16 years old in 2026. Sadie peaked in 1881 as the #69 name for girls, and it ranked #50 in 2025.

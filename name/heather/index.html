Heather name popularity since 1925

527,356 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Heather. Half of them were born in 1980 or earlier, so the typical Heather is about 46 years old in 2026. Heather peaked in 1975 as the #3 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Heather births per year, 1925–2025

1975 was the biggest year for Heather both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1965 and 1970, Heather became 5.9× more common, from 1 in 707 baby girls to 1 in 119.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2002 and 2007, its share of baby girls fell by 64%, from 1 in 869 to 1 in 2,410.

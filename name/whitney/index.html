Whitney name popularity since 1925

102,983 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Whitney (94.8% girls, 5.2% boys). Half of them were born in 1989 or earlier, so the typical Whitney is about 37 years old in 2026. Whitney peaked in 1986 as the #32 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Whitney births per year, 1925–2025

1986 was the biggest year for Whitney both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1981 and 1986, Whitney became 7.1× more common, from 1 in 1,280 baby girls to 1 in 180.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1996 and 2001, its share of baby girls fell by 62%, from 1 in 759 to 1 in 2,000.

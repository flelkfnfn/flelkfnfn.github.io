Megan name popularity since 1925

441,142 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Megan. Half of them were born in 1991 or earlier, so the typical Megan is about 35 years old in 2026. Megan peaked in 1985 as the #10 name for girls, and it ranked #889 in 2025.

Megan births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1990. Measured as a share of all babies, Megan peaked in 1985.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1970 and 1975, Megan became 4× more common, from 1 in 1,380 baby girls to 1 in 348.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2010 and 2015, its share of baby girls fell by 65%, from 1 in 739 to 1 in 2,090.

Braden name popularity since 1925

46,492 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Braden (0.6% girls, 99.4% boys). Half of them were born in 2005 or earlier, so the typical Braden is about 21 years old in 2026. Braden peaked in 2005 as the #133 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Braden births per year, 1925–2025

2005 was the biggest year for Braden both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1991 and 1996, Braden became 2.4× more common, from 1 in 4,600 baby boys to 1 in 1,890.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2008 and 2013, its share of baby boys fell by 59%, from 1 in 808 to 1 in 1,960.

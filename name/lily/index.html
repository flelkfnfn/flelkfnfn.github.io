Lily name popularity since 1925

182,252 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Lily. Half of them were born in 2011 or earlier, so the typical Lily is about 15 years old in 2026. Lily peaked in 2011 as the #15 name for girls, and it ranked #18 in 2025.

Lily births per year, 1925–2025

2011 was the biggest year for Lily both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1941 and 1946, Lily's share of baby girls fell by 43%, from 1 in 7,280 to 1 in 12,900.
  • Fastest rise: Between 1994 and 1999, it became 3.3× more common, from 1 in 2,760 baby girls to 1 in 836.

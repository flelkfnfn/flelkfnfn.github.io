Brooke name popularity since 1925

200,473 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Brooke (99.0% girls, 1.0% boys). Half of them were born in 1997 or earlier, so the typical Brooke is about 29 years old in 2026. Brooke peaked in 1996 as the #45 name for girls, and it ranked #313 in 2025.

Brooke births per year, 1925–2025

1996 was the biggest year for Brooke both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1965 and 1970, Brooke became 5.2× more common, from 1 in 12,100 baby girls to 1 in 2,310.

Colette name popularity since 1925

27,420 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Colette. Half of them were born in 1983 or earlier, so the typical Colette is about 43 years old in 2026. Colette peaked in 2025 as the #316 name for girls, and it ranked #316 in 2025.

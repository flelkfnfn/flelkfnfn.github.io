Trey name popularity since 1925

40,573 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Trey. Half of them were born in 2001 or earlier, so the typical Trey is about 25 years old in 2026. Trey peaked in 1999 as the #189 name for boys, and it ranked #890 in 2025.

Trey births per year, 1925–2025

1999 was the biggest year for Trey both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1988 and 1993, Trey became 3.2× more common, from 1 in 3,800 baby boys to 1 in 1,200.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2011 and 2016, its share of baby boys fell by 52%, from 1 in 1,950 to 1 in 4,070.

Dawson name popularity since 1925

53,729 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Dawson (2.6% girls, 97.4% boys). Half of them were born in 2012 or earlier, so the typical Dawson is about 14 years old in 2026. Dawson peaked in 2024 as the #139 name for boys, and it ranked #143 in 2025.

Dawson births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1999. Measured as a share of all babies, Dawson peaked in 2024.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1994 and 1999, Dawson became 18× more common, from 1 in 12,200 baby boys to 1 in 685.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1999 and 2004, its share of baby boys fell by 39%, from 1 in 685 to 1 in 1,130.

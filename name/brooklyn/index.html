Brooklyn name popularity since 1925

127,055 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Brooklyn (98.5% girls, 1.5% boys). Half of them were born in 2012 or earlier, so the typical Brooklyn is about 14 years old in 2026. Brooklyn peaked in 2011 as the #21 name for girls, and it ranked #118 in 2025.

Brooklyn births per year, 1925–2025

2011 was the biggest year for Brooklyn both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1989 and 1994, Brooklyn became 4.1× more common, from 1 in 11,200 baby girls to 1 in 2,710.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2020 and 2025, its share of baby girls fell by 43%, from 1 in 401 to 1 in 699.

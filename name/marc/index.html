Marc name popularity since 1925

140,887 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Marc. Half of them were born in 1972 or earlier, so the typical Marc is about 54 years old in 2026. Marc peaked in 1970 as the #60 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Marc births per year, 1925–2025

1970 was the biggest year for Marc both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1943 and 1948, Marc became 4.5× more common, from 1 in 6,340 baby boys to 1 in 1,400.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2000 and 2005, its share of baby boys fell by 50%, from 1 in 1,320 to 1 in 2,630.

Randal name popularity since 1925

29,091 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Randal. Half of them were born in 1961 or earlier, so the typical Randal is about 65 years old in 2026. Randal peaked in 1958 as the #190 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Randal births per year, 1925–2025

1958 was the biggest year for Randal both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1944 and 1949, Randal became 3.4× more common, from 1 in 13,200 baby boys to 1 in 3,940.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1969 and 1974, its share of baby boys fell by 41%, from 1 in 3,120 to 1 in 5,260.

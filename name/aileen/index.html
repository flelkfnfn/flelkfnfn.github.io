Aileen name popularity since 1925

31,293 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Aileen. Half of them were born in 1994 or earlier, so the typical Aileen is about 32 years old in 2026. Aileen peaked in 1926 as the #289 name for girls, and it ranked #799 in 2025.

Aileen births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 2009. Measured as a share of all babies, Aileen peaked in 1926.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1936 and 1941, Aileen's share of baby girls fell by 47%, from 1 in 5,480 to 1 in 10,200.
  • Fastest rise: Between 1995 and 2000, it became 2.1× more common, from 1 in 7,480 baby girls to 1 in 3,620.

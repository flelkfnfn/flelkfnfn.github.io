Janet name popularity since 1925

533,964 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Janet. Half of them were born in 1952 or earlier, so the typical Janet is about 74 years old in 2026. Janet peaked in 1942 as the #19 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Janet births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1954. Measured as a share of all babies, Janet peaked in 1942.

Perry name popularity since 1925

69,566 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Perry (4.8% girls, 95.2% boys). Half of them were born in 1960 or earlier, so the typical Perry is about 66 years old in 2026. Perry peaked in 1959 as the #132 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Perry births per year, 1925–2025

1959 was the biggest year for Perry both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1954 and 1959, Perry became 2.1× more common, from 1 in 1,630 baby boys to 1 in 776.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1967 and 1972, its share of baby boys fell by 43%, from 1 in 1,720 to 1 in 2,990.

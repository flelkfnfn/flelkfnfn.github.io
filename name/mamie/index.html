Mamie name popularity since 1925

28,199 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Mamie. Half of them were born in 1938 or earlier, so the typical Mamie is about 88 years old in 2026. Mamie peaked in 1888 as the #53 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

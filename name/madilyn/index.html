Madilyn name popularity since 1925

23,625 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Madilyn. Half of them were born in 2013 or earlier, so the typical Madilyn is about 13 years old in 2026. Madilyn peaked in 2012 as the #287 name for girls, and it ranked #464 in 2025.

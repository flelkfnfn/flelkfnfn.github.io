Ericka name popularity since 1925

23,466 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Ericka. Half of them were born in 1986 or earlier, so the typical Ericka is about 40 years old in 2026. Ericka peaked in 1971 as the #301 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Ericka births per year, 1925–2025

1971 was the biggest year for Ericka both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1967 and 1972, Ericka became 7.6× more common, from 1 in 14,400 baby girls to 1 in 1,890.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2007 and 2012, its share of baby girls fell by 45%, from 1 in 8,860 to 1 in 16,200.

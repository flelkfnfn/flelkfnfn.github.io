Fredrick name popularity since 1925

56,747 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Fredrick. Half of them were born in 1960 or earlier, so the typical Fredrick is about 66 years old in 2026. Fredrick peaked in 1880 as the #126 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

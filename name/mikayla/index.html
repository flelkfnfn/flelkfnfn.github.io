Mikayla name popularity since 1925

56,724 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Mikayla. Half of them were born in 2002 or earlier, so the typical Mikayla is about 24 years old in 2026. Mikayla peaked in 1998 as the #82 name for girls, and it ranked #748 in 2025.

Mikayla births per year, 1925–2025

1998 was the biggest year for Mikayla both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1990 and 1995, Mikayla became 8.1× more common, from 1 in 6,440 baby girls to 1 in 791.

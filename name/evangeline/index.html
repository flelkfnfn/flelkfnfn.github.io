Evangeline name popularity since 1925

32,974 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Evangeline. Half of them were born in 2013 or earlier, so the typical Evangeline is about 13 years old in 2026. Evangeline peaked in 2025 as the #147 name for girls, and it ranked #147 in 2025.

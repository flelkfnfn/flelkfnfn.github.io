Abel name popularity since 1925

64,856 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Abel. Half of them were born in 2011 or earlier, so the typical Abel is about 15 years old in 2026. Abel peaked in 2015 as the #124 name for boys, and it ranked #231 in 2025.

Abel births per year, 1925–2025

2015 was the biggest year for Abel both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 2010 and 2015, Abel became 2.9× more common, from 1 in 1,700 baby boys to 1 in 591.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2018 and 2023, its share of baby boys fell by 34%, from 1 in 677 to 1 in 1,030.

Fannie name popularity since 1925

32,127 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Fannie. Half of them were born in 1938 or earlier, so the typical Fannie is about 88 years old in 2026. Fannie peaked in 1883 as the #47 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

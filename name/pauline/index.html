Pauline name popularity since 1925

119,741 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Pauline. Half of them were born in 1938 or earlier, so the typical Pauline is about 88 years old in 2026. Pauline peaked in 1915 as the #32 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Cornelius name popularity since 1925

28,959 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Cornelius (1.6% girls, 98.4% boys). Half of them were born in 1971 or earlier, so the typical Cornelius is about 55 years old in 2026. Cornelius peaked in 1882 as the #130 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

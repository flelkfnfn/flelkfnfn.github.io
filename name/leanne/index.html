Leanne name popularity since 1925

29,632 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Leanne. Half of them were born in 1974 or earlier, so the typical Leanne is about 52 years old in 2026. Leanne peaked in 1969 as the #321 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Leanne births per year, 1925–2025

1969 was the biggest year for Leanne both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1951 and 1956, Leanne became 1.8× more common, from 1 in 8,340 baby girls to 1 in 4,530.

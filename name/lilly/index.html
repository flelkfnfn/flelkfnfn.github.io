Lilly name popularity since 1925

59,612 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Lilly. Half of them were born in 2010 or earlier, so the typical Lilly is about 16 years old in 2026. Lilly peaked in 2011 as the #103 name for girls, and it ranked #252 in 2025.

Lilly births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 2010. Measured as a share of all babies, Lilly peaked in 2011.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1938 and 1943, Lilly's share of baby girls fell by 46%, from 1 in 4,900 to 1 in 9,120.
  • Fastest rise: Between 1994 and 1999, it became 3.6× more common, from 1 in 13,200 baby girls to 1 in 3,630.

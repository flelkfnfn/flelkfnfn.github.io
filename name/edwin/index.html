Edwin name popularity since 1925

195,568 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Edwin. Half of them were born in 1963 or earlier, so the typical Edwin is about 63 years old in 2026. Edwin peaked in 1915 as the #53 name for boys, and it ranked #398 in 2025.

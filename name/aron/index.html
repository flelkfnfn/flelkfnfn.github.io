Aron name popularity since 1925

20,303 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Aron (1.7% girls, 98.3% boys). Half of them were born in 1996 or earlier, so the typical Aron is about 30 years old in 2026. Aron peaked in 2025 as the #711 name for boys, and it ranked #711 in 2025.

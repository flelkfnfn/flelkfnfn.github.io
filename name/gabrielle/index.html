Gabrielle name popularity since 1925

139,011 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Gabrielle (99.4% girls, 0.6% boys). Half of them were born in 2000 or earlier, so the typical Gabrielle is about 26 years old in 2026. Gabrielle peaked in 1998 as the #47 name for girls, and it ranked #633 in 2025.

Gabrielle births per year, 1925–2025

1998 was the biggest year for Gabrielle both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1985 and 1990, Gabrielle became 3.6× more common, from 1 in 1,920 baby girls to 1 in 536.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2010 and 2015, its share of baby girls fell by 49%, from 1 in 565 to 1 in 1,110.

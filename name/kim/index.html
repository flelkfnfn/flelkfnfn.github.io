Kim name popularity since 1925

214,689 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Kim (83.7% girls, 16.3% boys). Half of them were born in 1961 or earlier, so the typical Kim is about 65 years old in 2026. Kim peaked in 1960 as the #34 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Kim births per year, 1925–2025

1960 was the biggest year for Kim both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1950 and 1955, Kim became 18× more common, from 1 in 6,010 baby girls to 1 in 344.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1973 and 1978, its share of baby girls fell by 64%, from 1 in 664 to 1 in 1,860.

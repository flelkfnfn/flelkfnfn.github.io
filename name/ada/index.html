Ada name popularity since 1925

55,377 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Ada. Half of them were born in 1967 or earlier, so the typical Ada is about 59 years old in 2026. Ada peaked in 1880 as the #33 name for girls, and it ranked #219 in 2025.

Monica name popularity since 1925

248,557 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Monica. Half of them were born in 1978 or earlier, so the typical Monica is about 48 years old in 2026. Monica peaked in 1973 as the #40 name for girls, and it ranked #737 in 2025.

Monica births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1979. Measured as a share of all babies, Monica peaked in 1973.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1946 and 1951, Monica became 2.5× more common, from 1 in 3,440 baby girls to 1 in 1,390.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1996 and 2001, its share of baby girls fell by 60%, from 1 in 405 to 1 in 1,000.

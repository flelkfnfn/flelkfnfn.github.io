Imogene name popularity since 1925

18,432 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Imogene. Half of them were born in 1932 or earlier, so the typical Imogene is about 94 years old in 2026. Imogene peaked in 1927 as the #157 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

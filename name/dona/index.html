Dona name popularity since 1925

16,229 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Dona (99.1% girls, 0.9% boys). Half of them were born in 1948 or earlier, so the typical Dona is about 78 years old in 2026. Dona peaked in 1933 as the #302 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Dona births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1954. Measured as a share of all babies, Dona peaked in 1933.

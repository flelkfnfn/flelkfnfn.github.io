Blaine name popularity since 1925

32,347 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Blaine (3.8% girls, 96.2% boys). Half of them were born in 1988 or earlier, so the typical Blaine is about 38 years old in 2026. Blaine peaked in 1884 as the #211 name for boys, and it ranked #992 in 2025.

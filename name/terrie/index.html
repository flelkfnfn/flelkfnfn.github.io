Terrie name popularity since 1925

20,052 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Terrie (98.3% girls, 1.7% boys). Half of them were born in 1960 or earlier, so the typical Terrie is about 66 years old in 2026. Terrie peaked in 1961 as the #274 name for girls, and fewer than 5 girls got it in 2025.

Terrie births per year, 1925–2025

1961 was the biggest year for Terrie both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1950 and 1955, Terrie became 2.4× more common, from 1 in 5,340 baby girls to 1 in 2,270.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1970 and 1975, its share of baby girls fell by 51%, from 1 in 4,320 to 1 in 8,840.

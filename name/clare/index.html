Clare name popularity since 1925

28,925 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Clare (94.4% girls, 5.6% boys). Half of them were born in 1987 or earlier, so the typical Clare is about 39 years old in 2026. Clare peaked in 1884 as the #320 name for girls, and it ranked #924 in 2025.

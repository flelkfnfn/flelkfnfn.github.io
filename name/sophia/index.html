Sophia name popularity since 1925

425,380 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Sophia. Half of them were born in 2012 or earlier, so the typical Sophia is about 14 years old in 2026. Sophia peaked in 2012 as the #1 name for girls, and it ranked #5 in 2025.

Sophia births per year, 1925–2025

2012 was the biggest year for Sophia both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1925 and 1930, Sophia's share of baby girls fell by 44%.
  • Fastest rise: Between 1995 and 2000, it became 3.6× more common, from 1 in 996 baby girls to 1 in 277.

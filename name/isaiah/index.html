Isaiah name popularity since 1925

252,396 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Isaiah. Half of them were born in 2008 or earlier, so the typical Isaiah is about 18 years old in 2026. Isaiah peaked in 2006 as the #39 name for boys, and it ranked #57 in 2025.

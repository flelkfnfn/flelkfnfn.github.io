Landon name popularity since 1925

187,235 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Landon (0.6% girls, 99.4% boys). Half of them were born in 2010 or earlier, so the typical Landon is about 16 years old in 2026. Landon peaked in 2010 as the #33 name for boys, and it ranked #127 in 2025.

Landon births per year, 1925–2025

2010 was the biggest year for Landon both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 2001 and 2006, Landon became 4.8× more common, from 1 in 1,030 baby boys to 1 in 215.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2020 and 2025, its share of baby boys fell by 40%, from 1 in 361 to 1 in 601.

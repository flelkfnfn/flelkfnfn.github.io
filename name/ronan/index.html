Ronan name popularity since 1925

20,938 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Ronan (1.2% girls, 98.8% boys). Half of them were born in 2017 or earlier, so the typical Ronan is about 9 years old in 2026. Ronan peaked in 2025 as the #247 name for boys, and it ranked #247 in 2025.

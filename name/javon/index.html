Javon name popularity since 1925

18,406 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Javon (4.1% girls, 95.9% boys). Half of them were born in 2001 or earlier, so the typical Javon is about 25 years old in 2026. Javon peaked in 2001 as the #357 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Javon births per year, 1925–2025

2001 was the biggest year for Javon both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1988 and 1993, Javon became 2.2× more common, from 1 in 8,550 baby boys to 1 in 3,950.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2007 and 2012, its share of baby boys fell by 51%, from 1 in 2,900 to 1 in 5,930.

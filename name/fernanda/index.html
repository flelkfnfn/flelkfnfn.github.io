Fernanda name popularity since 1925

20,031 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Fernanda. Half of them were born in 2010 or earlier, so the typical Fernanda is about 16 years old in 2026. Fernanda peaked in 2009 as the #258 name for girls, and it ranked #551 in 2025.

Fernanda births per year, 1925–2025

2009 was the biggest year for Fernanda both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1997 and 2002, Fernanda became 3.7× more common, from 1 in 10,500 baby girls to 1 in 2,850.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2009 and 2014, its share of baby girls fell by 53%, from 1 in 1,440 to 1 in 3,050.

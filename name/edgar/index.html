Edgar name popularity since 1925

124,806 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Edgar. Half of them were born in 1989 or earlier, so the typical Edgar is about 37 years old in 2026. Edgar peaked in 1881 as the #51 name for boys, and it ranked #486 in 2025.

Edgar births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year on this chart was 2005. Measured as a share of all babies, Edgar peaked earlier, in 1881.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1984 and 1989, Edgar became 2.3× more common, from 1 in 2,160 baby boys to 1 in 950.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2007 and 2012, its share of baby boys fell by 40%, from 1 in 858 to 1 in 1,440.

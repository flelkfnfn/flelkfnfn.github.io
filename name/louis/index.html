Louis name popularity since 1925

295,259 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Louis (0.6% girls, 99.4% boys). Half of them were born in 1953 or earlier, so the typical Louis is about 73 years old in 2026. Louis peaked in 1908 as the #22 name for boys, and it ranked #249 in 2025.

Jennie name popularity since 1925

58,874 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Jennie. Half of them were born in 1954 or earlier, so the typical Jennie is about 72 years old in 2026. Jennie peaked in 1880 as the #24 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Jennie births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year on this chart was 1925. Measured as a share of all babies, Jennie peaked earlier, in 1880.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1967 and 1972, Jennie became 2.1× more common, from 1 in 3,740 baby girls to 1 in 1,760.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1982 and 1987, its share of baby girls fell by 46%, from 1 in 2,030 to 1 in 3,730.

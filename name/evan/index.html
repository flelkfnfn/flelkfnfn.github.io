Evan name popularity since 1925

289,838 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Evan (1.6% girls, 98.4% boys). Half of them were born in 2003 or earlier, so the typical Evan is about 23 years old in 2026. Evan peaked in 2009 as the #35 name for boys, and it ranked #153 in 2025.

Evan births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 2007. Measured as a share of all babies, Evan peaked in 2009.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1978 and 1983, Evan became 2.4× more common, from 1 in 1,420 baby boys to 1 in 588.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2010 and 2015, its share of baby boys fell by 39%, from 1 in 197 to 1 in 324.

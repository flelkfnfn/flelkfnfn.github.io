Margie name popularity since 1925

83,534 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Margie. Half of them were born in 1942 or earlier, so the typical Margie is about 84 years old in 2026. Margie peaked in 1933 as the #95 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

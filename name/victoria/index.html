Victoria name popularity since 1925

504,844 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Victoria. Half of them were born in 1994 or earlier, so the typical Victoria is about 32 years old in 2026. Victoria peaked in 1993 as the #19 name for girls, and it ranked #54 in 2025.

Victoria births per year, 1925–2025

1993 was the biggest year for Victoria both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1940 and 1945, Victoria became 3.4× more common, from 1 in 1,660 baby girls to 1 in 490.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2018 and 2023, its share of baby girls fell by 34%, from 1 in 238 to 1 in 360.

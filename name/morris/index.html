Morris name popularity since 1925

46,725 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Morris (0.8% girls, 99.2% boys). Half of them were born in 1948 or earlier, so the typical Morris is about 78 years old in 2026. Morris peaked in 1912 as the #82 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

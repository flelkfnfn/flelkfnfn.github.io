Kelly name popularity since 1925

554,235 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Kelly (85.5% girls, 14.5% boys). Half of them were born in 1976 or earlier, so the typical Kelly is about 50 years old in 2026. Kelly peaked in 1977 as the #10 name for girls, and it ranked #852 in 2025.

Kelly births per year, 1925–2025

1977 was the biggest year for Kelly both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1953 and 1958, Kelly became 19× more common, from 1 in 8,320 baby girls to 1 in 450.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2003 and 2008, its share of baby girls fell by 51%, from 1 in 668 to 1 in 1,350.

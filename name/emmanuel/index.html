Emmanuel name popularity since 1925

91,017 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Emmanuel. Half of them were born in 2007 or earlier, so the typical Emmanuel is about 19 years old in 2026. Emmanuel peaked in 2015 as the #151 name for boys, and it ranked #179 in 2025.

Wesley name popularity since 1925

232,391 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Wesley (1.3% girls, 98.7% boys). Half of them were born in 1988 or earlier, so the typical Wesley is about 38 years old in 2026. Wesley peaked in 2025 as the #52 name for boys, and it ranked #52 in 2025.

Wesley births per year, 1925–2025

2025 was the biggest year for Wesley both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1972 and 1977, Wesley became 2.5× more common, from 1 in 960 baby boys to 1 in 382.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1977 and 1982, its share of baby boys fell by 36%, from 1 in 382 to 1 in 599.

Brad name popularity since 1925

83,255 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Brad. Half of them were born in 1970 or earlier, so the typical Brad is about 56 years old in 2026. Brad peaked in 1975 as the #98 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Brad births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1960. Measured as a share of all babies, Brad peaked in 1975.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1948 and 1953, Brad became 5.5× more common, from 1 in 6,600 baby boys to 1 in 1,200.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1987 and 1992, its share of baby boys fell by 57%, from 1 in 1,820 to 1 in 4,240.

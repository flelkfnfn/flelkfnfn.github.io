Dustin name popularity since 1925

209,653 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Dustin (0.7% girls, 99.3% boys). Half of them were born in 1987 or earlier, so the typical Dustin is about 39 years old in 2026. Dustin peaked in 1984 as the #41 name for boys, and it ranked #729 in 2025.

Dustin births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1985. Measured as a share of all babies, Dustin peaked in 1984.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1968 and 1973, Dustin became 4× more common, from 1 in 3,880 baby boys to 1 in 964.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1995 and 2000, its share of baby boys fell by 57%, from 1 in 322 to 1 in 749.

Miracle name popularity since 1925

22,006 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Miracle (97.6% girls, 2.4% boys). Half of them were born in 2012 or earlier, so the typical Miracle is about 14 years old in 2026. Miracle peaked in 2019 as the #367 name for girls, and it ranked #492 in 2025.

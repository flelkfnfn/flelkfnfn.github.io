Elaine name popularity since 1925

238,699 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Elaine. Half of them were born in 1949 or earlier, so the typical Elaine is about 77 years old in 2026. Elaine peaked in 1945 as the #44 name for girls, and it ranked #305 in 2025.

Elaine births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1947. Measured as a share of all babies, Elaine peaked in 1945.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1965 and 1970, Elaine's share of baby girls fell by 42%, from 1 in 707 to 1 in 1,220.
  • Fastest rise: Between 2018 and 2023, it became 1.8× more common, from 1 in 3,620 baby girls to 1 in 1,990.

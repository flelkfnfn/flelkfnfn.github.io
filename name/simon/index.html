Simon name popularity since 1925

63,533 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Simon. Half of them were born in 2003 or earlier, so the typical Simon is about 23 years old in 2026. Simon peaked in 1888 as the #142 name for boys, and it ranked #230 in 2025.

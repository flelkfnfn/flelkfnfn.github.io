Vickie name popularity since 1925

110,229 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Vickie. Half of them were born in 1957 or earlier, so the typical Vickie is about 69 years old in 2026. Vickie peaked in 1956 as the #68 name for girls, and fewer than 5 girls got it in 2025.

Vickie births per year, 1925–2025

1956 was the biggest year for Vickie both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1941 and 1946, Vickie became 5.1× more common, from 1 in 6,710 baby girls to 1 in 1,310.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1973 and 1978, its share of baby girls fell by 66%, from 1 in 1,550 to 1 in 4,530.

Janie name popularity since 1925

60,321 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Janie. Half of them were born in 1953 or earlier, so the typical Janie is about 73 years old in 2026. Janie peaked in 1889 as the #148 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Janie births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year on this chart was 1947. Measured as a share of all babies, Janie peaked earlier, in 1889.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1941 and 1946, Janie became 1.6× more common, from 1 in 1,520 baby girls to 1 in 922.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1963 and 1968, its share of baby girls fell by 39%, from 1 in 2,050 to 1 in 3,350.

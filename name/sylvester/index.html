Sylvester name popularity since 1925

31,335 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Sylvester (1.1% girls, 98.9% boys). Half of them were born in 1952 or earlier, so the typical Sylvester is about 74 years old in 2026. Sylvester peaked in 1901 as the #139 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

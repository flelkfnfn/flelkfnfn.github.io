Justine name popularity since 1925

38,059 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Justine (93.7% girls, 6.3% boys). Half of them were born in 1988 or earlier, so the typical Justine is about 38 years old in 2026. Justine peaked in 1987 as the #176 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Justine births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1988. Measured as a share of all babies, Justine peaked in 1987.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1982 and 1987, Justine became 5× more common, from 1 in 5,430 baby girls to 1 in 1,100.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1996 and 2001, its share of baby girls fell by 57%, from 1 in 1,600 to 1 in 3,740.

Terence name popularity since 1925

34,023 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Terence. Half of them were born in 1968 or earlier, so the typical Terence is about 58 years old in 2026. Terence peaked in 1964 as the #241 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

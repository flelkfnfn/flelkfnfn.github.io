Ruthie name popularity since 1925

19,479 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Ruthie. Half of them were born in 1949 or earlier, so the typical Ruthie is about 77 years old in 2026. Ruthie peaked in 1935 as the #314 name for girls, and it ranked #781 in 2025.

Ruthie births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1927. Measured as a share of all babies, Ruthie peaked in 1935.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1961 and 1966, Ruthie's share of baby girls fell by 38%, from 1 in 8,700 to 1 in 14,100.
  • Fastest rise: Between 2017 and 2022, it became 2× more common, from 1 in 12,700 baby girls to 1 in 6,310.

Johnny name popularity since 1925

311,609 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Johnny (1.1% girls, 98.9% boys). Half of them were born in 1959 or earlier, so the typical Johnny is about 67 years old in 2026. Johnny peaked in 1945 as the #45 name for boys, and it ranked #458 in 2025.

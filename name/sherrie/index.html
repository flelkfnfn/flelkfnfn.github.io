Sherrie name popularity since 1925

35,141 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Sherrie. Half of them were born in 1962 or earlier, so the typical Sherrie is about 64 years old in 2026. Sherrie peaked in 1962 as the #215 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Sherrie births per year, 1925–2025

1962 was the biggest year for Sherrie both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1951 and 1956, Sherrie became 2.2× more common, from 1 in 3,320 baby girls to 1 in 1,500.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1973 and 1978, its share of baby girls fell by 52%, from 1 in 2,260 to 1 in 4,700.

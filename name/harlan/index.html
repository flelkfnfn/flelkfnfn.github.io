Harlan name popularity since 1925

22,383 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Harlan (2.2% girls, 97.8% boys). Half of them were born in 1956 or earlier, so the typical Harlan is about 70 years old in 2026. Harlan peaked in 1924 as the #253 name for boys, and it ranked #617 in 2025.

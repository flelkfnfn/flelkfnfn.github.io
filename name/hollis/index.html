Hollis name popularity since 1925

17,498 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Hollis (29.3% girls, 70.7% boys). Half of them were born in 1962 or earlier, so the typical Hollis is about 64 years old in 2026. Hollis peaked in 1908 as the #292 name for boys, and it ranked #894 in 2025.

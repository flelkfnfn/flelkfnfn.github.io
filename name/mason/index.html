Mason name popularity since 1925

343,822 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Mason (1.0% girls, 99.0% boys). Half of them were born in 2011 or earlier, so the typical Mason is about 15 years old in 2026. Mason peaked in 2011 as the #2 name for boys, and it ranked #39 in 2025.

Mason births per year, 1925–2025

2011 was the biggest year for Mason both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1983 and 1988, Mason became 4× more common, from 1 in 7,410 baby boys to 1 in 1,870.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2019 and 2024, its share of baby boys fell by 41%, from 1 in 156 to 1 in 265.

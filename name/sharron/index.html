Sharron name popularity since 1925

17,210 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Sharron (97.9% girls, 2.1% boys). Half of them were born in 1951 or earlier, so the typical Sharron is about 75 years old in 2026. Sharron peaked in 1943 as the #226 name for girls, and fewer than 5 girls got it in 2025.

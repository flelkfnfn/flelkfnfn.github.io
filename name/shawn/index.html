Shawn name popularity since 1925

339,597 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Shawn (9.7% girls, 90.3% boys). Half of them were born in 1977 or earlier, so the typical Shawn is about 49 years old in 2026. Shawn peaked in 1973 as the #27 name for boys, and it ranked #682 in 2025.

Shawn births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1971. Measured as a share of all babies, Shawn peaked in 1973.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1954 and 1959, Shawn became 4.4× more common, from 1 in 4,750 baby boys to 1 in 1,080.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2018 and 2023, its share of baby boys fell by 43%, from 1 in 2,230 to 1 in 3,910.

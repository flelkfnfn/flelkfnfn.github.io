Stevie name popularity since 1925

30,156 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Stevie (58.8% girls, 41.2% boys). Half of them were born in 1994 or earlier, so the typical Stevie is about 32 years old in 2026. Stevie peaked in 2025 as the #203 name for girls, and it ranked #203 in 2025.

Bobbi name popularity since 1925

24,280 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Bobbi. Half of them were born in 1975 or earlier, so the typical Bobbi is about 51 years old in 2026. Bobbi peaked in 1971 as the #314 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Bobbi births per year, 1925–2025

1971 was the biggest year for Bobbi both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1952 and 1957, Bobbi became 3.6× more common, from 1 in 17,500 baby girls to 1 in 4,820.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1997 and 2002, its share of baby girls fell by 57%, from 1 in 7,700 to 1 in 18,000.

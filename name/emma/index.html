Emma name popularity since 1925

632,387 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Emma. Half of them were born in 2008 or earlier, so the typical Emma is about 18 years old in 2026. Emma peaked in 1881 as the #3 name for girls, and it ranked #3 in 2025.

Bernice name popularity since 1925

98,680 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Bernice (98.1% girls, 1.9% boys). Half of them were born in 1936 or earlier, so the typical Bernice is about 90 years old in 2026. Bernice peaked in 1917 as the #42 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

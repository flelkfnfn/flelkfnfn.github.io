Gunner name popularity since 1925

23,508 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Gunner. Half of them were born in 2014 or earlier, so the typical Gunner is about 12 years old in 2026. Gunner peaked in 2015 as the #239 name for boys, and it ranked #506 in 2025.

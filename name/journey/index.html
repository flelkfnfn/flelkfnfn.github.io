Journey name popularity since 1925

22,893 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Journey (91.2% girls, 8.8% boys). Half of them were born in 2016 or earlier, so the typical Journey is about 10 years old in 2026. Journey peaked in 2018 as the #226 name for girls, and it ranked #411 in 2025.

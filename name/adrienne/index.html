Adrienne name popularity since 1925

67,172 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Adrienne (99.4% girls, 0.6% boys). Half of them were born in 1979 or earlier, so the typical Adrienne is about 47 years old in 2026. Adrienne peaked in 1983 as the #140 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

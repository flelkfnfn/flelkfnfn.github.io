Beverly name popularity since 1925

370,750 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Beverly (99.1% girls, 0.9% boys). Half of them were born in 1948 or earlier, so the typical Beverly is about 78 years old in 2026. Beverly peaked in 1937 as the #14 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Beverly births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1953. Measured as a share of all babies, Beverly peaked in 1937.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1925 and 1930, Beverly became 2.6× more common.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1971 and 1976, its share of baby girls fell by 47%, from 1 in 837 to 1 in 1,570.

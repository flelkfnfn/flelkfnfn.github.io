Tomas name popularity since 1925

28,960 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Tomas. Half of them were born in 1992 or earlier, so the typical Tomas is about 34 years old in 2026. Tomas peaked in 2003 as the #468 name for boys, and it ranked #691 in 2025.

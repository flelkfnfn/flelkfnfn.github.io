Ian name popularity since 1925

258,303 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Ian. Half of them were born in 2001 or earlier, so the typical Ian is about 25 years old in 2026. Ian peaked in 2005 as the #68 name for boys, and it ranked #83 in 2025.

Kaydence name popularity since 1925

18,459 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Kaydence (95.2% girls, 4.8% boys). Half of them were born in 2012 or earlier, so the typical Kaydence is about 14 years old in 2026. Kaydence peaked in 2010 as the #256 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Kaydence births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 2009. Measured as a share of all babies, Kaydence peaked in 2010.

  • Fastest rise: Between 2003 and 2008, Kaydence became 4.9× more common, from 1 in 7,810 baby girls to 1 in 1,610.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2016 and 2021, its share of baby girls fell by 58%, from 1 in 2,080 to 1 in 5,000.

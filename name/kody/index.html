Kody name popularity since 1925

24,641 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Kody (3.6% girls, 96.4% boys). Half of them were born in 1997 or earlier, so the typical Kody is about 29 years old in 2026. Kody peaked in 1994 as the #247 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Kody births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1993. Measured as a share of all babies, Kody peaked in 1994.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1983 and 1988, Kody became 3.3× more common, from 1 in 11,200 baby boys to 1 in 3,420.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1994 and 1999, its share of baby boys fell by 37%, from 1 in 1,660 to 1 in 2,620.

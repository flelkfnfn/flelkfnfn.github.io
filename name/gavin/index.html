Gavin name popularity since 1925

189,554 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Gavin. Half of them were born in 2008 or earlier, so the typical Gavin is about 18 years old in 2026. Gavin peaked in 2008 as the #30 name for boys, and it ranked #303 in 2025.

Gavin births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 2007. Measured as a share of all babies, Gavin peaked in 2008.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1995 and 2000, Gavin became 3.8× more common, from 1 in 1,600 baby boys to 1 in 419.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2020 and 2025, its share of baby boys fell by 56%, from 1 in 667 to 1 in 1,520.

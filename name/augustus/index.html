Augustus name popularity since 1925

18,130 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Augustus. Half of them were born in 2010 or earlier, so the typical Augustus is about 16 years old in 2026. Augustus peaked in 1880 as the #175 name for boys, and it ranked #407 in 2025.

Lora name popularity since 1925

43,497 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Lora. Half of them were born in 1965 or earlier, so the typical Lora is about 61 years old in 2026. Lora peaked in 1968 as the #203 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Lora births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1963. Measured as a share of all babies, Lora peaked in 1968.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1958 and 1963, Lora became 2.1× more common, from 1 in 2,510 baby girls to 1 in 1,210.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1968 and 1973, its share of baby girls fell by 46%, from 1 in 1,080 to 1 in 2,010.

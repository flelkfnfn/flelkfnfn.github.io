Betsy name popularity since 1925

46,524 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Betsy. Half of them were born in 1960 or earlier, so the typical Betsy is about 66 years old in 2026. Betsy peaked in 1959 as the #228 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

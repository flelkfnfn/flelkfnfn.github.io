Susan name popularity since 1925

1,104,774 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Susan. Half of them were born in 1957 or earlier, so the typical Susan is about 69 years old in 2026. Susan peaked in 1954 as the #5 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Susan births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1955. Measured as a share of all babies, Susan peaked in 1954.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1937 and 1942, Susan became 3.6× more common, from 1 in 403 baby girls to 1 in 112.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1967 and 1972, its share of baby girls fell by 48%, from 1 in 74 to 1 in 144.

Marcel name popularity since 1925

19,700 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Marcel (4.7% girls, 95.3% boys). Half of them were born in 1991 or earlier, so the typical Marcel is about 35 years old in 2026. Marcel peaked in 2022 as the #659 name for boys, and it ranked #731 in 2025.

Ramon name popularity since 1925

73,471 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Ramon (0.7% girls, 99.3% boys). Half of them were born in 1978 or earlier, so the typical Ramon is about 48 years old in 2026. Ramon peaked in 1975 as the #203 name for boys, and it ranked #852 in 2025.

Ramon births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1991. Measured as a share of all babies, Ramon peaked in 1975.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1925 and 1930, Ramon became 1.6× more common.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2009 and 2014, its share of baby boys fell by 40%, from 1 in 2,850 to 1 in 4,740.

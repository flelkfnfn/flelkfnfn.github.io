Sara name popularity since 1925

401,091 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Sara. Half of them were born in 1984 or earlier, so the typical Sara is about 42 years old in 2026. Sara peaked in 1981 as the #26 name for girls, and it ranked #198 in 2025.

Sara births per year, 1925–2025

1981 was the biggest year for Sara both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1971 and 1976, Sara became 2.8× more common, from 1 in 519 baby girls to 1 in 183.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2006 and 2011, its share of baby girls fell by 41%, from 1 in 431 to 1 in 734.

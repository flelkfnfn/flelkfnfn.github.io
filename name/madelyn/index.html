Madelyn name popularity since 1925

112,000 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Madelyn. Half of them were born in 2011 or earlier, so the typical Madelyn is about 15 years old in 2026. Madelyn peaked in 2015 as the #59 name for girls, and it ranked #71 in 2025.

Madelyn births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 2008. Measured as a share of all babies, Madelyn peaked in 2015.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1951 and 1956, Madelyn's share of baby girls fell by 40%, from 1 in 8,080 to 1 in 13,400.
  • Fastest rise: Between 1993 and 1998, it became 4.2× more common, from 1 in 3,810 baby girls to 1 in 916.

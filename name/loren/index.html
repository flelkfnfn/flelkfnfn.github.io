Loren name popularity since 1925

52,399 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Loren (24.3% girls, 75.7% boys). Half of them were born in 1964 or earlier, so the typical Loren is about 62 years old in 2026. Loren peaked in 1925 as the #216 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

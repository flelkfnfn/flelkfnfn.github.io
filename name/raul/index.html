Raul name popularity since 1925

86,748 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Raul (0.6% girls, 99.4% boys). Half of them were born in 1985 or earlier, so the typical Raul is about 41 years old in 2026. Raul peaked in 1995 as the #201 name for boys, and it ranked #634 in 2025.

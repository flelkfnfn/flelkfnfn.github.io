Luther name popularity since 1925

42,331 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Luther (0.7% girls, 99.3% boys). Half of them were born in 1947 or earlier, so the typical Luther is about 79 years old in 2026. Luther peaked in 1887 as the #63 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

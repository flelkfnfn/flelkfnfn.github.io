Cruz name popularity since 1925

33,622 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Cruz (10.4% girls, 89.6% boys). Half of them were born in 2010 or earlier, so the typical Cruz is about 16 years old in 2026. Cruz peaked in 2013 as the #279 name for boys, and it ranked #331 in 2025.

Beth name popularity since 1925

155,482 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Beth. Half of them were born in 1964 or earlier, so the typical Beth is about 62 years old in 2026. Beth peaked in 1964 as the #65 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Beth births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1961. Measured as a share of all babies, Beth peaked in 1964.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1948 and 1953, Beth became 2.1× more common, from 1 in 1,560 baby girls to 1 in 734.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1984 and 1989, its share of baby girls fell by 62%, from 1 in 1,030 to 1 in 2,720.

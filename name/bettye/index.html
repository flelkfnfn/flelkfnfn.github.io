Bettye name popularity since 1925

20,830 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Bettye. Half of them were born in 1940 or earlier, so the typical Bettye is about 86 years old in 2026. Bettye peaked in 1933 as the #220 name for girls, and fewer than 5 girls got it in 2025.

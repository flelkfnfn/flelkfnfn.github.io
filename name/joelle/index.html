Joelle name popularity since 1925

16,635 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Joelle (96.8% girls, 3.2% boys). Half of them were born in 1998 or earlier, so the typical Joelle is about 28 years old in 2026. Joelle peaked in 2017 as the #673 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Joelle births per year, 1925–2025

2017 was the biggest year for Joelle both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1968 and 1973, Joelle's share of baby girls fell by 54%, from 1 in 4,210 to 1 in 9,170.

Cecilia name popularity since 1925

97,770 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Cecilia. Half of them were born in 1992 or earlier, so the typical Cecilia is about 34 years old in 2026. Cecilia peaked in 2025 as the #108 name for girls, and it ranked #108 in 2025.

Lloyd name popularity since 1925

115,601 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Lloyd (0.6% girls, 99.4% boys). Half of them were born in 1947 or earlier, so the typical Lloyd is about 79 years old in 2026. Lloyd peaked in 1918 as the #51 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Kiara name popularity since 1925

57,289 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Kiara. Half of them were born in 2003 or earlier, so the typical Kiara is about 23 years old in 2026. Kiara peaked in 1999 as the #78 name for girls, and it ranked #229 in 2025.

Kiara births per year, 1925–2025

1999 was the biggest year for Kiara both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1987 and 1992, Kiara became 12× more common, from 1 in 13,600 baby girls to 1 in 1,180.

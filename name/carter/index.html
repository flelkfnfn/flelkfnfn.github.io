Carter name popularity since 1925

215,279 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Carter (4.7% girls, 95.3% boys). Half of them were born in 2013 or earlier, so the typical Carter is about 13 years old in 2026. Carter peaked in 2015 as the #24 name for boys, and it ranked #45 in 2025.

Carter births per year, 1925–2025

2015 was the biggest year for Carter both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1954 and 1959, Carter's share of baby boys fell by 34%, from 1 in 10,700 to 1 in 16,200.
  • Fastest rise: Between 1994 and 1999, it became 3.6× more common, from 1 in 3,550 baby boys to 1 in 988.

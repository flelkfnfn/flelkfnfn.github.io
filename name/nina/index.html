Nina name popularity since 1925

110,136 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Nina. Half of them were born in 1979 or earlier, so the typical Nina is about 47 years old in 2026. Nina peaked in 1887 as the #115 name for girls, and it ranked #324 in 2025.

Nina births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year on this chart was 1980. Measured as a share of all babies, Nina peaked earlier, in 1887.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1976 and 1981, Nina became 2.6× more common, from 1 in 2,580 baby girls to 1 in 981.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1980 and 1985, its share of baby girls fell by 39%, from 1 in 879 to 1 in 1,430.

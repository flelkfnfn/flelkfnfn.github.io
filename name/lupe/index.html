Lupe name popularity since 1925

20,735 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Lupe (74.5% girls, 25.5% boys). Half of them were born in 1946 or earlier, so the typical Lupe is about 80 years old in 2026. Lupe peaked in 1929 as the #318 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

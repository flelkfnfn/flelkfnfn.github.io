Sammie name popularity since 1925

23,896 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Sammie (26.0% girls, 74.0% boys). Half of them were born in 1949 or earlier, so the typical Sammie is about 77 years old in 2026. Sammie peaked in 1931 as the #296 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

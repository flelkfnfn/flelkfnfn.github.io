Cathleen name popularity since 1925

29,129 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Cathleen. Half of them were born in 1960 or earlier, so the typical Cathleen is about 66 years old in 2026. Cathleen peaked in 1958 as the #255 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Cathleen births per year, 1925–2025

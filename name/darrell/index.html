Darrell name popularity since 1925

157,012 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Darrell (0.7% girls, 99.3% boys). Half of them were born in 1961 or earlier, so the typical Darrell is about 65 years old in 2026. Darrell peaked in 1958 as the #90 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

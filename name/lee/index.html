Lee name popularity since 1925

246,045 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Lee (21.6% girls, 78.4% boys). Half of them were born in 1958 or earlier, so the typical Lee is about 68 years old in 2026. Lee peaked in 1900 as the #39 name for boys, and it ranked #727 in 2025.

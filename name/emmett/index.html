Emmett name popularity since 1925

68,485 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Emmett. Half of them were born in 2015 or earlier, so the typical Emmett is about 11 years old in 2026. Emmett peaked in 2021 as the #103 name for boys, and it ranked #121 in 2025.

Emmett births per year, 1925–2025

2021 was the biggest year for Emmett both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1944 and 1949, Emmett's share of baby boys fell by 39%, from 1 in 3,800 to 1 in 6,220.
  • Fastest rise: Between 2008 and 2013, it became 5.3× more common, from 1 in 4,260 baby boys to 1 in 802.

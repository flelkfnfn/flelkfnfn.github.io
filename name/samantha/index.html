Samantha name popularity since 1925

593,284 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Samantha. Half of them were born in 1995 or earlier, so the typical Samantha is about 31 years old in 2026. Samantha peaked in 1991 as the #5 name for girls, and it ranked #151 in 2025.

Samantha births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1990. Measured as a share of all babies, Samantha peaked in 1991.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1964 and 1969, Samantha became 6.6× more common, from 1 in 4,570 baby girls to 1 in 697.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2016 and 2021, its share of baby girls fell by 43%, from 1 in 358 to 1 in 631.

Piper name popularity since 1925

62,934 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Piper. Half of them were born in 2015 or earlier, so the typical Piper is about 11 years old in 2026. Piper peaked in 2015 as the #67 name for girls, and it ranked #155 in 2025.

Piper births per year, 1925–2025

2015 was the biggest year for Piper both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1998 and 2003, Piper became 5.5× more common, from 1 in 11,100 baby girls to 1 in 2,030.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2019 and 2024, its share of baby girls fell by 39%, from 1 in 526 to 1 in 860.

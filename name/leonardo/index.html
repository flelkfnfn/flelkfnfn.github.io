Leonardo name popularity since 1925

95,671 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Leonardo. Half of them were born in 2014 or earlier, so the typical Leonardo is about 12 years old in 2026. Leonardo peaked in 2022 as the #75 name for boys, and it ranked #91 in 2025.

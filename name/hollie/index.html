Hollie name popularity since 1925

19,022 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Hollie (95.2% girls, 4.8% boys). Half of them were born in 1982 or earlier, so the typical Hollie is about 44 years old in 2026. Hollie peaked in 1977 as the #356 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Hollie births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1983. Measured as a share of all babies, Hollie peaked in 1977.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1968 and 1973, Hollie became 3× more common, from 1 in 9,430 baby girls to 1 in 3,170.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1993 and 1998, its share of baby girls fell by 44%, from 1 in 4,980 to 1 in 8,830.

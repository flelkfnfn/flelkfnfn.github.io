Laura name popularity since 1925

709,162 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Laura. Half of them were born in 1970 or earlier, so the typical Laura is about 56 years old in 2026. Laura peaked in 1968 as the #11 name for girls, and it ranked #379 in 2025.

Laura births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1964. Measured as a share of all babies, Laura peaked in 1968.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1944 and 1949, Laura became 1.9× more common, from 1 in 591 baby girls to 1 in 318.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2005 and 2010, its share of baby girls fell by 46%, from 1 in 784 to 1 in 1,460.

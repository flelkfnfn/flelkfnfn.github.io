Celia name popularity since 1925

40,025 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Celia. Half of them were born in 1966 or earlier, so the typical Celia is about 60 years old in 2026. Celia peaked in 1895 as the #158 name for girls, and it ranked #774 in 2025.

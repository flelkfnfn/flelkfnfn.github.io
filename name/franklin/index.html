Franklin name popularity since 1925

124,491 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Franklin (0.6% girls, 99.4% boys). Half of them were born in 1956 or earlier, so the typical Franklin is about 70 years old in 2026. Franklin peaked in 1933 as the #33 name for boys, and it ranked #359 in 2025.

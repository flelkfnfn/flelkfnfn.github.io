Sheila name popularity since 1925

238,379 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Sheila. Half of them were born in 1960 or earlier, so the typical Sheila is about 66 years old in 2026. Sheila peaked in 1963 as the #50 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Sheila births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1962. Measured as a share of all babies, Sheila peaked in 1963.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1930 and 1935, Sheila became 4.2× more common.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1973 and 1978, its share of baby girls fell by 55%, from 1 in 486 to 1 in 1,080.

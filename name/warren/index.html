Warren name popularity since 1925

142,291 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Warren. Half of them were born in 1955 or earlier, so the typical Warren is about 71 years old in 2026. Warren peaked in 1921 as the #24 name for boys, and it ranked #240 in 2025.

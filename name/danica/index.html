Danica name popularity since 1925

16,512 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Danica. Half of them were born in 2007 or earlier, so the typical Danica is about 19 years old in 2026. Danica peaked in 2007 as the #307 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Danica births per year, 1925–2025

2007 was the biggest year for Danica both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 2001 and 2006, Danica became 6.2× more common, from 1 in 12,700 baby girls to 1 in 2,030.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2014 and 2019, its share of baby girls fell by 57%, from 1 in 3,230 to 1 in 7,430.

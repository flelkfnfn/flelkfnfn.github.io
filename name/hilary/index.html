Hilary name popularity since 1925

25,739 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Hilary (93.8% girls, 6.2% boys). Half of them were born in 1983 or earlier, so the typical Hilary is about 43 years old in 2026. Hilary peaked in 1990 as the #232 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Hilary births per year, 1925–2025

1990 was the biggest year for Hilary both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1958 and 1963, Hilary became 2× more common, from 1 in 15,700 baby girls to 1 in 7,710.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1992 and 1997, its share of baby girls fell by 90%, from 1 in 1,580 to 1 in 16,300.

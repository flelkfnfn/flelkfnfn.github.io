Jaclyn name popularity since 1925

49,722 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Jaclyn. Half of them were born in 1987 or earlier, so the typical Jaclyn is about 39 years old in 2026. Jaclyn peaked in 1979 as the #124 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Jaclyn births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1988. Measured as a share of all babies, Jaclyn peaked in 1979.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1976 and 1981, Jaclyn became 4.9× more common, from 1 in 4,510 baby girls to 1 in 923.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2003 and 2008, its share of baby girls fell by 60%, from 1 in 3,920 to 1 in 9,750.

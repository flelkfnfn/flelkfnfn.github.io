Doris name popularity since 1925

331,501 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Doris (99.3% girls, 0.7% boys). Half of them were born in 1936 or earlier, so the typical Doris is about 90 years old in 2026. Doris peaked in 1929 as the #6 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

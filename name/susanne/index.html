Susanne name popularity since 1925

25,249 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Susanne. Half of them were born in 1958 or earlier, so the typical Susanne is about 68 years old in 2026. Susanne peaked in 1946 as the #281 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

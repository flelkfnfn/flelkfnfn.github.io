Guadalupe name popularity since 1925

85,878 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Guadalupe (73.2% girls, 26.8% boys). Half of them were born in 1983 or earlier, so the typical Guadalupe is about 43 years old in 2026. Guadalupe peaked in 1997 as the #210 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Guadalupe births per year, 1925–2025

1997 was the biggest year for Guadalupe both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1988 and 1993, Guadalupe became 2× more common, from 1 in 2,840 baby girls to 1 in 1,440.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2013 and 2018, its share of baby girls fell by 54%, from 1 in 2,630 to 1 in 5,730.

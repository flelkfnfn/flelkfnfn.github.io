Dee name popularity since 1925

20,063 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Dee (74.9% girls, 25.1% boys). Half of them were born in 1957 or earlier, so the typical Dee is about 69 years old in 2026. Dee peaked in 1962 as the #381 name for girls, and fewer than 5 girls got it in 2025.

Dee births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1961. Measured as a share of all babies, Dee peaked in 1962.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1937 and 1942, Dee became 1.6× more common, from 1 in 8,870 baby girls to 1 in 5,700.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1969 and 1974, its share of baby girls fell by 58%, from 1 in 4,510 to 1 in 10,800.

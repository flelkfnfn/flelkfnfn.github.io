Jeannie name popularity since 1925

35,557 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Jeannie. Half of them were born in 1961 or earlier, so the typical Jeannie is about 65 years old in 2026. Jeannie peaked in 1957 as the #222 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Jeannie births per year, 1925–2025

1957 was the biggest year for Jeannie both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1936 and 1941, Jeannie became 2.6× more common, from 1 in 8,600 baby girls to 1 in 3,290.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1985 and 1990, its share of baby girls fell by 56%, from 1 in 5,720 to 1 in 12,900.

Graham name popularity since 1925

60,151 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Graham. Half of them were born in 2013 or earlier, so the typical Graham is about 13 years old in 2026. Graham peaked in 2025 as the #120 name for boys, and it ranked #120 in 2025.

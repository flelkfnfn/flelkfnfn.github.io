Sandy name popularity since 1925

78,017 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Sandy (88.4% girls, 11.6% boys). Half of them were born in 1963 or earlier, so the typical Sandy is about 63 years old in 2026. Sandy peaked in 1960 as the #126 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Sandy births per year, 1925–2025

1960 was the biggest year for Sandy both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1937 and 1942, Sandy became 4.9× more common, from 1 in 9,850 baby girls to 1 in 2,000.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1947 and 1952, its share of baby girls fell by 69%, from 1 in 1,310 to 1 in 4,210.

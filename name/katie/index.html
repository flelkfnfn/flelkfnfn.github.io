Katie name popularity since 1925

213,864 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Katie. Half of them were born in 1988 or earlier, so the typical Katie is about 38 years old in 2026. Katie peaked in 1986 as the #38 name for girls, and it ranked #646 in 2025.

Katie births per year, 1925–2025

1986 was the biggest year for Katie both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1975 and 1980, Katie became 4.2× more common, from 1 in 1,120 baby girls to 1 in 271.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2007 and 2012, its share of baby girls fell by 47%, from 1 in 640 to 1 in 1,210.

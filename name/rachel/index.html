Rachel name popularity since 1925

548,107 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Rachel. Half of them were born in 1988 or earlier, so the typical Rachel is about 38 years old in 2026. Rachel peaked in 1985 as the #13 name for girls, and it ranked #250 in 2025.

Rachel births per year, 1925–2025

1985 was the biggest year for Rachel both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1965 and 1970, Rachel became 3× more common, from 1 in 878 baby girls to 1 in 290.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2003 and 2008, its share of baby girls fell by 51%, from 1 in 228 to 1 in 463.

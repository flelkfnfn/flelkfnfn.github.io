Omar name popularity since 1925

105,424 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Omar. Half of them were born in 2000 or earlier, so the typical Omar is about 26 years old in 2026. Omar peaked in 2007 as the #132 name for boys, and it ranked #260 in 2025.

Omar births per year, 1925–2025

2007 was the biggest year for Omar both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1965 and 1970, Omar became 3.7× more common, from 1 in 12,400 baby boys to 1 in 3,370.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2007 and 2012, its share of baby boys fell by 35%, from 1 in 645 to 1 in 987.

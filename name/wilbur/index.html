Wilbur name popularity since 1925

32,214 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Wilbur. Half of them were born in 1939 or earlier, so the typical Wilbur is about 87 years old in 2026. Wilbur peaked in 1913 as the #91 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

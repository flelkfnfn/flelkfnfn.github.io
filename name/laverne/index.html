Laverne name popularity since 1925

40,034 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Laverne (77.2% girls, 22.8% boys). Half of them were born in 1940 or earlier, so the typical Laverne is about 86 years old in 2026. Laverne peaked in 1928 as the #165 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

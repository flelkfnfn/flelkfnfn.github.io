Antonia name popularity since 1925

31,868 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Antonia (92.0% girls, 8.0% boys). Half of them were born in 1977 or earlier, so the typical Antonia is about 49 years old in 2026. Antonia peaked in 1996 as the #395 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Antonia births per year, 1925–2025

1996 was the biggest year for Antonia both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1991 and 1996, Antonia became 2.3× more common, from 1 in 6,070 baby girls to 1 in 2,620.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2001 and 2006, its share of baby girls fell by 41%, from 1 in 4,350 to 1 in 7,400.

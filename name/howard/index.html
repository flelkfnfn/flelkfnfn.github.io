Howard name popularity since 1925

245,918 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Howard. Half of them were born in 1947 or earlier, so the typical Howard is about 79 years old in 2026. Howard peaked in 1912 as the #25 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Stuart name popularity since 1925

64,915 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Stuart. Half of them were born in 1960 or earlier, so the typical Stuart is about 66 years old in 2026. Stuart peaked in 1960 as the #161 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Robin name popularity since 1925

338,196 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Robin (86.3% girls, 13.7% boys). Half of them were born in 1963 or earlier, so the typical Robin is about 63 years old in 2026. Robin peaked in 1961 as the #27 name for girls, and it ranked #704 in 2025.

Robin births per year, 1925–2025

1961 was the biggest year for Robin both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1949 and 1954, Robin became 6.2× more common, from 1 in 1,340 baby girls to 1 in 215.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1997 and 2002, its share of baby girls fell by 56%, from 1 in 2,920 to 1 in 6,630.

Antoine name popularity since 1925

27,093 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Antoine (1.3% girls, 98.7% boys). Half of them were born in 1985 or earlier, so the typical Antoine is about 41 years old in 2026. Antoine peaked in 1976 as the #271 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Antoine births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1985. Measured as a share of all babies, Antoine peaked in 1976.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1967 and 1972, Antoine became 2.9× more common, from 1 in 8,990 baby boys to 1 in 3,090.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1990 and 1995, its share of baby boys fell by 49%, from 1 in 3,160 to 1 in 6,220.

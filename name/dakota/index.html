Dakota name popularity since 1925

134,562 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Dakota (30.8% girls, 69.2% boys). Half of them were born in 2001 or earlier, so the typical Dakota is about 25 years old in 2026. Dakota peaked in 1995 as the #56 name for boys, and it ranked #319 in 2025.

Dakota births per year, 1925–2025

1995 was the biggest year for Dakota both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1985 and 1990, Dakota became 13× more common, from 1 in 17,000 baby boys to 1 in 1,320.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2005 and 2010, its share of baby boys fell by 56%, from 1 in 734 to 1 in 1,680.

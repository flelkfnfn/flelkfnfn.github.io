Bertha name popularity since 1925

78,651 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Bertha. Half of them were born in 1940 or earlier, so the typical Bertha is about 86 years old in 2026. Bertha peaked in 1883 as the #7 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

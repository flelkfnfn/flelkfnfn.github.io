Lenora name popularity since 1925

26,471 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Lenora. Half of them were born in 1954 or earlier, so the typical Lenora is about 72 years old in 2026. Lenora peaked in 1881 as the #183 name for girls, and it ranked #539 in 2025.

Lenora births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year on this chart was 1927. Measured as a share of all babies, Lenora peaked earlier, in 1881.

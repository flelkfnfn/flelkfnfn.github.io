Krystal name popularity since 1925

75,376 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Krystal. Half of them were born in 1987 or earlier, so the typical Krystal is about 39 years old in 2026. Krystal peaked in 1985 as the #53 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Krystal births per year, 1925–2025

1985 was the biggest year for Krystal both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1980 and 1985, Krystal became 4.9× more common, from 1 in 1,430 baby girls to 1 in 290.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1992 and 1997, its share of baby girls fell by 52%, from 1 in 704 to 1 in 1,470.

Alec name popularity since 1925

54,537 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Alec (0.6% girls, 99.4% boys). Half of them were born in 1998 or earlier, so the typical Alec is about 28 years old in 2026. Alec peaked in 1995 as the #112 name for boys, and it ranked #977 in 2025.

Alec births per year, 1925–2025

1995 was the biggest year for Alec both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1989 and 1994, Alec became 5.7× more common, from 1 in 3,320 baby boys to 1 in 582.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2001 and 2006, its share of baby boys fell by 57%, from 1 in 902 to 1 in 2,090.

Freddy name popularity since 1925

26,652 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Freddy (0.6% girls, 99.4% boys). Half of them were born in 1973 or earlier, so the typical Freddy is about 53 years old in 2026. Freddy peaked in 1940 as the #324 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Freddy births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1947. Measured as a share of all babies, Freddy peaked in 1940.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1933 and 1938, Freddy became 1.8× more common.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2008 and 2013, its share of baby boys fell by 37%, from 1 in 6,240 to 1 in 9,850.

Roslyn name popularity since 1925

16,278 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Roslyn. Half of them were born in 1960 or earlier, so the typical Roslyn is about 66 years old in 2026. Roslyn peaked in 1927 as the #397 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Roslyn births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1956. Measured as a share of all babies, Roslyn peaked in 1927.

Marlin name popularity since 1925

20,507 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Marlin (8.0% girls, 92.0% boys). Half of them were born in 1957 or earlier, so the typical Marlin is about 69 years old in 2026. Marlin peaked in 1935 as the #276 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Marlin births per year, 1925–2025

1935 was the biggest year for Marlin both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1939 and 1944, Marlin's share of baby boys fell by 52%, from 1 in 3,000 to 1 in 6,220.
  • Fastest rise: Between 1969 and 1974, it became 1.6× more common, from 1 in 11,100 baby boys to 1 in 6,960.

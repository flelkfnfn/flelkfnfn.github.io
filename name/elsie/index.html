Elsie name popularity since 1925

86,161 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Elsie. Half of them were born in 1941 or earlier, so the typical Elsie is about 85 years old in 2026. Elsie peaked in 1896 as the #31 name for girls, and it ranked #123 in 2025.

Elsie births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year on this chart was 1925. Measured as a share of all babies, Elsie peaked earlier, in 1896.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1944 and 1949, Elsie's share of baby girls fell by 48%, from 1 in 1,210 to 1 in 2,350.
  • Fastest rise: Between 2007 and 2012, it became 2.7× more common, from 1 in 6,070 baby girls to 1 in 2,230.

Cherie name popularity since 1925

29,869 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Cherie. Half of them were born in 1966 or earlier, so the typical Cherie is about 60 years old in 2026. Cherie peaked in 1969 as the #284 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Cherie births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1970. Measured as a share of all babies, Cherie peaked in 1969.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1941 and 1946, Cherie became 2.2× more common, from 1 in 9,150 baby girls to 1 in 4,150.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1986 and 1991, its share of baby girls fell by 56%, from 1 in 5,050 to 1 in 11,600.

Leland name popularity since 1925

48,537 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Leland (0.9% girls, 99.1% boys). Half of them were born in 1966 or earlier, so the typical Leland is about 60 years old in 2026. Leland peaked in 1921 as the #171 name for boys, and it ranked #494 in 2025.

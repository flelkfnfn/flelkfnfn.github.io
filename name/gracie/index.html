Gracie name popularity since 1925

75,028 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Gracie. Half of them were born in 2006 or earlier, so the typical Gracie is about 20 years old in 2026. Gracie peaked in 2005 as the #94 name for girls, and it ranked #227 in 2025.

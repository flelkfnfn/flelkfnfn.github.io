Paxton name popularity since 1925

30,296 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Paxton (7.0% girls, 93.0% boys). Half of them were born in 2016 or earlier, so the typical Paxton is about 10 years old in 2026. Paxton peaked in 2016 as the #203 name for boys, and it ranked #338 in 2025.

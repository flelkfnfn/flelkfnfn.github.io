Ayanna name popularity since 1925

16,326 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Ayanna. Half of them were born in 2003 or earlier, so the typical Ayanna is about 23 years old in 2026. Ayanna peaked in 2003 as the #362 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Ayanna births per year, 1925–2025

2003 was the biggest year for Ayanna both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1989 and 1994, Ayanna became 3.2× more common, from 1 in 18,400 baby girls to 1 in 5,720.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2010 and 2015, its share of baby girls fell by 49%, from 1 in 3,680 to 1 in 7,290.

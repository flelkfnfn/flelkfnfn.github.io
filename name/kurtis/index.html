Kurtis name popularity since 1925

18,163 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Kurtis. Half of them were born in 1986 or earlier, so the typical Kurtis is about 40 years old in 2026. Kurtis peaked in 1987 as the #334 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Kurtis births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1989. Measured as a share of all babies, Kurtis peaked in 1987.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1975 and 1980, Kurtis became 1.9× more common, from 1 in 8,490 baby boys to 1 in 4,590.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1995 and 2000, its share of baby boys fell by 46%, from 1 in 4,380 to 1 in 8,120.

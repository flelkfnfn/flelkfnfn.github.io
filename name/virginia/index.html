Virginia name popularity since 1925

444,432 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Virginia. Half of them were born in 1943 or earlier, so the typical Virginia is about 83 years old in 2026. Virginia peaked in 1922 as the #7 name for girls, and it ranked #526 in 2025.

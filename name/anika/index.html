Anika name popularity since 1925

16,804 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Anika. Half of them were born in 2007 or earlier, so the typical Anika is about 19 years old in 2026. Anika peaked in 2006 as the #485 name for girls, and it ranked #955 in 2025.

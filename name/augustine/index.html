Augustine name popularity since 1925

16,909 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Augustine (15.6% girls, 84.4% boys). Half of them were born in 1988 or earlier, so the typical Augustine is about 38 years old in 2026. Augustine peaked in 2025 as the #545 name for boys, and it ranked #545 in 2025.

Judith name popularity since 1925

450,970 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Judith. Half of them were born in 1947 or earlier, so the typical Judith is about 79 years old in 2026. Judith peaked in 1940 as the #4 name for girls, and it ranked #790 in 2025.

Judith births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1943. Measured as a share of all babies, Judith peaked in 1940.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1934 and 1939, Judith became 6.4× more common.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1966 and 1971, its share of baby girls fell by 54%, from 1 in 526 to 1 in 1,150.

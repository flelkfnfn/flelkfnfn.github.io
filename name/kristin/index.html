Kristin name popularity since 1925

180,951 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Kristin (99.1% girls, 0.9% boys). Half of them were born in 1981 or earlier, so the typical Kristin is about 45 years old in 2026. Kristin peaked in 1981 as the #31 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Kristin births per year, 1925–2025

1981 was the biggest year for Kristin both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1962 and 1967, Kristin became 4.9× more common, from 1 in 2,240 baby girls to 1 in 459.

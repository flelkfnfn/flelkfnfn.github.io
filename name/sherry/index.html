Sherry name popularity since 1925

230,605 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Sherry. Half of them were born in 1961 or earlier, so the typical Sherry is about 65 years old in 2026. Sherry peaked in 1963 as the #49 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Jodi name popularity since 1925

80,657 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Jodi (98.8% girls, 1.2% boys). Half of them were born in 1971 or earlier, so the typical Jodi is about 55 years old in 2026. Jodi peaked in 1970 as the #95 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Jodi births per year, 1925–2025

1970 was the biggest year for Jodi both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1952 and 1957, Jodi became 6.6× more common, from 1 in 14,800 baby girls to 1 in 2,260.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1985 and 1990, its share of baby girls fell by 57%, from 1 in 1,580 to 1 in 3,670.

Shelly name popularity since 1925

86,220 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Shelly (97.9% girls, 2.1% boys). Half of them were born in 1968 or earlier, so the typical Shelly is about 58 years old in 2026. Shelly peaked in 1966 as the #89 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Shelly births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1964. Measured as a share of all babies, Shelly peaked in 1966.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1948 and 1953, Shelly became 5.1× more common, from 1 in 16,600 baby girls to 1 in 3,290.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1985 and 1990, its share of baby girls fell by 58%, from 1 in 1,940 to 1 in 4,600.

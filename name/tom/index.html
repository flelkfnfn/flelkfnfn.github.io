Tom name popularity since 1925

116,517 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Tom. Half of them were born in 1953 or earlier, so the typical Tom is about 73 years old in 2026. Tom peaked in 1883 as the #44 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Tom births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year on this chart was 1959. Measured as a share of all babies, Tom peaked earlier, in 1883.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1954 and 1959, Tom became 3.5× more common, from 1 in 1,470 baby boys to 1 in 421.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1970 and 1975, its share of baby boys fell by 68%, from 1 in 1,640 to 1 in 5,150.

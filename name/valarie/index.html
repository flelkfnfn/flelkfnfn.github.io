Valarie name popularity since 1925

18,576 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Valarie. Half of them were born in 1969 or earlier, so the typical Valarie is about 57 years old in 2026. Valarie peaked in 1964 as the #379 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

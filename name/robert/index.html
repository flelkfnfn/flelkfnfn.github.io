Robert name popularity since 1925

4,269,652 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Robert. Half of them were born in 1955 or earlier, so the typical Robert is about 71 years old in 2026. Robert peaked in 1931 as the #1 name for boys, and it ranked #92 in 2025.

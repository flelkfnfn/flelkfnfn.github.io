Julio name popularity since 1925

62,920 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Julio (0.7% girls, 99.3% boys). Half of them were born in 1993 or earlier, so the typical Julio is about 33 years old in 2026. Julio peaked in 1992 as the #201 name for boys, and it ranked #641 in 2025.

Julio births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1990. Measured as a share of all babies, Julio peaked in 1992.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1966 and 1971, Julio became 1.7× more common, from 1 in 5,080 baby boys to 1 in 2,930.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2006 and 2011, its share of baby boys fell by 37%, from 1 in 1,450 to 1 in 2,320.

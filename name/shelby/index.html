Shelby name popularity since 1925

163,297 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Shelby (91.1% girls, 8.9% boys). Half of them were born in 1994 or earlier, so the typical Shelby is about 32 years old in 2026. Shelby peaked in 1991 as the #33 name for girls, and it ranked #649 in 2025.

Shelby births per year, 1925–2025

1991 was the biggest year for Shelby both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1938 and 1943, Shelby's share of baby girls fell by 77%, from 1 in 644 to 1 in 2,840.
  • Fastest rise: Between 1986 and 1991, it became 10× more common, from 1 in 1,910 baby girls to 1 in 183.

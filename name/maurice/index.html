Maurice name popularity since 1925

113,973 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Maurice (1.6% girls, 98.4% boys). Half of them were born in 1968 or earlier, so the typical Maurice is about 58 years old in 2026. Maurice peaked in 1913 as the #95 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Anya births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 2007. Measured as a share of all babies, Anya peaked in 2025.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1996 and 2001, Anya became 4.1× more common, from 1 in 14,400 baby girls to 1 in 3,540.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2012 and 2017, its share of baby girls fell by 39%, from 1 in 2,240 to 1 in 3,670.

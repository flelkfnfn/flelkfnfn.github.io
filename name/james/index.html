James name popularity since 1925

4,578,137 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named James. Half of them were born in 1957 or earlier, so the typical James is about 69 years old in 2026. James peaked in 1944 as the #1 name for boys, and it ranked #6 in 2025.

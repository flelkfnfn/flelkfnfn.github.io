Wayne name popularity since 1925

330,268 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Wayne. Half of them were born in 1953 or earlier, so the typical Wayne is about 73 years old in 2026. Wayne peaked in 1947 as the #30 name for boys, and it ranked #655 in 2025.

Wayne births per year, 1925–2025

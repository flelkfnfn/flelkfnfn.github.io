Roy name popularity since 1925

311,048 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Roy (0.6% girls, 99.4% boys). Half of them were born in 1950 or earlier, so the typical Roy is about 76 years old in 2026. Roy peaked in 1897 as the #19 name for boys, and it ranked #525 in 2025.

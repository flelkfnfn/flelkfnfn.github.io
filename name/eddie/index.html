Eddie name popularity since 1925

196,223 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Eddie (5.3% girls, 94.7% boys). Half of them were born in 1956 or earlier, so the typical Eddie is about 70 years old in 2026. Eddie peaked in 1910 as the #59 name for boys, and it ranked #861 in 2025.

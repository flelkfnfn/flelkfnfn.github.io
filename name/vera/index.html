Vera name popularity since 1925

85,431 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Vera. Half of them were born in 1947 or earlier, so the typical Vera is about 79 years old in 2026. Vera peaked in 1899 as the #69 name for girls, and it ranked #205 in 2025.

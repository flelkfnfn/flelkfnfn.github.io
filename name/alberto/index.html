Alberto name popularity since 1925

61,696 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Alberto (0.7% girls, 99.3% boys). Half of them were born in 1990 or earlier, so the typical Alberto is about 36 years old in 2026. Alberto peaked in 1991 as the #218 name for boys, and it ranked #819 in 2025.

Alberto births per year, 1925–2025

1991 was the biggest year for Alberto both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1963 and 1968, Alberto became 1.6× more common, from 1 in 4,410 baby boys to 1 in 2,720.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2005 and 2010, its share of baby boys fell by 39%, from 1 in 1,760 to 1 in 2,860.

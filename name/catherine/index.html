Catherine name popularity since 1925

514,919 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Catherine. Half of them were born in 1959 or earlier, so the typical Catherine is about 67 years old in 2026. Catherine peaked in 1914 as the #18 name for girls, and it ranked #314 in 2025.

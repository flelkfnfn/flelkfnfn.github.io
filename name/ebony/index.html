Ebony name popularity since 1925

39,453 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Ebony (98.8% girls, 1.2% boys). Half of them were born in 1986 or earlier, so the typical Ebony is about 40 years old in 2026. Ebony peaked in 1982 as the #132 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Ebony births per year, 1925–2025

1982 was the biggest year for Ebony both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1971 and 1976, Ebony became 6.1× more common, from 1 in 9,960 baby girls to 1 in 1,640.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1996 and 2001, its share of baby girls fell by 54%, from 1 in 2,190 to 1 in 4,740.

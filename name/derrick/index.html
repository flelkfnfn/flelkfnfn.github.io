Derrick name popularity since 1925

146,333 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Derrick (0.7% girls, 99.3% boys). Half of them were born in 1983 or earlier, so the typical Derrick is about 43 years old in 2026. Derrick peaked in 1980 as the #74 name for boys, and it ranked #850 in 2025.

Derrick births per year, 1925–2025

1980 was the biggest year for Derrick both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1950 and 1955, Derrick became 3.3× more common, from 1 in 10,500 baby boys to 1 in 3,140.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2019 and 2024, its share of baby boys fell by 45%, from 1 in 3,090 to 1 in 5,590.

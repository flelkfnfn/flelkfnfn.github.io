Dallas name popularity since 1925

81,828 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Dallas (18.7% girls, 81.3% boys). Half of them were born in 1997 or earlier, so the typical Dallas is about 29 years old in 2026. Dallas peaked in 2025 as the #238 name for boys, and it ranked #238 in 2025.

Dallas births per year, 1925–2025

2025 was the biggest year for Dallas both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1941 and 1946, Dallas's share of baby boys fell by 40%, from 1 in 2,530 to 1 in 4,250.
  • Fastest rise: Between 1990 and 1995, it became 2.5× more common, from 1 in 3,480 baby boys to 1 in 1,390.

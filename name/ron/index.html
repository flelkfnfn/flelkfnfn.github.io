Ron name popularity since 1925

36,421 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Ron. Half of them were born in 1962 or earlier, so the typical Ron is about 64 years old in 2026. Ron peaked in 1960 as the #170 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Ron births per year, 1925–2025

1960 was the biggest year for Ron both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1947 and 1952, Ron's share of baby boys fell by 72%, from 1 in 1,990 to 1 in 7,020.

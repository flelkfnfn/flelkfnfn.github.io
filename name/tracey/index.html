Tracey name popularity since 1925

95,153 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Tracey (90.9% girls, 9.1% boys). Half of them were born in 1969 or earlier, so the typical Tracey is about 57 years old in 2026. Tracey peaked in 1970 as the #55 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Tracey births per year, 1925–2025

1970 was the biggest year for Tracey both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1952 and 1957, Tracey became 6.9× more common, from 1 in 14,400 baby girls to 1 in 2,080.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1993 and 1998, its share of baby girls fell by 65%, from 1 in 5,340 to 1 in 15,100.

Vicky name popularity since 1925

53,842 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Vicky (99.2% girls, 0.8% boys). Half of them were born in 1959 or earlier, so the typical Vicky is about 67 years old in 2026. Vicky peaked in 1957 as the #142 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Vicky births per year, 1925–2025

1957 was the biggest year for Vicky both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1940 and 1945, Vicky became 4.2× more common, from 1 in 10,000 baby girls to 1 in 2,400.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1974 and 1979, its share of baby girls fell by 50%, from 1 in 3,300 to 1 in 6,660.

Aaron name popularity since 1925

608,097 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Aaron (0.7% girls, 99.3% boys). Half of them were born in 1990 or earlier, so the typical Aaron is about 36 years old in 2026. Aaron peaked in 1981 as the #30 name for boys, and it ranked #80 in 2025.

Aaron births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1989. Measured as a share of all babies, Aaron peaked in 1981.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1966 and 1971, Aaron became 3.3× more common, from 1 in 686 baby boys to 1 in 210.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2017 and 2022, its share of baby boys fell by 34%, from 1 in 256 to 1 in 386.

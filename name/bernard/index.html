Bernard name popularity since 1925

146,040 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Bernard. Half of them were born in 1948 or earlier, so the typical Bernard is about 78 years old in 2026. Bernard peaked in 1921 as the #45 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

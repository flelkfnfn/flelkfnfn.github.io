Jada name popularity since 1925

67,656 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Jada. Half of them were born in 2004 or earlier, so the typical Jada is about 22 years old in 2026. Jada peaked in 2005 as the #76 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Jada births per year, 1925–2025

2005 was the biggest year for Jada both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1993 and 1998, Jada became 8× more common, from 1 in 5,200 baby girls to 1 in 652.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2019 and 2024, its share of baby girls fell by 70%, from 1 in 2,510 to 1 in 8,350.

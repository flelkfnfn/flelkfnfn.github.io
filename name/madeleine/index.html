Madeleine name popularity since 1925

42,191 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Madeleine. Half of them were born in 2003 or earlier, so the typical Madeleine is about 23 years old in 2026. Madeleine peaked in 1998 as the #217 name for girls, and it ranked #455 in 2025.

Madeleine births per year, 1925–2025

1998 was the biggest year for Madeleine both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1949 and 1954, Madeleine's share of baby girls fell by 50%, from 1 in 7,880 to 1 in 15,800.
  • Fastest rise: Between 1989 and 1994, it became 3.4× more common, from 1 in 6,630 baby girls to 1 in 1,930.

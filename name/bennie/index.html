Bennie name popularity since 1925

47,197 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Bennie (15.0% girls, 85.0% boys). Half of them were born in 1946 or earlier, so the typical Bennie is about 80 years old in 2026. Bennie peaked in 1904 as the #134 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

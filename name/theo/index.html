Theo name popularity since 1925

35,829 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Theo (4.6% girls, 95.4% boys). Half of them were born in 2021 or earlier, so the typical Theo is about 5 years old in 2026. Theo peaked in 2023 as the #78 name for boys, and it ranked #82 in 2025.

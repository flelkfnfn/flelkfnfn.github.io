Myra name popularity since 1925

54,473 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Myra. Half of them were born in 1959 or earlier, so the typical Myra is about 67 years old in 2026. Myra peaked in 1880 as the #158 name for girls, and it ranked #663 in 2025.

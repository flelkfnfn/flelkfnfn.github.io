Aisha name popularity since 1925

26,960 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Aisha. Half of them were born in 2001 or earlier, so the typical Aisha is about 25 years old in 2026. Aisha peaked in 1977 as the #175 name for girls, and it ranked #337 in 2025.

Aisha births per year, 1925–2025

1977 was the biggest year for Aisha both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1972 and 1977, Aisha became 14× more common, from 1 in 14,200 baby girls to 1 in 996.

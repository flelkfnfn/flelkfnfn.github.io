Shannon name popularity since 1925

347,739 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Shannon (85.0% girls, 15.0% boys). Half of them were born in 1978 or earlier, so the typical Shannon is about 48 years old in 2026. Shannon peaked in 1976 as the #17 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Shannon births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1970. Measured as a share of all babies, Shannon peaked in 1976.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1965 and 1970, Shannon became 5.3× more common, from 1 in 687 baby girls to 1 in 129.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2002 and 2007, its share of baby girls fell by 61%, from 1 in 1,020 to 1 in 2,590.

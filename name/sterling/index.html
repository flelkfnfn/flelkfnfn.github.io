Sterling name popularity since 1925

37,462 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Sterling (9.8% girls, 90.2% boys). Half of them were born in 1994 or earlier, so the typical Sterling is about 32 years old in 2026. Sterling peaked in 2025 as the #346 name for boys, and it ranked #346 in 2025.

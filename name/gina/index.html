Gina name popularity since 1925

152,970 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Gina. Half of them were born in 1971 or earlier, so the typical Gina is about 55 years old in 2026. Gina peaked in 1967 as the #54 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Gina births per year, 1925–2025

1967 was the biggest year for Gina both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1950 and 1955, Gina became 8.7× more common, from 1 in 9,260 baby girls to 1 in 1,060.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2006 and 2011, its share of baby girls fell by 56%, from 1 in 4,720 to 1 in 10,800.

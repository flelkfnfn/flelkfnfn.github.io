Stella name popularity since 1925

135,932 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Stella. Half of them were born in 2010 or earlier, so the typical Stella is about 16 years old in 2026. Stella peaked in 1889 as the #55 name for girls, and it ranked #52 in 2025.

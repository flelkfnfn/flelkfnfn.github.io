Winston name popularity since 1925

29,948 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Winston. Half of them were born in 1990 or earlier, so the typical Winston is about 36 years old in 2026. Winston peaked in 2025 as the #382 name for boys, and it ranked #382 in 2025.

Winston births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 2019. Measured as a share of all babies, Winston peaked in 2025.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1936 and 1941, Winston became 2.6× more common, from 1 in 5,960 baby boys to 1 in 2,270.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1941 and 1946, its share of baby boys fell by 62%, from 1 in 2,270 to 1 in 5,990.

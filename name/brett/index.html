Brett name popularity since 1925

162,513 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Brett (2.1% girls, 97.9% boys). Half of them were born in 1984 or earlier, so the typical Brett is about 42 years old in 2026. Brett peaked in 1986 as the #69 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Brett births per year, 1925–2025

1986 was the biggest year for Brett both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1953 and 1958, Brett became 7.5× more common, from 1 in 7,440 baby boys to 1 in 994.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2008 and 2013, its share of baby boys fell by 64%, from 1 in 1,910 to 1 in 5,250.

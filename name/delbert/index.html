Delbert name popularity since 1925

35,410 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Delbert. Half of them were born in 1944 or earlier, so the typical Delbert is about 82 years old in 2026. Delbert peaked in 1932 as the #151 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

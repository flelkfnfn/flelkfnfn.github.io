Tyrone name popularity since 1925

80,086 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Tyrone (0.9% girls, 99.1% boys). Half of them were born in 1972 or earlier, so the typical Tyrone is about 54 years old in 2026. Tyrone peaked in 1970 as the #132 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Tyrone births per year, 1925–2025

1970 was the biggest year for Tyrone both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1944 and 1949, Tyrone became 1.9× more common, from 1 in 4,370 baby boys to 1 in 2,250.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1976 and 1981, its share of baby boys fell by 39%, from 1 in 906 to 1 in 1,480.

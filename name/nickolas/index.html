Nickolas name popularity since 1925

37,790 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Nickolas. Half of them were born in 1995 or earlier, so the typical Nickolas is about 31 years old in 2026. Nickolas peaked in 1998 as the #259 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Nickolas births per year, 1925–2025

1998 was the biggest year for Nickolas both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1974 and 1979, Nickolas became 4.5× more common, from 1 in 12,900 baby boys to 1 in 2,840.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2012 and 2017, its share of baby boys fell by 50%, from 1 in 4,150 to 1 in 8,300.

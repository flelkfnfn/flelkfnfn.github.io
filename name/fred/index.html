Fred name popularity since 1925

225,653 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Fred. Half of them were born in 1944 or earlier, so the typical Fred is about 82 years old in 2026. Fred peaked in 1885 as the #14 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

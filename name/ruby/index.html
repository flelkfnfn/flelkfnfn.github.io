Ruby name popularity since 1925

247,415 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Ruby (99.2% girls, 0.8% boys). Half of them were born in 1953 or earlier, so the typical Ruby is about 73 years old in 2026. Ruby peaked in 1911 as the #22 name for girls, and it ranked #64 in 2025.

Ruby births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year on this chart was 1925. Measured as a share of all babies, Ruby peaked earlier, in 1911.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1963 and 1968, Ruby's share of baby girls fell by 46%, from 1 in 1,390 to 1 in 2,570.

Faith name popularity since 1925

145,461 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Faith. Half of them were born in 2003 or earlier, so the typical Faith is about 23 years old in 2026. Faith peaked in 2002 as the #48 name for girls, and it ranked #254 in 2025.

Faith births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 2003. Measured as a share of all babies, Faith peaked in 2002.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1994 and 1999, Faith became 3.3× more common, from 1 in 1,370 baby girls to 1 in 419.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2018 and 2023, its share of baby girls fell by 45%, from 1 in 688 to 1 in 1,260.

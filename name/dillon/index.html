Dillon name popularity since 1925

66,037 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Dillon (1.6% girls, 98.4% boys). Half of them were born in 1998 or earlier, so the typical Dillon is about 28 years old in 2026. Dillon peaked in 1992 as the #73 name for boys, and it ranked #930 in 2025.

Dillon births per year, 1925–2025

1992 was the biggest year for Dillon both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1987 and 1992, Dillon became 6.8× more common, from 1 in 2,670 baby boys to 1 in 395.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2014 and 2019, its share of baby boys fell by 43%, from 1 in 2,440 to 1 in 4,260.

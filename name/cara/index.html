Cara name popularity since 1925

52,745 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Cara. Half of them were born in 1985 or earlier, so the typical Cara is about 41 years old in 2026. Cara peaked in 1977 as the #189 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Cara births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1985. Measured as a share of all babies, Cara peaked in 1977.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1958 and 1963, Cara became 5.3× more common, from 1 in 15,000 baby girls to 1 in 2,830.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2020 and 2025, its share of baby girls fell by 42%, from 1 in 5,590 to 1 in 9,570.

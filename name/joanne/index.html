Joanne name popularity since 1925

210,034 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Joanne. Half of them were born in 1951 or earlier, so the typical Joanne is about 75 years old in 2026. Joanne peaked in 1935 as the #44 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Joanne births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1947. Measured as a share of all babies, Joanne peaked in 1935.

Chester name popularity since 1925

72,378 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Chester (0.7% girls, 99.3% boys). Half of them were born in 1946 or earlier, so the typical Chester is about 80 years old in 2026. Chester peaked in 1918 as the #54 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

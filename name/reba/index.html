Reba name popularity since 1925

24,146 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Reba. Half of them were born in 1944 or earlier, so the typical Reba is about 82 years old in 2026. Reba peaked in 1915 as the #234 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Reba births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year on this chart was 1925. Measured as a share of all babies, Reba peaked earlier, in 1915.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1963 and 1968, Reba's share of baby girls fell by 43%, from 1 in 8,000 to 1 in 14,100.
  • Fastest rise: Between 1987 and 1992, it became 1.6× more common, from 1 in 17,000 baby girls to 1 in 10,900.

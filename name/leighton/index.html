Leighton name popularity since 1925

21,282 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Leighton (58.3% girls, 41.7% boys). Half of them were born in 2015 or earlier, so the typical Leighton is about 11 years old in 2026. Leighton peaked in 2025 as the #342 name for girls, and it ranked #342 in 2025.

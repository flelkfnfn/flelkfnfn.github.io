Tracie name popularity since 1925

32,784 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Tracie (99.3% girls, 0.7% boys). Half of them were born in 1971 or earlier, so the typical Tracie is about 55 years old in 2026. Tracie peaked in 1970 as the #147 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Tracie births per year, 1925–2025

1970 was the biggest year for Tracie both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1956 and 1961, Tracie became 4.5× more common, from 1 in 12,800 baby girls to 1 in 2,860.

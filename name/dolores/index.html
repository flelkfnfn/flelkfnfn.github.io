Dolores name popularity since 1925

185,050 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Dolores (99.4% girls, 0.6% boys). Half of them were born in 1935 or earlier, so the typical Dolores is about 91 years old in 2026. Dolores peaked in 1930 as the #13 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

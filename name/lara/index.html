Lara name popularity since 1925

29,920 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Lara. Half of them were born in 1984 or earlier, so the typical Lara is about 42 years old in 2026. Lara peaked in 1969 as the #223 name for girls, and it ranked #693 in 2025.

Lara births per year, 1925–2025

1969 was the biggest year for Lara both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1966 and 1971, Lara became 4.5× more common, from 1 in 7,170 baby girls to 1 in 1,580.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1988 and 1993, its share of baby girls fell by 51%, from 1 in 3,190 to 1 in 6,550.

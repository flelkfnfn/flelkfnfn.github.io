Veronica name popularity since 1925

207,939 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Veronica. Half of them were born in 1979 or earlier, so the typical Veronica is about 47 years old in 2026. Veronica peaked in 1974 as the #71 name for girls, and it ranked #427 in 2025.

Veronica births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1980. Measured as a share of all babies, Veronica peaked in 1974.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1937 and 1942, Veronica became 1.8× more common, from 1 in 1,990 baby girls to 1 in 1,080.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1999 and 2004, its share of baby girls fell by 40%, from 1 in 627 to 1 in 1,040.

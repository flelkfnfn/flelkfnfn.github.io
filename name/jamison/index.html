Jamison name popularity since 1925

22,740 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Jamison (8.5% girls, 91.5% boys). Half of them were born in 2006 or earlier, so the typical Jamison is about 20 years old in 2026. Jamison peaked in 2019 as the #435 name for boys, and it ranked #786 in 2025.

Jamison births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 2017. Measured as a share of all babies, Jamison peaked in 2019.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1969 and 1974, Jamison became 3.3× more common, from 1 in 14,800 baby boys to 1 in 4,530.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1973 and 1978, its share of baby boys fell by 55%, from 1 in 4,280 to 1 in 9,550.

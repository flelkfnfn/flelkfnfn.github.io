Nia name popularity since 1925

25,432 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Nia. Half of them were born in 2005 or earlier, so the typical Nia is about 21 years old in 2026. Nia peaked in 2001 as the #310 name for girls, and it ranked #702 in 2025.

Nia births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 2000. Measured as a share of all babies, Nia peaked in 2001.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1988 and 1993, Nia became 3.4× more common, from 1 in 13,200 baby girls to 1 in 3,880.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2020 and 2025, its share of baby girls fell by 34%, from 1 in 2,620 to 1 in 3,990.

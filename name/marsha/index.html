Marsha name popularity since 1925

104,189 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Marsha. Half of them were born in 1954 or earlier, so the typical Marsha is about 72 years old in 2026. Marsha peaked in 1950 as the #68 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Marsha births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1952. Measured as a share of all babies, Marsha peaked in 1950.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1935 and 1940, Marsha became 4.7× more common, from 1 in 7,330 baby girls to 1 in 1,550.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1981 and 1986, its share of baby girls fell by 58%, from 1 in 2,980 to 1 in 7,150.

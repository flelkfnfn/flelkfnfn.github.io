Joan name popularity since 1925

469,739 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Joan (98.4% girls, 1.6% boys). Half of them were born in 1941 or earlier, so the typical Joan is about 85 years old in 2026. Joan peaked in 1932 as the #5 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Joan births per year, 1925–2025

1932 was the biggest year for Joan both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1926 and 1931, Joan became 4.3× more common.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1967 and 1972, its share of baby girls fell by 66%, from 1 in 716 to 1 in 2,090.

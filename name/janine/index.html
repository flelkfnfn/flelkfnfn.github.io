Janine name popularity since 1925

33,486 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Janine. Half of them were born in 1967 or earlier, so the typical Janine is about 59 years old in 2026. Janine peaked in 1965 as the #232 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Janine births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1960. Measured as a share of all babies, Janine peaked in 1965.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1949 and 1954, Janine became 2.8× more common, from 1 in 6,630 baby girls to 1 in 2,390.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1992 and 1997, its share of baby girls fell by 49%, from 1 in 6,400 to 1 in 12,600.

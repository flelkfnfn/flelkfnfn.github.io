Connor name popularity since 1925

236,190 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Connor (0.7% girls, 99.3% boys). Half of them were born in 2006 or earlier, so the typical Connor is about 20 years old in 2026. Connor peaked in 2004 as the #38 name for boys, and it ranked #166 in 2025.

Connor births per year, 1925–2025

2004 was the biggest year for Connor both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1986 and 1991, Connor became 8.1× more common, from 1 in 6,670 baby boys to 1 in 821.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2018 and 2023, its share of baby boys fell by 50%, from 1 in 298 to 1 in 598.

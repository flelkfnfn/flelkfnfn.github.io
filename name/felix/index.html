Felix name popularity since 1925

73,038 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Felix (0.9% girls, 99.1% boys). Half of them were born in 1994 or earlier, so the typical Felix is about 32 years old in 2026. Felix peaked in 2024 as the #179 name for boys, and it ranked #175 in 2025.

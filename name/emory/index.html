Emory name popularity since 1925

25,469 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Emory (42.2% girls, 57.8% boys). Half of them were born in 2010 or earlier, so the typical Emory is about 16 years old in 2026. Emory peaked in 1902 as the #242 name for boys, and it ranked #883 in 2025.

Anderson name popularity since 1925

32,591 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Anderson (5.1% girls, 94.9% boys). Half of them were born in 2012 or earlier, so the typical Anderson is about 14 years old in 2026. Anderson peaked in 2012 as the #280 name for boys, and it ranked #363 in 2025.

Elizabeth name popularity since 1925

1,398,405 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Elizabeth. Half of them were born in 1976 or earlier, so the typical Elizabeth is about 50 years old in 2026. Elizabeth peaked in 1880 as the #4 name for girls, and it ranked #17 in 2025.

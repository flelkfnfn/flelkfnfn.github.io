Corey name popularity since 1925

194,066 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Corey (4.7% girls, 95.3% boys). Half of them were born in 1988 or earlier, so the typical Corey is about 38 years old in 2026. Corey peaked in 1989 as the #55 name for boys, and it ranked #778 in 2025.

Corey births per year, 1925–2025

1989 was the biggest year for Corey both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1964 and 1969, Corey became 12× more common, from 1 in 4,350 baby boys to 1 in 363.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1995 and 2000, its share of baby boys fell by 49%, from 1 in 383 to 1 in 753.

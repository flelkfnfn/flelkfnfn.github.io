Irvin name popularity since 1925

28,273 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Irvin. Half of them were born in 1955 or earlier, so the typical Irvin is about 71 years old in 2026. Irvin peaked in 1881 as the #140 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Irvin births per year, 1925–2025

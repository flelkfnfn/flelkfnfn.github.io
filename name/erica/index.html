Erica name popularity since 1925

232,606 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Erica. Half of them were born in 1986 or earlier, so the typical Erica is about 40 years old in 2026. Erica peaked in 1983 as the #33 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Erica births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1986. Measured as a share of all babies, Erica peaked in 1983.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1966 and 1971, Erica became 6.2× more common, from 1 in 2,940 baby girls to 1 in 477.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2006 and 2011, its share of baby girls fell by 57%, from 1 in 1,220 to 1 in 2,820.

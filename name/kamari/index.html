Kamari name popularity since 1925

18,390 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Kamari (31.8% girls, 68.2% boys). Half of them were born in 2016 or earlier, so the typical Kamari is about 10 years old in 2026. Kamari peaked in 2023 as the #366 name for boys, and it ranked #391 in 2025.

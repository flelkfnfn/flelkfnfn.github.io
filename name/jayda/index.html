Jayda name popularity since 1925

21,291 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Jayda. Half of them were born in 2009 or earlier, so the typical Jayda is about 17 years old in 2026. Jayda peaked in 2009 as the #237 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Jayda births per year, 1925–2025

2009 was the biggest year for Jayda both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1996 and 2001, Jayda became 5× more common, from 1 in 12,500 baby girls to 1 in 2,530.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2020 and 2025, its share of baby girls fell by 59%, from 1 in 3,490 to 1 in 8,600.

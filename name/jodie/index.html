Jodie name popularity since 1925

29,810 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Jodie (90.5% girls, 9.5% boys). Half of them were born in 1972 or earlier, so the typical Jodie is about 54 years old in 2026. Jodie peaked in 1974 as the #249 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Jodie births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1970. Measured as a share of all babies, Jodie peaked in 1974.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1952 and 1957, Jodie became 3.6× more common, from 1 in 15,200 baby girls to 1 in 4,220.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1983 and 1988, its share of baby girls fell by 65%, from 1 in 2,940 to 1 in 8,480.

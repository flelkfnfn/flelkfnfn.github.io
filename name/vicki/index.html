Vicki name popularity since 1925

153,357 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Vicki. Half of them were born in 1957 or earlier, so the typical Vicki is about 69 years old in 2026. Vicki peaked in 1954 as the #50 name for girls, and fewer than 5 girls got it in 2025.

Vicki births per year, 1925–2025

1954 was the biggest year for Vicki both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1937 and 1942, Vicki became 5.6× more common, from 1 in 7,190 baby girls to 1 in 1,290.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1973 and 1978, its share of baby girls fell by 68%, from 1 in 1,030 to 1 in 3,200.

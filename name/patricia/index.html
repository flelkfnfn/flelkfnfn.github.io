Patricia name popularity since 1925

1,545,550 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Patricia. Half of them were born in 1951 or earlier, so the typical Patricia is about 75 years old in 2026. Patricia peaked in 1951 as the #3 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

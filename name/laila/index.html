Laila name popularity since 1925

43,197 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Laila. Half of them were born in 2011 or earlier, so the typical Laila is about 15 years old in 2026. Laila peaked in 2009 as the #135 name for girls, and it ranked #413 in 2025.

Laila births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 2008. Measured as a share of all babies, Laila peaked in 2009.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1998 and 2003, Laila became 9.6× more common, from 1 in 13,500 baby girls to 1 in 1,400.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2020 and 2025, its share of baby girls fell by 42%, from 1 in 1,240 to 1 in 2,130.

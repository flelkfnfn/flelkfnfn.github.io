Randy name popularity since 1925

334,410 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Randy (1.5% girls, 98.5% boys). Half of them were born in 1962 or earlier, so the typical Randy is about 64 years old in 2026. Randy peaked in 1956 as the #28 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Randy births per year, 1925–2025

1956 was the biggest year for Randy both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1941 and 1946, Randy became 6× more common, from 1 in 5,930 baby boys to 1 in 992.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2011 and 2016, its share of baby boys fell by 49%, from 1 in 2,260 to 1 in 4,440.

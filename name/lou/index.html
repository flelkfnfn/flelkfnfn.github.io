Lou name popularity since 1925

29,260 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Lou (88.6% girls, 11.4% boys). Half of them were born in 1953 or earlier, so the typical Lou is about 73 years old in 2026. Lou peaked in 1880 as the #125 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

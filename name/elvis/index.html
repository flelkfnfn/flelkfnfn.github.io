Elvis name popularity since 1925

16,932 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Elvis (0.6% girls, 99.4% boys). Half of them were born in 1987 or earlier, so the typical Elvis is about 39 years old in 2026. Elvis peaked in 1957 as the #311 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Elvis births per year, 1925–2025

1957 was the biggest year for Elvis both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1957 and 1962, Elvis's share of baby boys fell by 71%, from 1 in 3,570 to 1 in 12,300.
  • Fastest rise: Between 1972 and 1977, it became 2.5× more common, from 1 in 13,600 baby boys to 1 in 5,500.

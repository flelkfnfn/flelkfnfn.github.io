Gayle name popularity since 1925

64,210 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Gayle (93.4% girls, 6.6% boys). Half of them were born in 1952 or earlier, so the typical Gayle is about 74 years old in 2026. Gayle peaked in 1951 as the #124 name for girls, and fewer than 5 girls got it in 2025.

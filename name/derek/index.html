Derek name popularity since 1925

239,963 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Derek. Half of them were born in 1987 or earlier, so the typical Derek is about 39 years old in 2026. Derek peaked in 1987 as the #54 name for boys, and it ranked #284 in 2025.

Derek births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1989. Measured as a share of all babies, Derek peaked in 1987.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1949 and 1954, Derek became 3× more common, from 1 in 15,700 baby boys to 1 in 5,190.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1990 and 1995, its share of baby boys fell by 41%, from 1 in 254 to 1 in 428.

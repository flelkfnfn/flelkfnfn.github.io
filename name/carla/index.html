Carla name popularity since 1925

150,439 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Carla. Half of them were born in 1966 or earlier, so the typical Carla is about 60 years old in 2026. Carla peaked in 1962 as the #78 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Carla births per year, 1925–2025

1962 was the biggest year for Carla both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1942 and 1947, Carla became 2.2× more common, from 1 in 2,440 baby girls to 1 in 1,130.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2008 and 2013, its share of baby girls fell by 39%, from 1 in 3,560 to 1 in 5,850.

Jeremy name popularity since 1925

448,730 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Jeremy. Half of them were born in 1984 or earlier, so the typical Jeremy is about 42 years old in 2026. Jeremy peaked in 1977 as the #15 name for boys, and it ranked #292 in 2025.

Jeremy births per year, 1925–2025

1977 was the biggest year for Jeremy both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1966 and 1971, Jeremy became 26× more common, from 1 in 6,240 baby boys to 1 in 236.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1986 and 1991, its share of baby boys fell by 42%, from 1 in 113 to 1 in 194.

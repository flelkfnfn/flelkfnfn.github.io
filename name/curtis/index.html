Curtis name popularity since 1925

242,657 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Curtis (0.8% girls, 99.2% boys). Half of them were born in 1963 or earlier, so the typical Curtis is about 63 years old in 2026. Curtis peaked in 1962 as the #73 name for boys, and it ranked #985 in 2025.

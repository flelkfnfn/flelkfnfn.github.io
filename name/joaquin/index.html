Joaquin name popularity since 1925

35,040 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Joaquin. Half of them were born in 2008 or earlier, so the typical Joaquin is about 18 years old in 2026. Joaquin peaked in 2020 as the #307 name for boys, and it ranked #355 in 2025.

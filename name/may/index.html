May name popularity since 1925

18,847 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named May (98.0% girls, 2.0% boys). Half of them were born in 1947 or earlier, so the typical May is about 79 years old in 2026. May peaked in 1880 as the #57 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

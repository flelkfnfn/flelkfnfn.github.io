Marion name popularity since 1925

150,134 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Marion (67.4% girls, 32.6% boys). Half of them were born in 1939 or earlier, so the typical Marion is about 87 years old in 2026. Marion peaked in 1917 as the #45 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

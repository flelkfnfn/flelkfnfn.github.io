Latasha name popularity since 1925

30,325 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Latasha. Half of them were born in 1981 or earlier, so the typical Latasha is about 45 years old in 2026. Latasha peaked in 1980 as the #141 name for girls, and fewer than 5 girls got it in 2025.

Latasha births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1981. Measured as a share of all babies, Latasha peaked in 1980.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1969 and 1974, Latasha became 9.2× more common, from 1 in 11,300 baby girls to 1 in 1,230.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1992 and 1997, its share of baby girls fell by 73%, from 1 in 3,460 to 1 in 13,000.

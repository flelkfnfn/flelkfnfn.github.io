Declan name popularity since 1925

58,950 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Declan (0.7% girls, 99.3% boys). Half of them were born in 2017 or earlier, so the typical Declan is about 9 years old in 2026. Declan peaked in 2019 as the #95 name for boys, and it ranked #139 in 2025.

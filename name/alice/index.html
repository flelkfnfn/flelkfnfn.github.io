Alice name popularity since 1925

369,067 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Alice. Half of them were born in 1947 or earlier, so the typical Alice is about 79 years old in 2026. Alice peaked in 1880 as the #8 name for girls, and it ranked #65 in 2025.

Alice births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year on this chart was 1925. Measured as a share of all babies, Alice peaked earlier, in 1880.

  • Steepest fall: Between 1974 and 1979, Alice's share of baby girls fell by 40%, from 1 in 1,240 to 1 in 2,060.
  • Fastest rise: Between 2008 and 2013, it became 3.1× more common, from 1 in 1,840 baby girls to 1 in 597.

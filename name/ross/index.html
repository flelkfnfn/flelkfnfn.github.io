Ross name popularity since 1925

73,111 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Ross. Half of them were born in 1975 or earlier, so the typical Ross is about 51 years old in 2026. Ross peaked in 1889 as the #122 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Ross births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year on this chart was 1985. Measured as a share of all babies, Ross peaked earlier, in 1889.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1980 and 1985, Ross became 1.8× more common, from 1 in 1,720 baby boys to 1 in 981.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1998 and 2003, its share of baby boys fell by 54%, from 1 in 2,420 to 1 in 5,230.

Jami name popularity since 1925

22,410 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Jami (95.8% girls, 4.2% boys). Half of them were born in 1979 or earlier, so the typical Jami is about 47 years old in 2026. Jami peaked in 1977 as the #239 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Jami births per year, 1925–2025

1977 was the biggest year for Jami both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1957 and 1962, Jami became 2.3× more common, from 1 in 14,800 baby girls to 1 in 6,450.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1990 and 1995, its share of baby girls fell by 50%, from 1 in 4,150 to 1 in 8,370.

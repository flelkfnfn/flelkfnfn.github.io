Katlyn name popularity since 1925

18,438 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Katlyn. Half of them were born in 1997 or earlier, so the typical Katlyn is about 29 years old in 2026. Katlyn peaked in 1995 as the #223 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Katlyn births per year, 1925–2025

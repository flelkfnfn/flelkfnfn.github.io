Ainsley name popularity since 1925

20,896 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Ainsley (98.0% girls, 2.0% boys). Half of them were born in 2012 or earlier, so the typical Ainsley is about 14 years old in 2026. Ainsley peaked in 2014 as the #337 name for girls, and it ranked #483 in 2025.

Ainsley births per year, 1925–2025

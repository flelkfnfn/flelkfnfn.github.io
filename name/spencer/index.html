Spencer name popularity since 1925

126,233 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Spencer (4.5% girls, 95.5% boys). Half of them were born in 1997 or earlier, so the typical Spencer is about 29 years old in 2026. Spencer peaked in 1998 as the #84 name for boys, and it ranked #360 in 2025.

Spencer births per year, 1925–2025

1998 was the biggest year for Spencer both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1984 and 1989, Spencer became 3× more common, from 1 in 1,930 baby boys to 1 in 640.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2001 and 2006, its share of baby boys fell by 45%, from 1 in 521 to 1 in 940.

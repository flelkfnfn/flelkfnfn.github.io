Norma name popularity since 1925

231,200 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Norma. Half of them were born in 1940 or earlier, so the typical Norma is about 86 years old in 2026. Norma peaked in 1931 as the #22 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Rudolph name popularity since 1925

38,275 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Rudolph. Half of them were born in 1946 or earlier, so the typical Rudolph is about 80 years old in 2026. Rudolph peaked in 1927 as the #110 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

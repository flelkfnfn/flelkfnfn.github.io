Rufus name popularity since 1925

23,569 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Rufus. Half of them were born in 1948 or earlier, so the typical Rufus is about 78 years old in 2026. Rufus peaked in 1880 as the #88 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

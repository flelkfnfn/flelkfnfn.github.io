Ernest name popularity since 1925

215,700 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Ernest (0.6% girls, 99.4% boys). Half of them were born in 1950 or earlier, so the typical Ernest is about 76 years old in 2026. Ernest peaked in 1895 as the #24 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Nathaniel name popularity since 1925

277,160 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Nathaniel. Half of them were born in 1997 or earlier, so the typical Nathaniel is about 29 years old in 2026. Nathaniel peaked in 2002 as the #62 name for boys, and it ranked #140 in 2025.

Angelique name popularity since 1925

30,165 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Angelique. Half of them were born in 1994 or earlier, so the typical Angelique is about 32 years old in 2026. Angelique peaked in 1970 as the #301 name for girls, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Angelique births per year, 1925–2025

1970 was the biggest year for Angelique both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1963 and 1968, Angelique became 8.5× more common, from 1 in 18,400 baby girls to 1 in 2,150.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2014 and 2019, its share of baby girls fell by 66%, from 1 in 2,310 to 1 in 6,880.

Bobby name popularity since 1925

321,624 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Bobby (2.8% girls, 97.2% boys). Half of them were born in 1953 or earlier, so the typical Bobby is about 73 years old in 2026. Bobby peaked in 1936 as the #24 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Bobby births per year, 1925–2025

The biggest year by count was 1937. Measured as a share of all babies, Bobby peaked in 1936.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1925 and 1930, Bobby became 4.9× more common.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1939 and 1944, its share of baby boys fell by 38%, from 1 in 157 to 1 in 252.

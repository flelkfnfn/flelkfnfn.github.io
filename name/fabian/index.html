Fabian name popularity since 1925

44,134 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Fabian (0.7% girls, 99.3% boys). Half of them were born in 2003 or earlier, so the typical Fabian is about 23 years old in 2026. Fabian peaked in 2007 as the #253 name for boys, and it ranked #468 in 2025.

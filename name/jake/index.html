Jake name popularity since 1925

122,316 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Jake. Half of them were born in 2001 or earlier, so the typical Jake is about 25 years old in 2026. Jake peaked in 2004 as the #98 name for boys, and it ranked #457 in 2025.

Jake births per year, 1925–2025

2004 was the biggest year for Jake both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1968 and 1973, Jake became 2.8× more common, from 1 in 14,000 baby boys to 1 in 4,960.
  • Steepest fall: Between 2011 and 2016, its share of baby boys fell by 49%, from 1 in 611 to 1 in 1,200.

Geoffrey name popularity since 1925

54,276 babies born in the US from 1925 to 2025 were named Geoffrey. Half of them were born in 1976 or earlier, so the typical Geoffrey is about 50 years old in 2026. Geoffrey peaked in 1975 as the #167 name for boys, and it was outside the top 1,000 in 2025.

Geoffrey births per year, 1925–2025

1975 was the biggest year for Geoffrey both by count and as a share of all babies.

  • Fastest rise: Between 1940 and 1945, Geoffrey became 1.9× more common, from 1 in 7,830 baby boys to 1 in 4,190.
  • Steepest fall: Between 1991 and 1996, its share of baby boys fell by 54%, from 1 in 2,080 to 1 in 4,550.
